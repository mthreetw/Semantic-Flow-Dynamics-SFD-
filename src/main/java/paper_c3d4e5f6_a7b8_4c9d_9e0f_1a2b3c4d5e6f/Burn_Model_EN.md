## Abstract

Why is TSMC the only one of its kind in the world? Existing frameworks — the technology thesis, the capital thesis, the management thesis, the geopolitical thesis — all fail to answer this, because competitors with similar conditions exist (Samsung, Intel), yet no second company chose the pure-foundry model and achieved globally irreplaceable scale. This paper uses Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) v5.0 as its analytical tool, starting from this question to derive the Burn Model — a dynamics model for civilizations characterized by the Absence of Unified Core Xin and the coexistence of multiple ethnic groups.

The model starts from a single precondition: Absence of Unified Core Xin. It does not explain why Core Xin is absent — it only derives dynamics from this condition. The Absence of Unified Core Xin does not mean an empty interior: in a multi-ethnic environment, each ethnic group operates its own Positive Feedback Loop, directions mutually in conflict, net resultant force equal to zero. Zero resultant force does not mean being unaffected. Filtering has no unified direction at the group level but has local directions; Transformation outcomes diverge across ethnic groups; Collapse directions differ by ethnic group. Driving forces flow along ethnic fault lines, forming a Multi-Loop Nesting structure; each loop shares living space and injects into one another Signals that do not belong to it (Symbiotic Dissipation), rendering all loops unstable. Resources are divided across multiple simultaneously running loops. The net effect is burning — observed from the outside as high vitality, high achievement, high temperature; observed from the inside as resource dissipation accelerated by Symbiotic Dissipation. The more successful the compensatory behavior, the more concealed the Absence of Unified Core Xin, and the faster the burning accelerates.

Taiwan serves as the primary case for verification — returning to the moment of TSMC's founding in 1987, the identity vacuum at the moment of lifting martial law restores the commercial choice of "no branding" as the structural expression of a spiritual condition. Psychology's identity diffusion research serves as independent cross-validation — the behavioral patterns derived by the framework at the civilizational level are structurally isomorphic with the clinical characteristics observed by psychology at the individual level. African Americans (Core Xin violently stripped away) serve as a cross-civilizational comparison, verifying the model's generalizability under different initial conditions.

The Burn Model is the dual of the Pressure-Accumulation Model (Huang, 2026b). The Pressure-Accumulation Model describes centralized civilizations without transcendent checks and balances — energy is pressed in a single direction, periodically fracturing and rebuilding. The Burn Model describes civilizations with Absent Unified Core Xin — Multi-Loop Nesting competition, energy divided and dissipated across loops, no reconstruction mechanism. The logical trajectory internal to the model is: Exhaustion → Fragmented State itself crystallizes into Negative Core Xin → occupies the space → the nature of the problem shifts from filling a vacancy to replacement. Whether this trajectory is interrupted by other dynamics (Tradition Sedimentation, Core Xin generation) is undecidable. The burning phase is a window of opportunity. Two fates for the same cultural genome: iron and fire.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Burn Model, Absence of Core Xin, Multi-Loop Nesting, Symbiotic Dissipation, TSMC, Identity Diffusion, Post-Colonial Civilization

## I. Framework Overview

The analytical framework used in this paper is Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) v5.0 (Huang, 2026a). The following is a summary of only the concepts directly invoked in this paper.

The framework starts from two primitives — Individual, Stimulus — and three definitions — Xin, Semantic Flow, Signal — and establishes four postulates. The Law of Flux: Semantic Flow operates continuously. The Law of the Black Box: the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with the incoming Signal. The Law of Dissipation: Semantic Flow cannot be reduced to Signals without loss. The Law of Death: when an Individual perishes, Semantic Flow is irrecoverable.

From the postulates, three basic functions are derived: Filtering — the current direction of Semantic Flow determines what constitutes a Signal and what constitutes Noise; Transformation — a Signal is processed within Semantic Flow, the result being unpredictable; Collapse — Semantic Flow outputs as Signals, necessarily with loss. From the basic functions, the Positive Feedback Loop is derived: an Individual's output Signal reaches other Individuals, passes through Filtering, undergoes Transformation, and Collapses into new output Signals, and the loop runs.

The Positive Feedback Loop has no sustainable equilibrium (Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box + Law of Death). After an Individual perishes, new Individuals form Semantic Flow in the existing Signal Container environment — Generational Replacement.

Xin: the conscious state of an Individual. Irreducible, inexhaustible, observable only through its effects. The deep layers of Xin determine large-scale direction, from the Individual to civilization.

Core Xin: the deepest layer of Xin, determining the tendencies, processing approaches, and priority ordering of a civilization's responses when encountering Stimuli. When Core Xin is present, a civilization has a stable direction when facing external Signals — Filtering has a tendency, Transformation has a convergent trend, Collapse has a pattern. When Core Xin is absent, the direction of response is unpredictable and discontinuous.

The operational criteria have two conditions: first, continuous existence across historical fracture points, constituting identity; second, the civilization is willing to die for it. Both conditions are extracted from statistical results in historical regression testing, not speculation. (For the complete derivation, see Huang, 2026f, on the methodology for constructing the Chinese civilizational Xin model.) Core Xin is binary — either present or absent.

Signal Container: where Signals reside — books, classics, institutional texts, organizational structures. Signal Containers store and transmit Signals but do not generate Semantic Flow.

The nature of this paper. This paper is formal modeling, not historical induction. The model starts from the precondition of Absent Core Xin to derive dynamics, then uses cases for verification. The framework does not judge good or bad, right or wrong; does not predict time; does not track the inner lives of Individuals.

## II. The Problem

Why is TSMC the only one of its kind in the world?

TSMC's pure-foundry model — manufacture only, no design, no branding — is unique in business history. It is not that no competitors have tried; it is that no second company has achieved globally irreplaceable scale.

Existing explanations are each unable to achieve internal consistency within their own logic:

**The Technology Thesis.** The technology thesis holds that TSMC's success derives from leading process technology. But Samsung possesses comparable process technology, and Samsung chose to build a brand (Galaxy) while TSMC chose not to. The technology thesis cannot explain this difference within its own framework — if technology were the determining factor, companies with similar technology should take similar paths.

**The Capital Thesis.** The capital thesis holds that TSMC's scale derives from capital investment. But Intel's capital scale far exceeded TSMC's at the time of TSMC's founding. Intel chose to build a brand; TSMC chose not to. The capital thesis cannot explain within its own framework why the one with less capital chose the business model more dependent on Trust.

**The Management Thesis.** The management thesis holds that TSMC's precision manufacturing derives from management capability. But Japanese companies' precision management capability is no less than TSMC's, and Japanese companies all chose a proprietary brand path. The management thesis cannot explain within its own framework why equivalent management capability led to completely different business choices.

**The Geopolitical Thesis.** The geopolitical thesis holds that TSMC's strategic position derives from Taiwan's geopolitical situation. But South Korea's geopolitical pressure is similar to Taiwan's, and South Korea produced globally branded companies — Samsung, Hyundai, LG. The geopolitical thesis cannot explain within its own framework why similar geopolitical pressure produced brands in South Korea and contract manufacturing in Taiwan.

All four frameworks independently encountered the same problem: similar conditions, different outcomes. This means the decisive variable lies outside the range these frameworks address.

But the problem runs deeper than "similar conditions, different outcomes." TSMC's combination of behaviors is itself anomalous.

Lay out TSMC's behaviors: achieving unrivaled technical mastery — this requires an extremely powerful driving force. But driving forces normally correspond to returns: money, fame, power, mission. TSMC's behavior is to give up all credit. The brand belongs to Apple; the right to define the final product belongs to the design end; TSMC holds process patents — patents on *how* to make, not on *what* to make. An iPhone sells for a thousand dollars; TSMC only touches the slice that is chip fabrication. Engineers burn their health in shift rotations and don't even want their names — the global users of their chips don't know who they are.

Eliminate normal driving forces one by one. For money? TSMC's gross margins are extremely high, but that is a high percentage on a compressed revenue base — it sets the price of labor, not the price of products. A street-food stall can also have high gross margins; that doesn't change its position in the economic structure. TSMC has the ability to build a brand and chose not to. For fame? They actively conceal themselves. For a mission? What mission would make you become the best and then give the credit to someone else? For survival fear? Behavior driven by fear is conservative and defensive — not "become globally irreplaceable and then forgo credit." Morris Chang's personal business genius? One person's will cannot explain tens of thousands of engineers voluntarily entering this structure and continuously operating it for decades.

Each item individually has a normal explanation, but taken together — extreme capability, voluntarily relinquishing credit, disregarding personal cost — no normal driving force model can simultaneously cover this combination. An achievement of TSMC's magnitude must be backed by an extremely powerful spiritual drive, yet no one in the world has ever discussed what that drive is.

To understand this drive, one must return to the moment of TSMC's birth.

### 2.1 1987

In 1987, TSMC was founded. In the same year, Taiwan lifted martial law.

This is not a coincidence. TSMC was born at the deepest point of Taiwan's identity vacuum.

During the forty years of KMT martial law, the official narrative was: "We are the orthodox China; the mainland is a fallen territory; we will counterattack." By 1987, everyone knew this was a lie. The counterattack was not going to happen. The Republic of China's Chinese narrative was collapsing. But nativist consciousness had just begun to sprout, not yet formed into a substitute identity. Taiwan was stuck in the vacuum between two identities — the old "we are Chinese" was dying, the new "we are Taiwanese" had not yet been born.

Look more concretely at the texture of this vacuum. A Taiwanese family in 1987: the grandfather grew up in a Japanese-language environment, with a Japanese name, Japanese education, Japanese values — his memories of youth were encoded in Japanese. The father was forced by the KMT to speak Mandarin, told that everything Japanese was shameful, that he was Chinese, that his history began with the Yellow Emperor. The son had just discovered he could speak Taiwanese, but was unsure what identity Taiwanese represented, because Taiwanese is not a national language — it is not even a unified language. Three generations at the same dinner table, carrying three sets of forcibly installed identity cognitions, speaking three different languages.

At this moment, if you ask this family's son to go out and start a business and build a brand — a brand requires an answer to: Who am I? What name does he engrave? In what language? Representing what culture? Declaring what to the world?

He cannot answer. Not because he doesn't want to — it is that Taiwanese people of that era genuinely did not know who they were.

Morris Chang's own identity Signals are self-contradictory. Born in 1931 in Ningbo, Zhejiang, he fled the mainland for the United States at eighteen, spent twenty-five years at Texas Instruments and rose to group vice president. In a 2023 New York Times interview, he stated explicitly: "Ever since I fled Communist China, came to the United States and became a citizen in 1962, my identity has been American, and nothing else." But his behavior was inconsistent with this declaration — at fifty-four he came to Taiwan, built the world's most important semiconductor company in Taiwan, stayed for more than thirty years, and understood Taiwan's industrial culture precisely enough to design a business model that perfectly suited it. A person who truly identified as American would start a business in America — Silicon Valley was full of opportunities. He came to Taiwan. His verbal Signal says American; his behavioral Signal says Taiwanese. This contradiction itself is consistent with the model's derivation: original Core Xin severed (fled the mainland), substitute identity seized in a foreign land ("I am American"), the declared substitute identity inconsistent with the actual direction of behavior — the Signal characteristics of a Fragmented State.

The reason he chose the pure-foundry model, according to his autobiography, was pragmatic commercial judgment: "Taiwan's semiconductor technology at that time lagged far behind the world standard; its R&D, design, and marketing capabilities were all insufficient... He found that Taiwan had one potential advantage: manufacturing yield rates were reasonably good." (*Morris Chang: An Autobiography*, Vol. 2, CommonWealth Magazine Press, 2024.) This judgment was accurate — Taiwan was indeed capable of manufacturing, but not of design and marketing. What the model asks is: why is Taiwan's capability structure shaped this way? Able to execute but not to define, able to make but not to put its name on it — this is precisely the industrial expression of a Fragmented State civilization. Morris Chang described in business language the same phenomenon the model derived in dynamics language.

Then tens of thousands of Taiwanese engineers voluntarily entered this structure and continuously operated it for decades. Engineers' own descriptions are consistent with the behavioral patterns the model predicts. Morris Chang himself publicly used the phrase "screw culture" to describe TSMC, and stated that "employees' career planning is the employees' own business, not the company's" (quoted by a departing engineer on PTT Tech\_Job board). One engineer who left after two years recalled: "After joining TSMC, I realized I couldn't learn anything there. Whatever I'd learned in school was completely useless. TSMC simply needs high-quality manpower to minimize errors, nothing more." His anxiety "came from myself rather than from work pressure — whenever I thought about leaving TSMC after ten years when I no longer had the stamina, what would I still know how to do?" Twelve years after leaving TSMC, working in R&D at a second-tier manufacturer: "We successfully developed several generations of products together with my colleagues. That feeling... is something you could engrave on a tombstone. It's the feeling of having actually done something — of being able to account for your life." (PTT Tech\_Job board, 2024.) Another engineer who had worked rotating shifts for ten years said the reason he stayed was that salary and paid leave increased with seniority; every time he opened a job search site he fell into indecision — not because he loved the work, but because the cost of leaving was too high. (ETtoday, 2023.) These descriptions are consistent with the model's derivation: the structure provides high-capability validation and economic returns, but not directional accumulation; engineers stay in the structure not because the structure answers "who are we," but because the structure exempts them from the question.

Morris Chang's commercial judgment was accurate. But Taiwan's ability to execute this strategy — and to do so to globally irreplaceable scale — was not due to Morris Chang's individual genius alone; it is because Taiwan's industrial conditions in 1987 happened to suit a business that requires no credit. And Morris Chang himself happened to be a Fragmented State Individual with contradictory words and deeds — saying he was American while his feet walked to Taiwan — who designed a structure suited to a Fragmented State civilization. The fit between founder and civilization was double.

A brand is a declaration to the world of "who we are." Building a brand requires answering this question. Not building a brand means not answering it. Under the condition of Absent Unified Core Xin, a civilization's industrial capability naturally develops into extreme capability without its own direction — which is precisely the condition required by the pure-foundry model.

The problem moves from the commercial level to the civilizational level: why can Taiwan not answer "who are we"?

## III. The Model

### 3.1 Preconditions

> - C₀: Absence of Unified Core Xin
> - E₁: Capability, population, and cultural fragments intact
> - E₂: Reproduction Mechanism in operation

C₀ is the model's sole precondition. It does not explain why Core Xin is absent — this is the initial condition, not a causal attribution. E₁ and E₂ are environmental conditions, describing the fact that Absent Unified Core Xin does not equal an empty interior — the partial loops of multiple ethnic groups are still operating.

**Deriving multi-ethnic structure from C₀.** The premise of "Unified Core Xin" is that there are multiple groups that need to be unified. With Unified Core Xin absent, the unifying force is gone; the logical consequence is multiple groups each running their own loops. The multi-ethnic structure is not an additional independent condition — it is a derivation from C₀: Absent Unified Core Xin → Multi-Group Fragmentation. There is no case of "Absent Unified Core Xin but not multi-group" — if a group is completely homogeneous, it does not need a "unified" Core Xin to unify it.

### 3.2 Deriving Multi-Loop Dynamics from the Postulates

**Non-Uniform Filtering Drift ← Absent Unified Core Xin + Signal definition.** Signal is defined as a Stimulus capable of altering the direction of Semantic Flow, determined at the Individual's end. Unified Core Xin provides a group-shared Filtering direction — Stimuli compatible with the Core Xin direction have low Resistance, incompatible ones have high Resistance. Absent Unified Core Xin does not mean Filtering loses all direction — without the unifying force, multiple groups each maintain local Filtering tendencies (ethnic identity, historical memory, language preference), but these local directions are mutually in conflict, net resultant force equal to zero. Zero resultant force does not mean being unaffected. Result: Filtering at the group level has no unified direction, though within individual ethnic groups there may be directionality; furthermore, a history of repeated oppression can cause the Filtering structure to harden in particular directions — for Signals that trigger memories of oppression, Resistance rises permanently (Xin Sensitization).

**Transformation Divergence ← Absent Unified Core Xin + Law of the Black Box.** The Law of the Black Box guarantees that the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow is unpredictable. Under the condition of Unified Core Xin, Functional Convergence can still be achieved — the deep Semantic Flow directions of large numbers of Individuals converge, and the statistical distribution of Transformation outcomes has a central tendency. With Unified Core Xin absent, the deep directions of each ethnic group conflict with one another, and the statistical distribution of Transformation outcomes diverges at the group level. Result: the Transformation outcomes produced by the same Signal within the group diverge extremely widely. However, Functional Convergence is not entirely impossible — when Survival and Material Interest type Signals reach sufficient intensity, they can penetrate all Filtering and produce temporary cross-ethnic convergence (Threat-Activated Convergence); once the Signal disappears, convergence recedes.

**Collapse Dispersion ← Absent Unified Core Xin + Law of Dissipation.** Collapse is Semantic Flow outputting as Signals. Unified Core Xin provides a unified direction of Collapse — the group's output Signal patterns converge statistically, forming a recognizable collective image. With Unified Core Xin absent, the Collapse direction of each ethnic group is determined by its own local loop; the group's overall output Signal patterns lack a statistically central tendency. Result: large amounts of energy are output by each ethnic group separately, but do not converge into a recognizable whole.

### 3.3 Burn Cycle

Core Xin determines the tendency and priorities of a civilization's responses when encountering Stimuli. Absent Unified Core Xin means that when Stimuli arrive, Individuals are driven (the Law of Flux guarantees Semantic Flow does not stop), but the driving direction of the group as a whole is neither consistent nor continuous. Capability, population, and cultural fragments remain intact (E₁); large numbers of Individuals continuously receive Stimuli and continuously Collapse into output — driving force is present, but there is no unified direction.

**Definition of "energy."** "Energy" in this paper refers to the driving pressure a Stimulus exerts on an Individual — the pressure applied to the Individual by the entire process of Stimulus arriving, Filtering allowing passage, Semantic Flow direction changing, and Collapse into output. This is not a metaphor from physics; it is a concept derivable from postulates within the framework.

**Multi-Loop Nesting.** In a multi-ethnic environment, driving forces do not surge toward unstructured random outlets but flow along ethnic fault lines. Each ethnic group is running its own Positive Feedback Loop to reinforce its subjectivity; loops have directional relations of suppression/replacement between them, forming chains. The structure of the set of outlets is determined by the Signal Container fractures left by history — memories of armed clan conflict, colonial legacies, linguistic differences.

> **Burn Cycle:**
> - Absent Unified Core Xin → multiple ethnic groups each driven, directions mutually in conflict, resultant force zero
> - Law of Flux: Semantic Flow does not stop + E₁: capability and population intact
> - → Large numbers of Individuals continuously Collapse into output, but output direction has no statistical central tendency at the group level
> - → Driving forces flow along ethnic fault lines, forming Multi-Loop Nesting structure
> - Each ethnic group's Positive Feedback Loop operates simultaneously, injecting into one another Signals that do not belong to them
>
> **Symbiotic Dissipation:**
> - Shared living space → each loop's Signal environment is infiltrated by Signals from other loops
> - → Filtering structure continuously disrupted → all loops in an unstable state
> - All loops are running; all loops are leaking
>
> **Resource Consumption:**
> - Each loop consumes resources (talent, time, capital, attention, natural resources)
> - Multiple loops consuming simultaneously → resources divided across loops rather than concentrated
> - No loop reaches a stable state → no loop produces directional net accumulation
>
> **Net Effect:** Resources continuously consumed; accumulation offset by Symbiotic Dissipation
>
> **Exception:** When Survival and Material Interest type Signals arrive, all loops temporarily align (Threat-Activated Convergence) → recedes when Signal disappears
>
> **Open Question:** Whether the Ratchet Effect and Tradition Sedimentation constitute some form of long-term accumulation

### 3.4 Imitation Cycle

Special behavior of the Reproduction Mechanism under Absent Unified Core Xin.

The normal function of the Reproduction Mechanism is: micro-organizations replicate the Core Xin direction of the macro-structure, maintaining the loop across generations. With Unified Core Xin absent, the Reproduction Mechanism does not stop — it is inertial — but loses the unified original template.

In a Multi-Loop Nesting environment, the Reproduction Mechanism operates on two levels. At the level of ethnic identity, each ethnic group's Reproduction Mechanism operates separately — the Hoklo group transmits Hoklo identity, the Hakka group transmits Hakka identity — but each is continuously disrupted by Symbiotic Dissipation, depth limited. At the level of cultural consumption, the absence of Unified Core Xin causes the group's Resistance to external cultural Signals to be low; the Reproduction Mechanism converts to replicating the currently strongest external Signals.

> **Imitation Cycle (cultural consumption level):**
> - External Signal enters →
> - Unified Filtering direction absent → low Resistance at group level → Signal passes easily →
> - Reproduction Mechanism replicates the surface structure of the Signal →
> - The replicated output becomes the new Signal environment →
> - Reinforces the next round of imitation
> - Characteristics: fast speed, fast switching, shallow depth
> - The object of each round of replication may differ
> - Note: the Imitation Cycle describes behavior at the cultural consumption level. At the identity level, each ethnic group is running its own Positive Feedback Loop, mutually competing (Multi-Loop Nesting).

### 3.5 Compensatory Masking

> **Compensatory Masking:**
> - During loop operation, observable high-performance Signals are produced (economic figures, cultural vitality, social temperature, technological achievements)
> - High-performance Signals →
> - External observers judge the system as healthy →
> - No external corrective pressure →
> - Internal vacancy goes unaddressed →
> - Vacancy persists → more energy required to compensate →
> - Burn Cycle accelerates
> - The more successful the compensatory behavior, the more concealed the vacancy
> - The more concealed the vacancy, the more impossible correction becomes
> - Positive feedback: success accelerates burning

### 3.6 Temporal Structure

Unified Core Xin anchors three temporal dimensions. With Unified Core Xin absent, the three dimensions lose their unified anchor point at the group level — but this does not equal blankness.

**Past: Past Conflict.** Unified Core Xin provides a shared direction for filtering memory. With Unified Core Xin absent, the past is not a blank screen but a multi-narrative conflict — each ethnic group projects its own version, and the projections are mutually contradictory. Taiwan's Japanese colonial period is romanticized by one side and characterized as oppression by another — not because there is no historical memory, but because there are too many incompatible memories in conflict. Resultant force of zero; not without force.

**Future: Future Disunity.** Unified Core Xin provides a common direction, and that direction extends into a common future. With Unified Core Xin absent, the group level is unable to form a unified imagination of the future — each ethnic group has its own direction, but they do not converge. Planning tendency is compressed to short-term scales (electoral cycles), but this does not equal being "structurally unable to imagine the future" — each ethnic group has its own future imagination; they are simply mutually incompatible.

**Present: Present Compression.** The past is in conflict; the future disunified; large amounts of energy are compressed into the present. High intensity, high consumption, immediacy.

### 3.7 Generational Replacement

Law of Death: when an Individual perishes, Semantic Flow is irrecoverable. New Individuals form Semantic Flow in the existing Signal environment.

The Signal environment of Multi-Loop Nesting produces new Individuals growing up in multiple competing directions. What the Reproduction Mechanism transmits is not a vacancy but Fragmented State Transmission itself — new Individuals are immersed from birth in mutually conflicting Signals, forming Semantic Flow with multiple local directions but no unified direction. Each generation repeats the Burn Cycle.

The occasions in each generation's external environment that demand an answer to "who are we" continuously increase; the difficulty of maintaining a state of "zero resultant force but all forces present" continuously rises.

## IV. Verification

### 4.1 Historical Establishment of the Precondition: Core Xin from "Present" to "Absent"

**Methodological note.** The Core Xin of Chinese civilization can be established using a complete methodology, because the historical material supports it. Taiwan cannot run a regression test of the same granularity.

The path in this paper: assume C₀ as the initial hypothesis, use the postulates to derive dynamics, then examine whether the predictions match reality. The setting of C₀ is based on an independent derivation from Taiwan's loop succession analysis — that paper identifies that the space following the death of Taiwan's Chinese Core Xin Positive Feedback Loop is the Fragmented State of Multi-Loop Nesting, with each ethnic group running Positive Feedback Loops to reinforce its subjectivity, and none possessing the necessary conditions to serve as a replacement.

The following traces the historical process of Core Xin moving from "present" to "absent."

**The Crossing: Structural fragility of Core Xin.** The Han Chinese who crossed the sea to Taiwan carried the Core Xin of Chinese civilization. But the Xin of this group was already more fragile at the point of departure. They came risking death; the greater the distance from the center, the higher the Resistance to "the center." And the indigenous peoples' Xin is multi-centered; the mixing of two types of Xin reduced the purity of Core Xin.
