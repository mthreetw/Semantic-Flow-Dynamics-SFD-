# Taiwan Cycle Replacement Analysis under Semantic Flow Dynamics

v7.0 — Final Integrated Draft

---

# Abstract

This paper uses the Loop Replacement model of Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) as its core tool to conduct a multi-level dynamics analysis of Taiwan. Taiwan is an extreme case in which all analytical variables are simultaneously maxed out: five layers of colonial legacy, a chain-oppression structure involving at least five major ethnic groups, a continuous existential external threat, island insularity, time compression, and external dependency embedded within the First Island Chain security network.

Core argument: the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan is dying — this much is certain. The Election Study Center at National Chengchi University's continuous tracking data from 1992 to 2024 shows "Taiwanese identity" rising from 17.6% to 63.4%, while "Chinese identity" fell from 25.5% to 2.4%. But what replaces it is not a single Unified Alternative Cycle; it is a Multi-Cycle Nesting of several fragmented loops. Each ethnic group is running its own Positive Feedback Loop reinforcing its subjectivity, but the shared living space continuously dissipates the energy of every loop — producing Symbiotic Dissipation — leaving all loops in an unstable state. When external threat arrives, Signals of the Survival and Material Interest type penetrate all Filtering, producing temporary Threat-Activated Convergence; when the threat recedes, each loop resumes its separate operation. Taiwan's present state is not an equilibrium. It is a Contingent Structure maintained by a non-reproducible set of external conditions that happen to hold simultaneously. If any single condition breaks, the structure changes irreversibly.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Loop Replacement, Multi-Cycle Nesting, Threat-Activated Convergence, Allergic Xin, Symbiotic Dissipation, Contingent Structure, Scenario Analysis

# Part 0: Framework and Methodology

## 0.1 Framework Citation

The framework used in this paper is Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) v5.0. Definitions of all framework concepts are found in the original literature and its formalization document. The paper's primary tool is the SFD Loop Replacement model and its four necessary conditions. This paper also draws on analytical tools developed in the China Civilization Dynamics Analysis as comparative reference.

On the basis of SFD, this paper defines the following extended concepts (all using SFD's existing primitives, definitions, and postulates, introducing no new postulates): Multi-Cycle Nesting, Threat-Activated Convergence, Allergic Xin, Symbiotic Dissipation, and Contingent Structure. Each concept is defined at first use in the main text.

## 0.2 Why There Is No Explicit Core Xin

The conditions under which the China paper extracted Core Xin are: a Positive Feedback Loop operating stably for more than two thousand years, rebuilt into the same structure after ten fractures. Taiwan does not have these conditions. Democratic institutions have been operating for no more than forty years. Whether a single Positive Feedback Loop exists inside Taiwanese society is itself a question requiring examination. The object of this paper's analysis is: the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan is dying — what has happened in the space left after its death?

## 0.3 Initial Conditions: People Forced onto the Same Island

Every group in Taiwan came here involuntarily. Indigenous peoples were driven into the mountains by Han migrants. Hoklo settlers came across the strait because life on the Fujian coast had become untenable — the Qing government long prohibited migration to Taiwan, and those who made the crossing were people who could not survive at home. Hakka were squeezed into the hill country by the Hoklo. Mainlanders came as the losing side of the 1949 Civil War. Southeast Asian new residents arrived as marriage migrants under economic pressure.

These groups share no founding narrative, no common language (Mandarin was forcibly implanted by the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan), no shared historical experience, no shared economic interest. The upper limit of consensus that can crystallize from the single shared experience of "being forced here" is: not being forced again — consensus of negation, not consensus of positive content. This constitutes Taiwan's distinctive Initial Conditions.

But Initial Conditions are not a life sentence. The Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods of China were equally fragmented; the Qin and Han dynasties used several centuries of pressure to forge structure, and more than a thousand additional years were needed for Core Xin to harden. Initial Conditions can be overcome — but they require time.

## 0.4 Object of Analysis and Methodology

The Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan is the Positive Feedback Loop of Chinese Core Xin operating in Taiwan. Its Signal Containers include the Republic of China Constitution, the Mandarin-language policy, China-centric history education, and party-state organizations.

On the Unified Alternative Cycle: this paper does not presuppose a unified Unified Alternative Cycle. What exists in the space left after the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan dies is determined from empirical materials.

**Methodology:** Six steps. Historical scan to confirm the death of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan → describe Multi-Cycle Nesting → analyze Threat-Activated Convergence and Symbiotic Dissipation → mark external constraints → time problem and comparisons → scenario analysis.

## 0.5 Theoretical Positioning

### Toynbee (Challenge and Response)

Shared observation: Taiwan has repeatedly faced challenges. Point of divergence: Toynbee's linear framework (one civilization facing one challenge) does not handle multiple simultaneous layered challenges and fragmented responses. SFD allows a third possibility — fragmented response without collapse, managed through a procedural framework. Taiwan's actual state corresponds to the third possibility.

### Weber (Legitimacy of Domination)

Shared observation: Taiwan has undergone transformation from traditional to charismatic to legal-rational authority. Point of divergence: Weber is a static typology with no dynamics. Weber predicts that legal-rational authority is sustained by institutional inertia. SFD shows that Taiwan's democratic stability does not rely on inertia — it relies on the fact that no group can establish alternative hegemony.

### Marx (Economic Base)

Shared observation: economic factors affect identity. Point of divergence: Marx predicts that economic integration dissolves identity differences. Over the past thirty years, cross-strait economic interdependence has risen substantially while Taiwanese identity has continued to strengthen — precisely verifying SFD's prediction rather than Marx's.

### Anderson (Imagined Communities)

Shared observation: shared Signal environments produce the sense of "we." Point of divergence: Anderson does not explain why some imagined communities are stable (France), some fragment (Yugoslavia), and some exist only under threat (Taiwan). SFD's four necessary conditions answer these questions.

### Post-colonial Theory (Fanon, Bhabha, Said, Spivak)

Shared observations: identity formed through negation, hybridity, discursive domination, subaltern silence. Point of divergence: the four theorists share no common language. SFD translates all of them using the same set of concepts. Moreover, SFD handles the recursion that post-colonial theory avoids — the oppression structure within the colonized is analyzed at the next level using the same tool. But SFD cannot produce the kind of subjectivity description that Fanon can — the Law of the Black Box prohibits penetration of the semantic end. This is the price of the postulate.

### Wu Rwei-ren (Trapped Nationalism)

Shared observation: Taiwan is "trapped." Point of divergence: this paper shows that what may be trapped is not an already-formed nationalism but a fragmented alliance of negation. Wu Rwei-ren predicts that once external constraints are lifted, nationalism can express itself smoothly. SFD proposes another possibility: the alliance of negation fragments. The difference can be tested by future facts.

# Part I: Death of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan

## 1.1 Multiple Sequences

Taiwan has experienced five external cycles of domination — Dutch (1624–1662), Zheng (1662–1683), Qing (1683–1895), Japanese (1895–1945), Kuomintang (1945–1987). Five layers of colonial legacy stack on top of one another, each leaving different Signal Containers. Most post-colonial societies experience one or two colonizers; five layers is extremely rare worldwide.

## 1.2 Qing Rule (1683–1895): Shallow Penetration

The Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan ran idle. According to historian Lin Wei-sheng's count, armed ethnic conflicts occurred at least 60 times during the Qing period; Xu Da-ran's count reaches as high as 125. Together with large-scale uprisings — the Zhu Yi-gui Incident (1721), the Lin Shuang-wen Incident (1786–1788), the Dai Chao-chun Incident (1862–1865) — the saying "small revolt every three years, major revolt every five" is not an exaggeration.

The same Chinese Core-Xin Cycle operated stably on the mainland but ran idle in Taiwan — the multi-ethnic structure prevented a single Positive Feedback Loop from establishing itself from the outset. The Zhang-Quan clan conflicts in the Taipei area from 1853 to 1859 lasted six years, reducing large stretches of settlements to rubble. Hoklo and Hakka burned each other's villages; Han armed forces seized indigenous land. These historical debts entered Signal Containers — family oral histories, temple stele inscriptions. Signals of the Survival and Material Interest type decay most slowly.

## 1.3 Japanese Rule (1895–1945): New Environment

The Treaty of Shimonoseki ceded Taiwan. The Japanese colonial system was a new cycle of domination. Key effect: household registration, public schools, and railways for the first time pulled the entire island into a single Signal environment. The Japanization movement provoked rejection, but each group rejected it separately — the Yi-wei Anti-Japanese Resistance (1895) was scattered across regions with no unified command; the Wushe Incident (1930) was the action of a single Seediq tribal community; the Taiwan Cultural Association movement (1920s) was the affair of an intellectual elite. Resistance was never unified.

## 1.4 KMT Authoritarianism (1945–1987): Full Reconstruction and Idling

In 1945 the Kuomintang brought a complete Chinese Core-Xin Cycle. The February 28 Incident (1947) was a direct collision between the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan and the local population. The death toll is fiercely contested — the Executive Yuan's 1992 research report cited demographic methods to estimate 18,000 to 28,000 deaths; the Foundation's compensation statistics counted approximately 847 deaths and disappearances; the report's chief compiler Lai Ze-han considered "several thousands" more reasonable. Whatever the figures, the structural fact is this: the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan used force to suppress all publicly visible opposition Signals, but each group's response was non-unified from the start — Hoklo families passed down memory of February 28 within households, Hakka maintained community networks, indigenous peoples continued their own lives in the mountains. Resistance was fragmented; it never integrated into a unified opposition Positive Feedback Loop.

The idling of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle accelerated from the 1970s. Expulsion from the United Nations (1971), severing of diplomatic relations with the United States (1979) — external Signal Containers were systematically destroyed. Martial law lifted in 1987; institutional power collapsed.

## 1.5 Confirmation of the Death of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan

Continuous tracking data from the Election Study Center at National Chengchi University, 1992–2024:

"Taiwanese identity": 17.6% in 1992 → first surpassed "both" in 2008 → peaked in the Sunflower year of 2014 → fell back to 54.5% in 2018 → reached a new high in 2020 → 63.4% in 2024.

"Chinese identity": 25.5% in 1992 → fell to single digits in 2001 → 2.4% in 2024 (historic low).

"Both": 46.4% in 1992 → 31.0% in 2024.

Examining the survival status of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle from the perspective of dynamic mechanisms: first, the joint operation of the Lack of Endogenous New Signal Sources and the Continuous Expansion of Alternative Signals causes the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan to fall into Gap Expansion and Positive Feedback Loop idling. Second, the Shrinking of Old Signal Containers cuts off transmission, preventing the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan's Signals from being effectively passed across generations. Finally, under conditions of Irreversible Generational Replacement, old Individuals carrying old Semantic Flows continuously perish, while new Individuals cannot form old Semantic Flows within shrinking Signal Containers. With no new blood entering and old blood dying out, the Positive Feedback Loop of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan permanently fractures. **The direction of the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan's death is set.**

# Part II: After C₀'s Death — Multi-Cycle Nesting

## 2.1 Definition of Multi-Cycle Nesting

**Multi-Cycle Nesting ≡ multiple Positive Feedback Loops operating simultaneously within the same group of Individuals, with any two potentially standing in a relation of oppression or replacement.** Relations have direction and can form chains. The same Positive Feedback Loop can simultaneously be the new loop in one pair of relations and the old loop in another.

## 2.2 Taiwan's Nested Structure

**Outer layer:** The Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan dies. All groups share the direction of negation (refusing Beijing's rule), but their motivations differ.

**Inner layer 1 (Mainlanders → Hoklo):** Democratization has essentially completed the institutional replacement. All four necessary conditions are strongly satisfied. Anchor events: February 28 (1947), Formosa Incident (1979), first party alternation in power (2000).

**Inner layer 2 (Hoklo → Hakka):** "Taiwanese language" is equated with Hoklo; "nativist" is equated with Hoklo culture. The Hakka's source of oppression has shifted from Mainlanders to the Hoklo-dominated "nativist" discourse. Anchor event: the Return Our Mother Tongue Movement (December 28, 1988 — nearly ten thousand Hakka people took to the streets demanding the opening of Hakka-language broadcasting, bilingual education, and equal language policy). Signal Container construction: Hakka TV (2003), Basic Act for Hakka (2010), Hakka designated as a national language (National Language Development Act, 2018). The Continuous Expansion of Alternative Signals does not fully hold — Hoklo dominance can still produce a large volume of Signals. Progress is slow.

**Inner layer 3 (Han → Indigenous):** For indigenous peoples, Han people as a whole are the colonizers. Anchor events: Indigenous Peoples Basic Act (2005), Indigenous Peoples TV (2005), Indigenous Languages Development Act (2017), President Tsai Ing-wen's state apology to indigenous peoples on behalf of the nation (August 1, 2016). Substantive progress on land transitional justice is extremely slow. This layer is at an earlier stage.

**Inner layer 4 (established groups → new residents):** Has barely begun.

**Every group is simultaneously a new loop for some and an old loop for others.** The Hoklo are the new loop relative to Mainlanders, and the old loop relative to Hakka and indigenous peoples. There are no purely oppressed parties. Oppression relations form a chain: indigenous peoples decry Han people, Hakka decry Hoklo, Hoklo decry Mainlanders, new residents are ignored by all. This constitutes a complete Multi-Cycle Nesting.

## 2.3 "Non-Submission" — Reactive Response, Not Core Xin

Taking together the Initial Conditions and the Multi-Cycle Nesting structure, the underlying attitude of Taiwanese society can be described as: nobody submits to anybody. But Non-Submission is not Core Xin. Core Xin is a self-sustaining Positive Feedback Loop — it keeps running without external Signals. Non-Submission is not. When there is nothing pressing down on you, Non-Submission does not exist. It is a conditional Filtering response — when Signals arriving from a particular direction trigger especially high Resistance and especially strong rebound, but it falls silent when the Signal is absent. Core Xin is a star: it generates its own light. Non-Submission is the moon: it shines only when the sun illuminates it.

# Part III: Threat Activation and Symbiotic Dissipation

## 3.1 Definition and Derivation of Threat-Activated Convergence

**Threat-Activated Convergence ≡ when Signals of the Survival and Material Interest type reach sufficient intensity, the Semantic Flow directions of all Individuals in all Positive Feedback Loops within the Multi-Cycle Nesting temporarily align (in the direction of negation), causing Functional Convergence to jump upward.**

Derivation: Survival and Material Interest is the driver with the greatest penetrating power → Resistance is low for all Individuals' Filtering → when multiple groups simultaneously receive the same Survival and Material Interest Signal, all Semantic Flows shift simultaneously away from the source of threat → Functional Convergence temporarily rises. Intensity is measurable at the Signal end (level of military deployments, missile tests, diplomatic crises). When Signal intensity decreases → Functional Convergence falls back to baseline.

Contrast with already-crystallized Core Xin: the Functional Convergence of already-crystallized Core Xin sustains itself without depending on external Signal intensity. Threat-Activated Convergence depends on external Survival and Material Interest Signals; when the Signal disappears, it falls back.

## 3.2 Specific Cases

**1995–96 Missile Crisis:** Beijing test-fired missiles into Taiwan's waters before Taiwan's first direct presidential election. Taiwan society's response was cross-ethnic solidarity — voter turnout did not fall but rose, and Lee Teng-hui won with 54% of the vote. Signals of the Survival and Material Interest type penetrated all Filtering and produced Functional Convergence.

**2005 Anti-Secession Law:** Beijing passed legislation authorizing military force against Taiwan. More than a million people in Taiwan took to the streets in protest.

**2022 Pelosi Visit Military Exercises:** Beijing conducted large-scale military exercises around Taiwan. Taiwanese society again showed short-term cross-ethnic solidarity responses.

The shared structure of these three events: Survival and Material Interest Signal arrives → all groups' Filtering aligns → Functional Convergence rises → after the event, Signal intensity decreases → convergence returns to baseline.

## 3.3 Allergic Xin and the Ratchet Effect

**Allergic Xin:** The operating mechanism of "Taiwanese" identity resembles the allergic response of an immune system. First exposure to a threat Signal (allergen) → social sensitization (producing a persistent Signal Container: collective memory, institutional changes, narratives) → threat removed → sensitization does not disappear → second exposure → response is stronger and faster than the previous one.

The allergic mechanism has one critical property: **the intensity of response is not proportional to the objective danger of the stimulus.** Peanuts are harmless but can kill someone with an allergy. Similarly, a Beijing statement of low objective threat can provoke intense reactions in a sensitized Taiwanese society. Response intensity depends on the history of sensitization, not the objective intensity of the stimulus itself.

**Ratchet Effect:** After each Threat-Activated Convergence event, the baseline may rise permanently — repeated sensitization in the immunological sense. Mechanism: shared experience → Signal enters Signal Container (news coverage, collective memory, institutional changes) → the container does not disappear when the event ends → a new generation of Individuals forms Semantic Flows in an environment that includes the new containers → baseline rises. The height of Functional Convergence's baseline is positively correlated with the accumulation of sensitization history — the more sensitization history accumulates, the higher the baseline. The carrier of the ratchet is the accumulation of Signal Containers (sensitization history), not the deepening of Xin itself — this distinction is indistinguishable at the Signal end (Law of Dissipation).

Core open question: does repeated sensitization ultimately produce Lifelong Allergy — Xin no longer needing external stimulus to sustain itself, equivalent to Core Xin? It is also possible that Desensitization occurs — response gradually weakening after a prolonged absence of stimulus.

Signal-end observation: NCCU data shows "Taiwanese identity" falling back to 54.5% in 2018 (a low-threat period), but from 2019 onward it rose again past 60% and stabilized as Hong Kong's anti-extradition protests and Beijing's pressure intensified. Was the 2018 low higher than earlier lows? The 1992 baseline was 17.6%; the 2018 low of 54.5% is already far above this. Even in the lowest-threat periods, the baseline has continued to rise on a decadal scale. This provisionally supports the Ratchet Effect of repeated sensitization, but a longer time series and more precise threat-intensity data would be needed to confirm — especially: whether there has been a sufficiently long threat-free period to test whether Desensitization has occurred.

## 3.4 Symbiotic Dissipation

**Symbiotic Dissipation ≡ in Multi-Cycle Nesting, the shared living space continuously injects Signals that do not belong to each Positive Feedback Loop into it, constituting an External Source of Instability for all loops.**

Derivation: SFD's Loop Instability already establishes that the Signal Containers a loop depends on are changing, and that environmental change is not constrained by the loop. In Multi-Cycle Nesting, the environment of each Positive Feedback Loop includes the Signal outputs of other loops. The Signals of other loops constitute Noise or interference for this loop → this loop's Filtering structure is continuously disturbed → the loop cannot reach a stable state.

Every ethnic group in Taiwan is running its own Positive Feedback Loop to reinforce its subjectivity. But they all live on 36,000 square kilometers of island, communicate in Taiwanese Mandarin, eat at the same night markets, watch the same news, vote in the same elections. Shared life is not building something new between the fragments — it is dissipating the energy of every loop. Living every day with people from other ethnic groups, your Signal environment has Signals that don't belong to your loop mixed in; your Filtering structure is continuously disturbed. Intermarriage erodes group boundaries, producing individuals with hybrid identities. Taiwanese Mandarin dilutes the linguistic distinctiveness of each group.

Result: all loops are running, all loops are leaking, none has reached a stable state. This is the manifestation of SFD's Loop Instability in a multi-loop environment.

## 3.5 Contingent Structure

**Contingent Structure ≡ a structure maintained by a non-reproducible set of external conditions holding simultaneously, any fracture in which leads to irreversible change in the structure.** The distinction from equilibrium: after equilibrium is disturbed it returns to the same state; after a Contingent Structure is disturbed it moves to a different state and cannot be reversed.

Taiwan's present state: multiple loops all in an unstable state, mutually dissipating, none capable of reaching stability. The entire structure is maintained by external conditions. Not an equilibrium — a stroke of luck.

## 3.6 Positioning of Democratic Institutions

Democracy is not the institutional expression of shared belief. Democracy is "nobody submits to anybody but nobody can get away, so we need a procedure everyone can tolerate." Democracy is the institutionalization of Non-Submission.

Democratic stability does not rely on institutional inertia — it relies on the fact that no group can establish alternative hegemony. Democracy is everyone's second-best choice. But if the procedural framework operates long enough, it may sink into the level of Xin through the Generational Replacement mechanism already established in SFD. The United States Constitution began as a ceasefire agreement; two hundred and fifty years later it became something people would sacrifice themselves for. Taiwan's forty years is too short to confirm, but the trend is traceable.

# Part IV: External Constraints

Taiwan's Contingent Structure depends on three external conditions holding simultaneously — none of them controlled by Taiwan:

**No Military Intervention from Beijing:** The external military occupation threat has not yet materialized. This depends on Beijing's internal dynamics and its assessment of external deterrence.

**Maintenance of the First Island Chain Framework:** Taiwan is one node in the First Island Chain (Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, the Philippines), whose strategic value is determined by geographic structure. Changes in any node along the chain affect Taiwan's security environment.

**Solid Economic Foundation:** The condition that Taiwan's economy and external trade dependency remain stable. The degree of economic embeddedness affects the motivation of other nodes to protect Taiwan.

When No Military Intervention from Beijing, Maintenance of the First Island Chain Framework, and Solid Economic Foundation all hold simultaneously, the Contingent Structure can be maintained. Any single fracture → irreversible change. The scale of multiple conditions fracturing simultaneously (US-China collapse simultaneously) exceeds the boundaries of this model's object of analysis.

Beijing's dual role: first, Taiwan's largest manufacturer of Taiwanese identity — each threat is a Survival and Material Interest type Signal that activates the alliance of negation and supplies energy for crystallization. Second, Taiwan's potential terminator of the clock — military occupation destroys all Signal Containers. Manufacturer and terminator — logically irresolvable: Beijing reduces threat → identity dynamics weaken; Beijing increases threat → identity dynamics strengthen but invasion risk rises.

# Part V: The Time Problem and Comparisons

## 5.1 Starting from the Same Fragmentation

China's Spring and Autumn and Warring States periods were equally fragmented — it took two thousand years to crystallize. The United States was founded in fragmentation — it took two hundred and fifty years plus one civil war. Taiwan's democracy is forty years old, without a shared war, without a shared existential crisis.

Taiwan's structural predicament is not "who are we" — it is "do we have enough time to become 'us.'" This involves the question of Crystallization Speed.

## 5.2 Comparative Cases

**Japan:** Violent unification → hard-core crystallization. The imperial system plus the domain-and-feudal system for hundreds of years, plus the Meiji Restoration. Cost: extinction of Ainu culture, absorption of the Ryukyus. Taiwan lacks these conditions, and Non-Submission resists having one center dominate everyone.

**Yugoslavia:** Power enforced maintenance → power disappears → fragmentation. Tito died in 1980; fragmentation in 1991; the Srebrenica massacre in 1995 — only fifteen years after Tito's death. Taiwan's risk is lower than Yugoslavia's: the scale of historical blood-debt Signals is smaller; ethnic groups are already largely intermixed geographically; democratic institutions were established before authoritarian collapse. But the risk is not zero.

**United States:** Alliance of negation → Constitution → Civil War → slow crystallization. The path closest to Taiwan's. Key difference: the United States had the Civil War — four years of total war hardened Allergic Xin into Lifelong Allergy. Each Taiwan Strait crisis is a brief Threat-Activated Convergence that then recedes; accumulated sensitization falls far short of total war.

War is not the only path to crystallization. War's function is to accelerate and select — selecting out structures that cannot survive, and accelerating the hardening of structures that can. But the establishment of tradition is itself a crystallization mechanism — the next generation forms Semantic Flows in the Signal environment left by the previous generation; this is tradition. Democratic institutions operating for forty years: every election, every party alternation is a Signal that enters Signal Containers and shapes the Semantic Flow direction of the next generation. All civilizations are built by tradition; war is only an accelerator. The question is: without an accelerator, is Crystallization Speed fast enough — fast enough to complete crystallization before the clock is stopped by external force?

# Part VI: Scenario Analysis

The model's predictive power lies in: when the state of each condition changes, how does the structure change irreversibly? The following analyzes each in turn.

## 6.1 Beijing Acts

The condition of No Military Intervention from Beijing is lost. Democratic institution Signal Containers are destroyed; the Multi-Cycle Nesting loses its procedural framework.

The model predicts a fragmented response — some groups resist, some cooperate, some flee. Non-Submission is a reactive response, not Core Xin; it does not produce unified sacrifice beyond one's own group. Historically, every time Taiwan has been taken over by an external regime, large numbers of collaborators have emerged.

Correction variable: the baseline height accumulated by the Ratchet Effect. The 2024 baseline is approximately 63%. If the baseline reflects some already-deposited tendency, the proportion of unified resistance may be higher than historical precedent. But the baseline is measured at the Signal end; between it and the semantic end lies the gap of the Law of Dissipation.

## 6.2 Island Chain Withdrawal

The condition of Maintenance of the First Island Chain Framework is lost. Deterrence disappears; the character of Survival and Material Interest type Signals changes.

Path A: Allergic Xin enters the state of Lifelong Allergy — sustained high-intensity Signals may produce a stronger sensitization effect than intermittent crises, raising Crystallization Speed. Path B: sustained fear destroys the procedural framework — tearing society apart, democratic institutions come under pressure exceeding their limit.

## 6.3 Economic Collapse

The condition of Solid Economic Foundation is lost. Survival and Material Interest Signals shift from external to internal. Ethnic zero-sum competition rises sharply. If external threat simultaneously decreases, the adhesive disappears while internal tearing has no external pressure to offset it. This is the path closest to Yugoslavia.

## 6.4 Beijing Abandons Unification

The most counterintuitive scenario. The alliance of negation loses its adhesive. Allergic Xin loses its allergen — a long-term Desensitization test begins.

The Ratchet Effect faces a genuine test: does the baseline hold in a threat-free environment? If it holds → sensitization has become Lifelong Allergy → Xin is harder than judged. If it falls → sensitization depends on a continuous threat Signal → Desensitization occurs, confirming the reactive nature of Allergic Xin.

Simultaneously testing Wu Rwei-ren's hypothesis: does nationalism express itself smoothly, or does the alliance of negation fragment?

## 6.5 Democracy Overturned from Within

If traditional sediment runs deep enough, this should not happen. If it does happen, the sediment assessment is overturned. After overturning, the model predicts not a unified authoritarianism (Non-Submission prevents any single group's hegemony) but rather a fragmented weak order.

# Conclusion

## What Can Be Established

The Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan is dying; the direction is irreversible. Taiwan is a Multi-Cycle Nesting in a fragmented state, with every Positive Feedback Loop in an unstable state within Symbiotic Dissipation. The fragments share the direction of negation; under external threat, Threat-Activated Convergence occurs. The entire structure is a contingent stability under a non-reproducible set of conditions — not an equilibrium, but a stroke of luck.

## What Has Signs But Cannot Be Established

The Ratchet Effect — the polling baseline has continued to rise on a decadal scale. Traditional sediment — democratic institutions may be sinking from procedure into Xin through Generational Replacement. Non-Submission may have already gone beyond a purely reactive response and deposited into the middle layer of Filtering structure.

## What Is Structurally Undecidable

Whether the fragments can crystallize into Core Xin. Whether there is enough time. Whether Taiwan has the chance to become "we." The answer does not depend on Taiwan itself — it depends on the continuity of external conditions and sufficient time. Neither is in Taiwan's hands.

## The Framework's Contribution

Precise description of why Taiwan cannot currently answer "who are we." The same set of tools operates recursively at multiple levels — outer-layer geopolitical structure, middle-layer ethnic politics, inner-layer identity dynamics — handling a polyhedron that existing theories can each cover only one face of. Transforming "stroke of luck" from an emotion into an analyzable structure. Advancing from description to prediction through scenario analysis.

## Position in the Paper Tree

This paper is the first complete operational demonstration of the Loop Replacement model in a post-colonial multi-ethnic society, and simultaneously the starting point for the following nodes: comparative analysis (Taiwan vs. Yugoslavia vs. Japan vs. United States), depth analysis (recursive structure of indigenous peoples), parallel analysis (other post-colonial cases), external analysis (First Island Chain dynamics).

A map, not a navigation system.

# Falsifiability Conditions

(1) "Chinese identity" reverses and rises without external intervention for more than 25 years. → The judgment that the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan is dying is overturned.

(2) Taiwanese identity does not fall during a prolonged low-threat period exceeding 25 years. → The reactive-response judgment of Allergic Xin is overturned; sensitization has become Lifelong Allergy; Xin is harder than judged.

(3) After formal declaration of statehood, ethnic conflict intensifies dramatically. → The judgment that democratic institutions constitute a procedural framework requires revision.

(4) A non-war shared experience significantly accelerates crystallization within one generation. → The time-constraint judgment of war as accelerator requires revision.

(5) A society with Initial Conditions approximating Taiwan's crystallizes Core Xin within a century without war. → The Initial Conditions constraint requires revision.

(6) During military invasion, society displays unified collective resistance rather than fragmentation. → The judgment that Non-Submission cannot drive unified sacrifice is overturned.

(7) The daily-state baseline of "Taiwanese identity" falls significantly during a low-threat period exceeding 10 years. → The Ratchet Effect does not hold.

# Methodological Limitations

First: the determination that the Chinese Core-Xin Cycle in Taiwan is dying relies on Signal-end observations such as polling data; between the Signal end and the semantic end lies the gap of the Law of Dissipation.

Second: Allergic Xin is based on observations of three major crises (1996, 2005, 2022); the number of data points is limited.

Third: each layer of Multi-Cycle Nesting is given only skeletal analysis here; full expansion of each would constitute independent research.
