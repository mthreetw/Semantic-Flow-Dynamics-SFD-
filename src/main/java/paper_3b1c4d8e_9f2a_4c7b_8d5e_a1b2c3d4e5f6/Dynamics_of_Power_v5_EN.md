## Abstract

This paper derives the complete dynamical structure of power from the postulates of Semantic Flow Dynamics v5.0.

The starting point is survival: external threats exist, individuals have no capacity to resist them, and they must organize. Organization inevitably produces Rent Space — whoever decides how much security and resources you receive is rent-seeking. This is the root of power.

**Power is not a composite of four pathways. Power is the Taiji.**

The Yin Dimension: threat and benefit. Rent-Seeking through signal output within the structure. Coercive cost is positive, operable, and designable. The Yin Dimension requires Illusion to sustain — the monopolist believes he is in control, the controlled believes he is choosing, the Positive Feedback Loop runs, and Rent-Seeking does not need to rely on brute force at every moment.

The Yang Dimension: Sacredness. The direction of Semantic Flow changes on its own, without Rent-Seeking, without operability, without design (P2). The coercive cost required to maintain the constraint approaches zero — the direction of Semantic Flow is consistent with output, requiring no expenditure to maintain compliance. But the unification cost is not zero — those who follow voluntarily will each drift in different directions (P2 + P3), producing interpretive fractures.

Knowledge: the dot within the Taiji. Yin within Yang — the monopolist monopolizes knowledge for Rent-Seeking, but knowledge has a structural tendency to reduce information asymmetry, so the seeds of dissolving Rent-Seeking are hidden within the rent-seeking structure (though institutional counter-forces can delay this process). Yang within Yin — Sacredness requires propagation, propagation requires interpretation, and interpretive authority is itself an entry point to Rent Space. Knowledge is the transformation mechanism between Yin and Yang.

The Power Taiji is not a static image of Yin-Yang balance, but two dissipative cycles locked together in rotation. The Yin cycle: organization → Rent-Seeking → internal attrition → collapse → reorganization. The Yang cycle: Sacredness lands → carrier organization → carriers need to eat → Yin Dimension parasitizes → corruption → Sacred Backlash → carrier shatters → re-landing. Sacredness does not eat, but the organization that maintains Sacredness does — this is the root cause of the Yang Dimension's inevitable corruption. The two cycles dissipate in opposite directions: the Yin Dimension becomes increasingly concentrated as it turns (Rent-Seeking converges toward monopoly), while the Yang Dimension becomes increasingly dispersed (interpretive divergence causes fragmentation).

The paper's core derivations include:

**Demonstration: From Judges to the Division of the Kingdom.** Using the biblical narrative (Judges–Samuel–Kings) as a case study, the paper fully unfolds every gear of the Power Taiji within the early history of a civilization. The Book of Judges shows the Yang cycle running for a dozen rounds without resolution (P4 unsolvable); the monarchical period shows how the Yin cycle runs after the switch to a Yin structure (Rent-Seeking, the Information Paradox, unity corruption). Samuel's warning is the operational definition of Rent-Seeking, verified item by item in Saul, David, and Solomon.

**Unity and Separation.** Unity is Yin and Yang mixed together, corrupting each other. Separation is Yin and Yang each in their proper place, each operating independently. The essence of separation is protecting the Yang Dimension from being consumed by the Yin Dimension. Cross-civilizational historical comparison shows: unified structures last centuries, separated structures last millennia.

**The Information Paradox.** The consequence of Yin Dimension self-expansion. The monopolist is the person in the environment with the worst information. This is not an institutional defect; it is the inevitable product of the rent-seeking structure. No Information Paradox produced by the rent-seeking structure exists on the Yang Dimension — there is no structural incentive for followers to conceal information. The Yang Dimension may make misjudgments (cognitive errors), but the mechanism of misjudgment is different: it is not that the structure compels concealment, but that one simply did not know.

**Power Attenuation.** Commands are inevitably distorted in the chain of execution. The attenuation rate is a function of the Yin-Yang ratio at each node in the chain.

**The Succession Problem.** Humans die; power must be transferred. Three solutions, each with its cost.

**The Revolution Paradox.** The success or failure of a revolution does not depend on the revolution itself, but on whether an alternative Yang cycle has already been operating independently before the revolution. Vacuum-type revolutions destroy the old Yang Dimension but have no new Yang Dimension; the new regime is left with only the Yin Dimension. Alignment-type revolutions align the Yin Dimension to an already-existing Yang Dimension, with minimal damage. The places most in need of revolution are precisely those least equipped with the conditions for revolutionary success — the deeper the oppression, the harder it is for an alternative Yang cycle to grow independently.

**Moral Power and Military Power.** Yang Dimension versus Yin Dimension. Gandhi held the Sacredness of hundreds of millions of people; the British had only guns. The side without a Yang Dimension foundation faces exploding governance costs.

**The Carrier Problem and Sacred Backlash.** The Yang Dimension does not eat, but needs carriers who do; carriers who eat grow a Yin Dimension; the Yin Dimension corrodes the Yang Dimension; the Yang Dimension strikes back against the carrier. This cycle has no endpoint, only a reset.

**Signal Channels and Speed.** Technology and management tools do not change the direction the Taiji turns; they change the speed at which it turns. The direction is unchanged, but speed itself has structural consequences: the survival time of unstable structures is compressed from "centuries" to "years" or even "months." Technology does not decide who collapses; it decides how fast the collapse occurs.

**Structural Isomorphism.** The Power Taiji has the same structure in states, corporations, and families. Wherever there is organization, there is Rent Space, and there is Yin-Yang waxing and waning.

This paper uses the Taiji structure to unify the power theories of Weber, Marx, Foucault, Lukes, Bourdieu, and Arendt.

## I. Why Organize

External threats exist.

Predators, natural disasters, violence from other groups. Individuals have no capacity to resist threats alone. One person cannot defeat a wolf; ten people can.

People must band together. The mode of organization is determined by the physical nature of violence — organized violence defeats scattered violence. Ten people defeat one; a hundred defeat ten; a thousand defeat a hundred. Violence is a fractal structure (Civilization Mechanics Chart 2.2, Rong): a squad leader commands ten people, a centurion commands ten squad leaders, a general commands centurions, stacking upward. At the apex of the fractal is the Violence Monopolist — the individual or organization that monopolizes institutionalized violence within a given boundary.

This structure was not elected, not designed, not a social contract. It is the convergence direction determined by the physical nature of violence. Larger organizations defeat smaller ones; convergence toward monopoly is inevitable.

You enter because there are wolves outside. The moment you enter, you are safer than outside. But who decides how much security you receive, how many resources, what position — the person at the apex of the fractal. You entered to escape wolves, but once inside, what you face is not wolves but Rent Space.

**The distinction between Rent Space and Rent-Seeking:** Rent Space refers to "someone having the authority of allocation, and therefore having the capacity to extract disproportionate benefit." Monopoly inevitably produces Rent Space — this is a structural derivation, independent of the monopolist's motives or character. Whether the monopolist actually extracts is empirical observation, not structural derivation. A "selfless leader" does not change the structure — he chooses not to extract, but the space exists. The existence of the space itself is sufficient to drive all subsequent derivations: the incentive structure of subordinates, the Information Paradox, Power Attenuation — all derived from "the space exists," not from "the behavior occurs."

This is a coerced exchange. You trade freedom for security, but the exchange rate is not set by you. The monopolist sets it. You do not know the fair exchange rate (Law of the Black Box), nor does the monopolist (Information Paradox), but the monopolist has pricing power.

This is the origin point of power.

## III. The Power Taiji

### 3.1 You Can Only Operate Through Signals

[Framework ← Law of the Black Box] The Law of the Black Box closes off any possibility of directly controlling Semantic Flow. The only thing you can do is send a Signal. Whether the Signal passes through the other party's Filtering, and in which direction Semantic Flow changes after passing through — all of this lies outside your control.

### 3.2 The Yin Dimension: Threat and Benefit

[Framework ← CD Chapter IV]

A Signal arrives and the other party acts. Why? The biological drives provide two direct pathways:

**Threat (violence coerces people):** Non-compliance brings negative consequences. A can actively operate this (manufacturing negative consequences).

**Benefit (greed tempts people):** Compliance brings positive consequences. A can actively operate this (manufacturing positive consequences).

The common structure of both: **Rent-Seeking.** A extracts disproportionate returns from signal output within the structure. Threat extracts compliance by manufacturing fear; benefit extracts compliance by controlling resources. Both are operable and designable; coercive cost is positive.

This is the Yin Dimension.

### 3.3 The Yang Dimension: Sacredness

[Framework ← CD 4.4, Law of the Black Box]

Sacredness is the other face. It does not derive from biological drives.

When Sacredness appears, the direction of Semantic Flow changes on its own — not because of threat, not because of benefit, but because the direction of Semantic Flow has changed on its own. The Law of the Black Box closes off the source. Unknowable, undesignable, unmanufacturable.

Sacredness is not Rent-Seeking. When Gandhi said "we" to hundreds of millions of Indians, the direction of his Semantic Flow was consistent with the crowd's; there was no disproportionate extraction. When Jesus spoke to his disciples, the disciples followed voluntarily; Jesus was not extracting disproportionate returns.

This is the Yang Dimension. The coercive cost required to maintain the constraint approaches zero — the direction of Semantic Flow is consistent with output, requiring no expenditure to maintain compliance. The propagation of Sacredness itself has costs (Paul traveling by ship, churches fundraising), but those who have believed do not need a sword held to their throat to keep believing tomorrow. The Yin Dimension's cost is coercive cost; the Yang Dimension's cost is propagation cost. The two are different in nature.

### 3.4 Knowledge: The Dot Within the Taiji

[Framework ← CD 4.3 + Law of the Black Box]

Knowledge (curiosity guides people) spans both Yin and Yang. The same behavior — mastering information asymmetry — falls on one side or the other depending on whether it is used for Rent-Seeking or for eliminating Rent-Seeking.

**Yang within Yin:** The monopolist monopolizes knowledge for Rent-Seeking based on information asymmetry. But knowledge has a structural tendency to reduce information asymmetry — it can be learned, will leak, will spread, will be copied. The seeds of dissolving Rent-Seeking are hidden within the rent-seeking structure. However, institutional counter-forces (patents, military secrets, state secrets, monopolistic technological barriers) can delay or partially prevent this process. The spread of knowledge is not inevitable; it is tendential — the direction of the tendency is certain, and the speed depends on institutional Resistance.

**Yin within Yang:** Sacredness requires propagation; propagation requires interpretation; interpretation requires knowledge. Whoever controls interpretive authority has information asymmetry. The rabbi interprets scripture, the priest preaches, the prophet evangelizes — he knows what you do not. This begins as a Yang Dimension activity, but interpretive authority is itself an entry point to Rent Space. The moment interpretive authority of doctrine becomes monopolized, a Yin Dimension grows from within the Yang Dimension.

Knowledge is not a third pathway; it is the transformation mechanism between the two dimensions. It is the door through which Yin becomes Yang and Yang becomes Yin.

### 3.5 The Definition of Power

**Power Taiji: Within a structure, the Yin Dimension (the rent-seeking structure of threat and benefit) and the Yang Dimension (the voluntary structure of Sacredness) are in opposition and wax and wane in relation to each other, with knowledge as the transformation mechanism.**

Power (in the narrow sense) is the Yin Dimension: Rent-Seeking through signal output within a structure.

### 3.6 Why Not Four Pathways

The previous version listed threat, benefit, knowledge, and voluntary as four parallel pathways. The problem: by that definition, a dog barking to make you move aside, or a cat meowing to be fed — the Signal passes through Filtering, the output matches the direction — animals too would have "power." The definition has been narrowed, but explanatory power is stronger: the Yin Dimension and Yang Dimension are not four variants of the same thing; they are two opposing forces that transform into each other through knowledge.

## IV. Illusion

### 4.1 Illusion as a Mechanism of the Yin Dimension

[Framework ← Law of the Black Box + FW §3 + FW §4]

The monopolist believes he has power. He does not.

The only thing he does is emit a Signal. What happens after the Signal reaches the other party — the Law of the Black Box closes this off. The other party's Semantic Flow black-box processes the Signal, Collapses into an output. This output happens to look like compliance. The monopolist sees it and believes his power is operating.

It is not. It is the result that the other party's Semantic Flow has produced on its own. The monopolist's Signal is input, but the output is not determined by the input (Law of the Black Box).

And the other party? The other party believes they made a decision — "I weighed the pros and cons and decided to comply." This is also Illusion. The Decision Illusion (FW §3) — "rational decision-making" is a category conflation, calling the step where f exists and the step where f does not exist by the same name.

Both sides are under Illusion. The monopolist's Illusion: I am in control. The controlled party's Illusion: I am choosing.

### 4.2 The Function of Illusion

[Framework ← SFD 4.1 Positive Feedback Loop]

Illusion is the lubricant of the Yin Dimension. The monopolist cannot be threatening you or giving you benefits every single second. The Positive Feedback Loop runs — the monopolist believes he has power, continuously emitting Signals; the receiver believes the monopolist has power, Resistance decreases, the Signal passes through more easily — the rent-seeking structure does not need external force to sustain it at every moment.

When Illusion breaks, the Yin Dimension immediately reveals its cost. Either escalate threat and benefit, or collapse.

### 4.3 The Yang Dimension Has No Need for Illusion

The direction of Semantic Flow and the output are consistent; there is no gap for Illusion to fill. You follow voluntarily; you do not need the buffer of "believing you are choosing" — you are genuinely following your own direction.

### 4.4 The Most Dangerous Illusion

The most dangerous Illusion for the monopolist is not believing he has power, but failing to distinguish Yin from Yang — he believes others are voluntarily following (Yang Dimension), when in fact others are being sustained by threat and benefit (Yin Dimension plus Illusion). He mistakes an Illusion-lubricated Yin Dimension for the Yang Dimension.

The root of the Information Paradox in Chapter IX lies here: the monopolist not only cannot receive accurate information, he cannot even see whether his own power is Yin or Yang.

### 4.5 The Ultimate Answer to "Why Should I Listen to You?"

"Why should I listen to you?" — There is no "why."

There is no causal chain from the monopolist's will to your action. What exists is Signal, Filtering, Transformation, Collapse — every step is a black box, every step is a Forced Wager (FW §3). Break the Illusion on either side, and the Yin Dimension cycle breaks. The Yang Dimension does not rely on Illusion, so breaking Illusion only destroys the Yin Dimension, not the Yang Dimension. This is the unified mechanism of revolution, betrayal, and all power collapses.

## V. Two Dissipative Cycles

### 5.1 The Yin Cycle

[Framework ← Chapter III + Chapter I]

Organization → Rent-Seeking → interest differentiation → internal attrition → suppression → cost explosion → collapse → reorganization.

Rent-Seeking creates interest differentiation within the structure — those close to the monopolist receive more, those far away receive less. The structure was originally meant to resist external wolves, but now people inside have begun scheming against each other. The Information Paradox and Power Attenuation both emerge from here — the organization is not brought down by external threats, but hollowed out from within by Rent-Seeking.

The Yin Dimension has a built-in paradox: the organization exists to respond to threats, but the rent-seeking structure that sustains the organization is itself a threat. You entered to avoid the wolf, but once inside you find there are snakes. The snake will not kill you in one bite, but will continuously drain your blood — until the structure is more dangerous than outside, and people leave.

What is dissipated is resources and compliance. Each round of Rent-Seeking consumes the material foundation and trust within the structure. The endpoint is revolution or being consumed by an external threat.

And the monopolist has an incentive to maintain or even exaggerate external threats — enemy states, heretics, barbarians. The greater the threat, the less likely you are to leave, and the greater the Rent Space. If the threat disappears, why would you remain in the structure to be rent-extracted?

### 5.2 The Yang Cycle

[Framework ← Chapter III, 3.3 + Law of Death]

Sacredness lands → needs carriers → carrier organization → carriers need to eat → Yin Dimension parasitizes → corruption → Sacred Backlash → carrier shatters → re-landing.

Sacredness is a Signal, not a person. Signals do not eat. But Signals need carriers — scriptures need scribes, doctrines need transmitters, rituals need practitioners, cathedrals need builders. Carriers are people; people need to eat. People who need to eat require resources; resources require organization; organization produces a fractal structure; a fractal structure produces Rent Space.

The Yang Dimension has a structural curse: for Sacredness to endure, it must have carrier organizations; carrier organizations inevitably grow a Yin Dimension. The Yang Dimension cannot exist in pure form in reality. It is contaminated the moment it lands.

What is dissipated is unity. Each round of backlash shatters the carrier into smaller fragments. The endpoint is shattering to the point where it can no longer carry.

### 5.3 Coupling

[Framework ← 5.1 + 5.2]

The two cycles are mutually coupled. Yin Dimension expansion accelerates Yang Dimension corruption; Yang Dimension backlash destroys Yin Dimension structures. When the Yin Dimension collapses, it needs the Yang Dimension to provide new Legitimacy; when the Yang Dimension lands, it needs the organizational capacity of the Yin Dimension. Neither can exist without the other, but together they consume each other.

Stable periods are phases when the rates of the two cycles match. Turbulent periods are phases when one cycle accelerates and drags down the other.

The two dissipate in opposite directions — the Yin Dimension becomes increasingly concentrated as it turns (Rent-Seeking converges toward monopoly), while the Yang Dimension becomes increasingly dispersed (interpretive divergence causes fragmentation). One converges; one diverges; they are locked together. This is the core structure of the dynamics of power.

## VI. Cost and Stability

### 6.1 Cost Structure

[Framework] The cost structures of the Yin and Yang Dimensions are fundamentally different.

**The Yang Dimension has three types of costs, each of a different nature:**

(i) **Coercive cost approaches zero.** The direction of Semantic Flow is consistent with output; those who have believed do not need a sword held to their throat to keep believing tomorrow. This is the core structural advantage of the Yang Dimension.

(ii) **Propagation cost is not zero.** For a Signal to reach those who have not yet received it, carrier action is required — Paul traveling by ship, churches building cathedrals, missionaries going on expeditions, scriptures being translated. Propagation cost can be deposited and amortized by Signal Containers (books, institutions, technical standards), but will not reach zero.

(iii) **Carrier maintenance cost is not zero, and is the root cause of Yang Dimension contamination.** Those who propagate the Signal need to eat. Organizations need to operate. People who need to eat require resources; resources require organization; organization produces Rent Space (Chapter XIV). This is the micro-mechanism by which the Yang Dimension inevitably grows a Yin Dimension — not because faith itself has a cost, but because the people who carry faith have a cost.

**Unification cost is not zero.** Those who follow voluntarily will each drift in different directions (Law of the Black Box + Law of Dissipation), interpretive divergences accumulate, and the cost of unity is always present.

**Yin Dimension cost structure:**

**Threat: highest cost.** Every unit of threat requires violent apparatus, surveillance systems, and enforcement personnel. Moreover, threat has a counter-force — hatred positive feedback (CD 8.2); maintenance cost increases recursively.

**Benefit: continuous cost.** Stop feeding, and compliance stops.

**Knowledge (transformation point): cost depends on direction.** Monopolizing knowledge (Yin Dimension direction) requires continuously maintaining information asymmetry. Spreading knowledge (Yang Dimension direction) can be deposited in Signal Containers, with cumulative effects reducing cost.

All costs of the Yin Dimension are coercive costs — stop paying, compliance stops. The Yang Dimension's coercive cost approaches zero, but propagation cost and carrier maintenance cost are not zero. The key difference is not "whether there is a cost," but "the nature of the cost": the Yin Dimension pays the cost of maintaining compliance; the Yang Dimension pays the cost of propagating and sustaining carriers. The former must be continuous; the latter can be deposited.

### 6.2 Two Types of Fracture

[Framework ← 5.1 + 5.2 + Law of the Black Box + Law of Dissipation]

Yin Dimension fracture: caused by Rent-Seeking. Someone extracts too much; someone is extracted too much; dissatisfaction accumulates; the structure collapses. This is revolution.

Yang Dimension fracture: caused by interpretive divergence. No one is Rent-Seeking; everyone sincerely believes; but what they believe has drifted apart. This is the Reformation, doctrinal disputes, conflicts over the party line.

Almost all great historical schisms are an overlay of both types. The Yin Dimension's Rent-Seeking gives people the motivation to fracture; the Yang Dimension's interpretive divergence gives people the reason to fracture. Motivation plus reason — only then can it fracture.

### 6.3 Failure Conditions

[Framework ← Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box]

All structures are unstable (Law of the Black Box). The difference lies in the nature of the failure conditions.

Threat (Yin Dimension): You are unwilling; your behavior is sustained by external conditions. When conditions disappear, you will most likely leave. The failure condition is known — external conditions disappear. The timing is unknown.

Benefit (Yin Dimension): You are unwilling; your behavior is sustained by supply. When supply is cut, you leave. The failure condition is known — supply capacity disappears. The timing is unknown.

Knowledge (transformation point): You follow the other party because they know what you do not. When the information asymmetry disappears, you will no longer follow. The failure condition is known — information asymmetry disappears. The timing is unknown.

Sacredness (Yang Dimension): You are willing; there is no external condition sustaining you. The direction of Semantic Flow is consistent with output; no tension is accumulating. But the Law of the Black Box guarantees that the direction may change at any time. Judas betrayed Jesus — no sign, no warning. The failure condition itself is unknown.

### 6.4 Legitimacy

[Conclusion]

Legitimacy is simply the name for the state in which the Yang Dimension proportion is high.

"Mandate of Heaven" — having the Mandate of Heaven means Yang Dimension is high enough that the monopolist barely needs to spend any cost to maintain order. "Losing the Mandate of Heaven" = the Yang Dimension falls below a threshold; the monopolist relies heavily on the Yin Dimension; costs skyrocket; the surface is unchanged but the inside is hollow.

The reason "Heaven" is "Heaven" is the Law of the Black Box. Not something you chose; not something you designed. When it comes, it comes; when it leaves, you cannot retain it.

## VII. Unity and Separation

### 7.1 The Problem

[Framework ← Chapter III + Chapter V]

Once Sacredness appears, the constraining force and violence can be in the same person's hands (unity) or in different persons' hands (separation). Within the Taiji structure, unity is Yin and Yang mixed together, corrupting each other. Separation is Yin and Yang each in their proper place, each operating independently.

### 7.2 The Structure of Unity

[Framework ← Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box + CD 4.1 + CD 4.4]

The monopolist is human. Biological drives cannot be turned off (CD Chapter IV). Sacredness draws a line around expansion. In a unity structure, the line is in the monopolist's own hands.

**Why it is unstable: self-constraint and constraint by others are completely different in constraining force.** When you constrain yourself, and biological drives push you to cross the line, the only thing stopping you is yourself. The cost of smashing the line is zero — the line is yours; just change it. So under a unity structure, Sacredness is extremely easily corrupted (the line gets changed, reinterpreted, hollowed out), or conversely, Sacred Backlash comes for the monopolist (CD 8.1).

**Advantage: simple structure.** Decisions are fast; coordination between two independent entities is unnecessary.

```
Unity:
  Monopolist = Yin Dimension (violence + benefit) + Yang Dimension (constraining force)
  → Yin-Yang mixed → Yin Dimension corrodes Yang Dimension
  → self-constraint → constraining force → 0
  → Yang Dimension collapses → retreat to pure Yin Dimension → cost skyrockets
  → brittle upon contact
```

### 7.3 The Structure of Separation

[Framework ← Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box + CD 4.1]

**The essence of separation is protecting the Yang Dimension from being consumed by the Yin Dimension.**

Each side does its own thing: politics is not constrained to death by Sacredness's rules and can adapt to change. When the monopolist is pushed toward crossing the line, the line is in someone else's hands; it cannot be smashed; the monopolist is forced to stop.

**Cost: Sacredness is marginalized.** Once Sacredness is decoupled from power, the political side no longer needs it — the Yin Dimension suffices. Sacredness's position in the Signal environment continuously declines. Modern secularization is the structural consequence of separation.

```
Separation:
  Monopolist = Yin Dimension ≠ Yang Dimension
  → Yin-Yang separated → Yin Dimension pushes toward crossing line
  → Yang Dimension constrains → cannot be smashed → forced to stop
  → Yang Dimension maintained → cost low → buffer when threat arrives
  Cost:
  → Yang Dimension decoupled from Yin Dimension → position in Signal
     environment declines → secularization

Necessary condition: Yang Dimension carrier must have an independent voluntary
  source to sustain itself.
  If the carrier's own Yang Dimension foundation is eroded (corruption,
  militarization, loss of signal-production capacity), separation is separation
```

## IX. The Information Paradox

### 9.1 The Problem

Why can a centralized ruler not obtain relatively accurate information the way a democratic representative can? This is not a personal capacity problem; it is the structural consequence of Yin Dimension self-expansion.

### 9.2 Derivation

[Framework ← Law of the Black Box + SFD 3.3 Signal Distortion + SFD 3.2 Observer Effect]

**Centralized structure: subordinates profit from information asymmetry.** The monopolist needs information to make decisions, but information comes from subordinates' outputs. What is the incentive for subordinates? Only if the monopolist does not know the actual situation do subordinates have room to maneuver — reporting only good news, falsifying numbers, concealing problems; each is a concrete expression of Yin Dimension Rent-Seeking. Subordinates profit from the monopolist's ignorance; therefore they structurally produce distorted information.

**Polycentric structure: subordinates profit from the leader knowing the actual situation.** Democratic representatives' power comes from constituents. Constituents' interests depend on the representative making correct decisions. The representative making correct decisions depends on having accurate information. So constituents have an incentive to provide truthful information — if you deceive the representative, you suffer the consequences yourself. The incentive direction aligns with information quality.

Positive feedback: in a centralized structure, the greater the monopolist's power → the greater the room for subordinates to profit from information asymmetry → the more distorted the information → the more decisions deviate from reality → the more power needed to maintain → the more distorted the information.

### 9.3 Observer Effect

[Framework ← SFD 3.2] Even if the emperor learns the actual situation, whether he dares to deal with it is another question. Dealing with it means breaking subordinates' interest structure — the entire execution system operates on information asymmetry; moving one piece means moving the whole network. The Observer Effect (SFD 3.2) guarantees another layer: the monopolist's attempt to verify information is itself a new Signal; subordinates' Semantic Flow direction immediately changes — "the emperor is investigating" is itself a threat; information is immediately distorted again.

### 9.4 Conclusion

[Conclusion] **In a centralized structure, information distortion is not a personal defect of incompetent rulers; it is the inevitable product of the rent-seeking structure. Even brilliant rulers cannot escape — the more brilliant the ruler, the more afraid subordinates are, and the more refined the information distortion.**

Everyone Functionally Converges in front of the monopolist (SFD 4.1 Functional Convergence) — outputs all appear to be in compliance. The monopolist cannot tell which is the Yang Dimension and which is the Yin Dimension plus Illusion. He cannot even see whether his own power is Yin or Yang (4.4).

## X. Power Attenuation

### 10.1 The Problem

Why is policy execution problematic in a centralized structure, while execution in a polycentric structure is relatively complete?

### 10.2 Derivation

[Framework ← Law of the Black Box + Law of Dissipation]

**Centralized structure: decision-makers and executors are separate.** The monopolist issues commands; the commands are Signals (SFD 1.2 D3). The Signal passes through the prime minister, generals, county magistrates, village headmen, finally reaching the peasants. Every node on the chain is an independent black box (Law of the Black Box); every Collapse step has loss (Law of Dissipation).

But the root cause of attenuation is not just black boxes and dissipation — it is the rent-seeking structure. The same root as the Information Paradox: subordinates profit from information asymmetry. Every node on the chain of execution has an incentive to transform commands into a version advantageous to itself. This is not insufficient execution capacity; it is that the executors' interests are not aligned with the direction of the commands.

**Polycentric structure: decision-makers are the executors.** You execute what you yourself decided; incentives are completely aligned. You will not distort your own commands. So execution attenuation in a polycentric structure approaches zero.

**Cost: high consensus cost.** The problem with the polycentric structure is not execution; it is decision-making. Multiple decision-makers reaching consensus costs far more than one person making the call. Efficiency is lost at the decision end; recovered at the execution end.

### 10.3 Attenuation Rate

[Framework] In a centralized structure, the attenuation rate is a function of the Yin-Yang ratio at each node in the chain.

A node with a high Yang Dimension proportion: the direction of Semantic Flow is consistent with the command direction; the deviation of Transformation is small. Attenuation slow.

A node with a high Yin Dimension proportion: the direction of Semantic Flow is inconsistent with the command direction, but the output is forced to conform. The deviation is large and in an unpredictable direction (Law of the Black Box). Moreover, Yin-Dimension-driven nodes have an incentive to distort output (see Chapter IX Information Paradox). Attenuation fast; direction skewed.

A node with a high Knowledge (transformation point) proportion: the node understands the reason for the command; deviation is constrained by information asymmetry. But if the node itself judges the command to be wrong, the deviation may be corrective.

### 10.4 Conclusion

[Conclusion] **In a centralized structure, commands are inevitably distorted; the root cause is incentive misalignment from the rent-seeking structure. Polycentric structures execute completely because decision-makers are the executors; the cost is high consensus costs.**

Structural relationship (non-quantitative formula): command attenuation is the cumulative multiplicative effect of Transformation at each node along the chain of execution. Each node is an independent black box (Law of the Black Box); each step of Transformation has loss (Law of Dissipation); the deviation quantity is positively correlated with the Yin Dimension proportion at that node. The more nodes, the greater the cumulative multiplicative deviation. This is a structural derivation, not a quantitative model — it gives the direction and mechanism of attenuation, not numerical values.

## XI. The Succession Problem

### 11.1 The Problem

[Framework ← Law of Death] Humans die. No one can remain at the pinnacle of power permanently. Power must therefore be transferred. The question is not whether to have succession, but how to minimize the cost of the transfer.

Transfer cost: the chaos, conflict, and uncertainty between the old monopolist's death or departure and the new monopolist's arrival. The Yang Dimension cannot be inherited (Law of Death + Law of the Black Box — Semantic Flow returns to zero; the new monopolist's Signal pass-through rate is different); therefore every transfer has structural cost.

### 11.2 Three Solutions

**One: Personal will.** The monopolist decides the heir based on his own preferences. The cost appears lowest on the surface — one person decides. But all externalities are borne by the state. The heir may not be suitable; may not be accepted; losers may rebel. Uncertainty at the moment of transfer is highest.

**Empirical correspondence:** The Ottoman institution of fratricide. Li Shimin's Xuanwu Gate coup. Each time, the transfer cost exploded.

**Two: Legitimacy institution.** Use rules to determine the heir — the eldest son of the principal wife, bloodlines, clan law. Transfer cost is reduced by Legitimacy: everyone has expectations about the heir, reducing uncertainty. But the cost is that whoever rises may not be the suitable person. Externalities are still borne by the state, only shifting from "rebellion" to "incompetent ruler."

**Empirical correspondence:** China's agnatic seniority succession. Europe's laws of primogeniture. Japan's unbroken imperial lineage. Institutions reduced the chaos at the moment of transfer, but do not guarantee the quality of the heir.

**Three: Term system.** Fixed terms; replaced when the term expires. Transfer cost is lowest — everyone expects the departure; Legitimacy guaranteed by the institution; the Yang Dimension is hung on the institution, not on the individual. But the operating cost of the institution itself is very high: elections, campaigns, deliberation, checks and balances — all are costs.

**Empirical correspondence:** Democratic elections. The transfer cost at the moment of handover approaches zero (the loser leaves; the winner enters), but the daily cost of maintaining this system is extremely high.

### 11.3 Conclusion

[Conclusion] **The Succession Problem has no cost-free solution. The three solutions are costs that materialize in different places: the cost of personal will explodes at the moment of transfer; the cost of the Legitimacy institution materializes in the quality of heirs; the cost of the term system is continuously paid in institutional operation.**

## XV. Signal Channels and Speed

### 15.1 Direction Unchanged

[Framework ← Law of the Black Box + SFD 1.3 Signal Container]

Technology and management tools change the physical properties of Signal Channels — speed, coverage, bandwidth. They do not change the direction the Taiji turns.

The Law of the Black Box closes off the penetration of Semantic Flow — regardless of what technology you use to send a Signal, the other party's black box is still a black box. Technology changes the speed and quantity at which Signals arrive, not what happens after the Signal enters the black box.

So technology is not an exogenous variable of the framework; it is the physical property of the Signal Channel within the framework. When the Channel changes, the structure of the Taiji does not change.

### 15.2 Efficiency Is Double-Edged

[Framework ← Chapter III 3.4]

Every technological advance simultaneously amplifies both the Yin Dimension and the Yang Dimension. The printing press allowed the Church to monopolize interpretive authority more efficiently (Yin Dimension), and also allowed Luther to break the monopoly more efficiently (Yang Dimension Sacred Backlash). The Internet allows monopolists to surveil more efficiently (Yin Dimension), and also allows revolutionaries to organize more efficiently (Yang Dimension).

Which side is actually amplified more does not depend on the technology itself; it depends on the distribution of control over the technology — and the distribution of control is itself what the Taiji is determining. So technological progress does not determine the direction of history; it accelerates the realization of the direction of history.

### 15.3 Speed Changes Structure

[Framework ← 5.1 + 5.2 + Law of Dissipation]

Direction is unchanged, but speed itself has structural consequences.

**Yin cycle accelerates.** When Signal Channels speed up, the Rent-Seeking Positive Feedback Loop runs faster — information distortion is amplified faster; discontent accumulates and spreads faster; the escalation of suppression and counter-suppression in the Positive Feedback Loop speeds up. An empire relying on post horses for communication took decades to realize its instability. An empire with telegraphs realized its instability in years. A regime with social media realized its instability in weeks or even days.

**Yang cycle accelerates.** Sacred Backlash occurs faster. From Luther nailing his theses to the Reformation spreading across Europe took decades. From the Arab Spring in Tunisia to Egypt took weeks. The faster the Signal Channel, the faster the Yang Dimension strikes back against the carrier; the faster the carrier shatters.

**The survival time of unstable structures is compressed.** This is the structural consequence of speed. The direction of the Taiji has not changed — unity structures are still more fragile than separation structures; regimes with high Yin Dimension proportions still collapse. But the time scale of collapse is compressed from "centuries" to "years" or even "months." Technology does not decide who collapses; it decides how fast the collapse occurs.

### 15.4 Acceleration of the Information Paradox

[Framework ← Chapter IX + Law of the Black Box]

Accelerating Signal Channels has a special amplification effect on the Information Paradox.

The faster the Channel, the greater the volume of Signals the monopolist must process — but the monopolist's cognitive bandwidth is biological; it does not expand with technological progress. Technology amplifies the supply of Signals without amplifying the monopolist's capacity to process Signals. The information gap has not shrunk due to technological progress; it has actually expanded — subordinates can produce distorted information faster and in greater volume; the monopolist is drowned in reports he cannot finish reading; his discriminatory capacity is worse.

The reverse also holds: polycentric structures benefit more. Accelerating Signal Channels makes it easier for constituents to supervise representatives; incentive alignment occurs faster. Technological progress widens the information quality gap between centralized structures and polycentric structures.

### 15.5 Calibration

**Compression of the time scale of dynastic collapse.** Post-horse era: from the Chen Sheng–Wu Guang uprising to the Qin dynasty's destruction was about three years — but the Qin's Signal Channels were already the fastest of their time (express roads + relay stations). From the Yellow Turban Rebellion to the Three Kingdoms standoff was about thirty years. From Li Zicheng's uprising to Chongzhen's suicide was about seventeen years. The slower the Signal Channel, the longer the collapse process — not because the structure was more stable, but because the propagation speed of instability was constrained by the physical Channel.

**Telegraph era:** The Ottoman Empire introduced the telegraph in the mid-nineteenth century, originally to strengthen central control. But the telegraph simultaneously accelerated the spread of nationalist Signals. The time scale from the emergence to the eruption of the independence movement in the Balkans was significantly compressed compared to pre-telegraph provincial rebellions.

**Social media era:** Tunisia, December 2010. A street vendor self-immolated. The Signal spread through social media. Twenty-eight days later, Ben Ali fled. Egypt's Mubarak: eighteen days. From the Signal Channel of post horses to social media, the time scale of structurally identical collapses was compressed from "years" to "weeks."

**Acceleration of Yang Dimension backlash.** Luther nailed his theses in 1517; for Protestantism to take hold in Europe took decades. The printing press accelerated diffusion but was still constrained by physical distribution limits. #MeToo, from a single tweet to a global movement, took weeks. The structure is the same — the Yang Dimension's standards judge the Yin Dimension carriers — but the speed of the Signal Channel compressed the time scale of backlash by three orders of magnitude.

**Reverse case: technology delaying collapse.** China's digital surveillance system. Accelerated Signal Channels give the monopolist faster surveillance capacity; the monopolist uses technology to suppress the spread of Yang Dimension Signals. The instability has not disappeared (the direction of the Taiji is unchanged), but collapse is delayed — the monopolist is racing against the double-edged nature of Signal Channels. This is not a counterexample; it is the other side of speed: technology simultaneously accelerates the Yin Dimension's control and the Yang Dimension's propagation; which side runs faster determines the short-term outcome, but does not change the long-term direction.

### 15.6 Conclusion

[Conclusion] **Technology and management tools do not change the direction the Taiji turns; they change the speed at which it turns. Speed itself has structural consequences: the survival time of unstable structures is compressed; the Information Paradox is amplified; both the Yin cycle and the Yang cycle accelerate. Technology does not decide who wins; it decides how fast the outcome materializes.**

## XVIII. Falsifiability

### 18.1 Power Taiji

**Proposition:** The Power Taiji consists of the Yin Dimension (the rent-seeking structure of threat and benefit) and the Yang Dimension (the voluntary structure of Sacredness), with knowledge as the transformation mechanism between the two.

**Falsification condition:** Observe in existing historical cases a persistently stable power structure in which the Yin-Yang ratio persistently does not change — in which there is no waxing and waning. Retrospective testing can be conducted using group statistical measurements such as opinion polls, approval ratings, and maintenance cost ratios.

### 18.2 Rent-Seeking Definition

**Proposition:** The essence of power (Yin Dimension) is Rent-Seeking — extracting disproportionate returns through signal output within a structure.

**Extension condition:** Observe a compliance behavior that cannot be attributed to Rent-Seeking, cannot be attributed to Sacredness, and can derive an independent mechanism from the postulates. The Law of the Black Box closes off exhaustive enumeration — mechanisms not yet identified may exist.

### 18.3 Unity and Separation

**Proposition:** The cost of a unity structure is the Yang Dimension being corroded by the Yin Dimension; the cost of a separation structure is the Yang Dimension being marginalized. Unity structures structurally have shorter lifespans than separation structures.

**Falsification condition:** In systematic retrospective testing of existing historical cases, observe unity-structure polities with lifespans persistently exceeding contemporary separation-structure polities, with the Yang Dimension not having been corroded.

**Boundary note:** Lifespans extended by geographical isolation are not counterexamples (the threat did not arrive; it is not structural stability). Changing the monopolist without changing the structure is not a continuation.

### 18.4 Information Paradox

**Proposition:** The greater the power (the higher the Yin Dimension proportion), the more distorted the information the monopolist receives.

**Falsification condition:** Observe in existing historical cases or contemporary organizations that the monopolist's Yin Dimension persistently increases while the accuracy of information received persistently does not decline. Decision error rates and the degree of deviation between policies and reality can be used as proxy observations.

**Boundary note:** Institutional mechanisms for information correction (remonstrance officials, censorate) reduce the rate of distortion but do not change the direction — the mechanism operators themselves are subject to the rent-seeking structure.

### 18.5 Power Attenuation

**Proposition:** Commands are inevitably distorted in the chain of execution; the attenuation rate is positively correlated with the Yin Dimension proportion.

**Falsification condition:** Observe a chain of execution (three or more human nodes) in which the Yin Dimension proportion persistently remains high, but the deviation between the final execution and the original command persistently approaches zero.

### 18.6 Succession Problem

**Proposition:** The Succession Problem has no cost-free solution. The costs of the three solutions — personal will, Legitimacy institution, and term system — materialize in different places.

**Falsification condition:** In systematic retrospective testing of existing historical cases, observe a succession mechanism in which transfer costs persistently remain at zero and the quality of heirs is unaffected.

**Boundary note:** The term system's transfer cost approaching zero is not a counterexample — the daily operating cost of the institution itself is extremely high; the cost is transferred, not eliminated.

### 18.7 Revolution Paradox

**Proposition:** At the moment a vacuum-type revolution (with no independently operating alternative Yang cycle before the revolution) succeeds, the Yang Dimension proportion of the new regime approaches zero. Alignment-type revolutions (with an alternative Yang cycle already operating independently before the revolution) cause minimal structural damage and recover quickly.

**Falsification condition:** Observe a vacuum-type revolution after which the new regime's Yang Dimension proportion is immediately and persistently higher than the pre-revolution old regime's, and the high Yang Dimension does not depend on a Yang Dimension foundation that existed before the revolution. Or: observe an alignment-type revolution after which the structural damage is not significantly different from a vacuum-type revolution.

**Boundary note:** American independence is an alignment-type revolution — the Yang Dimension brought over by the Puritans had been operating independently for hundreds of years; the revolution aligned the political structure with the existing Yang Dimension reality. The French Revolution is a vacuum-type revolution — Enlightenment rationalism did not constitute an alternative Yang cycle; Yang Dimension vacuum; Yin Dimension filled in.

### 18.8 Carrier Problem

**Proposition:** Yang Dimension carrier organizations inevitably grow a Yin Dimension.

**Falsification condition:** Observe in existing historical cases a Yang Dimension carrier organization that operated continuously without any rent-seeking structure. Resource extraction behavior and the degree of internal interest differentiation within the carrier organization can be used as proxy observations.

### 18.9 Signal Channels and Speed

**Proposition:** Increases in Signal Channel speed do not change the direction of the Taiji, but compress the survival time of unstable structures.

**Falsification condition:** In systematic retrospective testing of existing historical cases, observe that after a significant increase in Signal Channel speed, the average survival time of unstable structures (high Yin Dimension proportion, unity structures) does not shorten and even extends. The calibration in Chapter XV already provides time scale compression data from the post-horse era to the social media era.

**Boundary note:** The monopolist using technology to enhance surveillance capacity may delay collapse, but does not change the direction. If the speed of enhancement of surveillance capacity persistently exceeds the speed of civilian diffusion of Signal propagation, separate judgment is required.

### 18.10 Two Dissipative Cycles

**Proposition:** The Yin Dimension becomes increasingly concentrated as it turns (converging toward monopoly); the Yang Dimension becomes increasingly dispersed (fragmenting).

**Falsification condition:** In systematic retrospective testing of existing historical cases, observe a structure in which the Yin Dimension persistently disperses rather than concentrates, or the Yang Dimension persistently unifies rather than fragments.
