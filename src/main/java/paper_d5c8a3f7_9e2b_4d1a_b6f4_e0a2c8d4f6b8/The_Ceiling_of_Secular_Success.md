# The Ceiling of Secular Success: Meta-Values and Civilizational Dynamics

*世俗成功的天花板——元價值觀與文明動力學*

Cheng Yu Huang / 黃正宇

## Abstract

East Asian societies (South Korea, Taiwan, Japan, China) display a strikingly similar constellation of symptoms: extreme academic pressure, extreme working hours, extremely low birth rates, high suicide rates, and the coexistence of lying flat and involution. The mainstream academic explanations — developmental state theory, compressed modernity, Confucian culture theory — each account for part of the path, but none can explain why the Philippines and Indonesia, which share similar conditions, did not take the same road.

This paper proposes a dynamics model. Modernization is itself a Positive Feedback Loop: work → income → improved quality of life → higher expectations → more work. Once activated, this loop passes through three stages (Activation → Absorption → Saturation) and continuously absorbs all available resources — including family, reproduction, health, and meaning. Whether the loop grinds people down depends on whether a Transcendental Mechanism exists: a Signal source the loop cannot absorb, pointing to something outside the loop's domain of computation. Among all human institutions, only religion has a core operating logic that lies outside the economic system's domain of computation — other institutions can be solved with money; religion cannot.

Using the formal tools of Semantic Flow Dynamics, this paper defines the dynamic properties of the Transcendental Mechanism, builds a formal model of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop, derives three conditional paths (Transcendental Mechanism exists with direction ≠ loop / does not exist / direction = loop), and cross-references each against empirical data from seventeen East and Southeast Asian economies. The result: 16/17 matches, zero counterexamples, plus one prospective test case (Mongolia — Symptom Cleavage, attached to a mismatch condition through Redundancy indicators). Malaysia provides the single strongest piece of evidence: same country, same institutions — Malay (Muslim) fertility rate 2.1, Chinese (no Transcendental Mechanism) fertility rate 0.8. Singapore replicates the same cleavage without the preferential policies for Malays (Malay 1.58 vs. Chinese 0.83), and provides a discriminant test by cutting within the Indian ethnic group along religious lines.

This paper is the upstream framework for the Critique of Chinese Education (Cheng Yu Huang, 2026). The four forces analyzed in the education critique are able to couple because the meta-values allow it — nothing presses down on secular success from above.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Positive Feedback Loop, Transcendental Mechanism, modernization, involution, meta-values, fertility rate

## I. The Problem

East Asian societies display a highly similar cluster of symptoms. South Korea's fertility rate is 0.72 (2023), the world's lowest; its suicide rate is 28.6 per 100,000, among the world's highest. Taiwan's fertility rate is 0.86. Japan's is 1.2, with examination hell deeply entrenched. China's is 1.18, with 996 work culture, gaokao pressure, and the lying-flat movement. Singapore's is 1.04. In Vietnam, 1.16 million people compete in a single national college entrance examination with a 30% admission rate; exam season is called "suicide season."

The mainstream academic explanations each make a contribution, but each has a blind spot.

**Developmental state theory** (Johnson, Amsden, Wade) explains how East Asian states mobilized state power to drive industrialization, but cannot explain why the Philippines, which underwent similarly authoritarian development, did not take the same path.

**Compressed modernity** (Chang Kyung-Sup) observes that East Asia compressed into a very short period a modernization process that took the West several centuries, and that this compression produced distortion. But "compression" is a speed variable, not a direction variable — why did compression result in involution rather than something else?

**Confucian culture theory** is intuitively closest, but too crude. Nearly thirty percent of South Korea's population is Christian, yet its involution is more severe than Taiwan's, which has a stronger Confucian character — a direct refutation of the simple causal chain "Confucianism → involution." Vietnam bears deep Confucian influence but is not part of the traditional definition of the Confucian sphere, yet walked an almost identical path.

The shared limitation of these explanations is that they search for causes at the institutional and cultural levels — but institutions and culture are themselves shaped by something deeper. A deeper explanatory variable is needed.

The question this paper asks: what determines whether a society, in the course of modernization, grinds its people down?

## III. The Model

### 3.1 The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop

Modernization is not an event — it is a Positive Feedback Loop.

**Methodological declaration.** The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is an empirical generalization of observable Signal patterns, not a derivation from postulates. The framework provides two things here: a formal guarantee — the possibility of a population-level positive feedback structure, its necessary instability, and its unsustainability are underwritten by the postulates; and a lexical constraint — this paper makes claims only about the Signal end (working hours, expenditure, fertility rates, attendance rates), not about the interior of Semantic Flow. In terms of the framework's boundary between the formal and empirical layers, the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is at the same level as the framework's driver typology: non-exhaustive, not necessarily valid, and rejectable by the reader without affecting the formal layer.

```
Step₀: Work → Income
Step₁: Income → Improved quality of life (improved material conditions)
Step₂: Improved quality of life → Rising expectations (adaptive shift)
Step₃: Rising expectations → More work (to maintain or surpass current level)
Step₄: → Return to Step₀
```

Dynamic properties of the positive feedback:

**Self-Reinforcement.** The raised expectations of each cycle become the starting point for the next. Expectations do not statistically fall — adaptive shift is a population-level statistical regularity (independently supported by the behavioral economics literature on hedonic adaptation and the Easterlin paradox family), not a formal necessity; the Law of the Black Box prohibits functional claims about the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow, and this paper makes no such claims — each link of the loop reads as a statistical tendency, with residuals always present. In a statistical sense: yesterday's luxury is today's necessity; today's necessity is tomorrow's baseline.

**Resource Absorption.** The loop continues to operate; its absorptive power continuously expands. It begins by absorbing leisure time, then family time, then reproductive decisions, then health, then the pursuit of meaning. Each resource absorbed is converted into fuel for the loop — family time is converted into overtime, reproduction is delayed to maintain competitiveness, health is consumed to sustain output.

**Irreversibility.** Absorbed resources are not automatically released. The reproductive window, once passed, is passed; health, once depleted, does not automatically recover; a sense of meaning, once displaced, does not automatically return.

In the framework's terms: the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is a population-level Positive Feedback Loop. Large numbers of Individuals' Semantic Flows operate in similar Signal environments — the visible returns of rising income constitute high-frequency Signals, reinforcing the Semantic Flow direction of "more work → better life," and output Signals (consumption behavior, work investment, educational investment) further reinforce the Signal environment; the loop is self-sustaining.

**Activation condition: absorption of Redundancy.** An individual's time and energy are not entirely available for work. Family, children, parents, neighborhood, social obligations — these occupy individual resources and prevent individuals from investing everything in the loop. These are Redundancy. Modernization is a values shift: it tells you these things are burdens; shed them, go to work, earn money, buy efficiency. This values shift takes time. While the shift is incomplete, individuals who cannot cope can retreat to tradition — go home for meals, be caught by parents, be supported by neighbors. Once the shift is complete, fallback routes disappear — the traditional catch-net mechanisms no longer exist; fail to adapt and you are discarded; if you cannot hold on, it is death. The Full Activation of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is not the moment modernization begins — it is the moment Redundancy is fully absorbed and fallback routes have disappeared.

### 3.2 Loop Stages

The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop does not run at full speed from the moment it activates. It has Loop Stages, and the stage determines the degree to which symptoms manifest.

**Activation Stage.** The loop begins operating, but traditional Redundancy remains abundant. Family structures are intact, community networks function, and individuals who cannot cope still have fallback routes. The loop's direction is already set (secular success has begun to occupy the top position in the value hierarchy), but its absorptive force has not yet fully deployed. Symptoms have not yet fully appeared — academic pressure may already be present, but suicide rates are still low because fallback routes remain. Vietnam is currently in this stage.

**Absorption Stage.** The loop absorbs Redundancy on a large scale. Family structures are eroded (nuclear families replacing extended families), community networks dissolve (urbanization), and traditional catch-net mechanisms gradually disappear. Symptoms begin appearing on a large scale, but some individuals can still hang on through residual Redundancy. China is currently in the transitional zone between this stage and the next — 996 work culture is already widespread, but fallback routes such as rural hometowns and parental support have not yet entirely disappeared.

**Saturation Stage.** Redundancy is exhausted; fallback routes have disappeared. The loop runs without resistance, absorbing all available resources. Full symptom eruption: extremely low fertility rates, high suicide rates, pervasive psychological crisis. South Korea and Japan are in this stage.

**Compression effect.** Stages can be compressed. The more violent the compression, the more acute the symptoms — because individuals do not have time to build new coping mechanisms before the old ones are destroyed. But compression is not uniform: the loop may advance first through some symptom channels (educational competition, unemployment, suicide) while Redundancy stock still shields others (family, reproduction). Mongolia is a typical case of Symptom Cleavage (see the Mongolia note in 4.2).

The stage concept resolves a specific diagnostic problem: Vietnam's exam pressure is already in place but its suicide rate is still low — not because the pressure is not intense, but because it is still in the transition from Activation Stage to Absorption Stage: the direction is set, but Redundancy has not yet been exhausted.

**Independent operationalization of Redundancy.** Stage theory has a gap that must be plugged. If "what stage a society is in" is back-inferred from symptoms (symptoms incomplete → therefore still in an early stage → therefore incomplete symptoms are normal), then any counterexample can be absorbed by "still on the way," and stage theory degenerates into an unfalsifiable free parameter. The fix: stages must be determined by variables other than symptoms. The definition of stage already is the Redundancy Stock — how much fallback route remains — so measure the fallback directly.

Fallback routes are two things, each corresponding to a ready-made indicator. First, is there someone to catch you: the Gallup World Poll annually asks respondents in each country, "If you were in trouble, do you have relatives or friends you can count on to help you whenever you need them?" (yes/no, national average, published in the World Happiness Report) — a direct measurement of the catch-net mechanism. Second, does the parental home still exist: the multi-generational co-residence rate (United Nations Database on Household Size and Composition) — if living together, a fallback exists to retreat to; if separated, the fallback route is physically absent.

Usage rule: whenever making a stage determination for a case, the case's Redundancy Stock indicator values must be listed first; only with supporting indicators may the determination be made; the absence of symptoms must not be used to back-infer the stage.

Filled values (Gallup social support, most recently available year). First, the comparison rule: this question asks whether someone "can" help — responses depend jointly on the willingness and the resource capacity of the kin network. In poorer societies, relatives may be willing to help but unable — the value is suppressed, which does not mean the family catch-net structure does not exist. Therefore a single cross-developmental-level ranking is meaningless; the correct use is horizontal comparison within comparable clusters and tracking change over time within the same country. The cluster for which the Redundancy threshold does explanatory work is societies with "non-functioning Transcendental Mechanism and operating Modernization Positive Feedback Loop." Values within this cluster: Mongolia 0.938 (reaching 0.951 in 2022, top-tier globally, on par with Iceland) > Japan 0.842 > Hong Kong 0.803 > China 0.797 > South Korea 0.759 (OECD same-source data, 2016) — South Korea is at the bottom of its cluster, consistent with its symptoms being the most severe. Vietnam is pending; expected to be higher than China. Societies with effective Transcendental Mechanisms and low Loop Strength are listed for reference (Indonesia 0.781, Malaysia 0.779, Cambodia 0.738, Myanmar 0.685, Laos 0.678), but do not participate in the Redundancy threshold argument — the low involution in these societies is accounted for by the Transcendental Mechanism and low Loop Strength, and their values are also depressed by the capability-suppression effect at the poverty end, making direct comparison with affluent societies inappropriate. Directional evidence on multi-generational co-residence: cross-generational co-residence has continuously declined in Japan, Taiwan, and South Korea; in China and Vietnam, despite decades of rapid development, cross-generational co-residence has barely declined; in Vietnam's 2020 national survey, only 8.6% of elderly people lived alone, and 74% wished to live with or near their children; in South Korea, households containing elderly members average only 2.4 people, among the world's lowest.

The discriminatory power of this measurement can be demonstrated with two endpoints: Mongolia 0.94 vs. South Korea 0.76 — both are societies in which the Transcendental Mechanism has been destroyed or is non-functioning, yet their fertility rates are 2.7 and 0.7 respectively. The difference is not in the Transcendental Mechanism (neither has one) but in fallback routes: in one society everyone knows that at worst they can go back to the steppe and herd sheep without starving; in the other, falling means falling with no floor. The Redundancy indicator quantifies this difference. This paper predicts that the Redundancy ranking is Vietnam > China > South Korea, consistent with the stage determinations for the three.

This operationalization simultaneously generates a falsification condition: if a society's dual Redundancy indicators fall below the East Asian average (fallback routes have disappeared) yet involution symptoms persistently fail to manifest — stage theory is wrong and 3.2 must be revised (see Section VII ledger F5).

**Independent operationalization of Loop Strength.** Similarly, the claim that "the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop has not activated" (North Korea, Maoist China) cannot remain at the descriptive level. Loop Strength is proxied by three ready-made statistics: gross tertiary enrollment rate, private education expenditure as a share of GDP, and wage employment as a share of the labor force. The determination rule is the same as for Redundancy: fill the indicators first, then make the claim that "the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop has not activated"; every use of this exemption must be accompanied by indicator evidence.

### 3.3 The Internal Logic of the Loop: Why It Cannot Stop Itself

The Positive Feedback Loop has no built-in stopping mechanism.

Material needs have a satiation point — once full, one does not wish to eat more. But what the Modernization Loop drives is not material needs per se, but the continuous rise of Expectation Direction. Expectations are the direction of Semantic Flow, not material conditions — Semantic Flow has no satiation point (Law of Flux: Semantic Flow operates continuously; there is no moment of stillness).

An individual may feel "enough," but this feeling itself is absorbed by the loop. You feel "enough," but your child needs tutoring fees, your mortgage must be paid, your colleagues are working overtime and if you do not you fall behind — the Signal of "enough" is drowned out by the Signals produced by the loop. The individual's Semantic Flow encounters the loop's Signal environment, and the result of Filtering is: the Resistance to "enough" is extremely high; the Resistance to "not enough" is extremely low.

The loop does not stop itself. It only stops in two situations: interrupted by an external force, or collapsing from fuel exhaustion. Lying flat is a collapse, not a stopping — after being ground down, a person exits the loop, but the loop itself continues to run, merely without that person's fuel.

Framework alignment note: "cannot stop itself" refers to the loop having no built-in braking mechanism, not the claim that it is sustainable. The framework's instability inference (Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box + Law of Death) guarantees that residuals within the loop continuously accumulate — no Positive Feedback Loop can be perpetually sustained; only the duration of its persistence differs. What this section claims is the absence of braking; what the framework guarantees is the impossibility of perpetuation. The two are compatible.

### 3.4 The Transcendental Mechanism

**Methodological declaration.** This paper does not study religion. What this paper studies is whether the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is interrupted, what interrupts it, and what the dynamic mechanism of interruption is. Empirically, nearly everything capable of interrupting the loop is religious in nature — this is an empirical finding, not a definitional presupposition. The definition of the Transcendental Mechanism is purely dynamic: a Signal source the loop cannot absorb. Whether it happens to be the Islamic Five Pillars or some non-religious institution, the framework does not care. The framework cares only about three operational questions (see the determination procedure below).

**Definition.** The Transcendental Mechanism is a Signal source the loop cannot absorb. It points toward something outside the loop's domain of computation — the loop cannot convert it into fuel; when it encounters it, it can only stop.

**Why almost only religion can do this.** The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop absorbs by converting resources into variables within the economic system. This means: anything whose operating logic lies within the economic system can ultimately be absorbed by the loop. Laws can be lobbied to change, circumvented, or purchased. Unions can be bought off or dismantled. Welfare systems run on tax revenues and can be cut under fiscal pressure. Labor laws can be gutted by 996 work culture. The operating logic of these institutions is resource allocation — and what the loop is best at is precisely reallocating resources. Money can enter the operating logic of these institutions and convert them into fuel or circumvent them.

Religion is different — not because it is "more noble," but because its operating logic lies outside the economic system. You cannot buy "the state of your soul before Allah" with money. You cannot substitute overtime for the Ramadan fast. You cannot cancel the Sabbath with GDP growth. When the loop encounters religion, it finds no conversion pathway — both sides operate in different currencies. This is the structural foundation of Inabsorbability: among all human institutions, only religion has a core operating logic that lies outside the domain of computation of the economic system.

**A clarifying note on folk religious practice.** The Transcendental Mechanism discussed in this paper is not the transactional folk worship common in many societies. Transactional folk worship — burning incense in exchange for divine protection, continuing to worship if it proves efficacious, switching temples if it does not — has a need-driven operating logic; the loop can absorb it, because both sides use the same logic. The supernatural is not the same as the transcendent. Praying to Mazu for wealth is supernatural, but not transcendent — its direction points inward, back toward the loop.

The Transcendental Mechanism discussed in this paper is the kind of thing before which the topmost position in the secular power hierarchy must bow down. The kings of Europe were required to kneel before God. The prime minister of Indonesia, in the middle of a meeting, kneels to pray when prayer time arrives. This is not personal piety — it is structure. The ceiling of secular success is not an abstract concept; it is a physical action. When the most powerful people in a society are required to stop before something, that something lies outside the loop's domain of computation. The loop cannot absorb it, because the loop's strongest forces (power, money) are required to stop when they encounter it.

Dynamic properties:

**Inabsorbability.** The loop absorbs resources by converting them into variables in the "more work → better life" equation. A family can be converted (a child's educational investment → future income), health can be converted (the body as a tool of work), but "the state of your soul before God" cannot be converted into a function of income. It lies outside the loop's domain of computation. When the loop encounters it, no conversion pathway is available.

**Periodicity.** The interruption effect of the Transcendental Mechanism depends on frequency. Islam's Five Pillars occur five times per day — five times each day, a person is pulled out of the loop and reminded that "your relationship with Allah is more important than your work." Catholic Mass occurs once per week. The Theravada ordination tradition provides at least one deep interruption in a lifetime, but the frequency of daily monastic interaction is low. The higher the frequency, the more often the loop is interrupted, and the less the degree to which resources are absorbed.

**Directionality.** Whether the Transcendental Mechanism truly interrupts the loop depends on whether its direction differs from the loop's direction. If the direction of the Transcendental Mechanism's Signal aligns with secular success — "believe in God and you will prosper" (prosperity gospel), "striving for the nation is the highest value" (state ideology occupying the transcendent position) — then it not only fails to interrupt the loop but actually provides additional driving force. This is worse than the absence of a Transcendental Mechanism — because it simultaneously occupies the transcendent position (preventing genuine transcendent Signals from entering) and confers transcendent authority on secular competition.

**Costliness.** Interrupting the loop is not free. The Transcendental Mechanism pulls people out of the loop; the loop thereby loses fuel, and worldly efficiency decreases. This is a structural exchange, not a side effect. The cost paid by the Islamic world for the Five Pillars is a loss in productivity — stopping everything five times a day to pray is a direct sacrifice to the Modernization Loop. The cost paid by the Philippines for Catholicism is lagging economic development and a large population working abroad as overseas laborers. If you want a ceiling to press down on secular success, you must bear the consequences of secular success being pressed down.

This is the logic of sacrifice. Every society is making sacrifices — the question is to what. You sacrifice modernization efficiency to the Transcendental Mechanism, the loop is interrupted, people are not ground down, but the economic cost is real. You sacrifice the Transcendental Mechanism to the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop, the loop runs without resistance, the economy grows rapidly, but people are ground down. You cannot give to neither — the loop and the Transcendental Mechanism contend for the same resources (a person's time, energy, attention); there is no equilibrium in which neither side pays a cost. You cannot deceive your way through — prosperity theology tries to have both ("believe in God and prosper"), and the result is that transcendence is sacrificed (the direction is twisted) while the loop is not interrupted, with the cost borne by the faithful. You cannot have it both ways — this is precisely where Korean Christianity fails, but the precise form of this failure must be stated clearly: Korean churches do not impose a low cost (pre-dawn morning prayer services, strongly enforced tithing, multiple weekly meetings); the problem is the direction of the cost — prayer content is directed toward loop objectives (pre-exam prayer sessions are the emblematic form), morning prayer does not reduce tutoring hours, tithing does not squeeze educational armament expenditure, and the cost is converted into investment for the loop rather than being deducted from secular success. The sacrifice criterion is thereby precisely established: not "whether it extracts a cost" but "whether the cost it extracts is deducted from secular success." A mechanism that channels religious fervor into competition is an afterburner for involution, not a ceiling.

**Determination procedure.** For any specific case, determining the status of the Transcendental Mechanism requires answering only three operational questions:

One: **Is there a Signal source that periodically pulls people out of the loop?** Islamic Five Pillars: yes, five times per day. Catholic Mass: yes, once per week. Taiwanese folk religious offering (baibai): no — the timing and frequency of folk worship is personally flexible, does not constitute a systematic interruption, and individuals may choose not to attend without social consequence.

Two: **Does the direction of this Signal point outside the loop?** Ramadan fasting — stopping work to face Allah: yes, direction ≠ loop. Prosperity gospel telling you that faith will bring wealth: no, direction = loop. Praying to Mazu for prosperity: no, direction = loop.

Three: **Can the loop convert it into its own fuel?** The Five Pillars cannot be skipped to work overtime (community pressure and religious obligation): cannot be absorbed. Japanese shrine visits can be compressed to one New Year visit: can be absorbed. Funeral Buddhism appears only when someone dies: already absorbed.

The combination of answers to the three questions corresponds to three path states. If all three are affirmative (periodic interruption exists, direction ≠ loop, cannot be absorbed), the determination is that the Transcendental Mechanism exists and is effective. If any is negative, the determination is non-existent or ineffective. If direction = loop, the determination is Path Three.

**Why East Asian faith traditions do not constitute a Transcendental Mechanism.** East Asia is not without faith — but the major faith traditions of East Asia fail all three operational questions.

Japanese Buddhism (Mahayana): after the Meiji Restoration, the temple system was incorporated as part of the state apparatus; priests were permitted to marry, eat meat, and inherit the position of head priest. Contemporary Japanese Buddhism functions primarily as a funeral service (Funeral Buddhism) — appearing only when someone dies, constituting no periodic interruption. The loop long ago absorbed it into a service industry.

Japanese Shinto: shrine visits are personally flexible and can be made once a year (New Year visit) or not at all. The content of visits is predominantly worldly prayer (passing exams, business success, traffic safety). Direction = loop, and it constitutes no systematic interruption.

Taiwanese folk religion: the frequency of praying to Mazu or the Earth God varies by individual and constitutes no institutionalized periodic interruption. More importantly, the core content of the faith is the supernaturalization of worldly needs — praying for safety, wealth, exam success. This is not a Signal pointing outside the loop but a worldly need expressed in supernatural form. The loop can not only absorb it but even convert it into fuel ("praying for exam success" directly serves academic competition).

Confucianism: scholars have debated for decades whether Confucianism possesses transcendence ("Heaven," "the Way," "benevolence" — whether they point beyond the secular). This paper does not enter this debate. This paper's criterion is dynamic: Confucianism has no institutionalized high-frequency interruption practices (no Mass, no Five Pillars, no Sabbath); no mechanism of Confucianism periodically pulls individuals out of the loop in daily life. Moreover, the core values of Confucianism (self-cultivation, regulating the family, governing the state, bringing peace to all under Heaven) overlap dynamically with secular success to a high degree — Confucianism does not interrupt the loop; it provides moral justification for the loop.

China's situation is more complex: Buddhism, Daoism, and folk religion have historically all existed, but the successive shocks of the twentieth century (the anti-traditionalism of the May Fourth Movement, the systematic destruction of the Cultural Revolution, the materialism following the Reform and Opening Up) swept away the remaining Transcendental Mechanisms. The current ideology of China ("Chinese Dream," "great rejuvenation of the Chinese nation") occupies the transcendent position, but its direction aligns with secular success: the national goal and personal success run on the same track. This is Path Three.

### 3.5 The Three Conditional Paths

Three paths are derived from the model:

**Path One: Transcendental Mechanism exists, direction ≠ loop direction.**

The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is running, but the Transcendental Mechanism periodically pulls individuals out of the loop. The individual's Semantic Flow is acted upon simultaneously by two sets of Signals — the loop's Signals ("work → income → better life") and the transcendent Signal ("your worth is not measured by how much you earn"). The frequency and intensity of the transcendent Signal determines the degree of interruption. The loop is not eliminated, but it is constrained — it cannot absorb all resources, cannot occupy the top position in the value hierarchy.

Prediction: low degree of involution, higher fertility rates, lower suicide rates, academic pressure does not constitute comprehensive social grinding.

**Path Two: Transcendental Mechanism does not exist.**

The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop runs without anything to interrupt it. Secular success occupies the highest position in the value hierarchy on its own. The loop runs without resistance, absorbing all available resources. In societies with a tradition of examination selection and industrial pressure, the loop couples with the educational system and produces Signal-matching positive feedback.

Prediction: high degree of involution, extremely low fertility rates, relatively high suicide rates, academic pressure constitutes comprehensive social grinding.

**Path Three: The position of the Transcendental Mechanism is occupied, but direction = loop direction.**

The transcendent position is not empty, but the Signal occupying it aligns with secular success. Prosperity gospel: "God wants you to succeed; your success is evidence of being blessed" — secular success is sacralized, and the loop receives transcendent authoritative endorsement. State ideology occupying the transcendent position: "striving for the nation/revolution is the highest value" — secular competition is converted into patriotic duty.

The dynamic effect is worse than Path Two. In Path Two, the transcendent position is empty — there is at least the possibility of being filled: new transcendent Signals can enter. In Path Three, the position is occupied; new transcendent Signals encounter higher Resistance — something is already sitting there, and that thing must first be moved before entry is possible. Moreover, the occupant uses transcendent language to package secular competition, making it harder for individuals to identify the nature of the loop.

Prediction: highest degree of involution. All symptoms more severe than Path Two.

### 3.7 Interface with the Critique of Chinese Education

This model is the prequel to the Critique of Chinese Education (Cheng Yu Huang, 2026).

The source of the fractal in the education critique (the same structure self-replicating at different scales) lies here: the same meta-condition — the absence of the Transcendental Mechanism — projects the same structure at every level of family, school, workplace, and society.

## IV. Empirical Verification

### 4.1 Method

Seventeen economies (six East Asian, eleven Southeast Asian), with two variables determined for each:

**Transcendental Mechanism status.** Determined case by case using the three operational questions of Section 3.4: (1) whether there exists a periodic, systematic interruption (institutionalized, not personally flexible); (2) whether the direction of the interrupting Signal points outside the loop (transcendent object vs. supernaturalization of worldly needs); (3) whether the loop can absorb the practice as fuel (whether the faith practice can be compressed, skipped, or converted into a tool serving secular success). All three affirmative: Transcendental Mechanism exists and is effective. Direction = loop: Path Three. All others: does not exist or ineffective.

**Degree of involution.** Majority rule across three indicators: (1) whether a nationally high-pressure entrance examination dominates education; (2) whether working hours pressure is significantly higher than the level expected for that development level; (3) whether the suicide rate is higher than the regional average. Two or more "yes" out of three: determined as high involution.

### 4.3 Key Evidence: Malaysia

Malaysia provides the single strongest piece of evidence.

Same country, same institutions, same economic structure, same educational system. Malay (Muslim) fertility rate 2.1; Chinese (no Transcendental Mechanism) fertility rate 0.8. A gap of 2.6 times.

This data controls for all mainstream alternative explanations. Developmental state theory: same country, same development model. Compressed modernity: same compression speed. Institutional theory: the same set of institutions. Economic structure: the same economy. The only difference is faith — Malays have a Transcendental Mechanism (Islamic Five Pillars, Ramadan), Chinese do not.

Institutional variables are controlled; the faith variable operates alone. The result: a 2.6-fold gap.

**Handling of confounding factors.** Malays are protected by Bumiputera affirmative action — civil service quotas, university admission quotas, corporate equity requirements — with lower economic competitive pressure than Chinese. Does this mean the fertility rate gap stems from differences in economic competitive pressure rather than differences in faith?

The task of ruling out this confounding factor is assigned to Singapore (see Singapore note in 4.2): Singapore's Malays have no Bumiputera preference, their socioeconomic status is overall lower, yet the same-direction divergence of 1.58 vs. 0.83 persists — after the policy variable is controlled, the divergence does not disappear; the policy explanation is eliminated.

Indian-ethnic data (2023: Indian-ethnic 1.6 vs. Chinese 0.8) is retained as a fact but is no longer counted as evidence for the religious mechanism (v3.5 downgrade). Reason: this premium is overdetermined. Malaysian Indians are predominantly descendants of plantation laborers; the socioeconomic composition pushes fertility rates higher along the standard gradient; and their ritual practice form (the Kavadi penance of Thaipusam involves substantive bodily cost and discipline) also differs from the flexible temple worship of the post-immigration professional population in Singapore. The religious mechanism and compositional effects cannot be separated here — in contrast with Singapore: Indian-ethnic people whose majority faith is Hinduism converge to below the Chinese level in Singapore (see the Indian ethnic discriminant test in 4.2), demonstrating that the label "Hinduism" does not carry stable interrupting force; what carries interrupting force is the frequency and cost structure of specific practice. Evidence that cannot separate mechanisms gives opponents a target; discarding it makes the Malaysia case harder.

Moreover, the Bumiputera policy itself does not explain the direction — why does protection produce higher fertility rates rather than more leisure, more consumption, or some other form of resource allocation? The model's explanation is: Bumiputera policy reduces the pressure of the loop (lower Loop Strength), but even without this policy, the Islamic Transcendental Mechanism would still constrain the loop's absorptive force. Both factors operate simultaneously — not either-or — and Singapore provides the control group for "without this policy."

### 4.4 Thailand: A Boundary Case for the Model

Thailand requires fine-grained handling by the model. Theravada Buddhism covers 95% of the population; the Transcendental Mechanism exists, but the fertility rate has already fallen to approximately 1.2 (2023).

The model's explanation: Theravada Buddhism's Transcendental Mechanism exists, but its Interruption Frequency and Signal Intensity are lower than the Abrahamic religions. Nirvana is personal liberation, not communal constraint. There is no weekly Mass, no daily Five Pillars, no confession. The ordination tradition provides at least one deep interruption in a lifetime, but the frequency of interruption in daily life is low.

Using the model's path function: low Interruption Frequency, low Signal Intensity → weak interruption effect → the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's absorptive force gradually infiltrates. Thailand's declining fertility rate trajectory is slower than East Asia (the ceiling is leaking, but leaking more slowly than having no ceiling at all), consistent with the model's prediction.

Thailand does not refute the model — rather, it verifies that the effect of the Transcendental Mechanism is continuous rather than binary: frequency and intensity determine the degree of interruption, not an on/off switch.

### 4.5 Falsification Conditions

The model's predictions can be overturned by future data:

One: if a society appears in which the Transcendental Mechanism is strong and direction ≠ loop, yet the degree of involution continues to rise (for example, the Philippines developing East Asian-style involution without a decline in Catholic faith), the model's core claim is falsified.

Two: if a society appears in which the Transcendental Mechanism is absent, under conditions of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop fully operating, the degree of involution continues to fall and not as a result of economic decline, the model's core claim is falsified.

Three: if the fertility rate gap between Malaysian Chinese and Malays disappears without the faith variable having changed, the model's strongest evidence is invalidated.

## V. Constraint Hierarchy

The mainstream academic consensus assumes a causal direction: institutions → culture → behavior. This paper does not use causality; it uses a Constraint Hierarchy: meta-values constrain the possibility space of institutions → institutions take shape within this space → behavior unfolds under institutional constraints. The meaning of the arrow is not "A causes B" but "A constrains the possibility space of B."

The Constraint Hierarchy does not mean the lower layer cannot act on the upper layer. Institutional power can do three things to the Transcendental Mechanism (Institutional Subtraction): **destroy** it — the Soviet Union's destruction of Mongolian Tibetan Buddhism, the Cultural Revolution's destruction of China's remaining faith traditions; **occupy** its position — Meiji State Shinto, Juche ideology, the "Chinese Dream"; **exploit** it — state religion, incorporation, connecting religious authority to secular power. All three have occurred repeatedly in history, and the case table in this paper contains examples. The one thing institutions cannot do (Institutional Addition): **create** a Transcendental Mechanism with direction ≠ loop. The first three are subtraction and appropriation; the fourth is addition. The asymmetry of the Constraint Hierarchy is only in addition — can subtract, cannot add.

Inability to add is not a historical induction — it is a structural impossibility derived from the definition. Slowing down the derivation, it proceeds as follows. Return first to the definition: the Transcendental Mechanism is a Signal source for which the loop finds no conversion pathway and cannot convert into its own variables. Now assume the opposite holds — some institution creates a candidate Transcendental Mechanism. Step one: the institution itself lies within the loop's domain of computation — this has already been argued in 3.4: laws can be lobbied, budgets can be cut, policies can be purchased. Step two: where does the authority of the created Signal source come from? From the institutional power that created it. And institutional power is the loop's internal currency — it can be accumulated, traded, purchased. Step three: then the act of "creation" itself is a ready-made Creation Path — whoever created it can modify it, revoke it, price it, trade with it. The loop does not need to struggle to find a way to absorb it; the handle for absorption was installed at manufacture. Step four: check against the physical criterion of 3.4 — the Transcendental Mechanism requires the topmost level of secular power to bow before it, but the creator will not bow before what it has made; a ceiling you have poured yourself cannot press down on yourself. Conclusion: a candidate mechanism created by institutions necessarily fails the determination procedure, and it can only fail in one of two ways — the direction collapses to = loop (because its authority is secular authority), or it is absorbed (because the Creation Path is the absorption path). Meiji State Shinto is the specimen of this derivation: the state creates a deity, the deity endorses the state, direction = loop, Path Three (its extreme form and collapse mode in the Japan note of 4.2). "A Transcendental Mechanism created by institutions" is a definitional contradiction, not an empirically rare event. Precisely stated, this is a synchronic impossibility: as long as the creator is still alive, the Creation Path is a living absorption path. History contains cases of "creator perishes, creation survives" — Brahminical authority in Hinduism co-constructed with ancient royal power, but those regimes have long perished, the Creation Path was cut by time itself, and the creation acquired an independent authority over a millennial timescale. Modi's Hindu nationalism attempts to create a "new Hinduism" — a positive specimen of this derivation: the creator is alive, the authority derives from state power, the direction collapses to = loop, the determination procedure places it directly into Path Three. Ancient regimes' creations can outlive their creators, but this has no operational significance for any present-day policy-maker — if you want to create a Transcendental Mechanism, you must first create it, then you must die, then wait several hundred years for your fingerprints to be worn off.

Destruction has no corresponding impossibility. Destruction requires no conversion pathway — it requires only force; the framework's loop replacement model already contains clauses for power-based suppression. But the Mongolia case proves only this first thing: institutional power can clear the Transcendental Mechanism. Whether the loop activates after the clearing depends on whether the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is running and whether Redundancy is exhausted — Mongolia satisfies neither condition: pastoral fallback routes constitute enormous Redundancy Stock (social support value top-tier globally), and after 1990 Tibetan Buddhism partially revived, so the Transcendental Mechanism is no longer zero (see Mongolia note in 4.2). The demonstration that "after the position is cleared, the loop activates without resistance" is assigned to China's 1978–2012 vacancy period: institutional power cleared the stock of faith, marketization ignited the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop, and all subsequent social behavior strictly unfolded according to the meta-values layer's new state — academic militarization, rising working hours, plummeting fertility rates. The mode of operation of institutions is to change the value of the upper-layer variable; after the change, the system still obeys the upper-layer variable. The foundation can be blown up; but what can be built on the rubble afterward is determined by the fact "there is now no foundation."

The Philippines displays the same law with a different value. The Philippines shares a large number of conditions with East Asian economies: Cold War framework, experience of authoritarianism, late-developing nation, external pressure, American influence, standardized education, examinations, poverty. But the institutional history of the Philippines has no systematic destruction of Catholicism — the Transcendental Mechanism is intact and in place. Under this condition, all subsequently built institutions take shape within the possibility space it constrains: the same educational standardization, the same examinations, the same poverty — the Fourfold Force Coupling does not engage, because Mass presses down the rank of secular success once per week. What the Philippines demonstrates is not that the Transcendental Mechanism is inviolable — Mongolia and the Cultural Revolution prove it can be eliminated — but the comprehensive reach of its constraining force when it is present.

Taken together, this is the complete statement of the Constraint Hierarchy: the meta-values layer constrains downstream things at all times — whether the Transcendental Mechanism is in place (Philippines), absent (China vacancy period, Taiwan), or occupied (South Korea, North Korea, re-occupation period China), downstream institutions and behavior organize according to its current state. The institutional layer's action on the meta-values layer is half-open — can destroy, can occupy, can exploit, cannot create. And inability to create is guaranteed by the definition of the Transcendental Mechanism, not dependent on induction.

Therefore, the Constraint Hierarchy is: meta-values (the state of the Transcendental Mechanism) constrain the position of secular success in the value hierarchy → constrain the possibility space of institutions → constrain the shape of specific institutions → constrain the distribution of behavior (degree of involution). Institutions can act upward, but only through subtraction; once the subtraction is done, the accounting still follows the balance of the upper-layer variable.

## VI. Relationship to Existing Literature

### 6.1 Weber

In *The Protestant Ethic and the Spirit of Capitalism*, Weber described a breach (the Weberian Breach): Calvinist predestinarianism led believers to seek evidence of salvation in worldly success. Secular success shifted from a means to an end.

Translated into this paper's model: Calvinism is an early case of Transcendental Mechanism direction = loop direction. The transcendent position was occupied (God), but the Signal direction aligned with secular success (secular success = evidence of salvation). What Weber described is the prototype of Path Three.

Korean prosperity gospel is the extreme development of the mechanism Weber described in the twentieth century. Yoido Full Gospel Church — the world's largest Pentecostal church, with 870,000 congregants — explicitly teaches "believe in God and you will be healthy and wealthy." If Weber could see Seoul, he would recognize this as something he had described.

### 6.2 Taylor

In *A Secular Age*, Charles Taylor analyzes the secularization of Western society — from a society in which "not believing in God was almost inconceivable" to one in which "believing in God is just one option among many."

This paper's model provides a comparative dimension that Taylor did not address: after secularization, what happened to the position of the Transcendental Mechanism? In the West (particularly Northern Europe), secularization occurred — but secular humanism to some extent inherited the function of the Transcendental Mechanism: "human dignity cannot be reduced to economic value." The transcendent position in East Asia was not emptied by secularization — it was never filled in the first place. Polytheism and folk religion are projections of worldly needs, not Transcendental Mechanisms.

### 6.3 Gauchet

In *The Disenchantment of the World*, Marcel Gauchet proposes that Christianity is "the religion that made the exit from religion possible" — the theological logic of Christianity contains within itself the seeds of secularization.

This paper's model is compatible with Gauchet but takes a different path: Gauchet is concerned with why the Transcendental Mechanism recedes (the internal logic of Christianity); this paper is concerned with what happens after the Transcendental Mechanism recedes (the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop loses its interruption mechanism). The two can be connected in series: Gauchet explains the process; this paper explains the consequences.

## VII. Predictions and Falsification Ledger

The model should produce falsifiable predictions. The Transcendental Mechanism is a time-varying variable — predictions are therefore stratified into two layers: the horizontal layer (at the same point in time, the ranking of effective Transcendental Mechanism intensity predicts the ranking of involution resistance) and the dynamic layer (when Transcendental Mechanism intensity changes, involution indicators track its trajectory, with a lag measured in generational units — the framework does not predict calendar time; "generation" is the native unit of the Law of Death).

**One: Secularization trend prediction.** Societies that are secularizing without a replacement transcendent framework should, as secularization advances, exhibit rising degrees of involution and accelerating fertility rate decline (the Secularization Decline Trajectory). The preconditions for this prediction have already been established on two fronts. Latin America: Pew (2026) confirmed the decline of Catholicism in Latin America over ten years — consequences under observation. Philippines: identity and practice have decoupled; eighty percent of the population still identifies as Catholic, but weekly Mass attendance fell from 64% in 1991 to 37% in 2013; in 2023, only 38% nationally attended religious services weekly — the determination procedure asks about practice, not identity; the Philippines' Transcendental Mechanism is sliding from "exists and effective" toward "exists but weakened." Over the same period, the total fertility rate fell from 2.7 (2017) to 1.9 (2022), dropping below replacement level for the first time on record, the steepest decline ever recorded, with Metro Manila already at approximately 1.2. The once-weekly interruption, after attendance was halved, reaches only half the population — the ceiling is still there, but half the people standing below it are gone. Confounding note: the Reproductive Health Law of 2012 and the pandemic effect operate over the same period; the Philippine Statistics Authority itself flags the steep 2022 decline as requiring further investigation of pandemic effects. This paper's claim is therefore not "Transcendental Mechanism decline is the sole cause of the fertility rate decline" but rather: after the Transcendental Mechanism declines, the Philippines will lose its divergence from the East Asian trajectory — academic militarization, working hours, and suicide rates will converge toward the East Asian pattern with Generational Replacement. No need to wait for Latin America — within the sample there is already a Catholicizing society in the process of secularization. This paper is putting its neck out.

**Two: Prosperity gospel penetration prediction.** Societies with high prosperity gospel penetration should exhibit symptoms more severe than purely secular societies — because Path Three is worse than Path Two.

**Three: Frequency prediction (conditionalized restatement).** Horizontal layer: in societies where the Transcendental Mechanism's Directional Gate is open, daily practice frequency (Five Pillars daily > weekly Mass > flexible almsgiving) is positively correlated with involution resistance. As of the most recent data, the horizontal ranking holds: Indonesia approximately 2.2 > Philippines 1.9 > Thailand approximately 1.2. Dynamic layer: the Philippines' recent rate of decline exceeds Thailand's — this is not a counterexample to the horizontal ranking, because the Philippines' Transcendental Mechanism intensity is itself collapsing (see Prediction One); this is precisely the dynamic layer prediction hitting. Falsifiable form: if Mass attendance persistently remains below forty percent while the Philippines' involution indicators persistently maintain a significant divergence from East Asia, the frequency mechanism is wrong.

**Four: Prediction ledger.** This model has no small number of moving parts: Directional Gate, Interruption Frequency, Effective Strength, Loop Strength indicator, Redundancy indicator, period segmentation. Having many dials is not a sin; hiding them is. The following consolidates the falsification conditions and ongoing predictions scattered throughout the paper into a ledger, each with a triggering data source. The model's credibility does not rest on the retrospective match rate of 16/17 but on the survival rate of the entries on this table over time.

Falsification conditions (triggering requires model revision or abandonment):

## VIII. Boundaries

**Questions this paper does not address:**

One: The truth or falsehood of the Transcendental Mechanism. The framework does not judge whether God exists or whether nirvana is real. The framework addresses the dynamic effects of the Transcendental Mechanism, not its metaphysical status.

Two: The moral judgment of involution. This paper does not judge involution as "bad" or the Transcendental Mechanism as "good." What each society gains in exchange for the Transcendental Mechanism (lower economic efficiency? higher social cohesion?) is a question for ethics, not dynamics.

Three: Policy prescriptions. The model describes structure, not prescription. "Establishing a Transcendental Mechanism" is not an operation that can be executed by policy — the origin of the Transcendental Mechanism lies outside institutions. This point is a direct inference from the structural derivation of Section V (cannot add), not a position statement.

Four: Time prediction. The framework does not predict the time scale of paths. Vietnam is currently on the road China walked in the 1990s — but when it reaches the bottom, the framework cannot derive that.

Five: Cross-regional comparison. The scope of comparison in this paper is limited to East and Southeast Asia. These economies share sufficient structural conditions — Cold War framework, late-development pressure, similar stages of demographic transition, overlapping geography and trade networks — to allow the Transcendental Mechanism variable to be meaningfully isolated. Expanding the scope of comparison to Northern Europe, the Middle East, and Latin America would introduce a large number of uncontrollable variables (welfare state regime, petroleum economy, types of colonial legacy), actually lowering the quality of causal inference. The cross-regional applicability of the model is a question for future research, not a claim of this paper. The model advances directional cross-regional predictions in Section VII, but verifying these predictions requires establishing independent control conditions for each region — this is beyond the scope of this paper.

Six: Sample independence. Seventeen economies are not seventeen independent observations. East Asian states share Confucian history and Cold War linkages; the four Theravada countries constitute the same civilization cluster; the two Catholic countries share the same origin — cases have phylogenetic correlation (Galton's problem in cross-cultural research), and treating them as independent samples when calculating match rates inflates the evidence base; the actual number of independent units is closer to four or five civilization clusters. This is precisely why this paper concentrates its evidentiary weight on within-country comparisons (ethnic stratification in Malaysia and Singapore) and the Section VII ledger: within-country comparisons are the only observations that escape phylogenetic correlation; prospective predictions are bets unaffected by retrospective match inflation.

Seven: Self-selection. A standing alternative explanation for ethnic and religious divergence is reversed causation: individuals who value family and reproduction remain in high-constraint practices, while secularized individuals depart first — religion is a filter, not a cause. But this alternative explanation presupposes the premise of individual free choice, and religion is a power structure, not a preference menu. Malaysia's Malays did not choose Islam because they value family — being born Malay is being Muslim; this is law and social structure, not the result of individual selection. Selection and interruption are not mutually exclusive alternative explanations — they are two faces of the same power structure: the power structure places you in it (selection) and then acts on you from within (interruption). The individual is caught between two power structures — the loop wants your time and energy; the religious authority also wants your time and energy — with only the choice of which side to comply with, and no freedom to "give neither." A purely self-selection explanation must assume that the proportion of family-valuing individuals among Malays is systematically far higher than among Chinese, and the most likely source of that assumption is precisely the long-term shaping by the religious power structure — pursue selection to the end, and you still hit structure.

**The boundaries of the framework are the boundaries of this paper.** The expressions of this paper are Signals. Readers with different Xin read different semantics from them. This paper cannot exempt itself from its own postulates.
