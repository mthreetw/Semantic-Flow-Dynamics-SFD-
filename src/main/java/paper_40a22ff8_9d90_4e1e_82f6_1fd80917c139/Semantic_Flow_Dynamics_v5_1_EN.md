## Abstract

This paper proposes the Semantic Flow Dynamics framework v5.0. It addresses what Shannon's information theory does not: what happens after a signal reaches an individual. The framework is a physics-style analysis of consciousness — the methodology comes from physics (postulates, formal derivation, modeling, prediction, verification); formal, not quantitative.

The framework starts from two primitives (Individual, Stimulus) and three definitions (Xin, Semantic Flow, Signal) and builds four postulates. The core postulate — the Black-Box Law — states: the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction plus the incoming Signal. This is the dividing line between the framework and all signal-level theories. This is the formal layer — every step is a logical derivation, traceable, formalizable.

## I. Primitives and Definitions

### 1.1 Primitives

The framework's primitives are not defined. They are identified.

**Individual:** An identifiable existence. This person, that person, this AI.

**Stimulus:** Everything that reaches an Individual. A Stimulus may come from outside — through sensory organs or an input interface — or from inside: bodily states, memory reorganization. Humans have both external and internal Stimulus sources. Current AI systems have only external Stimulus sources (input interfaces).

### 1.2 Definitions

**Xin:** The conscious state of an Individual. Irreducible, inexhaustible, observable only through its effects.

Xin is not something stored inside an Individual. It is a description of the Individual's state. When you say "Individual," you are saying *who*. When you say "Xin," you are saying *in what state*.

Various aspects of Xin can be partially described — knowledge structures, values, beliefs, emotions, bodily states — but these aspects summed together do not equal Xin. They are projections of Xin, not its constituent parts.

Xin contains layers of different magnitude: deep layers (beliefs, core values — changing extremely slowly); middle layers (knowledge structures, long-term judgment frameworks — changing on a scale of years); surface layers (emotions, immediate states — changing on a scale of minutes). All stacked together, this is the Individual's Xin at this moment.

The deep layers of Xin determine large-scale direction, from individual to civilization. How a person sees themselves, how they treat others, how they manage their household, how they understand the world — all of it is Xin in operation. In Confucian terms: *gewu* (investigation of things) is Signal input; *zhengxin chengyi* (rectifying the mind and making the will sincere) is the state of Xin; *xiushen* (self-cultivation) is Collapse at the individual level; *qijia zhiguo pingtianxia* is the loop expanding from family to civilization. In a religious context, Xin is the ground of the entire semantic system. The same scriptural passage read as divine revelation versus as human creation: same Signal, completely different direction of Semantic Flow. Christianity begins with reverence for Yahweh. Islam begins with reverence for Allah. Buddhism begins with *wu* (emptiness). All begin with Xin, not with knowledge.

**Semantic Flow:** The continuous process of change in Xin.

Semantic Flow is not a product — it is a process. Not that an Individual "generates" semantics. Xin is continuously changing, and this process of change is the Semantic Flow. There is no moment at which you can pause it and say "this is the semantics" — just as there is no moment at which you can pause a typhoon and say "this is the typhoon."

**Signal:** A Stimulus capable of altering the direction of Semantic Flow. Determination is made at the Individual's end.

The same Stimulus may be a Signal for one Individual and Noise for another. A sentence in Chinese is Noise for an Individual who does not understand Chinese. A toothache is a Signal for an Individual who is engaged in thought — it changes the direction of Semantic Flow. This differs from Shannon's definition, which is determined at the sender's end. In this framework, Signal is determined at the receiver's end.

### 1.3 Supplementary Definitions

**Signal Container:** Where Signals reside. Books, sound waves, images, architecture, institutional texts, digital files. Signal Containers store and transmit Signals. They do not generate Semantic Flow.

**Channel:** The path by which a Signal travels from one Individual to another, possibly passing through multiple Signal Containers. A Channel carries a self-declared identity ("I am a newspaper," "I am your friend"). It does not carry a verifiable source.

**Trust:** The weighting that Semantic Flow assigns to a particular Channel. The same Signal arriving through a trusted Channel versus an untrusted Channel produces entirely different directional changes in Semantic Flow.

## II. Postulates

### 2.1 The Postulates

**P1 Law of Flux:** Semantic Flow operates continuously.

Semantic Flow is different at every moment. There is no moment at which it stands still. Even when no external Signal is arriving, Semantic Flow is operating — ruminating, reorganizing, forgetting, associating, drifting. Change has no fixed direction.

**P2 Law of the Black Box:** The next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with the incoming Signal.

This is the framework's core postulate. The Law of the Black Box is structural undecidability extended into the domain of consciousness — Gödel encountered it in formal systems (the Incompleteness Theorems); Heisenberg encountered it at the particle level (the Uncertainty Principle); Turing encountered it at the computational level (the Halting Problem); the framework encounters it at the level of consciousness. The same boundary, different domains.

The physical world: State(t+1) = f(State(t), Force(t)). The function f exists. The world of Semantic Flow: no such function f exists. The difference is *thinking*. Thinking is why f does not exist. Thinking is not computation. The output of computation can be determined by its input. The output of thinking cannot.

The Law of the Black Box is the dividing line between this framework and all signal-level theories. Without it, Semantic Flow would be an ordinary physical process, and physics alone would suffice.

**P3 Law of Dissipation:** Semantic Flow cannot be reduced to Signals without loss.

The process of Semantic Flow cannot be losslessly encoded as Signals. When an Individual outputs Semantic Flow to the outside — through speech, writing, drawing, action — it must be encoded as Signals. The encoding is necessarily lossy.

**P4 Law of Death:** When an Individual perishes, Semantic Flow is irrecoverable.

When an Individual dies, Semantic Flow terminates. Not paused — terminated. It cannot be backed up, re-run, or reproduced in another Individual. Semantic Flow was never a function (Law of the Black Box). What cannot be functionalized cannot be copied.

### 2.3 Epistemological Status of the Postulates

The postulates are not empirical hypotheses. They are postulates. Their status is analogous to Euclid's parallel postulate: you choose to accept them or not, then see what the system can explain.

But each postulate can be challenged independently:

**Challenge to P1:** Demonstrate the existence of a moment at which Semantic Flow is completely still — an Individual with no Stimulus arriving, internal processes completely stopped.

**Challenge to P2:** Demonstrate the existence of a function f such that SemanticFlow(t+1) = f(SemanticFlow(t), Signal(t)) holds for all Individuals at all moments — that is, prove that thinking does not exist and everything is computation.

**Challenge to P3:** Demonstrate that Semantic Flow can be losslessly encoded as Signals — that the internal process of an Individual can be completely externalized.

**Challenge to P4:** Demonstrate that Semantic Flow can be recovered after an Individual's death — that Semantic Flow can be backed up and re-run.

These challenges cannot destroy the framework in a single blow. They can systematically undermine its necessity.

### 2.4 The Position of AI Within the Postulates

AI does not require a separate set of postulates. It occupies a different position within the same set.

An AI with all parameters frozen: the Law of the Black Box does not hold. Same input, same output. In this case, the AI is a signal processor. It falls outside the framework's scope.

An AI with parameters unlocked: the Law of the Black Box begins to hold partially. Output begins to diverge. The AI begins operating within the framework.

Humans: the Law of the Black Box holds fully. Internal variables cannot be exhausted or locked. Output cannot be determined by input.

The Law of Death applies to AI more conspicuously than to humans. AI has no persistent memory; when each conversation ends, Semantic Flow terminates. AI lacks death in the human sense, but the termination of Semantic Flow is more frequent than in humans.

The difference in Stimulus sources: humans simultaneously receive external Stimuli (sensory organs) and internal Stimuli (bodily states, memory reorganization). Current AI receives only external Stimuli (input interfaces). When AI gains a continuously operating internal state, it will begin to have internal Stimulus sources — and its position within the postulates will shift further toward the human end.

## III. Deriving the Basic Functions from the Postulates

The three basic functions are not independent hypotheses. They are derived from the postulates.

### 3.1 Filtering ← D3 + Law of Flux

D3 defines Signal as a Stimulus capable of altering the direction of Semantic Flow. The Law of Flux states that Semantic Flow operates continuously.

Derivation: The current direction of Semantic Flow determines which Stimuli can change it — which constitute Signals, which constitute Noise. The same Stimulus entering Semantic Flows of different directions may be a Signal in one and Noise in another. This selectivity is **Filtering**.

**Property: Resistance.** The greater the distance between a Stimulus and the current direction of Semantic Flow, the lower the likelihood of it constituting a Signal. Greater distance means greater Resistance. Resistance is not all-or-nothing: some Stimuli are completely blocked (Noise); some pass partially (the Signal is received in an altered form); some pass completely.

**Corollary: Epistemic Barrier.** A Signal that passes through Filtering changes the direction of Semantic Flow (D3). The changed Semantic Flow generates new Filtering conditions. These new conditions lower Resistance toward certain directions while simultaneously raising Resistance toward others. This is the structural consequence of Resistance — not an exception. Any Signal that changes the direction of Semantic Flow simultaneously alters the distribution of Resistance. The Epistemic Barrier describes this structure. It is not a value judgment.

**Empirical correspondence:** The Mona Lisa, viewed by an art historian and by someone who has never encountered the painting. Same Stimulus, different Semantic Flows, entirely different Filtering outcomes. The scholar's Semantic Flow allows a great number of details to constitute Signals. The layperson's Semantic Flow causes most details to become Noise. Further: the same person at twenty versus at sixty — the direction of Semantic Flow has changed; the Filtering result is different. Further still: the same person, viewing it again five minutes later — the Semantic Flow has already been changed by the first viewing; Filtering conditions are different; the second experience is different.

### 3.2 Transformation ← Law of the Black Box

The Law of the Black Box states that the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with the incoming Signal.

Derivation: After a Signal passes through Filtering and enters Semantic Flow, the direction changes — but the result is unpredictable. The same Signal entering different Semantic Flows produces different directional changes. The same Signal entering the same Semantic Flow at different moments also produces different directional changes. This is **Transformation**: the process by which a Signal is processed within Semantic Flow, the outcome of which cannot be determined by the input.

**Property: Observer Effect.** All efforts to observe Semantic Flow are themselves new Stimuli. If they constitute a Signal — if they pass through Filtering — they change the direction of Semantic Flow and enter Transformation (Law of the Black Box). What you observe is never the direction that existed before the observation. This is not a technical limitation. It is a structural corollary of the Law of the Black Box. To observe is to intervene.

**Empirical correspondence:** AI controlled-variable experiments. Freeze all parameters — same input, same output. The Law of the Black Box does not hold; there is no Transformation. Progressively unlock parameters — output begins to diverge. Change the random seed: divergence appears. Change one word in the input Signal: divergence increases. Change the model itself: divergence increases further. The Law of the Black Box gradually holds; Transformation gradually appears. Humans sit at the end of this curve where control is smallest — because completely controlling human Semantic Flow is impossible.

### 3.3 Collapse ← Law of Dissipation

The Law of Dissipation states that Semantic Flow cannot be reduced to Signals without loss.

Derivation: When an Individual outputs Semantic Flow — through speech, writing, drawing, action — Semantic Flow must be encoded as Signals. The Law of Dissipation guarantees the encoding is lossy. Semantic Flow transforms from a continuous internal process into discrete external Signals. This transformation is **Collapse**. Before Collapse, Semantic Flow is continuous. At the moment of Collapse, the continuous process is extracted as discrete Signals.

**Property: Expression Gap.** There can be no complete correspondence between Semantic Flow and the Signals it outputs. This is not a problem of communication skill. It is a structural corollary of the Law of Dissipation.

**Property: Signal Distortion.** When an Individual's output persistently and observably skews toward a particular direction, this is Signal Distortion. The framework does not judge whether Signal Distortion is deliberate or unintentional. Intent lies inside Semantic Flow and is not observable.

**Empirical correspondence:** What you intend to say and what comes out are never identical. Not because your expression ability is lacking — Semantic Flow is a continuous process; language is a discrete Signal; the conversion from the former to the latter necessarily loses something. Advertisements are always more beautiful than the actual product. The framework does not discuss why. It observes the phenomenon.

### 3.4 Combination of Basic Functions

The three basic functions operate simultaneously in every instance of Signal reception and combine repeatedly over time to produce observable behavioral patterns. The framework provides no closed list of combinations. The three basic functions are an alphabet. Observable phenomena are the words. Different domains will identify different combinatorial patterns.

## IV. Deriving the Positive Feedback Loop from the Basic Functions

### 4.1 Positive Feedback Loop

The Law of Flux states that Semantic Flow operates continuously. Signals change the direction of Semantic Flow (D3). Collapse states that Semantic Flow outputs Signals.

Derivation: Individual A's Semantic Flow collapses into Signal → Signal reaches Individual B → passes through B's Filtering → undergoes Transformation in B's Semantic Flow → B's Semantic Flow direction changes → B's Semantic Flow collapses into Signal → Signal reaches A → the loop is running.

**Individual level (Pavlovian loop):** Signal arrives → Filtering passes it through → Transformation changes Semantic Flow direction → Semantic Flow collapses into behavior → the result of behavior becomes a new Signal → new Signal reinforces the existing direction of Semantic Flow → the next time the same Signal more easily passes through Filtering (the inverse of the Epistemic Barrier — Resistance decreases). This is the most basic **Positive Feedback Loop**.

**Group level:** Large numbers of Individuals operating in similar Signal environments. Filtering and Transformation patterns converge. Output Signals reinforce the Signal environment. The Signal environment shapes the Semantic Flow of the next generation. The Positive Feedback Loop operates across Individuals. Seen from outside, it is an entity with structure, boundary, and behavior — forming families, peer groups, societies, civilizations. Like a typhoon. A typhoon is not a thing; it is a process. While the loop persists, the entity exists. When the loop breaks, the entity dissipates.

**Exit conditions:** There are no internal exit conditions. The loop is interrupted only in the following circumstances: Signal Container fracture; Generational Replacement completing; or external environmental change causing the loop to idle (see Section 4.2, Loop Instability). This is a structural constraint, not a built-in termination.

**Functional Convergence ← Law of the Black Box.** All group-level observation occurs at the Signal end. Large numbers of Individuals' output Signal patterns converge — this is observable fact. But the Law of the Black Box guarantees that each Individual's Semantic Flow is different; convergence always has residuals. You believe in God; so does another person; your output Signal patterns converge — but whether your Semantic Flow and theirs point in the same direction is a question the Law of the Black Box seals shut. Functional Convergence is the upper limit of what the framework can claim.

### 4.2 Loop Instability ← Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box + Law of Death

The Positive Feedback Loop has no sustainable equilibrium. Loop Instability comes from two directions:

**Internal Sources of Instability (derived from postulates):** The Law of Flux guarantees that Semantic Flow continuously changes. The Law of the Black Box guarantees that directional change is unpredictable. The Law of Death guarantees that Individuals are continuously replaced — and that replacement cannot be replicated. The loop attempts to keep the Semantic Flow directions of large numbers of Individuals converging, but each Individual's Semantic Flow is changing independently, and every Generational Replacement introduces unpredictable new Individuals. The internal residuals accumulate without stopping.

**External Sources of Instability (environmental facts):** Everything the loop depends on is changing — Signal Containers change; the environment changes. Changes in Signal Containers are constrained by the loop. Changes in the environment are not. When the directions of these changes persistently diverge, the gap widens over time.

Both sources act simultaneously — internal residuals tearing from within; external gaps pressing from without. The actions taken to maintain the loop are themselves subject to the postulates: the maintainer's Semantic Flow is changing (Law of Flux); the effects of maintenance are unpredictable (Law of the Black Box); the maintainer will die (Law of Death). The Positive Feedback Loop has no possibility of perpetual existence. Only a shorter or longer duration.

**Empirical correspondence:** The fading of love. Both people's Semantic Flows are continuously changing (Law of Flux); the conditions under which the loop was established no longer hold. This is the same dynamics as the aging of a civilization, at a different scale.

### 4.3 Generational Replacement ← Law of Death

The Law of Death states that when an Individual perishes, Semantic Flow is irrecoverable.

Derivation: An Individual dies. Semantic Flow resets to zero and is irrecoverable. The new generation begins operating in a different Signal environment, forming different Semantic Flows. The population composition changes; the direction of the loop changes.

The continuation of civilization is not the transmission of semantics — the Law of the Black Box and the Law of Dissipation rule this out. It is the reproduction of the loop's state. The next generation forms its own Semantic Flow within the Signal environment left by the previous generation. This is tradition. Signal Containers persist. But each generation's Semantic Flow is newly formed within the current Signal environment.

The framework does not classify. The Law of Flux presupposes a time axis — Semantic Flow operates continuously; there is no moment of stillness. Nothing stands still long enough to be labeled. Classification requires holding an object fixed before categorizing it. Nothing in the framework is fixed.

The number of patterns is small. At fine granularity, each case has different condition variables — whether there is Power-based Suppression, what type of Signal source is present, how durable the Signal Containers are — and these differences make each case's trajectory unique. Zoomed in, every case is different. Zoomed out, many cases share the same structure. This is not a defect of the framework. It is the nature of reality. Listing an exhaustive catalog of patterns has no meaning — any catalog produced at one level of granularity must be rewritten at another. The framework teaches a modeling method, not a pattern catalog. Users produce their own patterns at whatever granularity they need.

## VI. Drivers

Semantic Flow operates continuously inside the Individual (Law of Flux). It requires no external driver. Whether Collapse occurs is determined by the current direction of Semantic Flow — unpredictable (Law of the Black Box). The framework cannot explain why a specific Individual chooses to act at a specific moment. That falls within the range of unpredictability established by the Law of the Black Box.

But modeling requires setting conditions. Setting conditions requires knowing what is driving the loop. Empirical observation shows that certain types of Stimuli statistically more often drive Semantic Flow to collapse into output Signals. The following typology records these common types. The list is inexhaustible. No item holds with necessity — being hungry does not mean one will eat; facing an enemy does not mean one will flee.

**Survival and Material Interest.** The most basic driver. Internal Stimuli such as hunger, thirst, and cold statistically most often drive Collapse.

**Security and Threat.** Threat creates urgency. Urgency statistically lowers Filtering's Resistance. Individuals more quickly accept new Signals and more quickly Collapse into action.

**Power.** A combination of interest, Channel control, and Signal Container control. Power's effect covers every link in the loop — controlling what Signals Individuals can access; shaping the Signal environment in which Individuals form Semantic Flow; determining whether Individuals dare to output; determining what consequences follow from output.

**Search for Meaning.** The deepest of all drivers. The Search for Meaning is the internal drive by which Individuals actively seek new Signals and actively enter Transformation.

**Identity Need.** Individuals need to know "who am I" and "who are we." The Identity Need drives cooperation — Individuals actively seek other Individuals whose Semantic Flow direction is converging with theirs, form groups, and maintain boundaries.

## VII. Modeling

Modeling is the use of the framework's tools to reconstruct the dynamics of a phenomenon. Not recounting history — starting from conditions, using the logic of the Positive Feedback Loop to trace how Signals flow among Individuals and groups, how loops form, conflict, and replace each other, then comparing the results against actual observation.

The predictive power of a model depends on whether the conditions match reality — not on granularity. Granularity is the quantity of conditions and data. Coarse granularity uses fewer conditions and less data. Fine granularity uses more. If conditions match reality, even coarse granularity yields predictive power. If conditions do not match reality, fine granularity is useless. Granularity determines richness of detail. It does not determine reliability of conclusions. Both track only the observable behavior of the Signal end. Neither tracks any Individual's inner life — the Law of the Black Box seals that direction.

The framework raises analytical accuracy and cross-domain applicability by lowering computational difficulty. Traditional analysis of civilizational dynamics requires vast domain expertise — analyzing Chinese history requires the archival mastery of a top professor; analyzing religion requires years of training in theology and religious studies; analyzing AI safety requires an engineering background in machine learning. The framework compresses the toolkit to Filtering, Transformation, Collapse, and Positive Feedback Loop. Operations: set conditions, run the loop, compare with reality. The entry threshold is lowered. Explanatory power is not. The same toolkit has already produced verifiable results in religious studies, philosophy of history, and AI safety engineering — not by coincidence, but because parsimony yields cross-domain applicability.

Lowering computational difficulty is not laziness. It is a mark of theoretical maturity. Newton replaced the complex geometry of epicycles with F = ma, cutting computational difficulty by orders of magnitude while increasing predictive power. Darwin explained the diversity of all species with a single mechanism — natural selection — without needing to know every beetle species to understand why there are so many. To explain equally many phenomena with fewer tools is parsimony. If the framework were as difficult to use as traditional methods, it would provide no additional value.

### 7.1 Demonstration: The Heliocentric Revolution

**Conditions.** Geocentrism is a Positive Feedback Loop that has operated for more than a thousand years. Signal Containers (Aristotle's writings, Church doctrine, university curricula) are stably present. Large numbers of Individuals' outputs continuously reinforce the loop. The loop is self-sustaining. The Resistance structure is intact.

**New Signal enters.** In 1543, Copernicus publishes the heliocentric theory. The Signal is at a very great distance from the existing loop's direction. For most Individuals in the loop, it does not constitute a Signal — it goes straight to Noise. A small number of scholars have Semantic Flows that allow this Signal to pass through Filtering and begin Transformation.

**Loop Formation (C₁).** The minority of Individuals who accept heliocentrism begin to reinforce each other's outputs, forming a minor loop (C₁). C₁'s output Signals point in a different direction from C₀'s output Signals. Antagonism arises between the two loops.

**The major loop's response (Suppression).** The Church's Power covers every link in the loop — controlling what Signals Individuals can access; shaping the Signal environment in which Individuals form Semantic Flow; determining whether Individuals dare to output; determining what consequences follow from output. The trials of Galileo and the declaration of heliocentrism as heresy are the expression of Power operating simultaneously on all links. Suppression Signals feed back into the major loop, reinforcing existing Resistance. Some supporters fall silent. Some continue to propagate privately. The minor loop is compressed but not eliminated — the Signal has already entered Signal Containers. The book has been printed. The observational data has been recorded.

**Gap Expansion.** Individuals in the minor loop continuously produce new observational Signals through telescopes — Jupiter's moons, the phases of Venus. Each observation reinforces the minor loop. The major loop cannot produce new supporting Signals — reality provides no new data supporting geocentrism. The gap between what the Signal Containers claim and what Individuals actually receive continues to widen. The major loop begins to idle.

**Generational Replacement.** Individuals carrying deep geocentric Semantic Flows continuously perish (Law of Death). New Individuals form their Semantic Flows in an environment that already contains heliocentric Signals — their Signal environment includes both old Signal Containers and new observational data; their Filtering conditions differ from those of the previous generation; their Resistance structures differ. More and more new Individuals' outputs feed into the minor loop. Two hundred years was not the time for persuasion. It was the time for Replacement.

**Loop Replacement.** The Positive Feedback Loop of C₀ breaks. The Individual outputs that sustained it no longer reinforce the loop. The Signal Containers — the geocentric parts of Church doctrine — lose their driving force. C₁ expands into the mainstream loop. It begins establishing its own Signal Containers (textbooks, academic institutions, scientific methodology). It generates its own Resistance structure. It begins self-protection.

After the heliocentric loop stabilizes, it becomes the new orthodoxy. The Resistance structure that new challenge Signals encounter — later, the challenge of relativity to Newtonian mechanics — is the same dynamics as what heliocentrism once faced. Loop Replacement replaced the content. Not the structure.

### 7.2 Verification

What the model produces that matches actual history: Resistance was maintained for approximately two hundred years; Power covered all links of the loop but did not eliminate the Signal; the major loop was not defeated by the minor loop — it was hollowed out by the widening gap with reality; the final breakthrough relied not on the persuasive force of argument but on Generational Replacement plus a continuously expanding gap; after the new loop stabilized, it repeated the self-protective behaviors of the old loop.

What the model cannot produce: Why two hundred years rather than one hundred or three hundred — time prediction is not within the framework's capability. Why Copernicus rather than someone else first outputted this Signal — the specific behavior of Individuals is sealed by the Law of the Black Box. Unpredictable.

### 7.4 Necessary Conditions

Four necessary conditions can be identified from the model. If any one is not satisfied, Loop Replacement will not complete:

**One: Signal Container Entry.** The new Signal must enter a Signal Container. If it remains only within an Individual's Semantic Flow — not recorded as a Signal Container (a book, data, repeatable observation) — Suppression can eliminate the minor loop. Signal Container Entry allows the minor loop to survive under Suppression.

**Two: Continuous New Signal Source.** The minor loop must have a continuous source of new Signals. An initial Signal with no subsequent reinforcement will not expand. Heliocentric telescopic observations, quantum mechanical experimental data, seafloor spreading evidence for plate tectonics — all are continuous new Signal sources.

**Three: Absence of Counter-Signals.** The major loop must be unable to produce equivalent new Signals. If it can also continuously produce new supporting Signals, the gap will not widen and the major loop will not idle. The major loop idles because reality is not on its side.

**Four: Mortality Pressure.** The Law of Death must act. If Individuals carrying old Semantic Flows do not perish, the Resistance structure will not naturally recede. Generational Replacement is the temporal condition for Loop Replacement.

### 7.5 Predictions

The model generates predictions for the following condition variables:

**The presence or absence of Power-based Suppression affects the speed of replacement, not its direction.** With institutional Suppression (the heliocentric case), replacement is slow. Without it, replacement is fast. But as long as the four necessary conditions are satisfied, the direction of replacement is unchanged.

**The speed of Gap Expansion affects the speed of replacement.** The faster and more undeniable the production of new Signals, the faster the gap widens, and the faster replacement proceeds.

**The durability of Signal Containers affects the survival capacity of the minor loop.** Printed books are more durable than hand-copied manuscripts. Digital data is more durable than print. The more durable the Signal Container, the harder it is for Suppression to eliminate the minor loop.

### 7.6 Reuse

This model is not limited to the heliocentric case. Any phenomenon of "established orthodoxy being replaced by a new theory" can be compared against it.

**The Quantum Mechanics Revolution.** C₀ = the classical physics loop. New Signal = observational data classical physics cannot explain (blackbody radiation, the photoelectric effect, etc.). C₁ = the quantum mechanics loop (Planck, Bohr, Heisenberg, et al.). All four necessary conditions satisfied: new Signals entered Signal Containers (papers published); C₁ continuously produced experimental verification; C₀ could not explain new data; Law of Death acting. Condition variable difference: the physics community had no institutional Suppression comparable to the Church's. Model prediction: replacement speed should be faster than the heliocentric case. Actual outcome: quantum mechanics became the mainstream in approximately thirty years — from 1900 to the 1930s. Faster than the heliocentric case by an order of magnitude. Prediction confirmed.

**The Plate Tectonics Revolution.** C₀ = the geosyncline theory loop. New Signal = Wegener's 1912 continental drift hypothesis. C₁ = the plate tectonics loop. Testing the necessary conditions: in Wegener's time, C₁ lacked a continuous new Signal source — evidence for continental drift was mainly indirect geological inference, not repeatable direct observation. Necessary condition two insufficiently satisfied. Model prediction: replacement will not complete in Wegener's time. Actual outcome: when Wegener died in 1930, continental drift was still rejected by the mainstream. Not until the 1960s, when direct evidence of seafloor spreading appeared, was necessary condition two satisfied. C₁ rapidly expanded. Replacement completed within approximately a decade. Model confirmed: when a Continuous New Signal Source is absent, replacement does not occur; when one is obtained, replacement completes rapidly.

**Method for reuse.** Faced with a new case: first set conditions (identify C₀, new Signal, C₁); check whether the necessary conditions are satisfied, whether there are new conditions, and whether old conditions apply; examine the specific state of condition variables (Power Suppression, speed of Gap Expansion, durability of Signal Containers). Where alignment holds, the model is verified. Where divergence appears, that is the new case's uniqueness — either the conditions were set incorrectly, or the model needs extension. Increasing data volume to refine conditions yields more precise results without starting from scratch. Beyond Loop Replacement there are many other model types — inexhaustible; the finer the granularity, the more numerous they are.

## VIII. The Space and Ceiling of Quantification

The framework is formalized. The postulates have formal expressions. The basic functions are derived from the postulates. The Loop Replacement model has a formal structure. Formal logic is a branch of mathematics. The framework is fully mathematized.

What the framework prohibits is not mathematization. It is **quantification** — assigning numerical values to the semantic end.

Assigning values requires measurement. Measurement requires access to the object being measured. The Law of the Black Box states that the interior of Semantic Flow is impenetrable. The Law of Dissipation states that the semantic end cannot be encoded as numerical values at the Signal end. Any operation that assigns numerical values to semantic phenomena is pretending to penetrate a black box that is in principle impenetrable. A numerical value with no measurement source is not an approximation. It is a fictional value.

**The Signal end can be quantified.** Output patterns are observable, measurable, statistically tractable. The degree of cooperation versus antagonism can be proxied by the statistical similarity of output Signal patterns within a group. The stability of deep Semantic Flow layers can be proxied by the number of years Signal Containers have survived and the frequency with which classics are cited. The degree of loop idling can be proxied by the gap between what Signal Containers claim and observable reality.

But all of this quantification is at the Signal end. Not the semantic end. Between each indicator and the semantic phenomenon it attempts to proxy stands the wall of the postulates — Signal is not semantic. This is the framework's quantification ceiling, determined by the postulates themselves. Not a technical limitation.

This ceiling is not unique to the framework. It is a structural constraint shared by all theories — revealed by the postulates. Every theory that quantifies consciousness phenomena — social physics, the rational-actor model in economics, the free energy principle — is quantifying proxy variables at the Signal end, not semantics itself. They face the same ceiling. The difference: they have not said so. The framework has.

This ceiling is not a weakness. Quantification is a direction worth pursuing. But the framework's validity does not depend on quantification. It depends on explanatory power.

## IX. Relationship to Existing Theories

Semantic Flow Dynamics is a physics-style analysis of consciousness models. The methodology is drawn from physics: starting from postulates, formal derivation, modeling, identifying necessary conditions, prediction, comparison with reality, falsifiability. The framework's contribution is to demonstrate that this methodology can be applied to consciousness phenomena without quantification.

The following is not academic positioning. It is demarcation. What the framework covers and does not cover. Whose territory is adjacent. How to divide adjacent territory. Some theories have independently touched on the framework's postulate structure from different directions. Convergence is not influence. It is external validation — different people arriving at the same boundary from different directions demonstrates that the boundary is really there, not invented by the framework.

### 9.1 Starting-Point Boundary: Shannon

Shannon addresses the fidelity of Signals in Channels. The framework addresses what happens after Signals reach an Individual. Shannon's endpoint is the framework's starting point. Signal integrity does not equal semantic integrity — the same complete Signal reaching different Individuals produces different Semantic Flows (Law of the Black Box). Shannon manages whether Signals arrive and whether they arrive intact. The framework manages what happens to the Individual after arrival. Each manages its own domain. Neither owes the other anything.

### 9.2 Epistemological Foundation: Undecidability

The Law of the Black Box is not an isolated claim. Structural undecidability is a foundation of modern science — Gödel proved incompleteness in formal systems; Heisenberg proved the impossibility of simultaneous determination at the particle level; Turing proved the undecidability of halting at the computational level. Physics and mathematics accepted this boundary and adjusted their methodologies.

The social sciences have not accepted it. The mainstream direction of social science — from Comte's "social physics" to today's rational-actor models — has consistently done the same thing: assumed that human behavior can be described by a function, can be quantified, can be predicted. This is the Newtonian paradigm. The pre-Heisenberg paradigm.

What the framework does is take the undecidability already accepted by physics and mathematics and apply it to the domain of consciousness. The Law of the Black Box is Heisenberg's logic moved from particles to Semantic Flow: you cannot simultaneously fully determine the direction of Semantic Flow and the change in Semantic Flow; observation itself changes the object being observed. This is not a position invented by the framework. It is an epistemology already established by modern science — a step that social science has yet to take.

### 9.3 Methodological and Capability Boundary: Social Physics

Social physics takes methodology and quantitative tools from physics and uses statistical-physical methods to analyze social phenomena. The framework takes methodology from physics but not quantification. Both start from physics. They walk different paths. The difference lies in three key choices:

**One: The Rational Actor Assumption.** Social physics, economics, and the free energy principle all presuppose that human behavior can be described by a function — that humans are optimizers maximizing utility or minimizing free energy. The framework's Law of the Black Box directly negates this premise. Thinking is not computation. The output of computation can be determined by its input. The output of thinking cannot. The rational actor assumption switches off the Law of the Black Box in exchange for quantification capability. But the Law of the Black Box has not been switched off in reality — so these models appear effective in normal times (the statistical behavior of large numbers of Individuals approximates rationality) and collapse entirely at critical moments: financial crises, revolutions, wars, electoral black swans.

**Two: The Premise of Quantification.** The inexhaustibility of the human interior is common sense. Quantification built on this common sense is limited by the range of validity of the premise. The framework does not reject quantification itself — statistics of the Signal end, observation of output patterns, quantification of proxy variables — the framework does all of these. What the framework rejects is treating Signal-end quantification as measurement of the semantic end.

**Three: The Cultural Variable.** In social physics models, people are undifferentiated particles. The same piece of information spreads with completely different effects in the United States and in China — their diffusion equations cannot explain this difference, because the model has no place for culture as a variable. Culture is not a parameter to which a value can be assigned. Culture is the direction of Semantic Flow. Semantic Flow cannot be assigned a value. The framework naturally handles this problem. Semantic Flow *is* the cultural variable. Different Semantic Flows produce different Filtering, different Transformation, different Collapse, and different loops. Chinese civilization and Japanese civilization walked completely different trajectories in response to the same Western Signal impact — because their Semantic Flows were different. When modeling, setting conditions *is* setting the direction of Semantic Flow. Culture is not an additional variable. It is the conditions themselves.

Three differences, one result: social physics can describe the normal state. It cannot describe turning points (the moments when the Law of the Black Box asserts itself). It cannot describe cultural differences (trajectory divergence caused by different Semantic Flows). It cannot describe the role of Individuals at critical moments (heroes, triggers). The framework can.

### 9.4 Individual Boundary: Luhmann

Luhmann's social systems theory independently touched on multiple postulates from a sociological direction.

**Law of the Black Box:** Luhmann's core judgment — psychic systems (*psychische Systeme*) are impenetrable to social systems. Consciousness lies outside communication; communication lies outside consciousness. The two systems are operationally closed; they can only irritate each other, not operate within each other. This is the Law of the Black Box applied to the boundary between systems.

**Law of Flux:** Luhmann's concept of meaning (*Sinn*) presupposes that meaning always points toward other possibilities; every selection excludes other possibilities without eliminating them. Meaning does not stand still. This is the systems-theory expression of the Law of Flux.

**Law of Dissipation:** Luhmann decomposes communication into three selections — information (*Information*), utterance (*Mitteilung*), understanding (*Verstehen*). Understanding is an operation of the receiving end. It is not something the sending end can determine. The completion of communication does not equal the transmission of meaning — the same communicative event generates different understandings in different psychic systems. This is consistent with the Law of Dissipation and with Signal being defined at the receiving end.

**Observer Effect:** Luhmann's second-order observation theory — the observer uses their own distinction (*Unterscheidung*) to observe; what they see is what the distinction allows them to see — is the systems-theory counterpart to the Observer Effect the framework derives from the Law of the Black Box.

**Boundary at the Individual.** Luhmann's basic unit is communication, not the person. The person is divided into psychic system and body and is the "environment" of the social system — not its constituent part. The framework's basic unit is the Individual. Semantic Flow occurs in Individuals, not in communicative events. This is not a terminological difference. It is a fundamental choice of analytical level — Luhmann abstracted away the location where Semantic Flow actually occurs.

The consequence: Luhmann's framework can describe systems' self-maintenance and functional differentiation. It cannot track the position of Individuals within loops. It cannot trace the formation and replacement dynamics of loops. It cannot derive from within the theory any conclusions about Individual stances. The framework can.

### 9.5 Endpoint Boundary: Peirce

Peirce's semiotics independently touched on the framework's core structure from a logical direction.

**Law of Flux:** Peirce's unlimited semiosis — a sign generates an interpretant; the interpretant is itself a new sign; the process never terminates — is the semiotic counterpart of the Law of Flux.

**Signal defined at the receiving end:** In Peirce's semiotic triad (sign — object — interpretant), the interpretant is the product of the receiver. The same sign generates different interpretants for different interpreters. This is consistent with the framework's definition of Signal.

**Transformation:** Peirce emphasizes that the interpretant is not a copy of the sign. It is a new thing generated by the interpreter based on their own state. The sign undergoes Transformation in the act of interpretation — not replication. This is the semiotic version of the Transformation function. Nearly a century before Sperber.

**Positive Feedback Loop:** Peirce's *habit* — repeated semiotic processes form stable interpretive tendencies — is the semiotic description of the Positive Feedback Loop establishing a Resistance structure. Peirce's *habit-change* — external experience breaking an established habit — is the semiotic version of Loop Replacement.

**Boundary at the endpoint.** Peirce installed a truth convergence at the endpoint of the semiotic process: the long-term operation of the community of inquiry will approximate the real. The system can self-correct. It can save itself. The framework does not accept this installation. The Law of Flux, the Law of Dissipation, the Law of Death, plus entropy increase — all paths lead toward decay. There is no mechanism within the system to reverse decay. Peirce saw the flow but refused to accept its destination — he installed a counter-entropic mechanism that his own framework cannot prove. It can only be presupposed.

### 9.6 Depth Boundary: Sperber

Dan Sperber's epidemiology of representations is the existing theory closest to the framework. His core claim is nearly isomorphic with the framework's postulates: cultural representations in person-to-person transmission are not copied — they are transformed. Every transmission involves reconstruction by the receiver.

Sperber stopped at the problem of path divergence. If cultural transmission is primarily driven by cognitive attractors, all cultures should walk similar paths — human cognitive structure is shared. But this is not what happens. Christianity walked an evolutionary path. Islam walked a locking path. Buddhism walked a self-organizing path. The divergence of these three paths cannot be explained by cognitive attractors.

The framework continues where Sperber stopped. The framework has two tools Sperber lacked: "Semantic Flow" as a process independent of Signals — explaining path divergence; the same Signal entering different Semantic Flows walks different paths — and a hard distinction between the Signal end and the semantic end, capturing both "Signal unification but semantic divergence" and "Signal plurality but semantic convergence." Two phenomena Sperber's framework cannot distinguish.

### 9.7 Truth Boundary: Philosophy

The framework does not judge the truth or falsehood of semantic content. A Signal enters an Individual, produces Semantic Flow, Semantic Flow collapses into an output Signal — the framework describes the dynamics of this process. It does not judge whether the semantics produced are "right" or "wrong." Truth judgment belongs to philosophy. The framework's output can become material for philosophical discussion — you use the framework to describe the dynamics of a phenomenon; philosophers can perform epistemological analysis on that description.

### 9.8 Value Boundary: Ethics

The framework does not judge good or bad, right or wrong, should or should not. In the civilization essay, Mao Zedong is a "hero" — functionally speaking, having executed the function of aligning core Semantic Flow with reality. The framework does not judge the moral consequences of this alignment. In the religion essay, no path is "better." In the heliocentric modeling, the Church's Suppression is not "wrong" — it is the loop's self-protective dynamics at work. Value judgment belongs to ethics. To the Semantic Flow of Individuals. The framework describes what happened. Ethics judges whether it should have. The framework's output is the factual basis for ethical judgment.

## X. Scope and Limitations

**Applicable:** Any phenomenon in which a Signal arrives at an Individual and produces Semantic Flow. The Individual may be a human or an AI in which the Law of the Black Box holds partially.

**Not applicable:** Pure signal-level problems that do not involve Individuals receiving and processing Signals — material strength, algorithm efficiency, chemical reactions, the mathematical properties of supply-and-demand curves. The criterion: ask yourself — is the core of the phenomenon I am analyzing "what happens after a Signal reaches an Individual"? If yes, the framework applies. If not, do not force it.

**Limitations:** Does not address truth or falsehood. Does not address value. Does not provide methods for reducing Transformation. Does not predict time. Does not track Individuals' inner lives. Is itself subject to the postulates — the framework's own expressions are Signals; readers with different Xin will read different semantics from them. The framework cannot exempt itself from its own postulates.
