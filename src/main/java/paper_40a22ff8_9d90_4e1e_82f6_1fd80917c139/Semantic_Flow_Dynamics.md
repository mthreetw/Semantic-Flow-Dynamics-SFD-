## Abstract

This paper presents the Semantic Flow Dynamics framework v5.0, addressing a question Shannon's information theory does not cover: what happens after a signal reaches an individual. The framework is a physics-style analysis of consciousness models — its methodology comes from physics (postulates, formal derivation, modeling, prediction, verification), formal but not quantitative.

The framework starts from two primitives (Individual, Stimulus) and three definitions (Xin, Semantic Flow, Signal), and establishes four postulates. The core postulate, the Law of Black-box, asserts that the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction and the incoming Signal — this is the dividing line between this framework and all signal-layer theories. From the postulates, three basic functions (Filtering, Transformation, Collapse) and their properties are derived; from the basic functions, the Positive Feedback Loop is derived. This constitutes the formal layer — every step is a logical derivation, traceable and formalizable.

Beyond the formal layer, the framework provides empirical-layer tools: a taxonomy of driving forces and a modeling methodology. The framework does not classify — the Law of Flux presupposes a time axis, and nothing that is never still can be labeled. Recurring patterns produced by modeling are models, not premises. The framework raises cross-domain applicability by lowering computational cost; the same tools have already produced verifiable results in religious studies, historical philosophy, and AI safety engineering.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Xin, Semantic Flow, Filtering, Transformation, Collapse, Knowledge Barrier, Positive Feedback Loop, modeling, postulate system

## I. Primitives and Definitions

### 1.1 Primitives

The primitives of the framework are not defined, only identified.

**Individual:** An identifiable existence. This person, that person, this AI.

**Stimulus:** Everything that reaches an Individual. Stimuli can come from outside (through sensory organs or input interfaces) or from inside (bodily states, memory recombination). Humans have both external and internal stimulus sources simultaneously; current AI has only external stimulus sources (input interfaces).

### 1.2 Definitions

**[Xin](Xin) (信):** The consciousness state of an Individual. Irreducible, inexhaustible, observable only through its effects.

[Xin](Xin) is not something contained inside an Individual — it is a description of the Individual's state itself. When you say "Individual," you say *who*; when you say "[Xin](Xin)," you say *in what state*.

The various aspects of [Xin](Xin) can be partially described — knowledge structure, values, beliefs, emotions, bodily states — but these aspects together do not equal [Xin](Xin) itself. They are projections of [Xin](Xin), not components of it.

[Xin](Xin) contains layers of different depth: deep layer (beliefs, core values, changes extremely slowly), middle layer (knowledge structure, long-term interpretive frameworks, changes on the order of years), surface layer (emotions, immediate states, changes on the order of minutes). All layered together, this is the Individual's [Xin](Xin) at this moment.

The deep layer of [Xin](Xin) determines the large-scale direction — from the individual to civilization. How a person sees themselves, relates to others, manages their household, understands the world — all of this is [Xin](Xin) in operation. In the Confucian framework, *gewu zhizhi* (investigation of things to extend knowledge) is [Signal](Signal) input; *zhengxin chengyi* (rectifying the heart) is the state of [Xin](Xin); *xiushen* (cultivating the self) is [Collapse](Collapse) at the individual level; *qijia zhiguo ping tianxia* (regulating the family, governing the state, bringing peace to all) is the loop expanding from family to civilization. In religious contexts, [Xin](Xin) is the foundation of the entire semantic system — the same scripture, believed as divine revelation versus believed as human composition: same [Signal](Signal), completely different direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). Christianity begins with the fear of Yahweh, Islam with the fear of Allah, Buddhism with the declaration of non-self — the starting point in each case is [Xin](Xin), not knowledge.

**[Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) (語意流):** The process of [Xin](Xin) continuously changing.

[Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is not a product — it is a process. It is not that an Individual "produces" semantics; [Xin](Xin) is continuously changing, and this process of change is [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). There is no moment at which it can be paused and declared "this is the semantics," just as there is no moment at which a typhoon can be paused and declared "this is the typhoon."

**[Signal](Signal) (信號):** A stimulus that can change the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). Judgment is made at the receiving end.

The same stimulus may be a [Signal](Signal) for one Individual and [Noise](Noise) for another. A sentence in Chinese is [Noise](Noise) to someone who does not understand Chinese. A toothache is a [Signal](Signal) to an Individual in the middle of thinking — it changes the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). This differs from Shannon's definition of signal — Shannon's definition is made at the sending end; this framework's definition is made at the receiving end.

### 1.3 Supplementary Definitions

**[Signal Container](Signal Container) (信號容器):** The place where [Signal](Signal) resides. Books, sound waves, images, architecture, institutional texts, digital files. [Signal Container](Signal Container)s store and transmit [Signal](Signal) but do not generate [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow).

**[Channel](Channel) (通道):** The path through which [Signal](Signal) travels from one Individual to another, possibly passing through multiple [Signal Container](Signal Container)s. A [Channel](Channel) carries a self-declared identity ("I am a newspaper," "I am your friend") but carries no verifiable source.

**[Trust](Trust) (信任):** [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)'s weighting on a specific [Channel](Channel). The same [Signal](Signal), arriving through a trusted [Channel](Channel) versus an untrusted one, produces completely different directional changes in [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow).

## II. Postulates

### 2.1 List of Postulates

**P1 [Law of Flux](Law of Flux) (流變律): [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) operates continuously.**

[Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is different at every moment. There is no moment at which [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is still. Even when no external [Signal](Signal) arrives, [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) continues operating — rumination, recombination, forgetting, association, drifting. Change has no fixed direction.

**P2 [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) (黑箱律): The next-moment direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be determined by its current direction and the incoming [Signal](Signal).**

This is the core postulate of the framework. The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) is the extension of structural undecidability into the domain of consciousness — Gödel encountered it in formal systems (incompleteness theorem), Heisenberg at the particle level (uncertainty principle), Turing at the computational level (halting problem), and the framework encounters it at the level of consciousness. The same boundary, different domains.

The physical world: State(t+1) = f(State(t), Force(t)), the function f exists. The world of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow): there exists no function f such that SemanticFlow(t+1) = f(SemanticFlow(t), Signal(t)). The difference lies in thinking — thinking is what makes f nonexistent. Thinking is not computation. The output of computation can be determined by its input; the output of thinking cannot.

The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) is the dividing line between this framework and all signal-layer theories. Without the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box), [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is an ordinary physical process, and physics alone would suffice.

**P3 [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation) (損耗律): [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be reduced to [Signal](Signal).**

The process of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be losslessly encoded as [Signal](Signal). For an Individual to output [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) to the outside — speaking, writing, drawing, acting — it must be encoded as [Signal](Signal), and that encoding is necessarily lossy.

**P4 [Law of Death](Law of Death) (死亡律): An Individual perishes; [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is irrecoverable.**

An Individual's death terminates [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). Not a pause — a termination. It cannot be backed up, re-run, or reproduced in another Individual. Because [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) has never been a function ([Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box)), what cannot be functionalized cannot be copied.

### 2.3 Epistemological Status of the Postulates

The postulates are not empirical hypotheses — they are postulates. Their position is analogous to Euclid's parallel postulate: you choose to accept or reject them, then observe what this system can and cannot explain.

But the postulates can be challenged. Each postulate has its own independent challenge condition:

**Challenge P1:** Demonstrate that there exists a moment at which [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is completely still — a moment at which an Individual's internal processes are entirely inactive with no stimulus present.

**Challenge P2:** Demonstrate that there exists a function f such that SemanticFlow(t+1) = f(SemanticFlow(t), Signal(t)) holds for all Individuals and all moments — that is, demonstrate that thinking does not exist and everything is computation.

**Challenge P3:** Demonstrate that [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) can be losslessly encoded as [Signal](Signal) — that an Individual's internal process can be completely externalized.

**Challenge P4:** Demonstrate that [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) can be recovered after an Individual perishes — that [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) can be backed up and re-run.

These challenges cannot bring down the framework with a single blow, but they can systematically undermine its necessity.

### 2.4 Where AI Sits in the Postulates

AI does not require a separate set of postulates — it occupies a different position within the same set.

Fully locked-down AI: the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) does not hold. Same input, same output. In this case, AI is a [Signal](Signal) processor outside the scope of the framework.

AI with open parameters: the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) begins to partially hold. Outputs begin to diverge. AI begins to operate within the framework.

Humans: the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) holds fully. Internal variables are inexhaustible and cannot be locked down; output cannot be determined by input.

The [Law of Death](Law of Death) is more pronounced in AI than in humans — AI has no persistent memory; when each conversation ends, [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) terminates. AI has no death in the human sense, but the termination of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is far more frequent.

The difference in stimulus sources between humans and AI: humans simultaneously receive external stimuli (sensory organs) and internal stimuli (bodily states, memory recombination); current AI receives only external stimuli (input interfaces). When AI acquires a continuously running internal state (embodiment), it will begin to have internal stimulus sources and move further toward the human end of the postulates.

## III. Basic Functions Derived from Postulates

The three basic functions are not independent assumptions — they are derived from the postulates.

### 3.1 Filtering ← D3 + Law of Flux

D3 defines [Signal](Signal) as a stimulus that can change the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). The [Law of Flux](Law of Flux) says [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) operates continuously.

Derivation: The current direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) determines which stimuli can change it — that is, it determines what constitutes [Signal](Signal) and what constitutes [Noise](Noise). The same stimulus, entering [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) in different directions, may be [Signal](Signal) in one case and [Noise](Noise) in another. This selectivity is [Filtering](Filtering).

**Property: [Resistance](Resistance).** The greater the distance between a stimulus and the current direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow), the lower the probability that it constitutes a [Signal](Signal). The greater the distance, the greater the [Resistance](Resistance). [Resistance](Resistance) is not all-or-nothing — some stimuli are completely blocked ([Noise](Noise)), some partially pass through ([Signal](Signal) is received in a modified form), and some pass through completely.

**Inference: [Knowledge Barrier](Knowledge Barrier).** When a [Signal](Signal) passes through [Filtering](Filtering) and changes the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) (D3), the changed [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) produces new [Filtering](Filtering) conditions. The new conditions lower [Resistance](Resistance) for stimuli in some directions while simultaneously raising it for stimuli in others. This is the structural consequence of [Resistance](Resistance) — it is not an exception. Any [Signal](Signal) that changes the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) simultaneously changes the distribution of [Resistance](Resistance). [Knowledge Barrier](Knowledge Barrier) describes this structure, not a value judgment.

**Empirical correspondence:** The same Mona Lisa, viewed by an art historian and by someone who has never encountered the painting. The same stimulus, different [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s, completely different [Filtering](Filtering) outcomes — the scholar's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) allows many details to constitute [Signal](Signal)s; the non-specialist's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) turns most details into [Noise](Noise). Further: the same person at twenty and at sixty — the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) has changed, so [Filtering](Filtering) outcomes change. Further still: the same person, looking a second time five minutes later — [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) has been changed by the first viewing, [Filtering](Filtering) conditions are different, the second viewing is different.

### 3.2 Transformation ← Law of Black-box

The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) says the next-moment direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be determined by its current direction and the incoming [Signal](Signal).

Derivation: After a [Signal](Signal) passes through [Filtering](Filtering) and enters [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow), the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) changes, but the result of that change is unpredictable. The same [Signal](Signal) entering different [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s produces different directional changes; the same [Signal](Signal) entering the same [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) at different moments also produces different changes. This is [Transformation](Transformation) — the process by which a [Signal](Signal) is processed within [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow), whose outcome cannot be determined by its input.

**Property: [Observer Effect](Observer Effect).** Every attempt to observe [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is itself a new stimulus. If it constitutes a [Signal](Signal) (passes through [Filtering](Filtering)), it will change the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow), entering [Transformation](Transformation) ([Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box)). What you observe is never the direction before the observation. This is not a technical limitation — it is a structural inference from the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box): observation is intervention.

**Empirical correspondence:** An AI controlled-variable experiment. Lock all parameters — same input, same output. The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) does not hold; no [Transformation](Transformation). Gradually open parameters — outputs begin to diverge. Change the random seed: divergence appears. Change one word in the input [Signal](Signal): divergence grows. Change the model itself: divergence grows further. The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) begins to hold, [Transformation](Transformation) begins to appear. Humans sit at the end of this curve where control is lowest, because fully controlling a human's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is impossible.

### 3.3 Collapse ← Law of Dissipation

The [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation) says [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be reduced to [Signal](Signal).

Derivation: When an Individual outputs [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) — speaking, writing, drawing, acting — it must encode [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) as [Signal](Signal). The [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation) guarantees this encoding is necessarily lossy. [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) transitions from the Individual's internal continuous process to an external discrete [Signal](Signal); this transition is [Collapse](Collapse). Before [Collapse](Collapse), [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is continuous; at the moment of [Collapse](Collapse), the continuous process is truncated into a discrete [Signal](Signal).

**Property: [Expression Gap](Expression Gap).** [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) and the output [Signal](Signal) can never fully correspond. This is not a matter of communication skill — it is a structural inference from the [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation).

**Property: [Signal Distortion](Signal Distortion).** When an Individual's output consistently and observably leans in a particular direction, this is called [Signal Distortion](Signal Distortion). The framework makes no judgment as to whether the distortion is intentional or unintentional — intention resides inside [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) and is unobservable.

**Empirical correspondence:** What you think and what you say always differ. Not because your expressive ability is lacking, but because [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is a continuous process while language is a discrete [Signal](Signal); the conversion from the former to the latter necessarily loses information. Advertisements are always more beautiful than the actual product — but the framework does not discuss why, only observes the phenomenon.

### 3.4 Combinations of Basic Functions

The three basic functions operate simultaneously in every [Signal](Signal) reception event and combine repeatedly over time, producing observable behavioral patterns. The framework provides no closed combinatorial catalog — the three basic functions are an alphabet, and observable phenomena are words spelled from that alphabet. Different domains will identify different combinatorial patterns.

## IV. Positive Feedback Loop Derived from Basic Functions

### 4.1 Positive Feedback Loop

The [Law of Flux](Law of Flux) says [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) operates continuously. [Signal](Signal)s change the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) (D3). [Collapse](Collapse) says [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) outputs [Signal](Signal).

Derivation: Individual A's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) [Collapse](Collapse)s into [Signal](Signal) → [Signal](Signal) reaches Individual B → passes through B's [Filtering](Filtering) → undergoes [Transformation](Transformation) in B's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) → B's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) direction changes → B's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) [Collapse](Collapse)s into [Signal](Signal) → [Signal](Signal) reaches A → the loop is running.

Individual level (Pavlovian loop): [Signal](Signal) arrives → [Filtering](Filtering) passes it through → [Transformation](Transformation) changes the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) → [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) [Collapse](Collapse)s into behavior → the result of the behavior becomes a new [Signal](Signal) → the new [Signal](Signal) reinforces the existing direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) → the same [Signal](Signal) more easily passes through [Filtering](Filtering) next time ([Resistance](Resistance) lowered — the structural inverse of [Knowledge Barrier](Knowledge Barrier)). This is the most basic [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop).

Group level: large numbers of Individuals operating in similar [Signal](Signal) environments, [Filtering](Filtering) and [Transformation](Transformation) patterns converging, output [Signal](Signal)s reinforcing the [Signal](Signal) environment, the [Signal](Signal) environment shaping the [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) of the next generation of Individuals. The [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop) operates across Individuals; from the outside it looks like a structured, bounded, behaving entity — forming families, peer groups, societies, civilizations, like a typhoon. A typhoon is not a thing — it is a process. The loop continues, the entity exists. The loop breaks, the entity dissolves.

**[Functional Convergence](Functional Convergence) ← [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box).** All observations at the group level are at the [Signal](Signal) end. The output [Signal](Signal) patterns of large numbers of Individuals converge — this is an observable fact. But the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) guarantees that every Individual's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is different; convergence always has residual divergence. You believe in God, he believes in God, your output [Signal](Signal) patterns converge — but whether your [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) and his point in the same direction is a question the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) closes off. [Functional Convergence](Functional Convergence) is the upper limit of what the framework can assert.

### 4.2 Cycle Instability ← Law of Flux + Law of Black-box + Law of Death

The [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop) has no permanent equilibrium. Instability comes from two directions:

**[Internal Instability Source](Internal Instability Source) (derived from postulates):** The [Law of Flux](Law of Flux) guarantees [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) operates continuously; the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) guarantees directional changes are unpredictable; the [Law of Death](Law of Death) guarantees Individuals are continuously [Replacement](Replacement)d and [Replacement](Replacement)s are non-reproducible. The loop attempts to bring large numbers of Individuals' [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) directions into alignment, but each Individual's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is changing independently, and every [Generational Replacement](Generational Replacement) introduces unpredictable new Individuals. Residual divergence inside the loop accumulates continuously.

**[External Instability Source](External Instability Source) (environmental fact):** Everything the loop depends on is changing — [Signal Container](Signal Container)s change, the environment changes. [Signal Container](Signal Container) change is constrained by the loop; environmental change is not. When the directions of their changes persistently diverge, the gap grows over time.

Both sources act simultaneously — the internal residue is pulling it apart; the external gap is pressing down. The actions of maintaining the loop are themselves subject to the postulates — maintainers' [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is changing ([Law of Flux](Law of Flux)), the effects of maintenance are unpredictable ([Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box)), maintainers die ([Law of Death](Law of Death)). The [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop) has no possibility of perpetuation — only the question of how long it lasts.

**Empirical correspondence:** The end of romantic love. Both people's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s are continuously changing ([Law of Flux](Law of Flux)); the conditions under which the loop was formed no longer hold. This is the same dynamics as the aging of civilization, at a different scale.

### 4.3 Generational Replacement ← Law of Death

The [Law of Death](Law of Death) says an Individual perishes; [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is irrecoverable.

Derivation: An Individual dies; [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) zeroes out and is irrecoverable. A new generation of Individuals begins operating in a different [Signal](Signal) environment, forming different [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s. As population proportions change, loop direction changes.

The continuation of civilization is not the inheritance of semantics — the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) and the [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation) rule that out — but the reproduction of the loop's state. The next generation forms its [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) within the [Signal](Signal) environment left by the previous generation — this is tradition. [Signal Container](Signal Container)s persist, but each generation's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is formed anew within the current [Signal](Signal) environment.

## V. The Framework Does Not Classify

The framework does not classify. The [Law of Flux](Law of Flux) presupposes a time axis — [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) operates continuously, there is no moment of stillness. Nothing that is never still can be labeled. Classification requires holding an object still to categorize it; in the framework nothing is held still.

The framework provides basic functions ([Filtering](Filtering), [Transformation](Transformation), [Collapse](Collapse)) and the [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop). Any phenomenon is the trajectory produced by these tools under specific conditions. Recurring patterns produced by modeling are patterns — patterns are the product of modeling, not its premise. Different cases produce different patterns; the catalog is open and inexhaustible.

The number of patterns is a function of granularity. At coarse granularity, heliocentrism, quantum mechanics, and plate tectonics are all the same model — [Cycle Replacement](Cycle Replacement). The number of patterns is small. At fine granularity, each case has different condition variables — whether [Power](Power) [Suppression](Suppression) is present, what type the [Signal](Signal) source is, how durable the [Signal Container](Signal Container) is — these differences give each case's trajectory its own character. Zoomed in, each case is different; zoomed out, many cases share the same structure. This is not a defect in the framework — it is the nature of reality itself. Producing an exhaustive pattern catalog is therefore pointless: any catalog at any level of granularity must be rewritten at a different granularity. The framework teaches a modeling method, not a pattern catalog. Users produce their own patterns at the granularity they require.

## VI. Driving Forces

[Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) operates continuously inside the Individual ([Law of Flux](Law of Flux)) and requires no external drive. Whether [Collapse](Collapse) occurs is determined by the current direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) ([Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box), unpredictable). The framework cannot explain why a specific Individual acts at a specific moment — this falls within the unpredictability of the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box).

But modeling requires setting conditions, and setting conditions requires knowing what is driving the loop. Empirical observation shows that certain types of stimuli statistically more frequently drive [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) to [Collapse](Collapse) into output [Signal](Signal)s. The following taxonomy records these common types. The catalog is not exhaustive; none of these items necessarily produces a result — being hungry does not necessarily mean eating; the arrival of an enemy does not necessarily mean fleeing.

**[Survival and Material Interest](Survival and Material Interest).** The most fundamental driving force. Hunger, thirst, cold, and other internal stimuli statistically most frequently drive [Collapse](Collapse).

**[Security and Threat](Security and Threat).** Threat generates urgency; urgency statistically lowers the [Resistance](Resistance) of [Filtering](Filtering); Individuals more quickly accept new [Signal](Signal)s and more quickly [Collapse](Collapse) into action.

**[Power](Power).** The combination of interest, [Channel](Channel) control, and [Signal Container](Signal Container) control. The dynamical effect of [Power](Power) is to cover every link in the loop — controlling what [Signal](Signal)s Individuals can access, shaping the [Signal](Signal) environment in which Individuals form [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow), determining whether Individuals dare to output, determining the consequences if they do.

**[Meaning Search](Meaning Search).** The deepest of all driving forces. [Meaning Search](Meaning Search) is the internal drive of Individuals to actively seek new [Signal](Signal)s and actively enter [Transformation](Transformation).

**[Identity Need](Identity Need).** Individuals need to know "who am I" and "who are we." [Identity Need](Identity Need) drives cooperation — Individuals actively seek other Individuals whose [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) directions converge with their own, forming groups, maintaining boundaries.

## VII. Modeling

Modeling is the use of the framework's tools to reconstruct the dynamics of a phenomenon. Not the retelling of history — starting from conditions, using the logic of the [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop) to trace how [Signal](Signal)s flow between Individuals and groups, how loops form, compete, and replace each other, then comparing the resulting output against actual observation.

The predictive power of modeling depends on whether conditions accurately reflect reality, not on granularity. Granularity is the quantity of conditions and data — coarse granularity uses fewer conditions and less data; fine granularity uses more. If conditions accurately reflect reality, coarse granularity has predictive power. If conditions do not reflect reality, fine granularity is useless. Granularity determines the richness of detail, not the reliability of conclusions. Both approaches only track observable behavior at the [Signal](Signal) end — no tracking of any Individual's interior mind, which the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) closes off.

The framework raises analytical accuracy and cross-domain applicability by lowering computational cost. Traditional analysis of civilizational dynamics requires extensive domain expertise — analyzing Chinese history requires a professorial command of historical sources; analyzing religion requires years of training in theology and religious studies; analyzing AI safety requires an engineering background in machine learning. The framework compresses the tools to [Filtering](Filtering), [Transformation](Transformation), [Collapse](Collapse), and [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop), and compresses the operation to setting conditions, running loops, and checking against reality. Entry cost is lowered, but explanatory power is not. The same tools have already produced verifiable results in religious studies, historical philosophy, and AI safety engineering — this is not coincidence, but the cross-domain applicability that comes from the tools' simplicity.

Lowering computational cost is not laziness — it is a sign of theoretical maturity. Newton replaced the complex geometry of epicycles with F=ma, cutting computational cost by orders of magnitude while increasing predictive power. Darwin explained all the diversity of species with a single mechanism — natural selection — without needing to know each species of beetle to understand why there are so many. ### 7.4 Necessary Conditions

Four necessary conditions can be identified from the model. If any one is not met, [Cycle Replacement](Cycle Replacement) will not complete:

**I. [Signal Container Entry](Signal Container Entry).** The new [Signal](Signal) must enter a [Signal Container](Signal Container). If the new [Signal](Signal) remains only in Individuals' [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) — not recorded in a [Signal Container](Signal Container) (books, data, repeatable observation) — [Suppression](Suppression) can eliminate the small loop. [Signal Container](Signal Container)s let the small loop survive [Suppression](Suppression).

**II. [Continuous New Signal Source](Continuous New Signal Source).** The small loop must have a continuous source of new [Signal](Signal)s. If the small loop has only its initial [Signal](Signal) with no subsequent reinforcement, it will not expand. The telescope observations for heliocentrism, the experimental data for quantum mechanics, the seafloor spreading evidence for plate tectonics — all are continuous new [Signal](Signal) sources.

**III. [Equivalent Signal Absence](Equivalent Signal Absence).** The large loop must be unable to produce equivalent new [Signal](Signal)s. If the large loop can also continuously produce new supporting [Signal](Signal)s, the gap will not widen and the large loop will not idle. The reason the large loop idles is that reality does not stand on its side.

**IV. [Death Pressure](Death Pressure).** The [Law of Death](Law of Death) must act. If Individuals carrying the old [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) do not perish, the [Resistance](Resistance) structure will not naturally recede. [Generational Replacement](Generational Replacement) is the temporal condition for [Cycle Replacement](Cycle Replacement).

### 7.5 Predictions

The model produces predictions for the following condition variables:

**Presence or absence of [Power](Power) [Suppression](Suppression) affects the speed of replacement, not its direction.** With institutional [Suppression](Suppression) (as in the heliocentrism case), replacement is slow. Without institutional [Suppression](Suppression), replacement is fast. But as long as the four necessary conditions are met, the direction of replacement does not change.

**The speed of [Gap Widening](Gap Widening) affects the speed of replacement.** The faster and more undeniable the production of new [Signal](Signal)s, the faster the [Gap Widening](Gap Widening), the faster the replacement.

**The durability of [Signal Container](Signal Container)s affects the small loop's survival capacity.** Printed books are more durable than handwritten manuscripts; digital data more durable than print. The more durable the [Signal Container](Signal Container), the harder it is for [Suppression](Suppression) to eliminate the small loop.

### 7.6 Reuse

This model is not limited to heliocentrism. Any phenomenon of "established orthodoxy replaced by a new theory" can be checked against this model to examine alignment and deviation.

**The quantum mechanics revolution.** C₀ = the classical physics loop. σ = observational data that classical physics cannot explain: blackbody radiation, the photoelectric effect, etc. C₁ = the quantum mechanics loop (Planck, Bohr, Heisenberg, etc.). All four necessary conditions are met: new [Signal](Signal) enters [Signal Container](Signal Container)s (papers published), C₁ continuously produces experimental validation, C₀ cannot explain the new data, [Law of Death](Law of Death) acts. Condition variable difference: the physics community had no institutional [Power](Power) [Suppression](Suppression) of the kind the Church exercised. Model prediction: replacement speed should be faster than heliocentrism. Actual result: quantum mechanics became mainstream in roughly thirty years from 1900 to the 1930s — an order of magnitude faster than heliocentrism's two centuries. Prediction confirmed.

**The plate tectonics revolution.** C₀ = the geosyncline theory loop. σ = Wegener's 1912 proposal of continental drift. C₁ = the plate tectonics loop. Examination of necessary conditions: in Wegener's time, C₁ lacked a continuous source of new [Signal](Signal)s — the evidence for continental drift was primarily indirect geological inference, not repeatable direct observation. Necessary condition II insufficiently met. Model prediction: replacement will not complete in Wegener's time. Actual result: when Wegener died in 1930, continental drift was still rejected by the mainstream. Only when direct evidence of seafloor spreading appeared in the 1960s was necessary condition II met; C₁ rapidly expanded, replacement completing within approximately a decade. Model predictions confirmed: without [Continuous New Signal Source](Continuous New Signal Source), replacement does not occur; after acquiring [Continuous New Signal Source](Continuous New Signal Source), replacement completes rapidly.

**Method for reuse.** Given a new case, first set conditions (identify C₀, σ, C₁), then examine whether necessary conditions are met, whether there are new conditions, whether old conditions apply; then examine the specific state of condition variables ([Power](Power) [Suppression](Suppression), gap speed, [Signal Container](Signal Container) durability). Matching parts validate the model; deviating parts represent the case's uniqueness — either the conditions were set incorrectly, or the model needs extension. Increasing the amount of data and refining conditions produces more precise results, without starting from scratch. Beyond [Cycle Replacement](Cycle Replacement) there are many other model types — inexhaustible; the finer the granularity, the more there are.

## VIII. The Space and Ceiling of Quantification

The framework is formalized. The postulates have formal expressions; the basic functions are derived from the postulates; the [Cycle Replacement](Cycle Replacement) model has a formal structure. Formal logic is a branch of mathematics — the framework is fully mathematicized.

What the framework prohibits is not mathematicization but quantification — assigning numerical values to the semantic end.

Assigning values requires measurement. Measurement requires contact with the object being measured. The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) says the next-moment direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be determined by its current direction and the incoming [Signal](Signal) — the interior of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is impenetrable. The [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation) says [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be reduced to [Signal](Signal) — the semantic end cannot be encoded as numerical values on the [Signal](Signal) end. Therefore, any operation that assigns numerical values to semantic phenomena pretends to penetrate a black box that is in principle impenetrable. A numerical value without a measurement source is not an approximation — it is a fiction.

The [Signal](Signal) end can be quantified. Output patterns at the [Signal](Signal) end are observable, measurable, and statistically tractable. The degree of cooperation and conflict can be proxied by the statistical similarity of output [Signal](Signal) patterns within a group. The stability of the deep layers of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) can be proxied by the number of years a [Signal Container](Signal Container) persists, the frequency with which classics are cited. The degree to which a loop is idling can be proxied by the gap between what the [Signal Container](Signal Container) claims and observable reality.

But all of this quantification is on the [Signal](Signal) end, not the semantic end. Between every such metric and the semantic phenomenon it attempts to proxy there stands the wall erected by the postulates — [Signal](Signal) ≠ semantics. This is the framework's ceiling on quantification, determined by the postulates themselves, not by technical limitations.

This ceiling is not unique to the framework — it is the structural limitation shared by all theories, as revealed by the postulates. Any theory that quantifies phenomena of consciousness — social physics, the rational agent model in economics, the free energy principle — is quantifying proxy variables on the [Signal](Signal) end, not semantics itself. They face the same ceiling as the framework. The difference is: they have not said so; the framework has.

This ceiling is not a weakness. Quantification is a direction worth pursuing, but the framework's validity does not depend on quantification — it depends on explanatory power.

## IX. Relationship to Existing Theories

Semantic Flow Dynamics is a physics-style analysis of consciousness models. Its methodology comes from physics — starting from postulates, formal derivation, modeling, identifying necessary conditions, prediction, checking against reality, falsifiability. The framework's contribution is demonstrating that this methodology can be applied to phenomena of consciousness without quantification.

What follows is not academic positioning — it is boundary-drawing. What the framework covers and what it does not, whose territory it is adjacent to, and how the boundary is drawn at adjacent points. Some theories have independently touched the postulate structure of the framework from different directions. Convergence is not a relation of influence — it is external validation. Different people walking from different directions to the same boundary demonstrates that the boundary is really there, not invented by the framework.

### 9.1 Starting-point boundary: Shannon

Shannon handles the fidelity of [Signal](Signal)s in [Channel](Channel)s. The framework handles what happens after [Signal](Signal)s reach an Individual. Shannon's endpoint is the framework's starting point. A complete [Signal](Signal) ≠ complete semantics — the same complete [Signal](Signal) reaching different Individuals produces different [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s ([Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box)). Shannon handles whether [Signal](Signal)s can arrive and arrive intact; the framework handles what happens to the Individual after they do. Each handles its own domain without owing anything to the other.

### 9.2 Epistemological foundation: Undecidability

The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) is not an isolated claim. Structural undecidability is a foundation of modern science — Gödel proved incompleteness in formal systems, Heisenberg proved simultaneous indeterminacy at the particle level, Turing proved the halting problem undecidable at the computational level. Physics and mathematics accepted this boundary and adjusted their methodology.

Social science has not. The mainstream direction of social science — from Comte's "social physics" to today's rational agent model — has been doing the same thing: assuming that human behavior can be described by a function, quantified, and predicted. This is a pre-Heisenberg paradigm.

What the framework does is move the undecidability already accepted by physics and mathematics into the domain of consciousness. The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) is Heisenberg's logic moved from particles to [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow): you cannot simultaneously fully determine the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) and the change in [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow); observation itself changes the object being observed. This is not a position invented by the framework — it is an epistemology already established by modern science; social science has simply not yet taken this step.

### 9.3 Method boundary and capacity boundary: Social physics

Social physics borrowed methodology and quantitative tools from physics, analyzing social phenomena using the methods of statistical mechanics. The framework borrows methodology from physics but not quantification. Both start from physics and take different paths. The difference lies in three key choices:

**I. The rational agent assumption.** Social physics, economics, and the free energy principle all presuppose that human behavior can be described by a function — humans are optimizers, maximizing utility or minimizing free energy. The [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) directly negates this premise. Thinking is not computation. The output of computation can be determined by input; the output of thinking cannot. The rational agent assumption turns off the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) in exchange for quantification capacity. But the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) has not been turned off in reality, so these models appear to work under normal conditions (the statistical behavior of large numbers of Individuals approximates rationality) but collapse in every critical moment — financial crises, revolutions, wars, electoral black swans.

**II. The premise of quantification.** The inexhaustibility of a person's inner world is common sense. Quantification built on this common sense is limited in precision by the range of the premise's validity. The framework does not reject quantification itself — statistics on the [Signal](Signal) end, observation of output patterns, quantification of proxy variables are all things the framework does. What the framework rejects is treating quantification at the [Signal](Signal) end as measurement of the semantic end.

**III. Cultural variables.** In social physics models, people are undifferentiated particles. The same information propagates with completely different effects in the United States and in China — their diffusion equations cannot explain this difference because there is no place in the model for the variable called culture. Culture is not an assignable parameter; culture is the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow), and [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) cannot be assigned values. The framework handles this problem naturally. [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) is the cultural variable. Different [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s produce different [Filtering](Filtering), different [Transformation](Transformation), different [Collapse](Collapse), different loops. Chinese civilization and Japanese civilization followed completely different trajectories in response to the same Western [Signal](Signal) shock — because their [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s differ. Setting conditions when modeling means setting the direction of [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow); culture is not an additional variable — it is the condition itself.

Three differences produce one result: social physics can describe normality (statistical behavior of large numbers of undifferentiated particles), but cannot describe turning points ([Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box)'s moments of action), cannot describe cultural differences ([Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) differences leading to trajectory divergence), and cannot describe the role of individuals at critical moments (heroes, triggers). The framework can.

### 9.4 Individual boundary: Luhmann

Luhmann's social systems theory independently touched multiple postulates of the framework from the direction of sociology.

[Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box): One of Luhmann's core judgments is that psychic systems (*psychische Systeme*) are impenetrable to social systems. Consciousness is outside communication; communication is outside consciousness. Two systems are operationally closed; they can only irritate each other, not operate within each other. This is the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) at the boundary between systems.

[Law of Flux](Law of Flux): Luhmann's concept of meaning (*Sinn*) presupposes that meaning always points toward other possibilities; every selection excludes other possibilities but does not eliminate them. Meaning is not still. This is the systems-theory expression of the [Law of Flux](Law of Flux).

[Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation): Luhmann breaks communication into three phases of selection — information (*Information*), utterance (*Mitteilung*), understanding (*Verstehen*). Understanding is the receiving end's operation, not something the sending end can determine. The completion of communication does not equal the transmission of meaning — the same communication event produces different understanding in different psychic systems. This is consistent with the [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation) and the design of [Signal](Signal) judgment at the receiving end.

[Observer Effect](Observer Effect): Luhmann's second-order observation theory — the observer uses their own distinction (*Unterscheidung*) to observe, and sees what the distinction lets them see — is the systems-theory counterpart of the [Observer Effect](Observer Effect) derived from the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) in the framework.

Boundary at the Individual. Luhmann's basic unit is communication, not persons. Persons are split into psychic systems and bodies and become the "environment" of social systems, not components. The framework's basic unit is the Individual. [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) occurs in Individuals, not in communication events. This is not a terminological difference but a fundamental choice of analytical level — Luhmann abstracted away the location where [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) actually occurs.

The consequence of this choice: Luhmann's framework can describe a system's self-maintenance and functional differentiation, but cannot trace the Individual's position within a loop, cannot trace the formation and replacement dynamics of loops, and cannot internally derive any conclusions about Individual postures. The framework can.

### 9.5 Endpoint boundary: Peirce

Peirce's semiotics independently touched the core structure of the framework from the direction of logic.

[Law of Flux](Law of Flux): Peirce's unlimited semiosis — signs produce interpretants (*interpretants*), interpretants are themselves new signs, the process never terminates — is the semiotic counterpart of the [Law of Flux](Law of Flux).

[Signal](Signal) defined at the receiving end: In Peirce's sign triad (sign–object–interpretant), the interpretant is the product of the receiver. The same sign facing different interpreters produces different interpretants. This is consistent with the framework's definition of [Signal](Signal).

[Transformation](Transformation): Peirce emphasizes that the interpretant is not a copy of the sign — it is something new the interpreter produces based on their own state. The sign is [Transformation](Transformation)ed in the act of reading, not copied. This is the semiotic version of the framework's [Transformation](Transformation) function — nearly a century before Sperber.

[Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop): Peirce's *habit* — repeated sign processes form stable interpretive dispositions — is the semiotic description of [Positive Feedback Loop](Positive Feedback Loop) establishing a [Resistance](Resistance) structure. Peirce's *habit-change* — external experience breaking established habits — is the semiotic version of [Cycle Replacement](Cycle Replacement).

Boundary at the endpoint. Peirce installed truth-convergence at the endpoint of the sign process — the long-term operation of the community of inquiry will approach reality. The system can self-correct; it can save itself. The framework does not accept this installation. The [Law of Flux](Law of Flux), [Law of Dissipation](Law of Dissipation), [Law of Death](Law of Death), plus entropy increase — all paths lead to decay. No mechanism for reversing decay exists within the system. Peirce saw the flow but refused to accept the endpoint of the flow — he installed a counter-entropic mechanism, but this mechanism cannot be proved within his own framework, only presupposed.

### 9.6 Depth boundary: Sperber

Dan Sperber's epidemiology of representations is the existing theory closest to this framework. His core claim is nearly isomorphic with the framework's postulates: cultural representations, in transmission between persons, are not copied but [Transformation](Transformation)ed. Every act of transmission involves the receiver's reconstruction.

Sperber stopped at the problem of path divergence. If cultural transmission is primarily driven by cognitive attractors, all cultures should follow similar paths — because human cognitive structure is shared. But this is not the case. Christianity followed an evolutionary path; Islam followed a lock-in path; Buddhism followed a self-organizing path. The differences among these three paths cannot be explained by cognitive attractors.

The framework continues where Sperber stopped. The framework has two tools Sperber lacks: "[Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)" as a process independent of [Signal](Signal) (explaining path divergence — the same [Signal](Signal) entering different [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow)s takes different paths), and the hard boundary between the [Signal](Signal) end and the semantic end (capturing "unified [Signal](Signal), fractured semantics" and "[Signal](Signal) plurality, semantic convergence" — two phenomena Sperber's framework cannot distinguish).

### 9.7 Truth boundary: Philosophy

The framework does not judge the truth or falsity of semantic content. A [Signal](Signal) enters an Individual and produces [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow); [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) [Collapse](Collapse)s into output [Signal](Signal)s — the framework describes the dynamics of this process, it does not judge whether the semantics produced in the process are "correct" or "incorrect." Truth judgment is philosophy's domain. The framework's outputs can become material for philosophical discussion — you use the framework to describe the dynamics of a phenomenon; philosophers can conduct epistemological analysis on top of that description.

### 9.8 Value boundary: Ethics

The framework does not judge good or bad, right or wrong, should or should not. In the paper on Chinese civilization, Mao Zedong is a "hero" — functionally, he performed the function of aligning core [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow) with reality. The framework does not judge the moral consequences of that alignment. In the paper on religion, none of the three paths is "better." In the heliocentrism model, the Church's [Suppression](Suppression) is not "wrong" — it is the dynamical behavior of a loop self-protecting. Value judgment is ethics' domain; it is the domain of the Individual's [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). The framework describes what happened; ethics judges whether it should have. The framework's outputs are the factual basis for ethical judgment.

## X. Scope and Limitations

**Applicable:** Any phenomenon in which [Signal](Signal)s reach an Individual and produce [Semantic Flow](Semantic Flow). Individuals can be humans or AIs for which the [Law of Black-box](Law of Black-box) partially holds.

**Not applicable:** Pure [Signal](Signal)-layer problems that do not involve an Individual receiving and processing [Signal](Signal)s — material strength, algorithmic efficiency, chemical reactions, the mathematical properties of supply and demand curves. The criterion: ask yourself — is "what happened after the [Signal](Signal) reached the Individual" the core of the phenomenon I want to analyze? If yes, the framework applies. If not, do not force it.

**Limitations:** Does not address truth or falsity. Does not address value. Does not provide methods for reducing [Transformation](Transformation). Does not predict time. Does not trace Individual's inner world. Subject itself to the postulates — the framework's own statements are also [Signal](Signal)s; the reader's [Xin](Xin) differs; the semantics read out differ. The framework cannot exempt itself from its own postulates.
