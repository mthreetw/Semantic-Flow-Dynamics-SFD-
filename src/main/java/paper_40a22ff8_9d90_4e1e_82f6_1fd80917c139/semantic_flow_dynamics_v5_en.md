## Abstract

This paper presents the Semantic Flow Dynamics framework v5.0, addressing a problem not covered by Shannon's information theory: what happens after a signal reaches an individual. The framework is a physics-style analysis of consciousness modeling — its methodology derives from physics (axioms, formal derivation, modeling, prediction, verification) and is formal but not quantitative.

The framework begins with two primitives (**Individual**, **Stimulus**) and three definitions (**Xin**, **Semantic Flow**, **Signal**), and establishes four axioms. The core axiom, the Law of Black-box, asserts that the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with any Signal — this is the boundary between the framework and all signal-level theories. From the axioms, three basic functions (**Filtering**, **Transformation**, **Collapse**) and their properties are derived, which in turn yield the **Positive Feedback Loop**. This constitutes the framework's formal layer — every step is a logical derivation, traceable and formalizable.

Beyond the formal layer, the framework provides empirical-layer tools: a taxonomy of motivating forces and modeling methods. The framework does not classify — the Law of Flux presupposes a time axis; nothing stands still long enough to be labeled. Recurring patterns extracted through modeling are the products of modeling, not its preconditions. The framework raises analytical applicability across disciplines by lowering computational cost; the same toolkit has already produced verifiable results in religious studies, philosophy of history, and AI safety engineering.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Xin, Semantic Flow, Filtering, Transformation, Collapse, Epistemic Barrier, Positive Feedback Loop, modeling, axiomatic system

## I. Primitives and Definitions

### 1.1 Primitives

The framework's primitives are not defined — they are only identified.

**Individual:** Any identifiable existence. This person, that person, this AI.

**Stimulus:** Everything that reaches an Individual. A Stimulus may come from outside (via the senses or an input interface) or from inside (bodily states, memory recombination). Humans have both external and internal Stimulus sources; current AIs have only external Stimulus sources (input interfaces).

### 1.2 Definitions

**Xin:** The consciousness-state of an Individual. It is irreducible, inexhaustible, and observable only through its effects.

Xin is not a thing inside an Individual — it is a description of the Individual's own state. When you say "Individual," you say *who*; when you say "Xin," you say *in what state*.

The various facets of Xin can be partially described — knowledge structures, values, beliefs, emotions, bodily states — but these facets together do not equal Xin itself. They are projections of Xin, not its constituent parts.

Xin contains layers of different mass: the deep layer (beliefs, core values, changing very slowly), the middle layer (knowledge structures, long-term judgment frameworks, changing on the scale of years), and the surface layer (emotions, immediate states, changing on the scale of minutes). Taken together, these constitute an Individual's Xin at any given moment.

The deep layer of Xin determines direction on the largest scales, from the individual to civilization. How a person sees themselves, how they treat others, how they maintain a household, how they understand the world — all of this is the state of Xin in operation. In Confucian terms, *gewu zhizhi* (the investigation of things to extend knowledge) is Signal input; *zhengxin chengyi* (rectifying the mind and making the will sincere) is the state of Xin; *xiushen* (self-cultivation) is Collapse at the individual level; and *qijia, zhiguo, ping tianxia* (ordering the family, governing the state, bringing peace to all under heaven) is the loop expanding from family to civilization. In a religious context, Xin is the foundation of the entire semantic system — the same passage of scripture read as divine revelation versus read as human composition involves the same Signal but produces entirely different directions of Semantic Flow. Christianity begins with fear of Yahweh; Islam begins with fear of Allah; Buddhism begins with *wu* (emptiness/negation) — all begin with Xin, not with knowledge.

**Semantic Flow:** The continuous process of change in Xin.

Semantic Flow is not a product — it is a process. It is not that an Individual "generates" semantics; rather, Xin is continuously changing, and that changing process is Semantic Flow. There is no moment at which you can pause it and say "this is the semantics," just as there is no moment at which you can pause a typhoon and say "this is the typhoon."

**Signal:** A Stimulus capable of changing the direction of Semantic Flow. Determined on the Individual's end.

The same Stimulus may be a Signal for one Individual and Noise for another. A sentence in Chinese is Noise for an Individual who does not understand Chinese. A toothache is a Signal for an Individual in the middle of thinking — it changes the direction of Semantic Flow. This differs from Shannon's definition of signal, which is defined on the sender's end; the framework defines Signal on the receiver's end.

### 1.3 Supplementary Definitions

**Signal Container:** Where Signals reside. Books, sound waves, images, architecture, institutional texts, digital files. Signal Containers store and transmit Signals but do not generate Semantic Flow.

**Channel:** The path a Signal takes from one Individual to another, possibly passing through multiple Signal Containers. A Channel carries a self-declared origin ("I am a newspaper," "I am your friend"), but carries no verifiable source.

**Trust:** The weighting that Semantic Flow assigns to a particular Channel. The same Signal arriving from a trusted Channel versus an untrusted Channel produces entirely different changes in the direction of Semantic Flow.

## II. Axioms

### 2.1 List of Axioms

**P1 The Law of Flux: Semantic Flow operates continuously.**

Semantic Flow is different at every moment. There is no moment at which Semantic Flow is at rest. Even when no external Signal arrives, Semantic Flow is operating — ruminating, recombining, forgetting, associating, drifting. Change has no fixed direction.

**P2 The Law of Black-box: The next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with any Signal.**

This is the core axiom of the framework. The Law of Black-box is the extension of structural undecidability into the domain of consciousness — Gödel encountered it in formal systems (the incompleteness theorems), Heisenberg encountered it at the particle level (the uncertainty principle), Turing encountered it at the computational level (the halting problem), and this framework encounters it at the level of consciousness. The same boundary, different domains.

In the physical world: State(t+1) = f(State(t), Force(t)), and the function f exists. In the world of Semantic Flow: there exists no function f such that SemanticFlow(t+1) = f(SemanticFlow(t), Signal(t)). The difference lies in thought — thought is the reason f does not exist. Thought is not computation. The output of computation is determinable by its input; the output of thought is not.

The Law of Black-box is the boundary between this framework and all signal-level theories. Without the Law of Black-box, Semantic Flow would simply be an ordinary physical process, and physics would suffice.

**P3 The Law of Dissipation: Semantic Flow cannot be losslessly reduced to a Signal.**

The process of Semantic Flow cannot be losslessly encoded as a Signal. When an Individual outputs Semantic Flow to the outside — speaking, writing, drawing, acting — the Semantic Flow must be encoded as a Signal, and this encoding is necessarily lossy.

**P4 The Law of Death: When an Individual ceases to exist, Semantic Flow cannot be recovered.**

When an Individual dies, Semantic Flow terminates. Not a pause — a termination. It cannot be backed up, re-run, or reproduced in another Individual. Because Semantic Flow was never a function (the Law of Black-box), something that cannot be functionalized cannot be replicated.

### 2.3 The Epistemological Status of the Axioms

The axioms are not empirical hypotheses — they are axioms. Their status resembles Euclid's parallel postulate: you choose to accept or reject them, then observe what this system can explain.

But the axioms can be challenged. Each axiom has an independent challenge condition:

**Challenge P1:** Demonstrate that there exists a moment at which Semantic Flow is completely at rest — an Individual in whom, in the total absence of any Stimulus, internal processing has completely ceased.

**Challenge P2:** Demonstrate that there exists a function f such that SemanticFlow(t+1) = f(SemanticFlow(t), Signal(t)) holds for all Individuals at all moments — i.e., prove that thought does not exist and everything is computation.

**Challenge P3:** Demonstrate that Semantic Flow can be losslessly encoded as a Signal — i.e., prove that an Individual's internal process can be completely externalized.

**Challenge P4:** Demonstrate that Semantic Flow can be recovered after an Individual's death — i.e., prove that Semantic Flow can be backed up and re-run.

These challenges cannot knock out the framework with a single blow, but they can systematically undermine its necessity.

### 2.4 The Position of AI Within the Axioms

AI does not require a separate set of axioms — it occupies a different position within the same set.

An AI with all parameters locked: the Law of Black-box does not hold. Same input, same output. In this case, the AI is a Signal processor and falls outside the framework's scope.

An AI with unlocked parameters: the Law of Black-box begins to hold partially. Outputs begin to diverge. The AI begins to operate within the framework.

Humans: the Law of Black-box holds fully. Internal variables cannot be exhausted or locked, and outputs cannot be determined by inputs.

The Law of Death is more apparent for AI than for humans — AI has no persistent memory; when each conversation ends, Semantic Flow terminates. AI does not die in the human sense, but its Semantic Flow terminates more frequently than a human's.

The difference in Stimulus sources between humans and AI: humans receive both external Stimuli (senses) and internal Stimuli (bodily states, memory recombination); current AIs receive only external Stimuli (input interfaces). When an AI acquires a persistently running internal state, it will begin to have internal Stimulus sources and will move further toward the human end of the spectrum within the axioms.

## III. Deriving the Basic Functions from the Axioms

The three basic functions are not independent assumptions — they are derived from the axioms.

### 3.1 Filtering ← D3 + The Law of Flux

D3 defines a Signal as a Stimulus capable of changing the direction of Semantic Flow. The Law of Flux says Semantic Flow operates continuously.

Derivation: The current direction of Semantic Flow determines which Stimuli can change it — i.e., determines what constitutes a Signal and what constitutes Noise. The same Stimulus entering Semantic Flows oriented in different directions may be a Signal for one and Noise for another. This selectivity is Filtering.

**Property: Resistance.** The greater the distance between a Stimulus and the current direction of Semantic Flow, the lower the probability that the Stimulus constitutes a Signal. The greater the distance, the greater the Resistance. Resistance is not all-or-nothing: some Stimuli are entirely blocked (Noise), some pass through in altered form (the Signal is received only after its shape has been changed), and some pass through entirely.

**Corollary: Epistemic Barrier.** A Signal that passes through Filtering changes the direction of Semantic Flow (D3); the changed Semantic Flow produces new Filtering conditions. These new conditions lower Resistance against Stimuli in some directions while simultaneously raising Resistance against Stimuli in other directions. This is the structural consequence of Resistance — not an exception. Every Signal that changes the direction of Semantic Flow simultaneously changes the distribution of Resistance. The Epistemic Barrier describes this structure; it is not a value judgment.

**Empirical correspondence:** Consider the Mona Lisa. An art historian and a complete stranger both stand before it — the same Stimulus, different Semantic Flows, entirely different results from Filtering. The art historian's Semantic Flow allows a great many details to constitute Signals; the layman's Semantic Flow turns most details into Noise. Further: the same person viewing it at twenty versus at sixty has a Semantic Flow oriented differently, and Filtering yields different results. Further still: the same person who has just looked at it and then looks again — the Semantic Flow has already been altered by the first experience; the Filtering conditions differ, and the second experience differs.

### 3.2 Transformation ← The Law of Black-box

The Law of Black-box says the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with any Signal.

Derivation: After a Signal passes through Filtering and enters Semantic Flow, the direction of Semantic Flow changes — but the result of that change is unpredictable. The same Signal entering different Semantic Flows produces different directional changes; the same Signal entering the same Semantic Flow at different moments also produces different directional changes. This is Transformation: the process by which a Signal is processed within Semantic Flow, whose result cannot be determined by the input.

**Property: Observer Effect.** Every attempt to observe Semantic Flow is itself a new Stimulus. If it constitutes a Signal (passes through Filtering), it will change the direction of Semantic Flow, entering Transformation (the Law of Black-box). What you observe is never the direction that existed before observation. This is not a technical limitation — it is a structural corollary of the Law of Black-box. Observation is intervention.

**Empirical correspondence:** AI controlled-variable experiment. Lock all parameters — same input, same output. The Law of Black-box does not hold; no Transformation. Gradually release parameters — outputs begin to diverge. Change the random seed: divergence appears. Change one word of the input Signal: divergence grows. Change the model itself: divergence grows further. The Law of Black-box gradually holds; Transformation gradually appears. Humans occupy the end of this curve where the degree of control is smallest, because completely controlling a human's Semantic Flow is impossible.

### 3.3 Collapse ← The Law of Dissipation

The Law of Dissipation says Semantic Flow cannot be losslessly reduced to a Signal.

Derivation: When an Individual outputs Semantic Flow — speaking, writing, drawing, acting — the Semantic Flow must be encoded as a Signal. The Law of Dissipation guarantees that this encoding is necessarily lossy. Semantic Flow changes from a continuous process inside the Individual to a discrete Signal outside — this transition is Collapse. Before Collapse, Semantic Flow is continuous; at the moment of Collapse, the continuous process is captured as a discrete Signal.

**Property: Expression Gap.** There can be no complete correspondence between Semantic Flow and the Signal that is output. This is not a problem of communication skill — it is a structural corollary of the Law of Dissipation.

**Property: Signal Distortion.** When an Individual's output continuously and observably skews toward a particular direction, this is called Signal Distortion. The framework does not judge whether Signal Distortion is deliberate or unintentional — intent is inside Semantic Flow and is not observable.

**Empirical correspondence:** What you have in mind and what you say always have a gap. Not because your expressive ability is poor, but because Semantic Flow is a continuous process and language is a discrete Signal; the transition from the former to the latter necessarily loses something. Advertisements are always more beautiful than the actual product — but the framework does not discuss reasons, only observes phenomena.

### 3.4 Combination of the Basic Functions

The three basic functions operate simultaneously in every act of Signal reception, and they combine repeatedly through time to produce observable behavioral patterns. The framework does not provide an exhaustive list of combinations — the three basic functions are an alphabet, and observable phenomena are words spelled out with those letters. Different domains will identify different combinatorial patterns.

## IV. Deriving the Positive Feedback Loop from the Basic Functions

### 4.1 The Positive Feedback Loop

The Law of Flux says Semantic Flow operates continuously. Signals change the direction of Semantic Flow (D3). Collapse says Semantic Flow outputs Signals.

Derivation: Individual A's Semantic Flow Collapses into a Signal → the Signal reaches Individual B → passes through B's Filtering → undergoes Transformation within B's Semantic Flow → B's Semantic Flow direction changes → B's Semantic Flow Collapses into a Signal → the Signal reaches A → the loop is running.

**At the individual level (Pavlovian loop):** Signal arrives → Filtering allows it through → Transformation changes the direction of Semantic Flow → Semantic Flow Collapses into behavior → the result of the behavior becomes a new Signal → the new Signal reinforces the existing direction of Semantic Flow → the next time the same Signal arrives it passes more easily through Filtering (the reverse of the Epistemic Barrier — Resistance is lowered). This is the most basic Positive Feedback Loop.

**At the group level:** Large numbers of Individuals operate in similar Signal environments; their Filtering and Transformation patterns converge; output Signals reinforce the Signal environment; the Signal environment shapes the Semantic Flows of the next generation of Individuals. The Positive Feedback Loop operates across Individuals. Seen from outside, it appears as an entity with structure, boundaries, and behavior, forming families, peer groups, societies, civilizations — like a typhoon. A typhoon is not a thing but a process. The loop continues, the entity exists. The loop breaks, the entity disperses.

**Functional Convergence ← The Law of Black-box.** All observation at the group level takes place on the Signal side. The output Signal patterns of large numbers of Individuals converge — this is an observable fact. But the Law of Black-box guarantees that every Individual's Semantic Flow is different; convergence always retains a residual. You believe in God and he believes in God; your output Signal patterns converge — but whether your Semantic Flow and his are oriented in the same direction, the Law of Black-box seals off that question. Functional Convergence is the upper limit of what the framework can claim.

### 4.2 Loop Instability ← The Law of Flux + The Law of Black-box + The Law of Death

The Positive Feedback Loop has no perpetually stable equilibrium. Instability comes from two directions:

**Internal Source of Instability (derived from axioms):** The Law of Flux guarantees that Semantic Flow continues operating independently; the Law of Black-box guarantees that directional changes are unpredictable; the Law of Death guarantees that Individuals are continuously replaced and replacement is not replicable. The loop attempts to align the Semantic Flow directions of large numbers of Individuals, but every Individual's Semantic Flow is changing independently, and every Generational Replacement introduces unpredictable new Individuals. Residuals within the loop accumulate continuously.

**External Source of Instability (environmental fact):** Everything the loop depends on is changing — Signal Containers are changing, the environment is changing. Changes in Signal Containers are constrained by the loop; changes in the environment are not. When the trajectories of these changes continue to diverge, the gap expands over time.

Both sources act simultaneously — the internal residuals tear from within, the external gap presses from without. The actions taken to maintain the loop are themselves subject to the axioms — the maintainer's Semantic Flow is changing (Law of Flux), the effects of maintenance are unpredictable (Law of Black-box), and the maintainer will die (Law of Death). There is no possibility of a Positive Feedback Loop persisting indefinitely; the only variable is how long it survives.

**Empirical correspondence:** The disappearance of love. The Semantic Flows of two people are both continuously changing (Law of Flux); the conditions that established the loop no longer hold. This is the same dynamics as the aging of a civilization, at a different scale.

### 4.3 Generational Replacement ← The Law of Death

The Law of Death says an Individual ceases to exist and Semantic Flow cannot be recovered.

Derivation: When an Individual dies, Semantic Flow goes to zero and cannot be recovered. A new generation of Individuals begins operating in a different Signal environment, forming different Semantic Flows. The demographic ratio changes; the direction of the loop changes.

The continuation of civilization is not the transmission of semantics (the Law of Black-box and the Law of Dissipation rule out that possibility) — it is the reproduction of the loop's state. The next generation forms its own Semantic Flow within the Signal environment left by the previous generation — this is tradition. Signal Containers persist, but every generation's Semantic Flow is formed anew within the Signal environment of its own present.

## V. The Framework Does Not Classify

The framework does not classify. The Law of Flux presupposes a time axis — Semantic Flow operates continuously; there is no moment of rest. Nothing stands still long enough to be labeled. Classification requires fixing the object before sorting it; nothing in this framework is fixed.

The framework provides basic functions (Filtering, Transformation, Collapse) and the Positive Feedback Loop. Any phenomenon is the trajectory produced by these tools running under specific conditions. Recurring patterns extracted through modeling are the products of modeling — not its preconditions. Different cases produce different patterns; the list is open and inexhaustible.

The number of patterns is a function of granularity. At coarse granularity, heliocentrism, quantum mechanics, and plate tectonics are all the same model — Loop Replacement. The number of patterns is small. At fine granularity, the condition variables of each case differ — whether Power Suppression is present, the type of Signal source, the durability of Signal Containers — and these differences make the trajectory of each case unique. Zoom in and every case is different; zoom out and many cases are the same structure. This is not a defect of the framework — it is a property of reality itself. Therefore, listing an exhaustive set of patterns is pointless: any list compiled at one granularity must be recompiled at another. The framework teaches modeling method, not a catalog of patterns. Users produce their own patterns at whatever granularity they need.

## VI. Motivating Forces

Semantic Flow operates continuously inside the Individual (Law of Flux) without requiring external motivation. Whether Collapse occurs is determined by the current direction of Semantic Flow (Law of Black-box — unpredictable). The framework cannot explain why a specific Individual chooses to act at a specific moment — this falls within the unpredictability of the Law of Black-box.

But modeling requires setting conditions, and setting conditions requires knowing what drives a loop. Empirical observation shows that certain types of Stimuli more commonly drive Semantic Flow to Collapse into output Signals on a statistical basis. The following taxonomy records these common types. The list is not exhaustive; each item does not necessarily produce a result — being hungry does not mean you will eat; an enemy approaching does not mean you will run.

**Survival and Material Interests.** The most fundamental motivating force. Internal Stimuli such as hunger, thirst, and cold most commonly drive Collapse on a statistical basis.

**Safety and Threat.** Threat creates urgency; urgency statistically lowers the Resistance of Filtering; the Individual more quickly accepts new Signals and Collapses into action more quickly.

**Power.** A combination of interests, Channel control, and Signal Container control. Power's dynamic effect is to cover every link in the loop — controlling what Signals Individuals can be exposed to, shaping the Signal environment in which Individuals form Semantic Flow, determining whether Individuals dare to output, and determining the consequences of that output.

**Meaning-seeking.** The deepest of all motivating forces. Meaning-seeking is the Individual's internal drive to actively pursue new Signals and actively enter Transformation.

**Identity Need.** Individuals need to know "who am I" and "who are we." Identity Need drives cooperation — Individuals actively seek out other Individuals whose Semantic Flows are oriented similarly, form groups, and maintain boundaries.

## VII. Modeling

Modeling is using the framework's tools to reconstruct the dynamics of a phenomenon. It is not retelling history — it is starting from conditions, tracing how Signals flow between Individuals and groups through the logic of the Positive Feedback Loop, how loops form, clash, and replace each other, and then comparing the results against actual observation.

The predictive power of modeling depends on whether the conditions match reality, not on granularity. Granularity is a function of the quantity of conditions and data — coarse granularity uses fewer conditions and less data; fine granularity uses more of both. If the conditions match reality, coarse granularity yields predictive power. If the conditions don't match reality, fine granularity is useless. What granularity determines is the richness of detail, not the reliability of conclusions. Both track only observable behavior on the Signal side — the Law of Black-box seals off any Individual's inner life.

The framework raises the accuracy and cross-domain applicability of analysis by lowering computational cost. Traditional analysis of civilizational dynamics requires extensive domain expertise and theoretical training — analyzing Chinese history requires the source-command of a top-tier professor, analyzing religion requires years of theology and religious studies, analyzing AI safety requires a machine learning engineering background. The framework compresses the tools to Filtering, Transformation, Collapse, and the Positive Feedback Loop, and compresses the operations to: set conditions, run loops, compare to reality. The entry threshold is lower, but explanatory power is not. The same toolkit has already produced verifiable results in religious studies, philosophy of history, and AI safety engineering — this is not coincidence; it is the cross-domain applicability that comes from the toolkit's parsimony.

Lowering computational cost is not laziness — it is the mark of a mature theory. Newton replaced the complex geometry of epicycles and deferents with F=ma; computational cost was cut by orders of magnitude while predictive power rose. Darwin explained the diversity of all species with the single mechanism of natural selection; one need not know every species of beetle to understand why there are so many species of beetle. To explain an equal quantity of phenomena with fewer tools is parsimony. If this framework were as difficult to use as traditional methods, it would provide no additional value.

### 7.1 Demonstration: Heliocentrism

**Conditions.** The geocentric model is a Positive Feedback Loop that has been running for over a thousand years. Signal Containers (Aristotle's works, Church doctrine, university curricula) are stably present; the outputs of large numbers of Individuals continuously reinforce the loop. The loop is self-sustaining; the Resistance structure is intact.

**New Signal enters.** In 1543, Copernicus publishes the heliocentric model. The distance between this Signal and the existing loop's direction is extreme. For most Individuals inside the loop, this Signal does not constitute a Signal — it is immediately Noise. A small number of scholars' Semantic Flows allow this Signal to pass through Filtering and begin Transformation within their respective Semantic Flows.

**Loop Formation (small loop).** The small number of Individuals who accept the heliocentric model begin mutually reinforcing their outputs, forming a small loop. The output Signals of the small loop orient differently from those of the large loop; the two loops enter confrontation.

**The large loop's response (Suppression).** The Church's Power covers every link of the loop — controlling what Signals Individuals can be exposed to, shaping the Signal environment in which Individuals form Semantic Flow, determining whether Individuals dare to output, determining the consequences of that output. The trial of Galileo and the declaration of heliocentrism as heresy are expressions of Power operating simultaneously on all links. Suppression feeds back into the large loop, reinforcing its existing Resistance. The outputs of supporters are Suppressed — some fall silent, some continue circulating privately. The small loop is compressed but not eliminated, because the Signal has already entered Signal Containers (the books have been printed, the observational data have been recorded).

**Gap Expansion.** The Individuals of the small loop continue using telescopes to produce new observational Signals — Jupiter's moons, the phases of Venus — each observation reinforcing the small loop. The large loop has no way to produce new supporting Signals — reality does not provide new data supporting geocentrism. The gap between what the large loop's Signal Containers claim and what Individuals actually receive widens continuously. The large loop begins to run in neutral.

**Generational Replacement.** Individuals carrying the deep Semantic Flow of geocentrism continue to die (Law of Death). The new generation of Individuals forms Semantic Flow in an environment that already contains heliocentric Signals — their Signal environment contains both old Signal Containers and new observational data; their Filtering conditions differ from the previous generation's; their Resistance structure differs. More and more new Individuals' outputs flow into the small loop. The two hundred years was not the time needed for persuasion — it was the time needed for Replacement.

**Loop Replacement.** The Positive Feedback Loop of the large loop fractures. The Individual outputs that sustained it no longer reinforce the loop; the Signal Containers (the geocentric parts of doctrine) lose their driving force. The small loop expands into the mainstream loop and begins establishing its own Signal Containers (textbooks, academic institutions, scientific methodology), producing its own Resistance structure, and beginning to protect itself.

After the heliocentric loop Stabilizes, it becomes the new orthodoxy. The new challenging Signals it encounters (the later challenge of relativity to Newtonian mechanics) encounter a Resistance structure with the same dynamics as the one heliocentrism once faced. The content of the Loop Replacement changed; the structure did not.

### 7.2 Comparison

What the model produced that matches actual history: Resistance was maintained for approximately two hundred years; Power covered all links of the loop but did not eliminate the Signal; the large loop was not defeated by the small loop — it was hollowed out by the gap between it and reality; the final breakthrough depended not on the persuasive force of argumentation but on Generational Replacement combined with the continuously expanding gap; after the new loop Stabilized, it repeated the old loop's self-protective behavior.

What the model cannot produce: Why two hundred years rather than one hundred or three hundred — time prediction is outside the framework's capacity. Why Copernicus rather than someone else was the first to output this Signal — an Individual's specific behavior is sealed by the Law of Black-box and is unpredictable.

### 7.4 Necessary Conditions

From the model, four necessary conditions can be identified. If any one is not satisfied, Loop Replacement will not complete:

**One: Signal Container Entry.** The new Signal must enter a Signal Container. If a new Signal stays only within Individuals' Semantic Flows without being recorded into a Signal Container (a book, data, repeatable observations), Suppression can eliminate the small loop. Signal Containers allow the small loop to survive under Suppression.

**Two: Continuous New Signal Source.** The small loop must have a continuous new Signal source. If the small loop has only an initial Signal with no subsequent reinforcement, it will not expand. The telescope observations for heliocentrism, the experimental data for quantum mechanics, the seafloor spreading evidence for plate tectonics — all are continuous new Signal sources.

**Three: Absence of Equivalent Signal.** The large loop must be unable to produce equivalent new Signals. If the large loop can also continuously produce new supporting Signals, the gap will not expand and the large loop will not run in neutral. The reason the large loop runs in neutral is that reality is not on its side.

**Four: Death Pressure.** The Law of Death must operate. If the Individuals carrying the old Semantic Flow do not die, the Resistance structure will not naturally recede. Generational Replacement is the temporal condition for Loop Replacement.

### 7.5 Predictions

The model generates predictions on the following condition variables:

**The presence or absence of Power Suppression affects replacement speed, not replacement direction.** With institutional Suppression (the heliocentric case), replacement is slow. Without institutional Suppression, replacement is fast. But as long as the four necessary conditions are satisfied, the direction of replacement does not change.

**The rate of Gap Expansion affects replacement speed.** The faster and more undeniable the production of new Signals, the faster Gap Expansion, the faster the replacement.

**The durability of Signal Containers affects the small loop's survival capacity.** Printed books are more durable than hand-copied manuscripts; digital data is more durable than printed material. The more durable the Signal Container, the harder it is for Suppression to eliminate the small loop.

### 7.6 Reuse

This model is not limited to heliocentrism. Any phenomenon of "established orthodoxy being replaced by a new theory" can be compared against this model to check for alignment and deviation.

**The quantum mechanics revolution.** C₀ = the classical physics loop. σ = observational data that classical physics cannot explain (blackbody radiation, the photoelectric effect, etc.). C₁ = the quantum mechanics loop (Planck, Bohr, Heisenberg, et al.). All four necessary conditions are satisfied: new Signals entered Signal Containers (papers published), C₁ continuously produced experimental verification, C₀ cannot explain the new data, and the Law of Death operated. Difference in condition variables: the physics community had no institutional Power Suppression of the kind the Church exercised. Model prediction: replacement speed should be faster than heliocentrism. Actual result: quantum mechanics went from 1900 to becoming mainstream in the 1930s — about thirty years, an order of magnitude faster than heliocentrism's two hundred years. Prediction confirmed.

**The plate tectonics revolution.** C₀ = the geosynclinal theory loop. σ = Wegener's 1912 proposal of continental drift. C₁ = the plate tectonics loop. Checking the necessary conditions: in Wegener's time, C₁ lacked a continuous new Signal source — the evidence for continental drift was mainly indirect geological inference, not repeatable direct observation. Necessary condition two was insufficiently satisfied; model prediction: replacement would not complete in Wegener's time. Actual result: Wegener died in 1930 with continental drift still rejected by the mainstream. It was not until the 1960s, when direct evidence of seafloor spreading appeared, that necessary condition two was satisfied; C₁ expanded rapidly, and replacement completed within about ten years. The model's prediction confirmed: when a Continuous New Signal Source is lacking, replacement does not occur; once one is acquired, replacement completes rapidly.

**Method for reuse.** With a new case, first set the conditions (identify C₀, σ, C₁), then check whether the necessary conditions are satisfied, whether there are new conditions, and whether old conditions apply — then examine the specific state of the condition variables (Power Suppression, rate of gap expansion, durability of Signal Containers). Where the model aligns, the model is validated; where it deviates, that is the uniqueness of the new case — either the conditions were set incorrectly, or the model needs extension. Adding more data to refine conditions yields finer results without starting from scratch. There are many other model types beyond Loop Replacement — the list is inexhaustible; the finer the granularity, the more types there are. The framework teaches modeling method, not a catalog of models.

## VIII. The Space and Ceiling of Quantification

The framework is formalized. The axioms have formal expressions; the basic functions are derived from axioms; the Loop Replacement model has a formal structure. Formal logic is a branch of mathematics — the framework is entirely mathematical.

What the framework prohibits is not mathematization but quantification — assigning numerical values to the semantic side.

Assigning values requires measurement. Measurement requires access to the object being measured. The Law of Black-box says the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with any Signal — the interior of Semantic Flow is impenetrable. The Law of Dissipation says Semantic Flow cannot be losslessly reduced to a Signal — the semantic side cannot be encoded into numerical values on the Signal side. Therefore, any operation that assigns numerical values to semantic phenomena is pretending to penetrate a black box that is in principle impenetrable. A numerical value without a source of measurement is not an approximation — it is a fabrication.

The Signal side can be quantified. Output patterns on the Signal side are observable, measurable, and statistically analyzable. The degree of cooperation versus confrontation can be proxied by statistical similarity in the output Signal patterns within a group. The stability of deep Semantic Flow can be proxied by the number of years Signal Containers have survived and the citation frequency of classics. The degree to which a loop runs in neutral can be proxied by the gap between Signal Container claims and observable reality.

But all such quantification is on the Signal side, not the semantic side. Between each indicator and the semantic phenomenon it tries to proxy, there stands the wall of the axioms — Signal ≠ semantics. This is the quantification ceiling of the framework, determined by the axioms themselves, not a technical limitation.

This ceiling is not a limitation unique to the framework — it is a structural limitation shared by all theories, which the axioms reveal. Any theory that quantifies conscious phenomena — social physics, the rational actor model in economics, the free energy principle — is quantifying proxy variables on the Signal side, not semantics itself. They face the same ceiling as the framework; the difference is that they have not said so out loud, while the framework has.

This ceiling is not a weakness. Quantification is a direction worth pursuing, but the framework's validity does not depend on quantification — it depends on explanatory power.

## IX. Relationship to Existing Theories

Semantic Flow Dynamics is a physics-style analysis of consciousness modeling. Its methodology derives from physics — starting from axioms, formal derivation, modeling, identifying necessary conditions, predicting, comparing against reality, and falsifiability. The framework's contribution is demonstrating that this methodology can be applied to conscious phenomena without quantification.

The following is not academic positioning — it is boundary-drawing. What the framework governs, what it does not, whose territory it borders, and how those borders are divided. Some theories have independently touched the framework's axiomatic structure from different directions. Convergence is not a relationship of influence — it is external validation. Different people walking from different directions to the same boundary indicates that the boundary genuinely exists there; it was not invented by the framework.

### 9.1 Starting Boundary: Shannon

Shannon addresses the fidelity of Signals in Channels. This framework addresses what happens after a Signal reaches an Individual. Shannon's endpoint is the framework's starting point. Signal integrity does not equal semantic integrity — the same intact Signal reaching different Individuals produces different Semantic Flows (Law of Black-box). Shannon governs whether Signals can arrive and whether they arrive intact; the framework governs what happens to the Individual after they arrive. The two govern different things and owe nothing to each other.

### 9.2 Epistemological Foundation: Undecidability

The Law of Black-box is not an isolated claim. Structural undecidability is a foundation of modern science — Gödel proved incompleteness in formal systems, Heisenberg proved undeterminability at the particle level, Turing proved the halting problem undecidable at the computational level. Physics and mathematics accepted this boundary and adjusted their methodology.

Social science has not accepted it. The mainstream direction of social science — from Comte's "social physics" to today's rational actor models — has continuously done the same thing: assuming that human behavior can be described by a function, quantified, and predicted. This is the pre-Heisenberg paradigm, the Newtonian paradigm.

What the framework does is bring the undecidability that physics and mathematics have already accepted into the domain of consciousness. The Law of Black-box is Heisenberg's logic moved from particles to Semantic Flow: you cannot fully determine both the direction of Semantic Flow and the change in Semantic Flow simultaneously; observation itself changes the observed object. This is not a position invented by the framework — it is an epistemology already established by modern science; social science simply has not yet taken this step.

### 9.3 Method Boundary and Capability Boundary: Social Physics

Social physics borrowed methodology and quantitative tools from physics, applying statistical-physics methods to social phenomena. The framework borrowed methodology from physics but did not borrow quantification. Both start from physics and take different paths. The difference lies in three key choices:

**One: The rational actor assumption.** Social physics, economics, and the free energy principle all presuppose that human behavior can be described by a function — that humans are optimizers, maximizing utility or minimizing free energy. The framework's Law of Black-box directly negates this premise. Thought is not computation. The output of computation is determinable by its input; the output of thought is not. The rational actor assumption turns off the Law of Black-box in exchange for quantification ability. But the Law of Black-box has not been turned off in reality — so these models appear to work under normal conditions (statistical behavior of large numbers of Individuals approximates rationality) and collapse entirely at critical junctures: financial crises, revolutions, wars, electoral black swans.

**Two: The premise of quantification.** That the inner life of a human cannot be exhausted is common sense. Quantification built on this premise is limited in precision by the valid range of the premise. The framework does not reject quantification itself — statistical analysis of the Signal side, observation of output patterns, quantification of proxy variables: the framework does all of these. What the framework rejects is treating Signal-side quantification as measurement of the semantic side.

**Three: Cultural variables.** In social physics models, humans are undifferentiated particles. The same piece of information spreads with entirely different effects in the United States versus in China — their diffusion equations cannot explain this difference, because there is no place in the model for the variable "culture." Culture is not a parameter that can be assigned a value; culture is the direction of Semantic Flow, and Semantic Flow cannot be assigned a value. The framework handles this problem naturally. Semantic Flow is the cultural variable. Different Semantic Flows produce different Filtering, different Transformation, different Collapse, different loops. Chinese civilization and Japanese civilization, both encountering the same wave of Western Signals, took entirely different trajectories — because their Semantic Flows were different. When modeling, setting conditions means setting the direction of Semantic Flow; culture is not an additional variable — it is the condition itself.

These three differences produce one result: social physics can describe the normal state (statistical behavior of large numbers of undifferentiated particles); it cannot describe turning points (the moments when the Law of Black-box asserts itself), cannot describe cultural differences (trajectory divergences caused by different Semantic Flows), and cannot describe the role of individuals at critical moments (heroes, triggers). The framework can.

### 9.4 Individual Boundary: Luhmann

Luhmann's social systems theory has independently touched several of the framework's axioms from the direction of sociology.

The Law of Black-box: one of Luhmann's core judgments is that psychic systems (*psychische Systeme*) are impenetrable to social systems. Consciousness is outside communication; communication is outside consciousness. The two systems are operationally closed — they can only mutually irritate each other, not mutually operate. This is the Law of Black-box applied to the boundary between systems.

The Law of Flux: Luhmann's concept of meaning (*Sinn*) presupposes that meaning always points toward other possibilities; every selection excludes other possibilities without eliminating them. Meaning does not rest. This is the systems-theoretic formulation of the Law of Flux.

The Law of Dissipation: Luhmann breaks communication into three selective events — information (*Information*), utterance (*Mitteilung*), and understanding (*Verstehen*). Understanding is an operation on the receiver's side, not something the sender can determine. The completion of communication does not equal the transmission of meaning — the same communicative event produces different understanding in different psychic systems. This is consistent with the Law of Dissipation and the Signal-side definition of Signal.

The Observer Effect: Luhmann's second-order observation theory — the observer uses their own distinction (*Unterscheidung*) to observe and sees what the distinction allows them to see — is the systems-theoretic counterpart of the Observer Effect that the framework derives from the Law of Black-box.

The boundary lies at the Individual. Luhmann's basic unit is communication, not persons. Persons are split into psychic systems and bodies, treated as the "environment" of social systems, not constituent parts of social systems. The framework's basic unit is the Individual. Semantic Flow occurs in Individuals, not in communicative events. This is not a terminological difference — it is a fundamental choice of analytical level. Luhmann abstracted away the site where Semantic Flow actually occurs.

The consequence of this choice: Luhmann's framework can describe self-maintenance and functional differentiation of systems; it cannot track the Individual's position within a loop, cannot track the formation and replacement dynamics of loops, and cannot derive from within the theory any conclusions about the Individual's posture. The framework can.

### 9.5 Endpoint Boundary: Peirce

Peirce's semiotics independently touched the core structure of the framework from the direction of logic.

The Law of Flux: Peirce's unlimited semiosis — signs produce interpretants, interpretants are themselves new signs, the process never terminates — is the semiotic counterpart of the Law of Flux.

Signal defined on the receiver's side: In Peirce's sign triad (sign—object—interpretant), the interpretant is the product of the receiver. The same sign produces different interpretants for different interpreters. This is consistent with the framework's Signal definition.

Transformation: Peirce emphasizes that the interpretant is not a copy of the sign — it is a new thing produced by the interpreter based on their own state. A sign is Transformed in interpretation, not copied. This is the semiotic version of the framework's Transformation function — nearly a century before Sperber.

Positive Feedback Loop: Peirce's *habit* — repeated semiotic processes forming stable interpretive tendencies — is the semiotic description of the Positive Feedback Loop establishing a Resistance structure. Peirce's *habit-change* — external experience breaking established habits — is the semiotic version of Loop Replacement.

The boundary lies at the endpoint. Peirce installs truth convergence at the endpoint of semiotic process — the long-term operation of the community of inquiry will approach reality. The system can self-correct; it can rescue itself. The framework does not accept this installation. The Law of Flux, the Law of Dissipation, and the Law of Death, combined with entropy increase, mean all paths lead toward decay. There is no mechanism within the system to reverse decay. Peirce saw the flow but would not accept the flow's endpoint — he installed an anti-entropic mechanism, but this mechanism cannot be proven within his own framework; it can only be presupposed.

### 9.6 Depth Boundary: Sperber

Dan Sperber's epidemiology of representations is the closest existing theory to this framework. His core claim is nearly isomorphic with the framework's axioms: cultural representations are not copied in interpersonal transmission — they are Transformed. Every transmission involves the receiver's reconstruction.

Sperber stopped at the problem of path divergence. If cultural transmission is mainly driven by cognitive attractors, all cultures should follow similar paths — because the cognitive structure of humans is shared. But that is not the case. Christianity took an evolutionary path; Islam took a lock-in path; Buddhism took a self-organizing path. The differences between these three paths cannot be explained by cognitive attractors.

The framework continues where Sperber stopped. The framework has two tools Sperber lacks: "Semantic Flow" as a process independent of Signal (which explains path divergence — the same Signal entering different Semantic Flows produces different paths), and a hard boundary between the Signal side and the semantic side (which captures the two phenomena — "Signals unified but semantics split" and "Signals diverse but semantics converged" — that Sperber's framework cannot distinguish).

### 9.7 Truth Boundary: Philosophy

The framework does not judge the truth or falsity of semantic content. A Signal enters an Individual, produces Semantic Flow, and Semantic Flow Collapses into an output Signal — the framework describes the dynamics of this process and does not judge whether the semantics produced in the process are "correct" or "incorrect." Judgments of truth and falsity belong to philosophy. The framework's outputs can serve as material for philosophical discussion — use the framework to describe the dynamics of a phenomenon, and philosophers can perform epistemological analysis on top of that description.

### 9.8 Value Boundary: Ethics

The framework does not judge good or bad, right or wrong, or what ought to be. In the civilization paper, Mao Zedong is a "hero" — functionally, in that he performed the function of aligning the core Semantic Flow with reality. The framework does not judge the moral consequences of that alignment. In the religion paper, none of the three paths is "better." In the heliocentrism modeling, the Church's Suppression is not "wrong" — it is the dynamic behavior of a loop protecting itself. Value judgments belong to ethics; they belong to the Semantic Flow of each Individual. The framework describes what happened; ethics judges whether it should have. The framework's outputs are the factual basis for ethical judgments.

## X. Scope and Limitations

**Applicable:** Any phenomenon in which a Signal reaching an Individual produces Semantic Flow. The Individual may be a human or an AI in which the Law of Black-box partially holds.

**Not applicable:** Pure Signal-side problems that do not involve Individuals receiving and processing Signals — material strength, algorithmic efficiency, chemical reactions, the mathematical properties of supply-and-demand curves. Criterion: ask yourself — is the core of the phenomenon I am analyzing "what happened after a Signal reached an Individual"? If so, the framework applies. If not, do not force it.

**Limitations:** Does not address truth or falsity. Does not address values. Does not provide methods for reducing Transformation. Does not predict timing. Does not track the inner life of Individuals. Is itself subject to the axioms — the framework's own expressions are Signals; readers with different Xin will read out different semantics. The framework cannot exempt itself from its own axioms.
