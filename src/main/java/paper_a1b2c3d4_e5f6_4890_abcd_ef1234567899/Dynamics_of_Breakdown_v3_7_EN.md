# Dynamics of Breakdown v3.7

*Dynamics of Breakdown: How Civilizations Survive, Deform, and Die*

Cheng Yu Huang / 黃正宇

Upstream dependencies: *Institutional Dynamics* v1.0, *Semantic Flow Dynamics* v5.0, *Civilization Mechanics* v5.0

---

**Preamble:** This paper examines the dynamics of institutions following a Shock, at civilizational scale. Civilizations run multiple Templates simultaneously, building political, familial, military, and economic Institutions; each Institution has a Written Layer and an Unwritten Layer that govern how individuals act within it. The paper's question is: **what allows a civilization to survive across historical time?** Breakdown is the default; survival is the exception. The framework is scale-free — the same mechanism operates at the civilizational and individual levels under the same logic. The analytical scope of this paper is fixed at civilizational scale. The framework depends upstream on *Institutional Dynamics*, accepting all its definitions, postulates, and derived results.

### 1.2 Operational Definitions

**State Vector:** The complete state vector of the system at any given moment — a high-dimensional structure encompassing every current condition across all of society: economic structure, public sentiment, operational stability of Institutions, actual state of the Unwritten Layer, the center's capacity to govern localities, and the actual flow of resource distribution. The majority of the State Vector's dimensions are unobservable.

The State Vector has two classes of dimensions: **observable dimensions** (the outward forms of Institutions, rituals, surface-level operations) and **unobservable dimensions** (the actual state of the Unwritten Layer, public sentiment, the substantive distribution of internal power). The stabilizing property of Institutions can pull the observable dimensions back to their prior positions, but has no effect on the unobservable dimensions. (← Law of the Black Box)

**Template Coverage Range:** The set of conditions under which a Template, as an information structure, can prescribe behavior. When the State Vector falls within the Template Coverage Range, individuals have behavioral prescriptions and Functional Convergence can be maintained. When the State Vector falls outside the Template Coverage Range, individuals have no behavioral prescriptions and Functional Convergence collapses. Templates do not expand — the Template Coverage Range is the Template's initial structure.

**Template Library:** The set of Templates currently available to a civilization. Theory exists at the theoretical level in perpetuity, but must pass through one of the four entry pathways before entering the Template Library and becoming a candidate for institutionalization. Being in the Template Library does not mean having become an Institution — entry into the Template Library is a prerequisite for becoming an Institution, not the Institution itself. An Institution is what grows out of collective practice once enough people have accepted and enacted a Template; Institutions age and die. Theories in the Template Library do not perish — the question is only whether anyone finds them compelling. (Mohist theory still exists today; it simply has not entered any Template Library.)

**Resource:** The available objects defined by a Template. If something falls within no Template's Template Coverage Range, it does not exist for the system; neither Institutions nor individuals can mobilize it. Resources are not objectively given — they are the product of Template definition. The same thing may be a Resource under one Template and not under another.

**Breakdown:** The state in which a Shock moves the State Vector beyond the Template Coverage Range of all existing Templates, the Templates' theoretical frameworks can no longer explain the current situation, and Institutions can no longer stabilize. Breakdown does not equal Extinction — Breakdown is the starting point of prolonged instability, lasting until a Fallback Template capable of covering the new State Vector is found. The disappearance of an Institution is the observable output signal of Breakdown, not its cause.

**Shock:** Any change that moves the State Vector. A Shock itself is neutral; consequences are determined by the State Vector's position after it moves. The actual magnitude of a Shock cannot be determined until it has been fully Absorbed — this is not the Law of the Black Box; it is incomplete information.

**Absorption:** The process by which the system uses its existing Templates to respond to a Shock. Absorption always occurs, because existing Templates are the only tool the system possesses. Absorption is not repair — after Absorption the system does not return to its original position; the State Vector continues operating from its new position.

**Stress:** The internal pressure transmitted between Templates after a Shock enters the Template network. Stress is not the Shock itself; it is the system's internal response to the Shock.

**Stress Propagation:** The process by which Stress moves through the Template network. Stress always propagates along the path of least Resistance. Resistance is determined by multiple factors — geographic distance, tradition, law, political structure, historical grievances, economic dependency, cultural identity, and a large number of factors residing in the Unwritten Layer. These factors compound to produce an extremely complex Resistance distribution. The result of Stress Propagation may be a redistribution of Resources or a consumption of Resources, depending on the specific conditions along the propagation path. The specific propagation path is unpredictable beforehand and only partially traceable afterward.

**Sedimentation:** The result of the Absorption process entering historical conditions, becoming the starting point for the next movement of the State Vector. Sedimentation is irreversible.

**Irreversibility Theorem:** Any Shock, once Absorbed, necessarily Sediments its result as a historical condition. Institutions do not repair; there is only a new State Vector after Sedimentation. The system does not return to its original position. (← *Institutional Dynamics* §2.5 recursive structure; Law of Dissipation; Law of Death)

**Dual Nature of Institution:** Institutions simultaneously possess a stabilizing property and an accumulating property, unified and inseparable. The stabilizing property causes the observable dimensions of the State Vector to recover their original shape after a Shock; the accumulating property causes the result of each Shock to Sediment into the Unwritten Layer, driving the unobservable dimensions of the State Vector to drift continuously. Outward recovery does not mean the inner core has not changed.

**Fallback Template:** When an existing Template fails and the State Vector moves outside the Template Coverage Range, the candidate Templates in the Template Library capable of covering the new State Vector. Templates enter the Template Library through the four pathways, and then, once enough people have accepted its account of reality and put it into practice, an Institution grows out of collective practice. Two conditions must hold simultaneously for a Template to become an Institution: enough people accept its account of reality, and collective practice can transmit through the Unwritten Layer to the next generation. If either condition fails, the Institution cannot take root. A Fallback Template must be accepted and enacted by enough people before the State Vector stabilizes, or Functional Convergence cannot be rebuilt.

Acceptance is structurally constrained by the Sedimentation of the Unwritten Layer — when a foreign Template enters the Template Library, the Unwritten Layer will digest it into a form closer to its own Sedimentation before it can be accepted; Chinese Marxism and Japan's Western Institutions both followed this path. If the distance between the Unwritten Layer's Sedimentation and any available Template is too great, no Template can be digested, acceptance is never sufficient, and Institutions never take root — this is the structure of Haitianization. A more severe case is when the Unwritten Layer's Sedimentation is actively hostile to cooperation itself, so that acceptance of any Template requiring stranger-cooperation is never sufficient; the Coordination Radius is already compressed to near-zero while Institutions are still nominally in place. (cf. *China's Breakdown*)

**Silent Fracture:** The dynamic process by which the State Vector is continuously pushed by Shocks toward the boundary of a Template's explanatory power. Shocks enter; the State Vector moves toward the boundary; the portion of the situation the Template can cover grows smaller and smaller; the portion of the State Vector not covered by the Institution slowly spills over the edge — but the observable dimensions of the Institution, under the action of the stabilizing property, continue to operate normally, with no output signal. Silent Fracture is not a static structure; it is the process by which the State Vector continuously approaches the boundary while remaining silent at the observable layer. When conditions change, the observable dimensions catch up with the position the State Vector has already reached, and Breakdown signals appear suddenly.

**Knowledge Explosion Threshold:** The historical inflection point at which the rate of new Institution formation permanently falls below the rate at which the State Vector moves outside Template Coverage Ranges. Before this point, Transition has moments of completion. After it, the conditions for a stable Transition structurally disappear. This is a qualitative change, not a quantitative one.

**Historical Darkness:** The majority of historical events that enter the Unwritten Layer are irrecoverable. Historical Darkness is an ontological given, not a deficiency of data. (← Law of Dissipation; Law of Death; Unspeakability of the Unwritten Layer)

## II. Core Dynamics

### 2.1 Multi-Template Structure Within Civilizations

At any given moment, a civilization runs multiple Templates simultaneously. Confucianism and Legalism can coexist, each building different Institutions — political Institutions, family Institutions, military Institutions, economic Institutions — each with its own Written Layer and Unwritten Layer governing how people act within it.

Each Template has its own Template Coverage Range. A Shock can cause an Institution built by one Template to break down without affecting Institutions built by other Templates. The Opium War caused the Institution built on the "all nations come to pay tribute" Template to break down, while the political Institution built on the "supreme royal authority" Template continued to operate. This is the independent response of different Institutions to different Shocks within the same civilization.

Historians typically observe only the Breakdown of political Institutions, because they are most visible. But the Breakdown of family Institutions and economic Institutions can be equally severe; their output signals are simply harder to track. At civilizational scale, any Institution can face a Shock that its Template's theory cannot explain, and enter prolonged instability.

**Calibration: The Multi-Institutional Breakdown of the Western Roman Empire**

The Western Roman Empire's Breakdown (476 CE) is a classic case of multiple Institutions breaking down independently. Military Institutions broke down first — after the crisis of the third century, the empire was already being sustained by barbarian mercenaries. Economic Institutions — currency debasement, trading network fragmentation — broke down before the political Institutions. The political Breakdown signal visible in the observable dimensions in 476 CE was the output of a Silent Fracture that had run to completion, not its starting point. Each Institution had its own State Vector movement path; these were different Breakdown events, not one.

After Breakdown, the Template Library held multiple candidates, but acceptance was uneven. The Christian Template was the only one acceptable across ethnic groups; it was widely accepted and practiced, forming Church Institutions that covered spiritual order and partial administrative function — becoming stronger than during the imperial period. Germanic tribal Templates could be accepted at the scale of close-knit societies and form local Institutions, but could not cover the State Vector at imperial scale. Greco-Roman Templates still existed at the theoretical level, but lacked the social conditions for widespread acceptance; the pathway into the Template Library had been severed.

The result: no Template could rebuild Functional Convergence at imperial scale — the State Vector retreated to the scale covered by each available Template. Family Institutions and religious Institutions were actually more stable at the local scale; political fragmentation persisted. Persistent political instability was not due to an absence of order, but because the Christian Template itself did not prescribe political allegiance — who should be emperor fell outside its Template Coverage Range, and no political Institution could stand firm on the strength of acceptance of the Christian Template.

This calibration tests simultaneously: **2.1** (multiple Institutions running in parallel, each breaking down independently); **3.1** (prolonged instability is the default after Breakdown); **3.2** (the existence of a Fallback Template does not guarantee it can cover all scales); **3.6** (Anarchy is stability at reduced scale, not chaos).

### 2.2 The Single Mechanism

**A Shock moves the State Vector. The State Vector moves outside the Template Coverage Range. Breakdown occurs.**

The dynamics ask only one question: is the State Vector's high-dimensional configuration within the Template Coverage Range?

### 2.3 The Dual Nature of Institution

The stabilizing property and the accumulating property coexist — they are part of the essential nature of Institutions.

A Shock enters; the stabilizing property pulls the observable dimensions of the State Vector back — outward forms recover, everything appears unchanged. But simultaneously, the accumulating property Sediments the Shock's results into the Unwritten Layer, and the unobservable dimensions of the State Vector drift one step further.

The stronger the Institution, the stronger the stabilizing property, and the more completely the observable dimensions recover — but this simultaneously conceals the fact that the unobservable dimensions have already drifted. Stronger Institutions allow the Silent Fracture to run deeper; when Breakdown arrives, it is more sudden and more unpredictable.

**[Framework Derivation] This explains the typical pattern of dynastic collapse.**

In an unbroken series of disasters, each viewed in isolation appears as "the Institution successfully Absorbed the Shock," but viewed together they represent the unobservable dimensions of the State Vector moving step by step toward the boundary of the Template Coverage Range. What ultimately brings down the dynasty is often not the most severe event — the State Vector is already at the boundary, and any Shock can push it across.

**Calibration: The Accumulated Burden of Late Qing**

From the Opium War to the Revolution of 1911, this is the canonical case of the Dual Nature of Institution running the complete chain.

Each Shock — the Opium War (1842), the Taiping Rebellion (1850–1864), defeat in the First Sino-Japanese War (1895), the Hundred Days' Reform (1898), the Eight-Nation Alliance (1900) — the Qing court Absorbed using its existing Templates. Each time, the stabilizing property pulled the observable dimensions back: the emperor remained emperor, the examination system remained the examination system, ritual codes remained ritual codes. Each viewed in isolation appeared as "the Institution successfully Absorbed the Shock."

But the accumulating property was operating in parallel. What Sedimented into the Unwritten Layer after each Absorption continued to drift — after the Taiping Rebellion, the legitimacy of local militias; after the Self-Strengthening Movement, technological dependency; the alienation of the scholar-gentry from the center; after the Sino-Japanese defeat, the intelligentsia's fundamental challenge to the entire Template system; the continuous erosion of popular belief in the court's Sanctity. This drift in the unobservable dimensions left no output signal under the cover of each "outward successful recovery."

The accumulated burden is the state after the Silent Fracture has run deep enough — precisely because the outward dimensions recovered each time, no one could judge how far the State Vector was from the boundary. The Revolution of 1911 was not a particularly severe Shock; the State Vector had long since been at the boundary, and the observable dimensions finally caught up with the position the unobservable dimensions had reached long before.

This calibration tests: **2.3** (stronger Institutions allow the Silent Fracture to run deeper; Breakdown arrives more suddenly).

### 2.4 Stress Propagation Dynamics

A Shock enters the system, generating Stress within the Template network. Stress propagates along the path of least Resistance, and the result of propagation Sediments as historical conditions.

Resistance is determined by multiple factors, including but not limited to: geographic distance, tradition, law, political structure, historical grievances, economic dependency, and cultural identity. Most Resistance factors reside in the Unwritten Layer, are unobservable, and compound to produce an extremely complex Resistance distribution.

**[Framework Derivation] Conditional proposition:** The path of Stress Propagation is unpredictable beforehand — the complexity of Resistance distribution and the unobservability of the Unwritten Layer together foreclose the possibility of prediction. Partially traceable afterward — from observable output signals, part of the propagation path can be reconstructed by reverse inference, but most enters Historical Darkness.

**Calibration: Stress Propagation in the Taiping Rebellion**

The Taiping Rebellion was in the south; Stress propagated through the Template network in the south. Southern gentry were the path of least Resistance — geographically close, organized, resourced, and motivated to protect family property. Stress went this way; the southern gentry mobilized en masse, and Fallback Templates formed spontaneously here.

If the Taiping Rebellion had been a northern invasion, the geographic direction of Stress Propagation would have been entirely different; the Resistance of the southern gentry would have been relatively higher; Stress would not have reached them — the south would most likely have left it alone, as it did with the Southern Ming. Not because southerners lacked patriotism, but because Stress never propagated to them.

After the Taiping Rebellion was suppressed, the Fallback Templates (local militias, gentry political influence, fiscal flows shifting from center to localities) had already Sedimented into historical conditions; the original Template structure was irretrievable. Competition between old and new Templates generated persistent internal Stress; thereafter, every Shock caused the system to form new Fallback Templates more rapidly, until the Revolution of 1911 caused the observable dimensions to catch up with what the unobservable dimensions had reached long before.

This calibration tests: **2.4** (Stress propagates along the path of least Resistance; the propagation path is partially traceable afterward).

**Calibration: Resistance Design in Islamic Theocratic-Political Integration**

Islamic theocratic-political integration is a canonical case of Resistance distribution determined by institutional design. Islamic law and political Institutions are two faces of the same Template system; religion prescribes behavior, government enforces it, and the two are inseparable. This design makes Resistance extremely high in the direction of modernization — when a Shock enters the system, Stress propagates along the path of least Resistance; the path toward modernization has too-high Resistance, so Stress naturally does not flow there, and the State Vector does not move in that dimension over the long term.

This is not a passive result of geographic isolation; it is the Institution actively designing high Resistance into specific directions. The result is that the Template Coverage Range and the State Vector remain aligned over the long term, with Functional Convergence stably maintained. Saudi Arabia is the counterexample where this design was broken by external Shocks — oil revenues allowed external Resources to enter; Resistance in certain directions was lowered by Resource advantage; the State Vector began moving in the modernization dimension; the Resistance built by theocratic-political integration could no longer maintain the original propagation structure.

This calibration tests: **2.4** (Resistance distribution is determined by the Institutions formed by Templates; institutional design can keep Resistance in specific directions permanently high).

### 2.5 The Irreversibility of Sedimentation

**[Framework Derivation] Corollary I:** There is no repair. The observable dimensions of an Institution may recover; Sedimentation of the unobservable dimensions is irreversible. Once a Fallback Template is accepted by enough people, forms an Institution, and Sediments into historical conditions, the original Template structure is irretrievable. (Already tested by the Taiping Rebellion calibration.)

**[Framework Derivation] Corollary II:** Breakdown typically appears sudden. The stabilizing property maintains intact observable dimensions, but the State Vector has already been pushed toward the boundary of the Template's explanatory power through the Silent Fracture process. When conditions change, the observable dimensions catch up with the position the State Vector has already reached, and Breakdown signals appear suddenly. (Already tested by the Late Qing calibration.)

**[Framework Derivation] Corollary III:** Most is irrecoverable. History always has Historical Darkness; this is an ontological given.

### 2.6 How Shocks Act on Institutions

The Shocks an Institution may encounter are unbounded — including at minimum: war consuming Resources, new inventions exceeding the Template Coverage Range, cross-civilizational contact, famine, rising divorce rates, and countless other situations — and may cause phenomena including exhaustion of an Institution's Resources, systemic paralysis, or the system's inability to process the new State Vector. The framework intervenes after the State Vector has moved; it does not enumerate the mechanisms of movement beforehand. Drilling down is the work of sub-papers (cf. *The Ceiling of Secular Success: Meta-Values and Civilizational Dynamics*; *China's Breakdown*).

## III. Terminal-State Dynamics

### 3.1 After Breakdown: Prolonged Instability

The default output of Breakdown is prolonged instability, not Extinction. Existing Templates cannot explain the current situation; Institutions cannot tell people how to act — this state persists until a Fallback Template is accepted and practiced by enough people to form an Institution.

During the instability period, Stress continues to propagate through the Template network along the path of least Resistance. The result of Stress Propagation may accelerate the formation of Fallback Templates, or may cause the State Vector to continue drifting, making it harder to find a Fallback Template. The direction is unpredictable; results Sediment as historical conditions for the next round.

### 3.2 Stabilization Conditions

The stabilization condition is a Boolean: a Fallback Template exists, and is accepted and practiced by enough people before the State Vector stabilizes, forming an Institution.

If the condition holds, Functional Convergence is rebuilt under new conditions, the instability period ends, and Transition begins. If the condition does not hold, instability persists; the Terminal State moves toward Anarchy or Extinction.

The existence of a Fallback Template does not guarantee the stabilization condition holds — the Template Library may contain a Fallback Template, but Stress Propagation may not follow that path, or the State Vector may continue moving and cause even the existing Fallback Template to lose coverage capacity.

### 3.3 The Three Terminal States

**Terminal State:** The structural endpoint of a system after Breakdown. Terminal State = Transition ⊕ Anarchy ⊕ Extinction, a closed classification determined by two-layer conditions.

**Layer 1:** Can a Fallback Template be accepted and practiced by enough people before the State Vector stabilizes, forming an Institution? Yes → Transition. No → proceed to Layer 2.

**Layer 2:** Has the Feasibility Domain reached zero? Not yet → the State Vector retreats to the scale coverable by the Biological Drive and stabilizes → Anarchy. Reached zero → the State Vector itself disappears → Extinction.

**Transition**, **Anarchy**, **Extinction**.

### 3.4 The Two Forms of Transition

Transition is the process in which a Fallback Template catches the State Vector, and Functional Convergence is rebuilt under new conditions, after the State Vector has moved outside an old Template's Coverage Range. The mechanism is identical across all cases; the difference lies in the observable dimensions and the conditions for reaching a stable state. Transition = Abrupt Transition ⊕ Gradual Transition.

**Abrupt Transition:** Fast pace; a clear break in the observable dimensions; the disappearance of the old entity is identifiable.

**Calibration: The Japanese Meiji Restoration**

In 1867, Tokugawa Yoshinobu returned political power to the emperor; in 1868, Emperor Meiji began direct rule — the observable dimensions carry a precise timestamp. The entire Template Library of the bakuhan system was massively reorganized in an extremely short time: from feudal domain administration to centralized governance, from the warrior Template to the bureaucratic Template, from the closed-country Template to external openness. New Templates came mainly from external injection (Western institutional Templates rapidly entering the Template Library through the four pathways) and were widely accepted and practiced before the State Vector had fully stabilized; only 22 years elapsed from the return of power to the Meiji Constitution (1889). The disappearance of the old entity (the shogunate) is identifiable; the break is clear.

This calibration tests: **3.4 Abrupt Transition** (fast pace, clear break, old entity identifiable).

---

**Gradual Transition:** The continuity of the observable dimensions makes a break unidentifiable, but the unobservable dimensions of the State Vector have already become entirely different.

**Calibration: Two Centuries of Gradual Transition in the United States**

From Washington's time to today, the flag, Constitution, electoral Institutions, and separation of powers carry almost no observable break, creating the illusion that it is "the same thing." But the unobservable dimensions of the State Vector have drifted enormously: in scale, from an agricultural republic of 3 million to a global empire of 330 million; the federal government has gone from near-nonexistence to over one quarter of GDP; in the Unwritten Layer, the gentlemen's political conventions, respect for institutions, and norms of alternation — the things not written into the Constitution that make the Institutions function — are being continuously eroded; cultural identity has shifted from relative homogeneity to highly contested, with no consensus. The same constitutional framework operates on a State Vector that is an entirely different high-dimensional configuration.

This calibration tests: **3.4 Gradual Transition** (observable continuity concealing fundamental drift in unobservable dimensions).

### 3.5 Permanent Breakdown State

**Permanent Breakdown State:** The state, unique to the period after the Knowledge Explosion Threshold, in which the rate of new Institution formation permanently falls below the rate at which the State Vector moves outside Template Coverage Ranges; Fallback Templates recurrently appear but are disrupted by the next wave of Shocks before they can stabilize; and the conditions for reaching any Terminal State after Breakdown structurally disappear. The Permanent Breakdown State is not a Terminal State itself — it is a modern-exclusive slow-speed pathway toward Anarchy or Extinction, with a time scale that may span centuries.

**Calibration: Cross-Civilization Cross-Section**

The Permanent Breakdown State is not the peculiar predicament of a single civilization; it is the structural condition that all civilizations have entered jointly after the Knowledge Explosion Threshold. It therefore cannot be calibrated against a single case, but must be tested against common phenomena across civilizations.

Family Institutions — nearly all industrialized societies simultaneously exhibit rising divorce rates, falling birth rates, and fragmentation of family structure; no civilization has found a new family Template capable of stabilizing. Political Institutions — democratic, authoritarian, and theocratic political Templates have all experienced legitimacy crises in the same period; no political Institution can claim to have found a stable solution. Religious Institutions — secularization is advancing globally and simultaneously, but no alternative meaning system can fully catch the State Vector. That these three conditions appear simultaneously across different civilizations, different political systems, and different cultural backgrounds points to one conclusion: it is not that any particular civilization has failed — it is that the conditions for forming new Institutions have structurally disappeared at global scale.

This calibration tests: **3.5 Permanent Breakdown State** (modern-exclusive, globally shared, no single representative case).

**[Framework Derivation] The Permanent Breakdown State Has No Stable State**

The Permanent Breakdown State cannot logically be a stable state, because Institutions are mutually sustaining. One Institution's Breakdown, through Stress Propagation, threatens other Institutions; the chain effect causes the Feasibility Domain to continuously contract — family Institution Breakdown leads to population decline, population decline causes the labor force to shrink, economic Institutions come under pressure, military capacity falls, cultural transmission fractures. This Stress Propagation chain does not stop on its own, because no Template can explain the new State Vector and no stable Fallback Template can form.

Intergenerational understanding fracture makes matters worse. The intergenerational transmission of the Unwritten Layer fractures under sustained instability — the young and the old live within entirely different Template understandings; even the basic consensus needed to form a Fallback Template is disintegrating. The conditions for forming Fallback Templates are themselves deteriorating: not only is the State Vector moving too fast, the capacity to catch up is simultaneously shrinking.

Historically, when a civilization broke down, external civilizations' Fallback Templates could flow in to fill the vacuum — after the Western Roman Breakdown, the Christian Template and Germanic Templates entered from outside. The modern Permanent Breakdown State is one in which all civilizations have simultaneously entered this condition; there are no external Fallback Templates to borrow. This is a genuinely new situation.

The Permanent Breakdown State is therefore not a fourth Terminal State, but a modern-exclusive slow pathway toward Anarchy or Extinction — the time scale may be centuries, but the direction is determinate within the framework.

**[Framework Derivation] The Qualitative Shift Brought by the Knowledge Explosion:** Before the Knowledge Explosion Threshold, the rate at which the State Vector moves outside the Template Coverage Range and the rate at which new Institutions form are comparable; Transition has moments of completion. After the Knowledge Explosion Threshold, the former structurally and permanently exceeds the latter; the conditions for the cycle's completion disappear. The Permanent Breakdown State is therefore a modern-exclusive phenomenon.

**Calibration: Transmission Fracture in French Religious Institutions**

The Breakdown of French religious Institutions is a canonical case of the failure of transmission conditions. The overall picture is one of massive withdrawal from the dominant Template — 66% of Catholics never attend church; only 2% attend weekly Mass; the network of clergy is rapidly shrinking. The Unwritten Layer of religious Institutions — daily family ritual, community networks of belief, Catholic schools — has Sedimented into historical conditions through decades of secularization and is irretrievable.

But simultaneously there appeared a signal of Fallback Template acceptance — in 2025, 17,800 adults and adolescents were baptized, four to five times the number of 2022, more than 40% of them aged 18 to 25. This signal looks like institutional revival, but it is not.

These young people have accepted the Catholic Template's account of reality, but lack the conditions to transmit it to the next generation: no Catholic school network, no community of believers, parents who are not believers — they do not even know the language. Two conditions must hold simultaneously for a Fallback Template to become an Institution: enough people accept its account of reality, and collective practice can be transmitted through the Unwritten Layer to the next generation. The first condition holds in a small circle; the material basis for the second has already been destroyed by the Sedimentation of secularization.

This is the canonical result of a small number of people trying to make a Fallback Template into an Institution under the Permanent Breakdown State — not that no one finds the Template compelling, but that the transmission conditions no longer exist and the Institution cannot take root.

This calibration tests: **3.5 Permanent Breakdown State** (Fallback Template has some acceptance, but transmission conditions have failed; Institution cannot form).

### 3.6 Anarchy

At civilizational scale, with no Fallback Template accepted and practiced by enough people, the State Vector retreats to the scale coverable by the Biological Drive and stabilizes at the close-knit society scale. Not chaos — stability at reduced scale. (← *Institutional Dynamics* §1.7)

**[Framework Derivation] Internal Dynamics of Anarchy**

Anarchy is not the simultaneous failure of all Templates. It is the state in which high-order Templates (law, Violence Monopolist, stranger-coordination) cannot cover the new State Vector in their Template Coverage Range, while low-order Templates (close-acquaintance trust, clan protection, Biological Drive) continue to operate. Individuals have not lost behavioral rules; they have retreated, after the failure of high-order Templates, to the layer of least Resistance.

Stranger-coordination becomes extremely costly after high-order Templates fail — you don't know what the other party will do, and neither do they; behavioral expectation disappears. Close-acquaintance coordination is comparatively cheaper; Stress naturally flows that way. The **Coordination Radius** contracts from civilizational scale to close-knit society scale — this is the core driver of Anarchy; not a moral collapse, but the inevitable result of Stress Propagation.

This explains a phenomenon that appears paradoxical in wartime: extreme large-scale violence occurring simultaneously with intense small-scale mutual aid. This is not a contradiction — it is the output of the same mechanism at two scales. Fierce protection of one's own people; extreme uncertainty or even violence toward strangers — because the civilizational-scale Template has broken down but the close-acquaintance-scale Template is still intact. Wartime does not mean people have suddenly become bad; the high-order Templates constraining behavior toward strangers have failed; individuals retreat to low-order Templates; killing and plunder are the result of survival Templates taking the lead after high-order Templates fail.

Civilizational Breakdown does not mean everyone has lost their behavioral rules. People usually still have rules — it is just that the **Coordination Radius** of those rules has changed:

Before: stranger to stranger — "you cannot kill me."
After Breakdown: protection of one's own people; adversarial stance toward strangers.

Society has not gone from "having rules" to "having no rules at all" — it has retreated from large-scale stranger-coordination to small-scale close-acquaintance coordination. This is far more precise than "Anarchy = chaos," and constitutes the framework's complete account of §3.6.

### 3.7 Extinction

The Feasibility Domain reaches zero; the Physical Conditions no longer support any Template's operation; the State Vector itself disappears.

Extinction has no calibration cases in history — not because the cases are rare, but because Extinction is a state in which even individuals cannot survive; peoples who have died out entirely have left no records, and Historical Darkness here is absolute. The very cases that fully satisfy the definition eliminated the people capable of recording them. From the framework's perspective, Extinction is the actual default value for civilizations; recorded history itself is survivorship bias.

## Framework Boundaries

**Falsifiability conditions:** The framework is built on four upstream laws; violating any one falsifies the entire framework. Find a case in which the timing of Breakdown can be completely predicted from the observable dimensions without any assumption about the unobservable dimensions — this violates the Law of the Black Box. Find a case in which an Institution's unobservable dimensions fully recover to their pre-Shock state after a Shock — this violates the Law of Dissipation. Find a case in which a historical event that entered the Unwritten Layer can be completely recovered — this violates the Law of Death. Find a method that can directly observe the actual state of the Unwritten Layer rather than reverse-inferring it from observable outputs — this violates the Unspeakability of the Unwritten Layer.

**What can be said:** The mechanistic definition of Breakdown; the high-dimensional structure and dual dimensions of the State Vector; the Template Library and three-layer structure (theoretical layer, Template Library layer, Institution layer); the multi-Template structure within civilizations; the Dual Nature of Institution; Stress Propagation dynamics; Resources as the product of Template definition; the Irreversibility Theorem of Sedimentation; prolonged instability after Breakdown; stabilization conditions; typical pathways of how Shocks act on Institutions (descriptive, not classificatory); the two-layer conditional structure of the three Terminal States; the two forms of Transition; the qualitative shift of the Knowledge Explosion Threshold.

**What cannot be said:** The actual state of the State Vector's unobservable dimensions (← Law of the Black Box); the specific path of Stress Propagation (← complexity of Resistance distribution; unobservability of the Unwritten Layer); the actual magnitude of a Shock before it is fully Absorbed (← incomplete information); when a Fallback Template will be accepted by enough people and form an Institution (← Law of the Black Box); the specific timing of Breakdown (← Silent Fracture is unobservable); recovery of the Historical Darkness portion (← Law of Dissipation; Law of Death); a complete theory of how Shocks act on Institutions (← the possibilities are unbounded).

**Methodological boundary:** Historical Darkness is an ontological given. The unpredictability of Stress Propagation paths is not the Law of the Black Box — it is incomplete information. The factors determining the Resistance distribution are real; they are simply too complex, mostly residing in the Unwritten Layer, incalculable beforehand, and only partially traceable afterward. Case analysis operates within the range of observable output signals. Reverse inference is always incomplete, but incompleteness is not the same as invalidity. Stranger-coordination does not require eliminating human unknowability; it only requires a shared Template to make behavior predictable — this is precisely why the Law of the Black Box does not destroy the framework. (← Law of the Black Box; Functional Convergence)

## Theoretical Dialogue

**Weber's Institutional Sociology:** The closest structural predecessor. Weber describes how Institutions form legitimacy, the irreversibility of bureaucratic Institutions, and the dynamics of rationalization. But Weber is descriptive — he says what Institutions look like, not why they break down or survive under Shocks. This framework attempts to take one step beyond Weber's foundation, asking about mechanisms rather than descriptions.

**Complex Systems Theory and Phase Transitions:** The closest parallel in dynamical language. State Vector, Template Coverage Range, Silent Fracture approaching criticality — this structure is parallel to the concept of phase transitions in physics. The physics of a shattering vase can describe the mechanism of shattering but cannot predict the shape of the fragments; the framework's position is the same. Complex systems theory is typically mathematical; this framework abandons quantification in exchange for a more honest epistemological boundary — acknowledging that key variables are unobservable rather than pretending they can be measured with proxy variables.

**Ibn Khaldun's Asabiyyah:** The closest historical precursor. Ibn Khaldun in the fourteenth century already observed the relationship between the rise and fall of group cohesion and civilizational flourishing and decline. His asabiyyah is very close to this framework's Coordination Radius — both are saying that the scale of a group's cooperative capacity determines civilizational survival conditions. The difference: Ibn Khaldun lacked the language of Template and State Vector; the mechanism is intuitive, not structural.

**Arnold Toynbee's Comparative Civilization Studies:** The closest parallel in scale and ambition. Toynbee's challenge-and-response framework explains civilizational dynamics in a way that parallels this framework's Shock and Absorption conceptually. But Toynbee's framework is ultimately teleological, implying that civilizations have a life cycle. This framework explicitly rejects that assumption — Breakdown is the result of a mechanism, not of fate.

**Wittgenstein's Epistemological Boundary:** The closest parallel in methodological style. Say clearly what can be said; clearly delineate what cannot — this is rare among social-scientific theoretical frameworks. Most social-scientific theories attempt to say more; this framework attempts to say more precisely. The framework boundary is not a defect; it is honesty.

**Fundamental Divergence from Existing Breakdown Theories:** Jared Diamond (*Collapse*), Nassim Taleb (Black Swan), Daron Acemoglu and James Robinson (*Why Nations Fail*) all attempt to identify the causes of Breakdown — which variable causes Breakdown. This framework's question is different: it does not ask about the causes of Breakdown; it asks about the mechanism. Causes are unbounded; mechanisms are finite. This epistemological choice allows the framework to simultaneously acknowledge complexity and maintain explanatory power.
