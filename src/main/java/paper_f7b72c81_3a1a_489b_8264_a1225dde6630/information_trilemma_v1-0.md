## Abstract

Large language models (LLMs) are the first information systems in human history forced to simultaneously ingest knowledge across all scale levels. Physics, biology, history, law, casual conversation — all compressed into a single set of parameters. What humanity spent millennia carefully keeping apart has been packed into one architecture. This creates a structural predicament.

This paper proposes the Information Trilemma: in any information system, when Scope remains open, logical consistency and factual truth cannot simultaneously be satisfied. The three vertices are Scope (simultaneously accommodating adequate knowledge systems across multiple distinct scale levels), Logic (internal inferential consistency of the system), and Truth (correspondence with verifiable reality at a specific scale level).

The trilemma holds for a single core reason: the basic rules governing reality exist across all scale levels, and Logic holds across scale levels, but concrete phenomena are the combinatorial manifestation of rules at a specific scale level — and combinatorial manifestation changes across scale levels. This is an observed fact, independent of emergence, chaos, or nonlinearity. Rule-weight scale-dependence alone is sufficient for the trilemma to hold. The trilemma's constraint operates at the combinatorial manifestation layer, not the rule layer — a successful unified field theory would not dissolve it. The trilemma does not explain why multiple distinct scale levels exist, nor why combinatorial manifestation changes across scale levels, just as the second law of thermodynamics does not explain why entropy increases — it derives constraints from the observed fact.

LLM training data naturally spans vast numbers of scale levels, making the trilemma an engineering reality rather than a theoretical curiosity. Hallucination is not a bug; it is a structural cost. But structural does not mean unmitigable — RAG, tool calls, and domain fine-tuning all exchange Scope for Logic and Truth, the same strategy humans use when dividing knowledge into disciplines.

This paper is positioned as an empirical heuristic, not a formal theorem — a member of the same family as the CAP theorem and the Mundell-Fleming trilemma, but grounded in observed fact rather than formal proof. Its value lies not in proving impossibility, but in providing a trade-off framework for data curation and model design: you are making a three-choose-two decision. Know which vertex you are sacrificing, and sacrifice it deliberately.

**Keywords:** Information Trilemma, Scale Level, Combinatorial Manifestation, LLM, data curation, hallucination

# Part I: The Problem — LLMs Ingested Every Scale Level

---

## 1. LLMs Are the First Systems to Actually Do This

Human [Knowledge](Knowledge) systems have always been compartmentalized. Physics is divided by scale level, medicine by specialty, engineering by domain, history by period. Every functional [Knowledge](Knowledge) system begins by drawing a circle — "I only handle what is inside here" — and only then can it achieve [Logic](Logic)al consistency and correspondence with reality.

Science is not divided into disciplines because humanity was too lazy to unify them. Every discipline is an operation that locks in a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). Once unification is attempted — grand unified theories, theories of everything — decades pass without result, because the attempt to reopen [Scope](Scope) while preserving [Logic](Logic) and [Truth](Truth) is precisely what the trilemma forbids.

LLMs broke this convention. Training a large language model means feeding it Wikipedia, textbooks, papers, news, forums, code, legal documents, and fiction — all learned through a single unified architecture. This is not a design choice; it is an operational necessity. To answer any question, the model must be trained on data from every domain.

The result: what humanity spent millennia carefully separating has been packed wholesale into a single set of parameters.

---

## 2. The Proposition

**In any information system, when [Scope](Scope) remains open, [Logic](Logic)al consistency and factual [Truth](Truth) cannot simultaneously be satisfied.**

This is not an engineering "not yet achieved" — it is a structural "cannot be achieved" — an observed structural constraint, not a proven impossibility. The limitation does not arise from insufficient resources or poor algorithms; it arises from the structure of the input data itself.

---

## 3. Definitions

**[Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) (Combinatorial Manifestation):** The aggregate effect C(R) of a rule set interacting under specific conditions. [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) is directly observable and measurable — it is the observable phenomenon-complex under a given set of conditions. The rule set itself is invariant across conditions, but the relative weights and interaction modes of individual rules vary with conditions, producing different [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation)s under different conditions.

**[Scale Level](Scale Level) (Scale Level):** The equivalence class of conditions that produce the same [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation), denoted s. All conditions producing the same [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) belong to the same [Scale Level](Scale Level); conditions producing different [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation)s belong to different [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. When written C(R, s), s names the set of conditions that produce the same [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation). [Scale Level](Scale Level)s are not given a priori — they are induced from observed differences in [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation).

**[Logic](Logic) (Logic):** Internal inferential consistency of the system. The same inferential rules hold without contradiction from start to finish.

**[Truth](Truth) (Truth):** Correspondence with verifiable reality at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). Capable of mapping to actually observable results.

**[Knowledge](Knowledge) (Knowledge):** A structured, text-recorded understanding of reality at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). [Knowledge](Knowledge) is not reality — it is a lossy record of reality (see Section 10). For LLMs, [Knowledge](Knowledge) is the text in the training data.

**[Scope](Scope) (Scale):** Simultaneously accommodating adequate [Knowledge](Knowledge) systems across multiple distinct [Scale Level](Scale Level)s.

[Scope](Scope) is not data volume. [Knowledge](Knowledge) at each [Scale Level](Scale Level) has its own combinatorial logic, its own empirical parameters, its own emergent phenomena, and requires sufficient [Knowledge](Knowledge) density to sustain effective operation. Greater [Scope](Scope) means simultaneously maintaining more local [Knowledge](Knowledge) systems. For LLMs, [Scope](Scope) is the diversity of [Knowledge](Knowledge) in the training data — how many distinct [Scale Level](Scale Level)s it spans.

# Part II: The Core Argument — Combinatorial Manifestation Changes Across Scale Levels

---

## 4. Logic Crosses Scale Levels, Rules Cross Scale Levels, Combinatorial Manifestation Does Not

[Logic](Logic) is cross-scale-level. The four fundamental forces are also cross-scale-level — gravity, electromagnetism, the strong nuclear force, and the weak nuclear force all exist at every [Scale Level](Scale Level). The rules have not changed; [Logic](Logic) has not changed.

But any concrete phenomenon in reality is not the expression of a single rule — it is the combinatorial effect of multiple rules at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). Why is water liquid? Electromagnetism dominates intermolecular interactions, quantum mechanics governs electron orbitals, and gravity is negligible at this [Scale Level](Scale Level). Move to a neutron star, and the same four forces are present, but the relative weights of the strong nuclear force and gravity are entirely different, producing entirely different material behavior.

The rules have not changed, but the relative weights of rules vary with [Scale Level](Scale Level). The mode of combination varies with [Scale Level](Scale Level).

[Truth](Truth) is not the rules. [Truth](Truth) is the [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) of rules at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). This mode of combination cannot be derived [Logic](Logic)ally from the rules themselves — it can only be observed from reality at that [Scale Level](Scale Level).

---

## 5. This Argument Does Not Depend on Emergence

[Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) changes across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s without requiring emergence to explain it. Electromagnetism and gravity have nearly identical field structures (both are inverse-square), yet electromagnetism is approximately 10³⁶ times stronger than gravity, and electric charges cancel while mass does not. These two simple differences alone are sufficient to produce entirely different [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation)s from the same rules at the atomic [Scale Level](Scale Level) versus the astronomical [Scale Level](Scale Level).

No chaos, no nonlinearity, no complex systems theory required. [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) changing across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s is a more fundamental observed fact than emergence. Emergence is an aggravating condition — it makes the differences in [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) more pronounced — but it is not a necessary condition. Even without emergence, [Scale Level](Scale Level)-dependence of rule weights alone is sufficient for the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) to hold.

This matters because emergence itself is a contested concept — some argue it is reducible. The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) does not need to engage that debate. Its premises stand on observed facts that no one can deny.

---

## 6. Why Combinatorial Manifestation Differs Across Scale Levels — Why the Trilemma Holds

[Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation)s differ across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. This is not theory; it is observed fact. The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) does not need to explain why they differ — only to observe that they do. [Scale Level](Scale Level)s are defined by [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) differences (see Section 3). The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) does not explain why multiple distinct [Scale Level](Scale Level)s exist — just as the second law of thermodynamics does not explain why entropy increases, it observes that it does and derives from that fact. This is a deliberate boundary drawn by this paper, not an omission.

Derivation path:

i. [Truth](Truth) at scale level s₁ depends on the [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) of rules at s₁, C(s₁).

ii. [Truth](Truth) at scale level s₂ depends on the [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) of rules at s₂, C(s₂).

iii. C(s₁) ≠ C(s₂). This is an observed fact.

iv. A [Logic](Logic)al framework L₁ built at s₁ is grounded in C(s₁). When applied to s₂, because C(s₂) ≠ C(s₁), it is not guaranteed to correspond with [Truth](Truth) at s₂.

v. When [Scope](Scope) simultaneously covers s₁ and s₂, a unified [Logic](Logic) and [Truth](Truth) at all [Scale Level](Scale Level)s cannot simultaneously be satisfied.

For LLMs: training data simultaneously contains [Knowledge](Knowledge) from s₁, s₂, ..., sₙ, each locally [Truth](Truth)ful at its own [Scale Level](Scale Level), but mutually [Logic](Logic)ally incompatible. A model fitting all this data with a single parameter set must sacrifice [Logic](Logic)al consistency or factual [Truth](Truth) somewhere.

---

## 7. Distinction from Unified Field Theory

The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) is not a claim that rules cannot be unified — it is a claim that [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) cannot be captured by a unified [Logic](Logic)al framework.

Even if someone discovers a unified field theory tomorrow — a beautiful set of equations unifying all four forces — using those equations to calculate the boiling point of water still requires substituting [Scale Level](Scale Level)-specific boundary conditions, making approximations, and choosing truncations. The equations are unified, but the path from equations to concrete phenomena differs at every [Scale Level](Scale Level). A unified field theory unifies the rules, not the [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation)s.

The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s constraint does not lie at the rule layer — it lies at the combinatorial layer between rules and phenomena. Even with rules unified, [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) still cannot be bridged across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. This means the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s constraint is deeper than intuition suggests.

---

## 8. The Trilemma Self-Reinforces

[Knowledge](Knowledge) at each [Scale Level](Scale Level) can be [Logic](Logic)ally perfect within that level. Planetary mechanics is self-consistent; quantum mechanics is self-consistent. But at the moment of crossing [Scale Level](Scale Level)s, [Logic](Logic)al consistency breaks — at a black hole singularity, general relativity says density is infinite, quantum mechanics says this is meaningless. Both are right within their own [Scale Level](Scale Level)s; together they contradict.

The more complete and rigorous [Knowledge](Knowledge) is at each [Scale Level](Scale Level), the sharper the [Logic](Logic)al conflict becomes when crossing [Scale Level](Scale Level)s — because each has developed a complete system, and the foundational assumptions of those systems do not hold at other [Scale Level](Scale Level)s.

The better each vertex performs individually, the more catastrophically the third collapses.

For LLMs: the higher the quality of training data and the more complete the coverage of each domain, the more pronounced the contradictions in cross-domain reasoning. Not because the data is poor, but because good data from different domains fundamentally conflicts.

# Part III: The Specific Toxicity of Training Data

---

## 9. Every Domain's Data Carries Its Own Poison

LLM training data comes from every domain, and every domain's data carries cross-[Scale Level](Scale Level) contradictions — not a data quality problem, but the structure of reality itself.

Physics data is self-contradictory across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. General relativity and quantum mechanics are each internally consistent but mutually incompatible. The model learns two frameworks without any data telling it when to use which — because humanity itself has not yet figured this out.

Biology data describes a system that does not optimize for [Logic](Logic)al consistency. The same functional requirement has entirely different solutions across species; from genes to individuals, each layer within a species produces phenomena the layer below cannot predict; biological systems are filled with evolutionary residue — redundancy and contradiction. Asking a model to learn [Logic](Logic)ally consistent [Knowledge](Knowledge) from this data demands something the data never had.

Humanities data carries not only cross-[Scale Level](Scale Level) contradictions, but "[Truth](Truth)" actively shaped by power. The same basic rules (people need food; people die) combine into entirely different institutions across different times and places, with the mode of combination largely determined by deliberate exercise of power. The model has no capacity to distinguish observed fact from narrative constructed under a specific power structure.

On the surface, these are different problems in different domains. Beneath the surface, it is the same structure. There is only one poison; each domain encounters it from a different angle. The difference lies in the dimensionality of the combinatorial variables: physics combinations arise naturally, with lower dimensionality; humanities combinations are shaped by power, with extremely high dimensionality. The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s constraining force increases accordingly.

# Part IV: The Aggravating Condition — Knowledge Itself Is Defective

---

[Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) changing across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s is a sufficient condition for the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma). Even if [Knowledge](Knowledge) were perfect and lossless, as long as [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation)s differ across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s, the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) holds.

But the [Knowledge](Knowledge) LLMs ingest is not perfect and lossless. [Knowledge](Knowledge) defects are observable facts that make the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s constraints more severe.

---

## 10. Knowledge Is Lossy

[Knowledge](Knowledge) does not fully reflect reality. This is an observed fact.

The loss is structural: time is discarded — dynamic processes become static records; continuous transitions between [Scale Level](Scale Level)s are discarded — replaced by discrete disciplinary systems; the full detail of [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) is discarded — replaced by empirical parameters and magic numbers; context is discarded — replaced by seemingly universal propositions.

LLM training data is the textualization of this lossy [Knowledge](Knowledge). Every layer drops something, yet every layer's output appears complete.

---

## 11. Specific Defects in Knowledge

**Experiments fail to replicate.** Large numbers of research findings cannot be independently reproduced. The replication crisis is not a problem specific to individual fields — it is a structural feature of the [Knowledge](Knowledge)-production process. Expecting a model trained on this data to satisfy the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) is not computation; it is wishful thinking.

**Empirical parameters cannot be explained.** Workable theories are typically full of empirical parameters and magic numbers. An engineer tunes a parameter that works; asked why 0.7 rather than 0.8, the answer is "I tried it." Papers write "empirically determined" — which translates as "I don't know why but it works." The [Knowledge](Knowledge) that is most practically useful is precisely the [Knowledge](Knowledge) that least satisfies the standard of "[Logic](Logic)al rigor."

**Conclusions contradict each other.** Different studies reach opposite conclusions on the same question — not because someone made an error, but because different experimental conditions constitute different [Scale Level](Scale Level)s, and [Truth](Truth) at different [Scale Level](Scale Level)s can legitimately differ.

**Theory rationalizes retroactively.** Large amounts of theoretical explanation are post-hoc, not derived in advance. Why this learning rate works for deep learning, why dropout at 0.5, why ReLU over sigmoid — most answers are retrospective attribution, not prior derivation.

[Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) divergence is the structural constraint; [Knowledge](Knowledge) defects are the aggravating condition. Both act simultaneously on LLM training data.

# Part V: Specific Constraints on LLMs

---

## 12. LLMs Are Recompression of Incomplete Knowledge

[Knowledge](Knowledge) already incompletely reflects reality. LLMs training on this incomplete [Knowledge](Knowledge) perform another compression on an incomplete foundation. Every layer drops something, yet every layer's output appears complete and confident.

LLM training does not need "true and [Logic](Logic)ally rigorous data" — it needs data that covers the diversity of human language and [Knowledge](Knowledge). [Scope](Scope) here is not merely "large in quantity" but "large in diversity" — spanning a large number of distinct [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. To cover the breadth of human [Knowledge](Knowledge), one must include conflicting viewpoints, [Logic](Logic)ally imprecise material, and even factually incorrect data — because that is what [Knowledge](Knowledge) in the real world looks like.

---

## 13. Architectural Constraints

Current mainstream architecture (Transformer) is fundamentally performing statistical correlation, not [Logic](Logic)al inference. The "[Logic](Logic)" it learns is patterns induced from large volumes of text, not deduction from axioms. Statistical induction and [Logic](Logic)al deduction are fundamentally different cognitive mechanisms; forcing one architecture to do both simultaneously necessarily involves compromise somewhere.

Modeling can partially bypass this constraint — physics simulation, weather forecasting, protein folding: these models contain time, causality, continuous evolution. But all modeling operates at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). What modeling can do is recover causal structure within a closed system with known rules and a known [Scale Level](Scale Level). What modeling cannot do is decide which model to use — when to apply Newtonian mechanics, when to switch to relativity. That judgment cannot be resolved by modeling. The success of modeling does not break the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma); it surrenders the [Scope](Scope) vertex.

---

## 14. Hallucination Is Structural, But Manageable

LLMs systematically favor [Knowledge](Knowledge) combinations with "large [Scope](Scope), seemingly rigorous [Logic](Logic), but uncontrollable [Truth](Truth)."

Because the essence of the most practically useful [Knowledge](Knowledge) — the author's feel for a problem, intuition, and failed experiments never written up — has never been textualized. LLMs can only learn [Knowledge](Knowledge) already recorded as text, and that [Knowledge](Knowledge) is already incomplete.

Simultaneously, unreproducible experimental results, overturned conclusions, and mutually contradictory meta-analyses in the training data are textually indistinguishable from reliable [Knowledge](Knowledge). LLMs have no capacity to distinguish them, because distinction requires not text analysis but interaction with reality at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level) — and LLMs have only text, not world.

LLM hallucination is not a bug. It is the inevitable cost of using a unified architecture to span incommensurable [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. Natural language has never carried [Scale Level](Scale Level) markers — the context that [Knowledge](Knowledge) loses when textualized includes "at which [Scale Level](Scale Level) this proposition holds" (see Section 10). LLMs learn from these unmarked texts and their outputs inherit the form of "apparently universal [Truth](Truth)." The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) structurally guarantees ¬[Truth](Truth)(s) at some [Scale Level](Scale Level)s, but outputs carry no [Scale Level](Scale Level) qualifiers — so hallucination presents as confident error, not flagged uncertainty.

But "structural" does not mean "unmitigable." Hallucination rates have been declining. The ways in which they decline precisely confirm the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma): humans evade the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) by dividing into disciplines — restricting [Scope](Scope); LLMs evade it by dividing tasks, dividing domains, adding external verification — RAG temporarily narrows [Scope](Scope) to retrieved documents, tool calls outsource [Truth](Truth) judgment to verifiable systems, domain fine-tuning locks a general model to a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level). Different strategies, same [Logic](Logic): all trade [Scope](Scope) for [Logic](Logic) and [Truth](Truth) compatibility.

The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) does not say hallucination cannot be reduced — it says every method of reducing hallucination sacrifices [Scope](Scope). It is impossible to reduce hallucination to zero while keeping [Scope](Scope) fully open.

---

## 15. The Three-Choose-Two of Data Curation

Data curation is making trade-offs among the three vertices. This is the most direct engineering application of the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma).

Tighten cleaning rules: [Logic](Logic)al consistency rises, but data that "looks contradictory but actually reflects [Truth](Truth) at different [Scale Level](Scale Level)s" gets cut. Relax cleaning rules: diversity is preserved, but noise and contradictions enter. No set of rules can simultaneously maximize all three vertices. What you are doing is three-choose-two.

Concrete pain points:

The same medical question: a 2010 meta-analysis and a 2024 study reach opposite conclusions. Which do you keep? Keep both: [Logic](Logic)al contradiction. Keep only the new one: potentially discarding [Truth](Truth) that still holds under specific conditions — perhaps the 2010 conclusion remains correct for specific populations, specific dosages. This cannot be resolved with better rules, because the definition of "better" is itself [Scale Level](Scale Level)-dependent.

The same engineering parameter: a textbook says 0.7; a senior engineer's blog says 0.85 under specific conditions. Cut the blog: practical [Knowledge](Knowledge) lost. Keep the blog: contradiction with the textbook introduced. And what "specific conditions" means often is not explicitly recorded — it lives in the engineer's feel, never textualized.

Cross-cultural data is worse. The same legal concept has different meanings in different jurisdictions; the same historical event has different accounts in different countries' textbooks. When curating, whose version is authoritative? Choose one: [Truth](Truth) at a specific [Scale Level](Scale Level) is sacrificed. Keep both: [Logic](Logic) becomes inconsistent.

The priority ordering among the three vertices is not fixed. An engineer's magic number — normally beneath serious consideration — becomes critical when proper data is unavailable. Even if an information system can trade off among the three vertices, it does not know which to prioritize right now. That judgment requires understanding the current context — a [Scale Level](Scale Level) problem — which circles back to the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s own constraints.

The takeaway for data curators: you are not looking for an optimal solution; you are making a three-choose-two decision. Decide which vertex your current task requires sacrificing, and sacrifice it deliberately, rather than pretending all three can be had.

# Part VI: Boundary Conditions and Formal Description

---

## 16. Where the Trilemma Does Not Apply

Fractals are structures where the [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) of a single rule is identical across all [Scale Level](Scale Level)s — the same pattern repeating infinitely. Fractals achieve cross-[Scale Level](Scale Level) consistency by completely abandoning complexity in exchange for [Scale Level](Scale Level) invariance.

Geometry is cross-[Scale Level](Scale Level), [Logic](Logic)ally consistent, and [Truth](Truth)ful within its assumptions. Geometry achieves this because it does not process combinatorial forces — it only describes spatial structure, without involving how things within that space interact.

The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s conditions of applicability: [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) changes across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. The criterion is interaction — where there is interaction, there is combination; combination may vary with [Scale Level](Scale Level); the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) applies. Pure structural, non-interactive formal systems (geometry, fractals, pure mathematics) can be cross-[Scale Level](Scale Level)-consistent. Once actual phenomena are involved — physical, biological, humanistic — the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) is present.

For LLMs, this draws a line: models can be cross-[Scale Level](Scale Level)-consistent in pure formal reasoning (mathematics, logic), but once empirical [Knowledge](Knowledge) is involved, the [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma)'s constraints activate.

## 17. Formal Description

The following is not a formal proof but a structured statement of observed facts and the derivation path.

```
Definitions:
  Scope   = simultaneously covering scale levels s₁, s₂, ..., sₘ
             with adequate knowledge subsets K(sⱼ) at each level
  Logic   = ∀ propositions p, q ∈ System: ¬(p ∧ ¬p)
  Truth(s) = System output maps to verifiable reality at scale level s

Observed facts:
  F1: Basic rules R exist across all scale levels
  F2: Concrete phenomenon = Combinatorial Manifestation of R at scale level s, C(R, s)
  F3: C(R, s₁) ≠ C(R, s₂) when s₁ ≠ s₂
      (observed fact; independent of emergence)
  F4: K(s) does not fully reflect C(R, s)
      (Knowledge is a lossy record; aggravating condition, not necessary for trilemma)

Derivation:
  From F2: Truth(s) depends on C(R, s)
  From F3: C(R, s₁) ≠ C(R, s₂)
  ∴ Logical framework L₁ built at s₁ grounded in C(R, s₁)
    When L₁ applied to s₂, because C(R, s₂) ≠ C(R, s₁),
    Truth(s₂) is not guaranteed
  ∴ When Scope is open, unified Logic and Truth at all scale levels
    cannot simultaneously be satisfied

  Note: "not guaranteed" is empirical induction, not mathematical proof.
  The trilemma is an empirical heuristic, not a theorem.

Boundary condition:
  If ∀s₁, s₂: C(R, s₁) = C(R, s₂) (Combinatorial Manifestation
  invariant across scale levels, as in fractals), trilemma does not apply.

Aggravating condition (Knowledge defects):
  K(s) does not fully reflect C(R, s) (observed fact, F4)
  ∴ Even within a single scale level, Logic and Truth are not guaranteed
    to be simultaneously satisfied
  Combinatorial Manifestation divergence + Knowledge defects act together;
  trilemma constraints strengthen.

Hallucination derivation:
  From trilemma: ∃s: ¬Truth(s)
  From F4 + Section 10: natural language carries no scale level markers;
    Knowledge textualization discards scale level context;
    LLM outputs inherit "apparently universal" form
  ∴ ∃s: ¬Truth(s) ∧ output claims universal truth → hallucination

LLM constraints:
  Training data D = ∪ K(sⱼ), j = 1, ..., m
  LLM fits D with unified parameters θ
  By trilemma: no θ such that ∀s: Truth(s) holds under unified Logic
  Mitigation strategies (RAG, tool calls, fine-tuning) = restrict Scope
    to obtain Logic ∧ Truth
```

## 18. Relationship to Other Trilemmas

Trilemmas form a family. The CAP theorem says distributed systems must choose two of: consistency, availability, partition tolerance. Mundell-Fleming says a country must choose two of: free capital movement, fixed exchange rates, independent monetary policy. This paper's [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) says choose two of: [Scope](Scope), [Logic](Logic), [Truth](Truth).

The structure is identical: three desirable properties cannot all be had; the system has an inherent trade-off ceiling; this is a structural problem, not a resource problem.

The difference lies in the source and hardness of the constraint. CAP has a formal proof; Mundell-Fleming has model support. This paper's [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) is grounded in observed fact — an empirical heuristic, not a theorem. It is broader than CAP and Mundell-Fleming (applicable to any empirical [Knowledge](Knowledge) system) but softer (lacking formal proof). This paper does not pursue formal proof. The value of an empirical heuristic in engineering practice does not depend on formalization — engineers need a framework to guide trade-offs, not a theorem that passes peer review.

## 19. Scope of Application and Limitations

**Applicable:** Any [Knowledge](Knowledge) system where [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) changes across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s. LLM training data naturally falls in this category, spanning all empirical [Knowledge](Knowledge) domains — physical, biological, humanistic.

**Not applicable:** Pure formal systems where [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) does not change across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s — fractals, geometry, pure mathematics. LLM performance in these domains is not subject to [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) constraints.

**Limitations:** The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) does not predict which specific component will fail. It does not determine the priority ordering among the three vertices — priority is [Scale Level](Scale Level)-dependent, varying with context and supply and demand. It does not provide solutions — it describes a structural constraint, not a problem that engineering methods can overcome. The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) does not explain why multiple distinct [Scale Level](Scale Level)s exist, nor why [Combinatorial Manifestation](Combinatorial Manifestation) changes across [Scale Level](Scale Level)s — it only observes that this is so and derives constraints from the observation. The [Information Trilemma](Information Trilemma) is itself [Knowledge](Knowledge), and is itself incomplete.
