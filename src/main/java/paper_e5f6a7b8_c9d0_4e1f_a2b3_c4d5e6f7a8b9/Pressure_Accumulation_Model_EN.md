## Abstract

This paper employs Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) v5.0 as its analytical tool to construct a formal model of the dynamics of centralized civilizations. Starting from the structural requirements of the Positive Feedback Loop, the model derives the Pressure Accumulation Model: centralization necessarily leads to Resource Concentration; Resource Concentration necessarily leads to the accumulation of bottom-layer pressure; simultaneously, local agents necessarily accumulate independence. These two tracks feed each other, ultimately causing the container to fracture. From the same Positive Feedback Loop, Supreme Kingship is derived: the loop structurally rejects any authority claiming to stand above the political center, because independent authority nodes divert resources and power, weakening the loop. The Immune Response of Supreme Kingship operates at two moments: during the Accumulation phase, directly cutting off growing transcendent authority (Yellow Turbans, Buddhist Suppressions); and at the moment of Reconstruction, turning back to cut off transcendent authority that survived the warring period — even if it was the force that brought the victor to power (the Ming Cult at the end of the Yuan). The model identifies a Branch Condition: whether an isomorphic structure is rebuilt after Rupture depends on whether, at the moment Reconstruction is complete, any transcendent authority survived the warring period and cannot be eliminated by the new loop. This condition is not a static prerequisite but a dynamic state continuously maintained by Supreme Kingship — Isomorphic Organizations act as a distributed immune system, blocking the growth of transcendent authority at the micro level. Without a surviving and indestructible Independent Executive Body, Rupture is followed by isomorphic Reconstruction and the loop restarts; with a surviving and indestructible Independent Executive Body, isomorphic Reconstruction after Rupture is not inevitable. This is verified against six cases — China, Rome, Islam, India, Russia, and Japan — and the Branch Condition holds in all of them. The Pressure Accumulation Model is a universal dynamics model for centralized civilizations. China's distinctiveness lies not in the loop itself but in the Branch Condition: China's loop has never allowed any transcendent authority possessing an Independent Executive Body to survive to the moment when Reconstruction is complete.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Pressure Accumulation Model, centralization, Positive Feedback Loop, Supreme Kingship, transcendent authority, Signal Container, distributed immunity

## I. Framework Overview

The analytical framework used in this paper is Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) v5.0 (Huang, 2026). The following summarizes only the concepts directly invoked in this paper.

The framework begins with two primitives (Individual, Stimulus) and three definitions (Xin, Semantic Flow, Signal) and establishes four postulates. The Law of Flux: Semantic Flow operates continuously. The Law of the Black Box: the next-moment direction of Semantic Flow cannot be determined by its current direction together with the incoming Signal. The Law of Dissipation: Semantic Flow cannot be reduced to Signals without loss. The Law of Death: when an Individual perishes, Semantic Flow is irrecoverable.

From the postulates, three basic functions are derived. Filtering: the current direction of Semantic Flow determines what constitutes a Signal and what constitutes Noise. Transformation: a Signal is processed within Semantic Flow with an unpredictable result. Collapse: Semantic Flow outputs as a Signal, with necessary loss. From the basic functions, the Positive Feedback Loop is derived: an Individual's output Signal reaches other Individuals, passes through Filtering, Transformation, and Collapse, generates a new output Signal, and the loop runs.

The Positive Feedback Loop has no perpetual equilibrium state (Law of Flux + Law of the Black Box + Law of Death). After an Individual perishes, new Individuals form their Semantic Flow within the environment of existing Signal Containers (Generational Replacement).

Signal Container: the place where Signals reside — books, classics, institutional texts, organizational structures. Signal Containers store and transmit Signals but do not generate Semantic Flow. Signal Containers survive an Individual's death and are the material basis for the loop's cross-generational continuation.

The nature of this paper. This paper is formal modeling, not historical induction. The model derives dynamics starting from the structure of the Positive Feedback Loop, then verifies against historical cases. The framework makes no normative judgments, makes no time predictions, and does not track individuals' inner lives. The parent paper (Huang, 2026b) §3.1's Pressure Accumulation Model presupposes the Chinese Xin model. This paper derives the universal Pressure Accumulation Model directly from the structure of the Positive Feedback Loop; the parent paper version is a special case of this model under Chinese Xin model conditions.

## II. The Model

### 2.1 Preconditions

**Centralized Positive Feedback Loop**: the Positive Feedback Loop operating stably (centralization)

**Resource Concentration**: maintaining the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop requires resources to concentrate at the center

**Local Agent Requirement**: the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop requires local agents to execute control

Resource Concentration is a structural requirement of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop — the center must command resources in order to maintain control over the entire domain. The Local Agent Requirement is a structural requirement of scale — the center cannot directly manage every Individual and must rely on local agents.

### 2.2 The Pressure Accumulation Model

**Phase One: Accumulation.** The Centralized Positive Feedback Loop operates stably. Resources continuously concentrate at the center (Resource Concentration) — this is positive feedback: the stronger the center, the greater its extraction capacity. Bottom-layer resources continuously decrease, ultimately producing famine. This is not incidental governance failure; it is the necessary product of the core structure.

Simultaneously, local agents continuously accumulate resources and autonomy in the course of executing control. The center needs them and must therefore grant them power; once power is granted, they evolve toward independent nodes.

The two tracks feed each other. The uprisings along the Bottom Pressure Line require suppression; suppression depends on local agents; the Agent Pressure Line therefore receives more resources and independence. The center's suppression of the Agent Pressure Line requires recentralizing power and resources; recentralization accelerates the Bottom Pressure Line. Pressing either side feeds the other.

**Phase Two: Sawthrough.** Bottom-layer uprisings. Suppression requires resources. More resources are extracted from localities. More localities fall into famine. More uprisings. The Positive Feedback Loop accelerates collapse. Container Exhaustion. The final collapse does not occur because the final uprising is particularly powerful — it occurs because all previous uprisings had already sawed the container down to its last layer.

**Phase Three: Rupture.** The container fractures; the center disappears. Signal Containers survive — texts, classics, institutional memory, micro-level Isomorphic Organizations. The loop's direction has not been replaced.

**Phase Four: Warring.** Signal Containers point in a unified direction; everyone competes for the same position. The duration of the Warring phase depends on whether someone can defeat all others. The specific trajectory of the Warring phase depends on the Branch Condition (Section 2.5).

**Phase Five: Reconstruction.** The victor rebuilds the center. The loop operates stably; return to Phase One.

### 2.3 Supreme Kingship

The Positive Feedback Loop of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop requires resources and power to concentrate at the center (Resource Concentration). Any authority node independent of the center diverts resources and power, weakening the loop. Therefore, the very operation of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop structurally rejects any authority claiming to stand above the political center. This property is referred to below as Supreme Kingship.

Supreme Kingship requires no strategy and no conscious judgment — it is the structural behavior of the Positive Feedback Loop. Any individual occupying the highest position, facing an authority claiming to be higher than themselves, will find rejection of that Signal to be a structural requirement for maintaining the loop.

Supreme Kingship carries costs. Every act of rejecting an independent authority forfeits economic opportunity, epistemic diversity, or technological possibility. Costs accumulate and feed directly into the Accumulation phase. But so long as the resource base can absorb them, the loop continues.

### 2.4 The Immune Response: Logical Necessity and Historical Fingerprints

The existence of Supreme Kingship produces a logical necessity: throughout history there must have been attempts by transcendent authority to emerge, and there must have been suppression. If no record of the suppression of transcendent authority can be found anywhere in two thousand years of history, the mechanism of Supreme Kingship does not hold.

Whether the Immune Response succeeds depends on a simple condition: who has the bigger fist at that moment. When the loop is operating stably, the center commands overwhelming resources, and the Immune Response almost always succeeds.

The Immune Response operates at two moments.

**Accumulation-Phase Immunity.** The loop is operating stably; the center has overwhelming resources. A transcendent authority attempts to grow; the center directly cuts it down. The Yellow Turban Uprising (the transcendent claim of the Way of Great Peace), the Three-Wu-One-Zong Buddhist Suppressions (the structural strike when Buddhism had grown too large), and the Kangxi Prohibition of Christianity (the Pope's claim of authority higher than the emperor's) — all are fingerprints of Accumulation-Phase Immunity.

**Reconstruction-Moment Immunity.** This is a more critical mechanism than Accumulation-Phase Immunity. After Rupture, the Warring phase begins, the center disappears, and the big fist is gone — this is the transcendent authority's only survival window. What happens if a transcendent authority survives this window, or even becomes the driving force of the Warring phase? The model predicts: so long as it lacks an Independent Executive Body capable of withstanding the new loop after Reconstruction, the moment the victor rebuilds the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop, Supreme Kingship immediately activates and turns back to eliminate it — even if it was the force that brought the victor to power. The victor need not deliberate: the moment they occupy that position, the loop activates, and Supreme Kingship is structural behavior; the transcendent authority that sent them up must die. The Ming Cult at the end of the Yuan precisely validates this mechanism (see Section 3.1).

This mechanism directly produces the judgment logic of the Branch Condition: the transcendent authority surviving the Warring window does not mean the Branch Condition fails. The Branch Condition failing requires that the transcendent authority survive the warring period and that the new loop cannot eliminate it. The European Church satisfies this condition (it has an Independent Executive Body; barbarian kings cannot cut it down). The Ming Cult in China does not satisfy it (it lacks an Independent Executive Body; once Zhu Yuanzhang activated the state apparatus, it shattered).

Therefore, the judgment moment for the Branch Condition is not "the moment of Rupture" but more precisely "the moment Reconstruction is complete": whether any transcendent authority survived the warring period and the new loop cannot eliminate it.

### 2.5 Branch Condition

Accumulation → Sawthrough → Rupture is the universal dynamics of all Centralized Positive Feedback Loops. Whether an isomorphic structure is rebuilt after Rupture depends on one condition: at the moment Reconstruction is complete, whether any transcendent authority survived the warring period and the new loop cannot eliminate it.

This condition is not a static prerequisite; it is a dynamic state continuously maintained by Supreme Kingship. The Immune Response operates both during the Accumulation phase and at the moment of Reconstruction — Accumulation-Phase Immunity cuts off growing transcendent authority; Reconstruction-Moment Immunity cuts off transcendent authority that survived the Warring window. So long as immunity continues to succeed, the condition continues to hold.

No surviving independent transcendent authority: the political center is the highest position. After Rupture, Signal Containers tell everyone that there can only be one highest position; everyone competes for the same position; the isomorphic structure is rebuilt.

A surviving and indestructible independent transcendent authority: there is an independent authority above the political center. After Rupture, this independent authority survives; the political center can be multiple, because the highest position does not lie at the political level. Isomorphic Reconstruction is not inevitable.

The Branch Condition is abbreviated below as the state: ¬∃ surviving and indestructible independent transcendent authority (with Independent Executive Body) at the moment Reconstruction is complete.

### 2.6 Independent Executive Body

Why does the Branch Condition point to an "Independent Executive Body" rather than merely a "transcendent idea"? The answer is derived by working backward from the loop's Loop Transformation Capacity.

Section 2.7 will argue that the loop actively remodels all signal material introduced into it, making it compatible with the loop's direction. This means that transcendent ideas existing purely in texts — however sublime — are signal material; the loop will remodel them away. The transformation of Confucianism's "lord as lord, minister as minister" from a bilateral obligation into unilateral submission is a demonstration of this Loop Transformation Capacity. Ideas have no capacity for self-protection.

What can withstand remodeling must be an entity structure that the loop cannot dissolve — with its own personnel, its own finances, its own organization, its own channels for exercising power. "Independent Executive Body" is not a descriptive label added from outside; it is the logical dual of Loop Transformation Capacity: since the loop can remodel everything at the pure signal level, what can anchor a different direction after Rupture must be more than signal material — it must have a material, organizational, executable structure that the loop cannot digest through Filtering and Transformation.

Criterion for identifying an Independent Executive Body: whether the Signal Containers contain a structure simultaneously satisfying two conditions — first, it claims authority higher than the political center (transcendence); second, its executive capacity does not depend on authorization by the political center, and the political center cannot unilaterally shut it down (independence).

### 2.7 The Loop's Remodeling of Signal Containers

The loop does not merely sustain itself through Signal Containers; the loop actively remodels Signal Containers. Any signal material introduced — Confucianism, Legalism, Buddhism, Communism — passes through the loop's Filtering and Transformation, is remodeled into a shape compatible with the loop's direction, and then reinforces the loop as a Signal Container.

The original Confucianism of Confucius was not the signal system of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop. Confucius's ideal was the Zhou — feudal system, ritual and music, distinction between lord and minister but not absolute submission. "Lord as lord, minister as minister" was bilateral: if the lord is not lordly, the minister need not be ministerial. Upon entering the Han dynasty's loop, Dong Zhongshu's "elevating Confucianism to the exclusion of all other schools" remodeled Confucianism into the operating system of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop: the Resonance of Heaven and Man placed the emperor at the center of the cosmos; the theory of auspicious and inauspicious omens made the emperor the sole person responsible to Heaven; the Three Cardinal Bonds transformed all relationships into unilateral vertical submission. "Lord as lord, minister as minister" shifted from bilateral obligation to unilateral submission. This was not the natural evolution of Confucianism; it was the product of the loop's Filtering and Transformation — only portions compatible with the loop's direction were retained and amplified; incompatible portions were Silenced.

The original Communism presupposed the withering of the state, the dispersal of power. Upon entering China, it was remodeled into Party leadership over everything — structurally isomorphic with every Centralized Positive Feedback Loop in Chinese history.

The causal direction is: the loop remodels signal material; signal material does not determine the loop's direction. This guarantees that after Rupture, Signal Containers carry a unified direction — regardless of the original raw material, once processed by the loop, the resulting Signal Containers all point in the same direction.

### 2.8 Dual Function of Isomorphic Organizations

Isomorphic Organizations — clans, lineage groups, academic factions, political factions, party organizations — internally replicate the structure of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop: one center governs all. The parent paper (Huang, 2026b) has argued that Isomorphic Organizations serve the loop's Reproduction Function: after Rupture, Isomorphic Organizations act as micro-templates, driving the new container to be rebuilt in the same shape.

This paper supplements a second function of Isomorphic Organizations: the Distributed Immunity Function. Isomorphic Organizations do not only rebuild the isomorphic structure after Rupture; they block the growth of transcendent authority during the operation of the loop itself. The patriarch is the highest authority within the family, corresponding to the emperor being the highest authority under heaven — the micro-structure leaves no place for transcendent authority. This means that the immunity of Supreme Kingship is not executed only by the center; the entire micro-structure of society is executing it. The center's immunity is centralized (the emperor orders the persecution of Buddhism); the immunity of Isomorphic Organizations is distributed (every family, every organization's internal structure is rejecting transcendent claims).

This explains why every transcendent challenger in Chinese history was eliminated. The Way of Great Peace faced not only the emperor's suppression but the rejection of the entire isomorphic system. Compare Europe: the Church faced only the rejection of kings; the micro-structure of society did not reject transcendent authority — the parish itself was part of the micro-structure.

### 2.9 Taming and the Collapse Sequence

What Supreme Kingship rejects is not religion itself but any religion's claim to a structural position higher than the political center. Buddhism survived in China for two thousand years, and Taoism also survived for two thousand years — both in good standing. But they were both Tamed: they did not dare claim that dharma stood above imperial power. The persecution of Buddhism was not aimed at eliminating Buddhism; it was a beating administered when Buddhism had grown large enough to threaten Supreme Kingship. Once the beating was done, Buddhism had learned its lesson, continued to exist, but no longer challenged the structure.

Taming is a continuous process that requires the loop to keep operating to maintain pressure. When the loop dies, Taming fails. Rome's timeline precisely validates this: Constantine co-opted Christianity, and Taming succeeded — the emperor convened the Council of Nicaea; the emperor was the Church's supreme arbiter. Then the Western Roman Empire collapsed. The Church, as the only surviving trans-regional organization, reversed from a Tamed state into an independent authority in the vacuum. It was not that Taming failed to work; the loop died, and Taming failed as a result.

The Eastern Roman Empire perfectly validates the control group: the same Christianity — with the eastern emperor alive, the Eastern Orthodox Church remained obedient: the emperor appointed the patriarch, and the Church was subordinate to the state. The Centralized Positive Feedback Loop alive, Taming persisted. This pattern was transmitted intact to Russia — the Tsar controlled the Eastern Orthodox Church; the Branch Condition held; the Accumulation loop ran; after collapse, isomorphic Reconstruction.

## III. Verification

### 3.1 China

All preconditions are satisfied: Resource Concentration (tax systems), Local Agent Requirement (prefectural officials, gentry, local party secretaries).

The following verifies each component of the model. For each component, the two most temporally distant cases are selected to exclude special cases.

**Accumulation — mutual feeding of the two pressure tracks.**

*Eastern Han (25–220 CE).* Emperor Guangwu relied on powerful clans to establish the dynasty; repayment took the form of allowing them to retain manors and private armies. Accumulation begins: the emperor needs powerful clans to manage localities (Local Agent Requirement); in the course of management, the clans annex land and expand their power. The Bottom Pressure Line activates (peasants lose their land); the Agent Pressure Line activates (clans grow powerful). Emperors Huan and Ling attempted to use eunuchs to suppress the gentry and powerful clans (suppressing the Agent Pressure Line); expanding eunuch power requires resources, aggravating the Bottom Pressure Line (feeding the first track). The Yellow Turban Uprising erupts in 184 CE (the Bottom Pressure Line explodes); the court lacks the capacity to suppress it and relies on powerful clans to raise their own troops to pacify the rebellion (feeding the Agent Pressure Line); powerful clans upgrade from local elites to warlords — Dong Zhuo, Yuan Shao, and Cao Cao are all products of this dynamic. The mutual feeding of the two tracks is textbook-clear.

*Qing (1644–1912 CE).* Spanning seventeen hundred years, the structure is the same. From the Jiaqing era onward, population pressure intensifies land scarcity (the Bottom Pressure Line activates). The Taiping Rebellion erupts in 1850 (the Bottom Pressure Line explodes); the Qing central government lacks suppression capacity and relies on local elites to build their own armies — Zeng Guofan's Xiang Army, Li Hongzhang's Huai Army (feeding the Agent Pressure Line). Suppression succeeds, but regional governors-general now possess independent military and fiscal power. In 1900, allied forces of eight nations enter Beijing; governors-general of the southeastern provinces conclude the "Mutual Protection of Southeast China" agreement with foreign powers, openly refusing to execute central government orders — the Agent Pressure Line peaks. Unable to suppress the bottom layer (the Bottom Pressure Line persists) or control the localities (the Agent Pressure Line peaks), the container fractures.

**Supreme Kingship — fingerprints of the Immune Response.**

The existence of Supreme Kingship means that throughout history there must have been attempts by transcendent authority and acts of suppression. The following three cases cover both Accumulation-Phase Immunity and Reconstruction-Moment Immunity, spanning from the late Yuan dynasty to the contemporary era, verifying the structural consistency of the Immune Response.

*Ming Cult at the End of the Yuan (1351–1368 CE).* The clearest case of Reconstruction-Moment Immunity. As Yuan society collapsed, the White Lotus and Ming Cult networks mobilized the Red Turban Uprising under the banner of "Maitreya descends, the Radiant King appears." The Ming Cult had transcendent claims (divine authority above secular power) and a distributed organizational network (secret societies permeating the grassroots of society); during the warring period it was the core mobilizing force at the bottom layer. Zhu Yuanzhang himself rose from within this system — he was a subordinate of the Red Turban general Guo Zixing, and the very dynastic name "Ming" carries traces of the Ming Cult. Transcendent authority was not only not eliminated during the warring period; it was the warring period's winner. Then Zhu Yuanzhang ascended to the throne and immediately issued edicts prohibiting the Ming Cult and the White Lotus, killing the organization from which his own power had emerged. The Great Ming Code explicitly classified "heterodox doctrines disturbing right order" as serious crimes; the White Lotus and Ming Cult were listed as evil cults and strictly forbidden. This was not Zhu Yuanzhang's personal ingratitude; it was the structural behavior of Supreme Kingship after the loop's Reconstruction — the divine authority claimed by the Ming Cult (the Radiant King, Maitreya) was useful during the warring period (mobilizing the bottom layer) and fatal after the loop's Reconstruction (a source of authority higher than the emperor now exists). Whoever occupied that position would do the same thing.

This case directly parallels Europe: barbarian kings also came to power and obtained legitimacy through the Church, and once in power they also wanted to reject the Church. The difference lies in the fist: the Church had an independent economic base, an independent personnel system, and a trans-regional organizational network; barbarian kings could not cut it down. The Ming Cult did not — its organizational base was secret societies with no independent temple estates, no public hierarchical structure, no institutional inertia capable of surviving across political regimes. The moment Zhu Yuanzhang's state apparatus was activated, the Ming Cult shattered.

*Kangxi Prohibition of Christianity (1704–1724 CE).* Accumulation-Phase Immunity. Matteo Ricci spent decades preaching in China with a strategy of cultural accommodation — permitting ancestor veneration, not challenging the political structure. The loop was not triggered. In 1704, Pope Clement XI issued a prohibition forbidding Chinese Catholics from venerating their ancestors and declared that the Pope held final authority over Chinese church affairs. This was a structural claim — someone is greater than the emperor. Kangxi's response precisely validates the mechanism of Supreme Kingship: the problem was not the content of the doctrine (Kangxi had no objection to Catholic doctrine itself) but the structural claim ("you say someone is greater than I"). Kangxi's language was explicit: Westerners do not understand Chinese affairs, yet they seek to govern Chinese affairs; this cannot be accepted. The prohibition was then issued. Decades of groundwork laid by Matteo Ricci were swept away by a single structural trigger — not a deliberate policy choice by the emperor, but the loop's structural rejection.

*Contemporary Containment (2012–present).* Spanning six hundred years, the same dynamic. NGOs, independent media, civil society, independent judiciary — each claims a legitimacy independent of the political center; each is a structural signal that "someone is beyond your governance." Supreme Kingship's treatment is identical: rejection. The specific means differ (Kangxi used prohibition; today uses firewalls and legislation), but the dynamics are consistent — not a judgment of content but a structural rejection. Costs also accumulate: the cost of Kangxi's prohibition was losing a channel for the introduction of Western science and technology; the cost of contemporary containment is the loss of market efficiency, innovative capacity, and international trust. Costs feed directly into the Accumulation phase.

**Signal Containers remodeled by the loop.**

*Confucianism (5th century BCE → 2nd century BCE).* The original Confucianism of Confucius was an order theory, not a theory of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop. Confucius's ideal was the Zhou — feudal system, ritual and music, distinction between lord and minister but not absolute submission. "Lord as lord, minister as minister" was bilateral — if the lord is not lordly, the minister need not be ministerial. Upon entering the Han dynasty's loop, Dong Zhongshu's "elevating Confucianism to the exclusion of all other schools" remodeled Confucianism into the operating system of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop: the Resonance of Heaven and Man placed the emperor at the center of the cosmos; the theory of auspicious and inauspicious omens made the emperor the sole person responsible to Heaven; the Three Cardinal Bonds transformed all relationships into unilateral vertical submission. "Lord as lord, minister as minister" shifted from bilateral obligation to unilateral submission. This was not the natural evolution of Confucianism; it was the product of the loop's Filtering and Transformation — only portions compatible with the loop's direction were retained and amplified; incompatible portions were Silenced.

*Communism (19th century → 20th century).* Spanning two thousand years, entirely different raw material, isomorphic result of remodeling. Marx's Communism presupposed the withering of the state, the dispersal of power, and collective proletarian leadership. Upon entering China's loop: the withering of the state became Party leadership over everything; the dispersal of power became democratic centralism; collective leadership became a personality cult (Mao), then collective leadership (Deng), then a return to personal centralization of power (Xi). Structurally isomorphic with every Centralized Positive Feedback Loop in Chinese history. The causal direction is clear: it was not Communism that determined China's political structure; it was China's loop that remodeled Communism into the shape it needed.

**Warring — Signal Container directional unity guarantees eventual Reconstruction.**

*Five Barbarian Invasions (316–589 CE).* After the collapse of the Western Jin, sixteen states successively appeared in the north; the Eastern Jin, Song, Qi, Liang, and Chen successively ruled in the south. The warring period persisted for nearly three hundred years. But the direction of Signal Containers was always unified: all regimes, whether Han or non-Han, claimed themselves as the "legitimate heir to All Under Heaven." Fu Jian of the Former Qin attempted unification and failed. After the Northern Wei Tuoba clan unified the north, they fully sinicized — changing surnames, wearing Han clothing, adopting Han institutions — not because Han culture was "highly attractive" but because governing China requires entering China's Signal Containers, and what the Signal Containers pointed toward was "one center." The Sui dynasty ultimately unified. Three hundred years of warring, and the result was still isomorphic.

*Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms (907–979 CE).* Spanning four hundred years, smaller in scale, identical in structure. After the Tang collapsed, five short-lived dynasties successively ruled the north, and ten independent regimes existed in the south. Each claimed legitimacy. Seventy years of warring, then Zhao Kuangyin unified. Difference from the Five Barbarian Invasions: no ethnic factor, smaller scale, shorter warring period. Similarity to the Five Barbarian Invasions: Signal Containers pointed in a unified direction; everyone competed for the same position; isomorphic Reconstruction ultimately resulted.

### 3.2 Cross-Civilization Comparison

Verification of the Branch Condition requires cross-civilization comparison. The following analyzes the structural judgment for each of six cases.

**Rome / Western Europe.** Branch Condition fails. The sequence of Taming is key: Constantine successfully co-opted Christianity; the emperor was the Church's supreme arbiter — Taming succeeded. But after the Western Roman Empire collapsed, the Church, as the only surviving trans-regional organization, reversed from a Tamed state into an independent authority in the vacuum. The Pope possessed a power base independent of the political center and could excommunicate kings. Barbarian kings also wanted to reject the Church — with exactly the same motivation as Zhu Yuanzhang killing the Ming Cult. The difference lay in the fist: the Church had an independent economic base, an independent personnel system, and a trans-regional organizational network; the new loop could not cut it down. After Rupture, transcendent authority was alive, standing, and indestructible. Europe never rebuilt a unified empire.

The Eastern Roman Empire / Russia provides the control group. The same Christianity — with the eastern emperor alive, the Eastern Orthodox Church remained Tamed: the emperor appointed the patriarch, and the Church was subordinate to the state. The Centralized Positive Feedback Loop persisted; Taming persisted. When the Tsars collapsed and when the Soviet Union collapsed, the Eastern Orthodox Church survived both times, but what it did first upon each survival was to find the next master — after the Tsar died it attached to the Soviets; after the Soviets died it attached to Putin. The Eastern Orthodox Church has never considered that it should stand independently; its doctrinal position is that the Church is subordinate to the emperor. Surviving but not standing up equals no independent transcendent authority. The Branch Condition holds. Russia rebuilds isomorphically.

**Islam.** Branch Condition fails. The Ulama's authority comes from their interpretation of scripture, not from caliphal appointment. After the empire collapsed, multiple regimes each claimed legitimacy.

**India.** Branch Condition fails, and in the most extreme form. Brahmin authority comes from Brahman; the caste system penetrates every village. The political center has fractured countless times; caste has never fractured. A stable Centralized Positive Feedback Loop was never established. India's transcendent executive body requires no pope — the rules are embedded in every family's marriage rules and dietary prohibitions; political power simply cannot find a switch to shut it down.

**Russia.** Branch Condition holds (see control group analysis in the Rome / Western Europe section above). The Eastern Orthodox Church is subordinate to the emperor; it lacks the will and capacity to stand independently. After the Soviet Union's collapse, the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop was rebuilt.

**Japan.** The Centralized Positive Feedback Loop was never stably established. At the physical level: the topography of islands and mountains makes it physically infeasible for resources to continuously concentrate at a single center. At the signal level: the Shinto shrine network is distributed throughout every village; the emperor, as the symbolic apex of Shinto, possesses a divine lineage that was not granted by the political center and cannot be revoked by the political center either. The shrine network is a distributed transcendent executive body — there is no head to cut off, no switch to shut off.

Killing an emperor as an individual and eliminating the institution of the emperor are entirely different things. Historically, individual emperors being deposed has occurred multiple times. But eliminating the institution of the emperor is equivalent to making an enemy of every shrine in Japan — no one has that fist, and no one can make that calculation work. Oda Nobunaga may have come closest to harboring that intention, but he died before completing it.

The existence of the shrine network has welded "the highest position" firmly onto the distributed network; it cannot be seized. The highest position any military house can win is only the second tier (Shogun); the dynamic mechanism of "the position is vacant — whoever wins takes it" cannot be activated at the topmost tier. The Positive Feedback Loop lacks the structural prerequisite. The Pressure Accumulation Model does not apply.

## IV. Present Positioning

China is currently in Phase One (Accumulation). The structure is the same as in every previous dynasty: recentralization of power is suppressing the Agent Pressure Line (eliminating the independence of local agents); the means of suppression is concentrating resources; the Bottom Pressure Line is accelerating (economic slowdown, youth unemployment). The dynamic of mutual feeding between the two tracks has not changed. Signal Containers have switched surface labels (from the Mandate of Heaven to the People), but the structural direction has not changed — one center, vertical submission, rejection of independent nodes.

The previous cycle, from the 1850 Taiping Rebellion to the 1979 Reform and Opening Up, spanned one hundred and thirty years, with casualties numbering in the hundreds of millions. True peace has existed only from 1979 to the present — roughly forty years.

This cycle's conditions include nuclear weapons, modern military-industrial capacity, 1.4 billion people, and AI iterating on a monthly timescale.

## V. Model Boundaries and Falsifiability

### 5.1 Model Boundaries

Does not explain why China's loop has always been able to eliminate transcendent authority (including at the Reconstruction moment) while Europe's has not — this is a meta-Xin question, outside the framework's boundary. Does not explain why the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop exists — this is a precondition; origins are not addressed. Does not predict time. Does not predict specific triggers. Makes no normative judgments.

### 5.2 Falsifiable Conditions

Any of the following, if falsified by fact, requires structural revision of the model:

One. A civilization where the Branch Condition holds (Supreme Kingship successfully eliminates transcendent authority in both the Accumulation phase and at the Reconstruction moment) stably fails to rebuild isomorphically after Rupture — and not because of external force intervention.

Two. A civilization where the Branch Condition fails (there is a surviving and new-loop-indestructible independent transcendent authority at the moment Reconstruction is complete) stably rebuilds isomorphically after Rupture — and not because the transcendent authority's executive body was co-opted.

Three. Some Centralized Positive Feedback Loop's Accumulation process does not produce the mutual feeding of the two pressure tracks — i.e., Resource Concentration does not lead to the Bottom Pressure Line, or the Local Agent Requirement does not accumulate the Agent Pressure Line.

Four. Modeling another civilization using the same method produces a structure that contradicts this model.

### 5.3 Open Questions

**Conditions for loop termination.** The Pressure Accumulation Model describes the continuous operation of the loop, not its termination. Currently identifiable termination possibilities: the Signal Containers come to contain an indestructible transcendent authority; the rejection costs of Supreme Kingship exceed the resource base with no alternative; external forces physically destroy all Reproduction Function mechanisms. None of the three has been confirmed to have occurred.

**Origins of the Independent Executive Body.** Whether the direction of Signal Containers stably changes after Rupture depends on whether an Independent Executive Body anchors the new direction. Europe has the Church; Islam has the Ulama; India has the caste system. Such a structure has never appeared in China's Signal Containers. The injection of external signals during the warring period can change the surface direction of Signal Containers (the Republican Era has already demonstrated this), but without an Independent Executive Body anchoring it, the new direction is unstable and snaps back to the isomorphic form (the Republican Era has equally demonstrated this). Whether some mechanism exists that can produce an Independent Executive Body during the warring period remains open.

**Viability of the reconstruction product.** Historically, loops rebuilt after Rupture have all been able to operate normally because the external signal environment's rate of iteration has been low enough that the gap accumulated during the warring period was not large enough to render the reconstruction product non-viable. As external iteration speed continues to accelerate, whether reconstruction products remain viable is open.

## VI. Relationship to Existing Theories

**Geographic determinism (Diamond).** Diamond uses topography to explain Chinese unification and European fragmentation. Geography is a constant and cannot explain dynamics — on the same terrain, China is sometimes unified and sometimes fragmented. This model's Branch Condition is the structure of Signal Containers, not topography. But topography can serve as an explanatory variable for the preconditions of the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop — China's North China Plain makes resource concentration physically feasible; Japan's island-and-mountain topography makes the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop impossible to establish. Topography as an explanation of preconditions and topography as an explanation of dynamics are two different things.

**Hydraulic hypothesis (Wittfogel).** Wittfogel uses irrigation requirements to explain centralization. Archaeological evidence shows that state formation preceded large-scale irrigation — the causal direction is reversed. This model does not address the origins of centralization.

**Steppe threat theory (Ko, Koyama, Sng).** The unidirectional threat from northern nomads forced China to concentrate resources. The steppe threat disappeared after the Qing; China still rebuilt the Centralized Positive Feedback Loop. This model's explanation does not depend on external threat.

**The First Great Divergence (Scheidel).** Scheidel describes how after Rome's collapse Europe entered a durable multi-polar state and notes that the independent position of the Christian Church was one factor. But he himself evaluates the importance of Christianity for imperial failure as "extremely weak" and does not elevate it to a Branch Condition. This model's contribution is threefold: first, elevating the phenomenon Scheidel observed to a formal conditional variable; second, distinguishing the sequence of Taming and collapse — the question is not the existence of the Church but the state of the Church at the moment of collapse (Tamed vs. independent); third, using the contrast between Eastern and Western Rome to validate the effectiveness of this distinction. Using a single variable to unify the explanation of the different trajectories of six civilizations — this is what Scheidel's framework was unable to do. Scheidel himself noted that this question "remains neglected in the research."

**Ultra-stable structure (Jin Guantao).** Shares the observation of "periodic oscillation and structural repair." The difference lies in ontology: Jin Guantao treats economy, politics, and ideology as parallel sub-structures; this model treats institutions and economy as expression results of the loop. Jin Guantao's "patriarchal isomorphic body" corresponds to this model's Isomorphic Organizations, but this model further identifies the Dual Function of Isomorphic Organizations — the Reproduction Function plus the Distributed Immunity Function. China's post-reform trajectory — dramatic economic change but political structure snapping back to centralization — better fits this model's predictions.

**Fukuyama's theory of political order.** Fukuyama attributes the absence of rule of law to the absence of transcendent religion. This model's explanatory direction is the opposite: China does not lack transcendent authority; Supreme Kingship structurally rejected all authority claiming transcendent status — and throughout history the attempts of transcendent authority did genuinely occur (Way of Great Peace, the growth of Buddhism, the introduction of Catholicism), with each attempt cleared by the Immune Response. The language of the Kangxi Prohibition precisely validates this: the problem was not the doctrinal content but the Pope's claim of authority higher than the emperor's.
