# Critique of Chinese Education: Signal Matching and Modeling

---

## Abstract

Chinese education faces a deep structural problem: what it is good at and what this era actually needs have diverged severely. What it excels at is training students to produce standard answers on examinations — what we call "signal matching." But what modern society actually needs is the ability to construct effective models when confronting novel problems and to continuously adjust and adapt — what we call "modeling capacity." These two things look similar; they are in fact vastly different.

This paper borrows a set of systems-analysis tools to explain: How did this misalignment form? Why was this system so successful in the past? Why is it becoming increasingly unsustainable? What forces have been sustaining it, and what forces are now slowly tearing it apart?

The paper analyzes the convergence of four historical forces (industrial standardization, Confucian culture, the civil examination tradition, and resource constraints imposed by prolonged poverty), reveals three positive feedback loops operating within the education system, and identifies nine currently active erosion lines. It concludes that a fundamental contradiction has emerged between the capacities that the economy requires and the degree of controllability that power demands — and this is what makes reform so difficult.

This paper offers no policy recommendations. It aims only to explain why this system does what it is currently doing.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Signal Matching, Modeling Capacity, Positive Feedback Loop, Chinese Education

---

## I. The Problem

The education system's criterion (degree of signal matching) and the capacity that human civilization actually depends on (modeling capacity) exhibit a structural divergence. This paper models Chinese education using the formal tools of Semantic Flow Dynamics to analyze the dynamics of this structural divergence: what it is, what mechanisms sustain it, what is sacrificed and what is gained, who is sacrificed, and how its dynamics change as conditions change.

This paper offers no policy recommendations. It provides structural explanation — why this system does what it does, why it once succeeded, and why common reform pathways tend to be absorbed by the system's own dynamics.

## III. Learning, Education, and the System

### 3.1 Learning

As defined by the framework: Learning is change in Xin. Semantic Flow never stops; learning is occurring at every moment. No classroom, textbook, teacher, or examination is required. Learning is neutral — any change in Xin in any direction is learning.

### 3.2 Education

Education is not the same as learning. Education is directed learning — the direction being to make a person better. "Better" is a value choice; every educational thinker has answered this question. Dewey said: provide experience for growth. Confucius said: teach according to the student's nature to become a junzi. Socrates said: dialogue brings one closer to truth. The directions differ, but all presuppose a "better." In the language of the framework: Education provides conditions and direction for the change of Xin, with the direction pointing toward the expansion of capacity and the enhancement of adaptability.

Education provides direction, but cannot guarantee outcomes — the Black-Box Law ensures Transformation is unpredictable. What education can do is provide conditions; what it cannot do is determine results.

### 3.3 The System

The education system replaces "better" with "more matching." The direction shifts from "making people better" to "making people's outputs match specified Signals." This substitution is the distortion. The distortion is not from learning to system — it is from education to system. Education itself has direction; the system replaced that direction.

System-defined learning: the ability to produce Collapses within a designated scope that match designated Signals.

### 3.4 Two Criteria

**Examination Criterion:** The degree to which a Collapse output within a designated scope matches a designated Signal. Closed matching.

**Modeling Capacity Criterion:** The adaptability of Collapse outputs when confronting new realities. Open correspondence. Science, economics, art, and engineering are all built on modeling capacity.

Modeling capacity requires a positive definition. In the language of the framework: Modeling capacity is the ability to adjust direction after Semantic Flow encounters reality and a gap appears. Signal matching measures the distance between output and a designated Signal — whether the answer is correct. Modeling capacity measures the distance between output and reality — whether the model's results match actual observations. The former is closed, with answers predetermined; the latter is open, with reality supplying no predetermined answers.

This distinction is not invented by this paper. The mental models theory in cognitive science (Johnson-Laird) holds that human reasoning essentially involves constructing and operating models in the mind, not matching memorized answers. Ericsson's research on deliberate practice demonstrates that simple repetition does not produce expertise — adjustment targeted at gaps does. Repetition is the operation of signal matching; deliberate practice is the operation of modeling. The OECD and UNESCO identify critical thinking as a core 21st-century competency; the research consensus is that critical thinking can be explicitly taught, but what is required is not memorization — it is active analysis, questioning premises, and evaluating arguments. Different words, same thing: the source of capacity lies not in signal matching but in the Transformation that occurs after Semantic Flow collides with reality.

The Law of Attenuation creates a structural blind spot. What a person has learned lives inside Semantic Flow — others cannot see it, and the person cannot express it completely (the Law of Attenuation ensures that encoding necessarily involves loss). But the system needs to determine "whether this person has learned," so it needs verification methods at the Signal end. Verification is not limited to examinations alone — practical tasks, experiments, internships, and portfolio reviews are all methods, but they differ in cost. Examinations are the cheapest: no equipment, no on-site presence, no one-on-one interaction, maximum standardization. Poverty pushes the cheapest option into the default (expanded in Section IV). "Enforceable by power" is an additional layer, arising from power's needs, not from the Law of Attenuation — power requires a controllable criterion, and examinations happen to be controllable. Three forces — the unobservability created by the Law of Attenuation, the cost constraints created by poverty, and the controllability demands created by power — converge on the same solution: examinations. This explains "why examinations" (the choice of verification method). Section IV explains "why signal matching" (the locking of the entire educational direction) — examinations are one implementation of signal matching, but the roots of signal matching go deeper than examinations.

Modeling capacity cannot be standardized — the Black-Box Law ensures Transformation results are unpredictable, and what is unpredictable cannot serve as a criterion.

The gap between education and the system is what this paper models. The next section explains why this gap exists and why it persists.

## IV. Initial Conditions: The Coupling of Four Forces

China's modern educational structure is not a clean design. It is the product of four forces colliding.

### 4.1 Industrial Standardization

The Prussian model, introduced at the end of the nineteenth century and the beginning of the twentieth. Its design objective: mass production of standardized executors. Enclosed spaces, uniform timetables, uniform Signals, uniform criteria, compulsory attendance.

### 4.2 Confucian Culture

Reverence for teachers, deference to authority, moral cultivation. Provides local legitimacy for the school's power structure — "respecting the teacher" is itself the justification.

### 4.3 Civil Examination Residue

The imperial examination system operated for 1,300 years (from the Sui-Tang period to 1905). Examinations determine fate, memorization is the core operation, credentials lead to power — these were not invented by modern schools; they are products of the examination cycle, perpetuated through intergenerational transmission.

### 4.4 Poverty

The hardest of the four forces. China was extremely poor when it established its modern education system, and the majority of families still have limited disposable resources today. The wealth gap means that the affluent long ago stopped playing by these rules — studying abroad, private tutoring, international schools. Those who remain in the system have no other options.

Memorization requires no laboratory, no equipment, no small classes, no teacher with strong modeling capacity — it suffices for the teacher to have memorized the material themselves. The wealthy never spent their days memorizing. Memorization is not the essence of education; it is education for the poor, the lowest-cost solution under resource constraints.

### 4.5 The Coupling of Four Forces

The four forces overlap at one point — all require signal matching. Industry demands execution to specification; Confucianism demands conduct according to the classics; the civil examinations demand answers matching approved texts; poverty demands the cheapest possible operation. Signal matching became the intersection. The sacrifice is therefore fourfold. The structure is particularly stable, because removing any single force leaves the other three still holding it up.

### 4.6 Probe: The Absence of Logic

Logic is not merely a subject; it is a Filtering structure. Once a student acquires the Filtering condition of "is this reasoning valid?", that condition does not operate only within a single course — the teaching of the Analects shifts from memorization to debate, historical narratives require chains of evidence, political course premises come under scrutiny. The signal-matching criterion dissolves under the Filtering of logic. Insert logic as a probe into the system, and the system's rejection response is the diagnostic result.

Among the four forces, three reject logic, one does not, and one amplifies. Industry does not reject logic — engineers need logic, quality control requires logic, and Germany, the birthplace of the Prussian model, includes philosophy in secondary education. The forces that reject logic are the other three: the deep Xin of Confucian culture ties the legitimacy of knowledge to authority rather than to the process of reasoning; the criterion of civil examination residue tests matching rather than reasoning validity; political power (Power Demand) treats the questioning of premises as a threat. Poverty does not directly reject logic, but under conditions where the other three already reject it, poverty provides additional justification — teaching logic costs more than teaching memorization.

Taiwan, Japan, and South Korea are the control group for this analysis. Japanese secondary education has an "Ethics" course, but it teaches intellectual history, not reasoning training; the place of philosophy in the South Korean curriculum is occupied by "moral education." The political conditions in all three places are entirely different — Japan and South Korea are democratized, Taiwan has undergone political transformation — yet logic is equally absent. Removing the political variable, rejection still occurs, demonstrating that the Confucian cultural force operates independently of politics. China's particularity lies in politics and culture reinforcing each other in the same direction — a dual lock.

The counterexample is France. Philosophy is a compulsory subject for all secondary school students, and the first examination of the baccalauréat is a four-hour philosophy essay. French philosophy education is not a history of philosophy — it teaches concepts (freedom, reason, morality) and requires students to deconstruct and construct arguments themselves. The roots of this tradition are the Enlightenment: the deep Xin of France defines "questioning premises" as the foundation of civic competence. Same probe, inserted into the French system, zero rejection. Different deep Xin, different Filtering structure, different capacity to accommodate.

The absence of logic is not the result of any single policy; it is the signature of the coupled four-force structure. Analysis at the cognitive-structural level is an independent research direction, outside the scope of this paper.

## V. The Model

### 5.1 Loop Structure

```
Economic Demand (what capacities are needed)
Power Demand (what degree of controllability is needed)
  → Criterion (power sets the criterion: what is tested; responds to both Economic Demand and Power Demand)
    → Execution (schools execute: how to teach)
      → Output (output: what kind of people)
        → Reception (society receives: match or gap)
          → Feedback (feedback: can the pain reach the power end)
            → Criterion (power maintains criterion / re-anchors)

Output also feeds back into Execution:
  Output → Teachers (return to Execution as operators)
  Output → Parents (send the next generation into Execution)
  Output → Decision-makers (enter Criterion, maintain the criterion)

Execution self-reinforces:
  Output (admission rates) → Execution's authority → better students + more resources + local government support
  → Execution's power grows → Execution further strengthens the same operations → Output (higher admission rates)
```

Three closed loops: the Main Loop determines what the loop runs on. The Self-Replication Loop determines who runs it. The Execution Self-Reinforcement Loop determines how hard the loop runs. All three loops lock simultaneously and mutually reinforce.

### 5.2 Definitions of Each Node

**Social Demand —** Social demand is not a single demand; it is the superposition of two demands. Economic Demand is what capacities are needed. Power Demand is what degree of controllability is needed. Previously, Economic Demand and Power Demand did not conflict — the economy needed standardized executors; power needed obedient people; same solution. Now Economic Demand and Power Demand are diverging — the economy needs modeling capacity, but modeling capacity implies independent thinking, and independent thinking threatens Power Demand.

**Criterion —** The anchor of the loop. All downstream nodes are held by the anchor. Current anchor: the college entrance examination (gaokao), degree of signal matching.

**Execution —** Current operation: memorization (the lowest-cost solution under the coupling of four forces). Execution self-reinforces within the Positive Feedback Loop — admission rates are authority capital, authority attracts students and resources, and local governments use admission rates as performance metrics. The Hengshui Model is the product of the Execution Self-Reinforcement Loop running to the extreme.

**Output —** Output does not only enter Reception; it simultaneously feeds back — becoming teachers, parents, decision-makers. The loop runs on its own products. The Epistemic Barrier is transmitted intergenerationally here. Output is a model simplification: large numbers of people are filtered out during Execution (dropouts, elimination, middle-school tracking). Those filtered out are the fuel of the loop, not its products.

**Reception —** Match (stable) or gap (punishing).

**Feedback —** Key variable: who absorbs the pain. Falls on power → pathway open. Falls on students → pathway closed.

### 5.3 Stable State

For the Positive Feedback Loop to run, the precondition is that Output is genuinely useful. This precondition held for a considerable length of time.

Using this loop, China pulled an extremely impoverished country into industrialization at extremely low cost. The economy needed large numbers of people who could execute to specification (Economic Demand); the loop mass-produced such people (Output); the market received them (Reception); the benefit pathway was realized (pass → credentials → job → income); the lower classes saw the returns and actively sent the next generation into the loop. Economic Demand and Power Demand both matched Output; the loop ran stably. Forty years of economic growth did not happen outside the loop — it was sustained by the loop's products.

Stability triply reinforced: the Self-Replication Loop (products feed back as operators), the benefit-pathway positive feedback (visible returns drive investment), and lower-class active support (the gaokao is the only upward channel for the poor — not coercion, but rational choice).

The problem with the loop is not that it was always failing — it once succeeded under the right conditions, and conditions have changed. The key lies in the distinction made in 3.4: the Economic Demand of the industrialization stage required exactly signal matching — execute to specification, produce to instruction, operate by procedure — and that is precisely what the loop output. The gap between signal matching and modeling capacity did not constitute a problem during that stage, because the market itself did not require modeling capacity. Once Economic Demand shifted from "needing standardized executors" to "needing modeling capacity," the loop continued outputting the same thing — but the market stopped accepting it. Section VI analyzes precisely how, after this turning point, the Positive Feedback Loop shifts from self-reinforcement to self-erosion.

### 5.4 The Source of Modeling Capacity Is Not the School

Modeling capacity requires three conditions: encountering real problems with no preset answers, receiving feedback on the gap between output and reality, and having space to adjust the direction of Semantic Flow based on that gap. All three conditions are necessary. Preset-answer exercises are not real problems — the gap is eliminated. Right-or-wrong grading is not gap feedback — it tells you how far you are from a designated Signal, not how far you are from reality. Standard procedures allow no space for adjustment — the direction is locked.

What schools provide is precisely the inverse of these three conditions: preset answers, right-or-wrong grading, standard procedures. Schools are not the source of modeling capacity; they are a desert for modeling capacity.

But school is not the only Signal environment for students. Family, peers, the internet, part-time work, romantic relationships — all are Signal sources; all can provide real problems with no preset answers. Subject knowledge is material; modeling capacity requires personal experience of using that material to collide with reality. Chinese education does produce people with modeling capacity — not because schools cultivated it, but because schools failed to occupy all of a person's time.

### 5.5 Verification of Extreme Closure: The Hengshui Model

If the source of modeling capacity lies outside school, cutting off all external sources should lead to collapse.

China's super-secondary schools — Hengshui High School, Maotanchang High School — implement 24-hour military-style management. Results: adolescent depression screening rate 24.6% (Institute of Psychology, Chinese Academy of Sciences, 2021); mental disorder prevalence 17.5% (National Health Commission, 2021); non-suicidal self-injury 27.4%. After a student at Hengshui Second High School jumped from a building, iron railings were installed. A wave of students taking medical leave has spread to elementary schools, with slots having waiting lists. Post-graduation sequelae: social disconnection, loss of motivation, drastic personality changes.

The primary force producing super-secondary schools is local governments' pursuit of admission-rate performance metrics — directly verifying "schools are accountable only upward." Installing iron railings is the physical expression of the loop's self-protection: the structure is not changed; the exit from collapse is blocked.

### 5.6 Sacrifice Structure

Sacrificed: modeling capacity, individual difference, capacity for questioning, free space for change in Xin.
Gained: large-scale predictability, social operational efficiency, extremely low cost, a controllable population.
Who is sacrificed: those with no alternative options. Those filtered out sacrifice more — they do not even receive the promised returns.

The trade is no longer worthwhile for Economic Demand (what is gained is no longer valuable), but remains worthwhile for Power Demand (what is sacrificed is precisely what power does not want).

## VI. The Instability of Positive Feedback

A Positive Feedback Loop is not a perpetual motion machine. Nine erosion lines act simultaneously on every node of the loop.

### Erosion Within Execution

**Student mental collapse.** Loop intensity exceeds the material's tolerance limit. Internal implosion of the Positive Feedback Loop itself.

**Dropout and attrition.** Those filtered out sacrifice years and receive nothing; lower-class motivation to support the loop is eroded.

### Erosion of Execution's Fuel

**Declining birth rate.** The pool of students shrinks; the Execution Self-Reinforcement Loop runs short of fuel.

**Emigration abroad.** The best fuel flows out of the loop.

### Erosion of the Benefit Pathway

**Enrollment expansion and credential inflation.** The premise "passing the exam = good job" is diluted.

**Economic slowdown.** The job market contracts; the Reception end's absorption capacity shrinks.

### Erosion of the Self-Replication Loop

**Lying flat.** Active withdrawal from the loop. No competing, no striving, no reproducing, no consuming. Cuts directly through the Self-Replication Loop's supply line.

### Erosion of Closure

**Information penetration.** Students see post-graduation reality while still inside the school. Closure is broken, accelerating lying flat and mental collapse.

### Erosion of Social Demand and Cost

**AI.** Simultaneously depresses the market value of signal matching, provides cheaper alternatives for personalized education, and pulls out the foundational premise that credentials hold value. The most powerful erosion line — but not the only one.

Nine erosion lines acting simultaneously; every loop in the three-loop structure is leaking.

## VII. Empirical Verification

The model's predictions can be checked line by line against currently observable phenomena.

### 7.1 Collapse of the Benefit Pathway

A 2024 Zhaopin report shows the employment rate of new graduates at only 55.5%. The anticipated national scale of 2025 university graduates reaches 12.22 million (Ministry of Education, 2024). National Bureau of Statistics data shows the urban surveyed unemployment rate for those aged 16–24 (excluding students) peaking at 18.9% in August 2025. Average starting salary for bachelor's degree holders stands at only 4,526 yuan, up just 800 yuan over a decade — lower than skilled workers or food delivery couriers (Zhaopin, 2026).

Graduate school applications dropped from 4.38 million to 3.43 million, declining for three consecutive years. Applications for the national civil service exam surpassed 3.51 million, exceeding graduate school applicants. Young people have shifted from competing for credentials to seeking stability — the benefit returns of credentials are collapsing.

### 7.2 Lying Flat and Active Withdrawal

Ministry of Civil Affairs statistics show that 2024 marriage registrations fell to 6.106 million couples, a 20.5% decline from 2023, the lowest since 1980 (Ministry of Civil Affairs, 2025). The term "mouse person" has accumulated hundreds of millions of views on social media. Young people are openly stating they "cannot even support themselves" and have no plans to marry or have children.

### 7.3 Emigration

Some young people have stated directly that "walking out on the street, one sees store after store with shutters down," and ultimately chose to leave China.

### 7.4 Information Penetration and the Uselessness of Schooling

"The old logic of learning is failing; many families feel that the old ways of investing in education and their understanding of what good education means are increasingly ineffective." The "schooling is useless" sentiment has generated anxiety in some populations — the gap between expectation and reality has shaken the traditional belief that "studying hard leads to success."

### 7.5 AI Erosion

Reports from multiple recruitment platforms indicate that large and medium-sized enterprises have deployed AI résumé screening systems at scale, with credentials carrying progressively less weight in initial screening.

### 7.6 Pathways That Bypass the School

Douyin, live streaming, self-media — the capacities they require are entirely modeling capacities. No credentials needed; direct connection to the market. The moat around the school loop is bypassed.

### 7.7 The Tutoring Industry

When parents acquire money, it is invested into the same loop — tutoring programs, practice books, repetitive training. This verifies the intergenerational transmission of the Epistemic Barrier: when loop products become consumers, they purchase more of the loop.

### 7.8 Correspondence

All nine of the model's erosion lines correspond to actual data. The gap is no longer a prediction — it is a reality already occurring, already large enough to be directly visible in statistical data and to have formed a social consensus at the level of sentiment.

The model's falsification conditions are equally clear: reversal of the direction of any one of the nine erosion lines — sustained decline in youth unemployment with rising credential premium, rebound in marriage and birth rates, reversal of the emigration trend, recession of lying flat accompanied by rising rates of active loop participation — would constitute partial falsification of the model. If most erosion lines reverse simultaneously, the model's claim of "simultaneous erosion" would require fundamental revision. The model is not post-hoc explanation; it is a structural prediction that future data can overturn.

## VIII. Power's Response

### 8.1 Power is Already Moving

In 2026, the Ministry of Education and four other ministries issued the "AI + Education" Action Plan. Translated into the language of the framework: power's judgment is that the goal is no longer to cultivate "finished products" but people who can rapidly construct cognitive frameworks in unknown domains, integrate resources across disciplinary boundaries, and iterate persistently in the face of failure. This is modeling capacity. Power has seen the gap; the judgment is correct.

### 8.2 But the Mode of Implementation Verifies Loop Self-Protection

Power's approach is not to dismantle the structure but to insert AI into the old structure. AI is used for intelligent grading, question answering, and learning-situation diagnostics. Artificial intelligence becomes a new curriculum, a new examination subject. The gaokao remains; standardized textbooks remain; standardized assessment remains.

The same dynamics as "quality education becoming a new examination subject." The loop captures what can be institutionalized and filters out what cannot.

### 8.3 The Criterion Has Not Changed; The Structure Has Not Changed

As long as the gaokao remains a signal-matching criterion, all teaching activities are ultimately pulled back to signal matching. The criterion is the loop's anchor; without changing the anchor, the ship cannot move.

The Ministry of Education has implemented enrollment reductions or suspensions for majors with low employment rates, and increased enrollment quotas in AI and integrated circuits — but the mode of operation remains adjusting what is tested and what is recruited, not changing the anchor. The criterion has not changed.

## IX. The Three Layers of Resistance to Re-Anchoring

### 9.1 The Feedback Pathway is Closed

The pain falls on students, not on power. The cumulative effect of the nine erosion lines is changing the conditions of Feedback — mass unemployment threatens stability, mental collapse becomes a social issue, lying flat and not reproducing weaken national strength — but the transmission pathway is long and indirect.

### 9.2 The Collapse Cost of Re-Anchoring

All interest structures attached to the old anchor collapse simultaneously. This is a bone marrow transplant — the old must first be killed, there is an interim vacancy, and the donor does not exist. Modeling capacity cannot be assessed through standardized examination (the hard constraint of the Black-Box Law); as for what the new anchor would be, no one in the world has an answer. Finland is groping. Singapore is groping. No one has solved it.

Historical parallel: the abolition of the imperial examinations in 1905. People who had spent a lifetime reading the Four Books and Five Classics became overnight casualties. The scale of contemporary re-anchoring is several orders of magnitude larger than abolishing the civil examinations.

### 9.3 The Contradiction Between Economic Demand and Power Demand

The deepest layer of resistance. Re-anchoring would satisfy Economic Demand (the economy needs modeling capacity), but would threaten Power Demand (power needs controllable people). Independent thinking, the capacity to question — the economy needs it; the political regime fears it. No solution simultaneously satisfies both.

Power does not re-anchor not because it cannot see the problem, and not only because the cost of re-anchoring is high — it is because it fundamentally does not want the outcome of re-anchoring.

## X. Conclusion

### 10.1 Three Paths

**No Re-Anchoring.** Nine erosion lines continue, the economic end continues to deteriorate, but Power Demand is protected. Slow death.

**Re-Anchoring.** Collapse cost is enormous; the donor does not exist; even if successful, it threatens Power Demand. Bone marrow transplant, donor unknown; if the transplant succeeds, the immune system may attack the host.

**Inserting AI into the Old Structure.** The current choice. Administering medication to prolong life; buying time. The contradiction is not resolved, but collapse is not triggered. Performing a bone marrow transplant without an available donor is a death sentence — this is not stupidity; it is rationality.

### 10.2 What Is Certain and What Is Not

Economic-end collapse is structural and unavoidable. The gap between Economic Demand and Output will only widen.

Political-end collapse is not inevitable. Power can choose to bear economic costs in order to maintain Power Demand. Historically, large numbers of regimes have endured for long periods under poor economic conditions.

The education system is caught in the contradiction between Economic Demand and Power Demand, with no solution that satisfies both simultaneously. This is not an optimizable problem; it is a structural contradiction.

### 10.3 The Fractal

This paper models the cross-section of education. But the model's structure does not depend on the particularity of education. The contradiction between Economic Demand and Power Demand, the three loops of the Positive Feedback Loop, the nine erosion lines, the three layers of resistance to re-anchoring — none of these are exclusive to education. The same group of people, carrying similar Xin structures, enter economics, law, politics, and media, producing similar structures in every domain. Education is one cross-section of this structure, not the lesion. The same pattern replicates itself across different scales — this is the fractal. East Asian societies share Confucian culture and examination-selection traditions; the variable in the model is the intensity of Power Demand and the proportional configuration of the four forces; the structure does not change. Refinement is work for the future.

### 10.4 Reuse

The model of this paper can be aimed at any system composed of people, with a power structure, and with a Positive Feedback Loop. Each of the nine erosion lines can independently be expanded into complete research. Cross-national comparison, micro-level verification (field observation of a single classroom), and cross-domain reuse (analysis of the contradiction between Economic Demand and Power Demand in economics, the judiciary, and the media) are all subsequent work unfolding from the same model. This paper is a node, not an endpoint.

### 10.5 Boundaries

The framework does not predict pathways or timing. The framework delivers its conclusion here. What comes next is the work of human beings.

This paper's expression is a Signal. Readers with different Xin will read different semantics. This paper cannot exempt itself from its own postulates.
