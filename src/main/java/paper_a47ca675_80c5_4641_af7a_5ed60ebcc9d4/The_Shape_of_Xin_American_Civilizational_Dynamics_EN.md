## Abstract

This paper is an empirical demonstration of the limits of the Semantic Flow Dynamics (SFD) framework. Using the same tools and the same operator, it models a target civilization for which the operator lacks internal embodied sense, thereby testing where the operator boundary predicted by the axioms actually falls. The scan covers the Christian tradition (approximately 2,000 years), the British constitutional tradition (approximately 800 years), and the period from the North American colonies to the present (approximately 400 years). Core finding: the American Xin-Model takes the form of a Multi-Core Structure — Law Above All, Inviolable Individual, Government by Consent, Self-Governance, Commerce as Virtue — architecturally distinct from China's single-core structure. The Dynamic Mechanism is Multi-Core Tension and the Calibration Spiral; the Reproduction Mechanism is Self-Governing Bodies. Part Four's stress tests actively attack five core claims; two survive with qualifications, two are revised, and one is downgraded. The framework as a whole is reclassified from "theoretical validation" to "framework application." Part Five establishes this reclassification as an empirical result of the framework itself: from the Black Box Law and the Loss Law it derives the Operator Boundary Theorem, demonstrating that the modeling precision ceiling for an external operator is a structural corollary of the axioms, not a matter of operator competence. On this basis, the paper's outputs are reclassified into three Constraint Models — the Multi-Core Constraint Model, the Calibration Constraint Model, and the Mobilization Constraint Model — demonstrating that meaningful structural conclusions remain derivable even under incomplete information.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Xin-Model, American civilization, Multi-Core Structure, Operator Boundary Theorem, Constraint Model, framework limits

## Part Zero: Methodology and Positioning

### 0.1 Nature of This Paper

This paper is an empirical demonstration of the limits of the Semantic Flow Dynamics framework. The methodology follows framework v5.0. The paper aims to answer two questions: first, does a Xin-Model produced by applying the same method to another civilization turn out to be isomorphic with the Chinese Xin-Model? Second, under what conditions can the framework do what, and under what conditions can it not?

The answer to the first question is in Parts One through Three: the two Xin-Models are not isomorphic — they differ not only in content but in architecture. The answer to the second question is in Part Five: an external operator's modeling precision has a structural ceiling, and that ceiling is a corollary of the axioms, not a question of operator capability. The two answers together map the framework's complete capability profile.

### 0.2 Why America

Three reasons. First, the contrast between American and Chinese political structures is extremely high. Second, American historical materials are abundant and its landmark events well established. Third, America provides an opportunity to test whether the framework presupposes a single-core structure — if America also turned out single-core, the framework might simply be manufacturing an isomorphism illusion.

### 0.3 Revision to Scan Scope

The original version began scanning from the Mayflower (1620). This version revises that to a three-layer scan:

**Layer One: Deep Christian presuppositions (approximately 2,000 years).** American Xin did not emerge from nothing in North America. The great majority of early colonists were Christians who crossed the ocean carrying the deep presuppositions of Christian civilization. These presuppositions are the root system of American Xin and must be included in the scan.

**Layer Two: British constitutional and cultural tradition (approximately 800 years).** American political institutions and cultural habits were directly inherited from Britain. The scan traces which Xin transmitted from Britain to North America survived, which died, and which mutated.

**Layer Three: Mutations catalyzed by the North American environment (approximately 400 years).** From the Mayflower to the present. The distinctive environmental conditions of North America — no aristocracy, no monopoly state church, virtually limitless land, physical absence of royal power — forced mutations in Xin.

The rationale for the revision: beginning the scan in 1620 is like cutting away the roots and examining only the trunk. American Xin is not a four-hundred-year product; it is a variant of Christianity's two-thousand-year root system expressing itself in a particular environment. The choice of scan origin directly determines the depth of the Xin-Model.

### 0.4 Operator Boundary Declaration

The precision of the SFD framework is limited by the operator's depth of embodied sense of the target civilization. The author of this paper has internal embodied sense of Chinese civilization, and only external observation of American civilization. This means:

**Externally reachable precision:** Identifying top-tier candidate Xin, judging that two civilizations are not isomorphic, rough ordering.

**Internally unreachable precision:** Fine-grained ordering among top-tier Xin, the specific behavior of the Dynamic Mechanism under extreme pressure, the micro-level operation of the Reproduction Mechanism.

The conclusions of this paper hold within the range of external precision. Items requiring internal precision are marked "pending revision by an operator with internal American embodied sense." This boundary declaration is not a rhetorical gesture of modesty — Part Five will demonstrate that it is a structural corollary of the axioms.

A note on the criterion: the scan materials come from Christian and British history, but the criterion does not lie in history — it lies in today's Americans. The standard for the disappearance test is "if gone, is it still America?" — a judgment about the current identity of Americans, which an external observer can perform at a coarse-grained level.

### 0.4.1 Framework Degrees-of-Freedom Declaration

The framework carries a structural risk that must be made explicit here: the scan scope, the merger criterion, and the ordering rationale are all determined by the operator, allowing substantial degrees of freedom. This means that different operators applying the framework to the same civilization may produce different Xin-Models, and each version may be internally consistent. This paper does not evade this problem. To reduce the risk of spurious self-consistency generated by degrees of freedom, Part Four supplements the analysis with an independent stress-testing procedure that attempts to actively overturn the paper's own core conclusions. Conclusions that survive are provisionally retained; those that are destabilized are downgraded to "hypotheses pending verification."

### 0.5 Note on Historical Materials

Same as the original version: the historical materials in this paper are based on scholarly consensus; no new claims about specific historical facts are made. The paper's contribution lies in the structural repositioning of these events.

## Part One: Building the Xin-Model

### 1.1 Three-Layer Scan

#### Layer One: Deep Christian Presuppositions

Six deep presuppositions are extracted from the Christian tradition; these were carried to North America by European immigrants.

**C1. Existence of transcendent authority.** Above the human world there is a higher existence (God); all secular power stands beneath it. No one is ultimate. Judaism already held this presupposition; Christianity universalized it.

**C2. Untrustworthy human nature (Original Sin).** Human beings are naturally prone to corruption and cannot achieve perfection through their own efforts. Augustine systematized this; Calvin intensified it into "total depravity." Political corollary: unrestricted power cannot be entrusted to anyone.

**C3. Law Above All above rulers.** God's Law binds everyone, including kings. A king who violates the law loses legitimacy.

**C4. Church-State duality.** "Render unto Caesar what is Caesar's, and unto God what is God's." Christian civilization from the outset presupposes two power centers that check each other.

**C5. Equality of individual souls before God.** Every soul is of equal worth; secular rank does not equal spiritual rank. The theological root system of "all men are created equal."

**C6. Covenant tradition.** God enters into covenant with humanity. A covenant is bilateral, conditional, and violable. Government too is a form of covenant.

#### Layer Two: British Constitutional and Cultural Tradition

**E1. Constrained royal power.** Magna Carta (1215) established that the king is not above the law. The Petition of Right (1628) and the Bill of Rights (1689) repeatedly reaffirmed this.

**E2. Parliamentary tradition (representative government).** From the assembly of barons to parliamentary sovereignty (1689), governance requires the consent of those governed (at least the elite).

**E3. Common law.** Law grows from custom and precedent, not royal decree. Law has a life independent of royal power.

**E4. Due process.** Magna Carta, Clause 39: no free man shall be arrested, imprisoned, or stripped of property except by lawful judgment.

**E5. Protestant individualism.** After the Reformation, the individual communicates directly with God without clerical intermediation. The Puritans further intensified this: individual judgment takes precedence over institutional authority.

**E6. Inviolability of private property.** The central object of common law protection. Property is the material foundation of liberty.

**E7. Legitimacy derives from procedure and consent.** The Glorious Revolution (1689) established that a king who breaks the covenant may be deposed.

**B1. Hierarchical deference.** British society long organized itself by class. Imitation appeared in the early colonies but weakened sharply after the Revolution. Its fate in America: dead. Its opposite (equality) became non-negotiable consensus.

**B2. Empiricism and pragmatic attitude.** The tradition of Bacon, Locke, and Hume: what matters is practical effect, not theory. Its fate in America: fully inherited and intensified into pragmatism.

**B3. Island mentality → isolationism.** Britain kept its distance from Continental affairs. Its fate in America: inherited and amplified. The Monroe Doctrine, isolationism before both World Wars, Trump's "America First."

**B4. Maritime trading culture — Commerce as Virtue is legitimate.** Britain from the sixteenth century was a maritime trading nation; the merchant class held social standing. Its fate in America: fully inherited and taken to an extreme. Commercial success came to be viewed as proof of virtue.

**B5. Voluntary association tradition.** Guilds, mutual aid societies, clubs, charitable organizations. The state is not the only social organizer. Its fate in America: fully inherited and erupted. The "art of association" Tocqueville observed.

**B6. Fair Play.** Games have rules, winning and losing should be graceful, cheating is more shameful than losing. Its fate in America: inherited. Daily training in procedural fairness.

**B7. English language.** Not merely a communication tool. Legal vocabulary and political concepts — rights, liberty, property, due process — directly shaped political thinking. Its fate in America: fully inherited, and the Signal infrastructure of the entire system.

#### Layer Three: Mutations Catalyzed by the North American Environment

**A1. No aristocracy.** North America had no hereditary aristocratic class. Social mobility was high from the beginning.

**A2. Geographic exit right.** Don't like it — leave. Land was virtually unlimited. The cost of exit was extremely low.

**A3. Self-Governance in practice.** The colonies Self-Governed from the outset. The Mayflower Compact. Town meetings. Congregationalist church governance.

**A4. Physical absence of royal power.** The king was three thousand miles away. Royal power withered in North America for lack of a Reproduction Mechanism environment.

**A5. Right to bear arms.** Frontier survival conditions required armed self-defense. The physical guarantee of Inviolable Individual independence.

#### Immigrant Selectivity — An Auxiliary Validation of the Scan (weak evidence, multiple factors)

One auxiliary validation tool for the scan results: the difficulty of assimilation for different immigrant groups. Christian European immigrants (Irish, German, Italian, Polish) — assimilation was relatively smooth, possibly because they carried compatible deep presuppositions. Non-Christian immigrants (Chinese, Indian) — assimilation was significantly harder; Chinatowns persisted for over a hundred years.

**Qualification:** This argument is weak evidence and cannot support strong conclusions. Immigrant assimilation difficulty is affected by multiple factors: racist legislation (the Chinese Exclusion Act 1882–1943), language barriers, economic conditions, discrimination due to visible difference, and more. Even among Christian European immigrants, Irish Catholics and Italian immigrants suffered severe discrimination in America for decades. Therefore, Xin compatibility is at most one factor in assimilation difficulty and cannot serve as the sole explanatory variable. Attributing the complexity of immigrant assimilation to a single variable of "Xin compatibility" risks laundering racial discrimination as civilizational difference. This paragraph is retained as an auxiliary observation but is not used as the primary basis for judgments about the root system of the Xin-Model.

### 1.5 Xin-Model

#### Core Structure: Multi-Core Structure

The American Xin-Model is not single-core. No relationship of "one overrides all" exists among the three top-tier Xin — Law Above All, Inviolable Individual, and Government by Consent. They have independent origins, independent histories; they depend on one another but are not subordinate to one another.

Remove any one, and the rest deform:
- Remove Law Above All: Inviolable Individual loses its guarantee; Government by Consent degenerates into mob rule.
- Remove Inviolable Individual: Law Above All may become an instrument of oppression; Government by Consent becomes majority tyranny.
- Remove Government by Consent: Law Above All and Inviolable Individual become rules imposed by elites.
- Remove Self-Governance: Law Above All, Inviolable Individual, and Government by Consent become ornaments of centralized power.
- Remove Commerce as Virtue: America might still be America, but a very different one.

This is the fundamental structural difference from the Chinese Xin-Model. China is single-core — everything else can be sacrificed for the Core Xin. America is Multi-Core — the top-tier Xin are mutually dependent; remove one and the system deforms.

#### Dynamic Mechanism: Multi-Core Tension

China's Dynamic Mechanism is "no fixed occupant for the position; whoever wins, takes it" — competition for the sole center. America's Dynamic Mechanism is the ongoing Multi-Core Tension among its multiple cores.

**Domestic expression: institutional competition and calibration.** The several Xin of the Multi-Core Structure naturally generate friction. When friction escalates to the systemic level, calibration proceeds through institutional means (elections, legislation, courts), moving the system to a new equilibrium. After each calibration the system is more complete than before — it does not return to its origin.

Historical cases: the Civil War was Inviolable Individual (equality) overriding Commerce as Virtue (property/commerce); the calibration result was abolition. The New Deal was Law Above All (the law) extending into the economic domain; the calibration result was the social safety net. The Civil Rights Movement was Inviolable Individual overriding the racial Xin; the calibration result was equal rights. Watergate was the confirmation that Law Above All stands above the president.

**International expression: periodic oscillation between missionary impulse and isolationist impulse.** The "split personality" of American foreign behavior derives from the alternating dominance of different Xin within the Multi-Core Structure:

- Law Above All + Christian universalism → Missionary impulse: we have the truth, you are in darkness, we are obligated to save you. Democracy promotion everywhere.
- Self-Governance → Isolationist impulse: manage your own affairs, let others manage theirs. The Monroe Doctrine.
- Commerce as Virtue → Go wherever commercial interests lead.
- Inviolable Individual → Anti-imperial: we cannot occupy others' lands and make them subjects. Fight and leave.

These four forces wax and wane in different periods. When an external threat simultaneously violates all Xin, all forces unify — this is the condition for going all-out.

**The criterion for going all-out: Number of Violated Xin — how many Xin are violated.** Nazi Germany is the only enemy to have simultaneously violated all five Xin: placing the Führer above Law Above All (anti-Law Above All), exterminating "inferior races" (anti-Inviolable Individual), abolishing democracy (anti-Government by Consent), extreme centralization eliminating Self-Governance (anti-Self-Governance), state control of the economy (anti-Commerce as Virtue). Therefore World War II is the only war in which America went all-out.

All other wars were half-hearted, because only some Xin were violated: Vietnam violated Inviolable Individual and Commerce as Virtue but not Law Above All or Self-Governance; Iraq violated Inviolable Individual but Commerce as Virtue had no direct conflict with the adversary; the Cold War violated Inviolable Individual, Government by Consent, and Commerce as Virtue, but nuclear weapons made direct war impossible. The fewer Xin violated, the weaker the domestic consensus, and the stronger the isolationist backlash.

#### Reproduction Mechanism: Self-Governing Bodies

America's Reproduction Mechanism is Self-Governing Bodies — churches, universities, corporations, community organizations, nonprofits. Each Self-Governing Body replicates the complete shape of the Multi-Core Structure at the micro level:

**Churches:** Doctrine (Law Above All), congregational equality (Inviolable Individual), voting for pastors (Government by Consent), congregational self-governance (Self-Governance), independent financial management (Commerce as Virtue). Competition among multiple denominations is itself the reproduction of Multi-Core Structure.

**Universities:** Academic norms (Law Above All), academic freedom (Inviolable Individual), faculty self-governance (Government by Consent), departmental autonomy (Self-Governance), financial self-sufficiency (Commerce as Virtue). Independent of government.

**Corporations:** Corporate law (Law Above All), employee rights (Inviolable Individual), board elections (Government by Consent), departmental autonomy (Self-Governance), profit motive (Commerce as Virtue).

**Community organizations and nonprofits:** Charters (Law Above All), member equality (Inviolable Individual), voting (Government by Consent), local self-governance (Self-Governance), independent fundraising (Commerce as Virtue).

The Reproduction Mechanism has built-in calibration: Commerce as Virtue naturally expands in peacetime, but when that expansion violates the floor set by Law Above All or Inviolable Individual, it triggers systemic backlash. The intensity of the backlash depends on the tier of the Xin violated: violate Law Above All or Inviolable Individual and the backlash is fiercest (Civil War–grade); violate Self-Governance and the backlash is moderate (New Deal–grade); imbalance within Commerce as Virtue alone and the backlash is weakest (ordinary regulation).

The American tradition of pursuing ideals over economic development is evidence that the Reproduction Mechanism is functioning healthily. The Lincoln Memorial is a Signal Container — continuously reminding Americans of the price once paid for Inviolable Individual. This collective memory is itself part of the Reproduction Mechanism.

#### Internal Tensions of the Xin-Model

The tension within the Multi-Core Structure does not lie in any single Xin being too strong (that is China's problem), but in the fact that the Xin can be used to attack one another.

Inviolable Individual (equality) can be used to attack the root system of Law Above All (Christianity) — when "everyone has the right to be themselves" is extended into domains that conflict with Christian doctrine, the church is forced to choose between revising its doctrine and being defined as a "discriminator"; either path weakens the Signal Containers of Law Above All.

Yet this tension itself is not a disease — it is the normal operation of the Multi-Core Structure. The Civil War was a conflict between Inviolable Individual and Commerce as Virtue; the result was abolition — progress. The Civil Rights Movement was a conflict between Inviolable Individual and the racial Xin; the result was equal rights — progress. Beneath a shared common language, conflict is often the beginning of calibration and progress.

#### The Dynamic Nature of Multi-Core Structure

The fundamental characteristic of the Multi-Core Structure is: conflict is the norm, movement is the heartbeat, and standstill is the crisis.

Single-core structures pursue stability. China's ideal state is "all-under-heaven at peace" — everything in its place, nothing moving. Movement is disorder; disorder must be corrected. Stability is the goal; movement is disease.

Multi-Core Structure does not pursue stability but equilibrium. Equilibrium and stability are two different things. Stability is static; equilibrium is dynamic — like riding a bicycle, it requires continuous movement to avoid falling. Stopping is what causes the fall.

American history is a history of continuous movement: independence movement → abolition movement → labor movement → women's movement → progressive movement → civil rights movement → anti-war movement → environmental movement → same-sex marriage movement. It has never stopped. Each movement is some Xin feeling suppressed and rising to demand calibration. Once calibration is complete, a new imbalance emerges and a new movement begins. This is not something wrong with America — it is the normal heartbeat of the Multi-Core Structure. Americans themselves know this — "the right to peaceable assembly and petition" is written into the First Amendment; the right to movement is protected by Law Above All.

China's heartbeat is: long stillness → sudden eruption → return to stillness. America's heartbeat is: continuous beating, never stopping.

From this emerges a key contrast: **China's crisis is eruption; America's crisis is standstill.** China fears movement — movement means the pressure has exploded. America fears non-movement — standstill means the calibration mechanism has seized up, and tensions are accumulating with no outlet. The danger of polarization is not that the two sides are fighting — it is that the channels (Congress, courts, elections) for converting conflict into institutional calibration are being called into question. Channels blocked while movement continues is not a normal heartbeat but an arrhythmia. Yet arrhythmia and cardiac arrest are two different things.

#### Garments Inventory

The Garments the Multi-Core Structure has worn: Puritan covenant theology → Enlightenment republicanism → Jacksonian democracy → Manifest Destiny → Progressivism → New Deal liberalism → Cold War anti-communism → Neoconservatism → Identity politics.

Key difference from China: China's Garments supply is shrinking (Core Xin is structurally incompatible with modernity); America's Garments supply does not face this problem — Multi-Core Structure is in principle compatible with market economies, rule of law, and free information flow. America's Garments problem is not insufficient material but that different periods emphasize different Xin, and the cut of the Garments changes accordingly.

## Part Two: Historical Regression Tests

Five rupture points are used to test the Xin-Model. Each rupture point answers four questions: what broke? what did not break? where did the energy for reconstruction come from? where does the Xin-Model fail to explain?

### 2.1 The American Revolution (1776)

**What broke:** The political connection between the colonies and Britain, the legitimacy of British royal power in North America.

**What did not break:** The Multi-Core Structure was already fully operational before independence. Law Above All (rule-of-law tradition) came from Britain. Inviolable Individual (individual rights) came from the Protestant tradition. Government by Consent (representative government) came from colonial assembly practice. Self-Governance came from town meetings. Commerce as Virtue came from colonial trade. The Revolution did not invent Xin — it separated the already-existing Multi-Core Structure from the British framework, removing the one component (royal power) that was incompatible with it.

**Reconstruction energy:** Initial construction, not reconstruction. Law Above All provided the supreme principle (the Constitution); Inviolable Individual provided the rights framework (the Bill of Rights); Government by Consent provided the procedure (the electoral system); Self-Governance provided the template (states' rights and local self-governance experience).

**What the Xin-Model does not explain:** Slavery was retained as a founding compromise — the conflict between Inviolable Individual (all men are created equal) and the racial Xin was suppressed at the founding. The cost of that suppression erupted eighty-five years later.

### 2.2 The Civil War (1861–1865)

**What broke:** The perception of the Union as a voluntary association, the Southern plantation economic structure, slavery.

**What did not break:** The calibration within the Multi-Core Structure. Both sides in the war invoked different components of the Xin-Model — the North invoked Inviolable Individual (equality of persons), the South invoked Self-Governance (states' rights). Result: Inviolable Individual overrode Self-Governance at the level of political secession, but Self-Governance was preserved in non-political domains. Law Above All operated throughout — elections proceeded normally in 1864, during the war. The Reproduction Mechanism continued operating at the grassroots level.

**Reconstruction energy:** Inviolable Individual drove the Reconstruction Amendments (Thirteenth, Fourteenth, Fifteenth) — abolition, equal protection, voting rights. This is Multi-Core calibration, not a return to the origin.

**What the Xin-Model does not explain — and what it can explain:** Reconstruction failed. "Black people are not fully human" was itself a Xin, embedded in the Southern Reproduction Mechanism (plantations, churches, families, everyday language). The Thirteenth, Fourteenth, and Fifteenth Amendments changed Signal Containers, not Xin. The racial Xin donned the Garments of Self-Governance ("states' rights"), using the language of Core Xin to protect itself — a phenomenon of Xin parasitism. The Civil Rights Movement waited nearly a hundred years, not because the Core Xin responded slowly, but because the inertia of the racial Xin required the cumulative transformation of the Signal environment before it could be eroded.

### 2.3 The New Deal (1933)

**What broke:** The ideology of laissez-faire economics, the federal government's default role as "small government."

**What did not break:** The extension of the Multi-Core Structure. Roosevelt's argument: unchecked economic power is as dangerous as unchecked political power. The New Deal extended the jurisdiction of Law Above All from political power to economic power. The separation of powers withstood pressure testing but survived (the court-packing plan failed). Self-Governance was compressed but not abolished. Commerce as Virtue was constrained but not negated.

**Reconstruction energy:** Not reconstruction, but calibration. Law Above All extended into the economic domain; Commerce as Virtue was placed within the framework of Law Above All.

**What the Xin-Model does not explain:** Roosevelt won four terms. Post-hoc immune response: the Twenty-Second Amendment (1951, limiting the president to two terms). The immune response was delayed approximately eleven years.

### 2.4 Watergate (1974)

**What broke:** The legitimacy narrative of the "imperial presidency."

**What did not break:** The purest expression of Law Above All — a president who attempted to place himself above the law was removed by the institutional system itself. Media (expression of Inviolable Individual — freedom of speech), judiciary (enforcement of Law Above All), and Congress (institution of Government by Consent) were all fully activated. Nixon's resignation was itself a victory for the Multi-Core Structure.

**Reconstruction energy:** Institutional accumulation — the War Powers Act, government ethics laws, the independent counsel statute. Calibration to a higher level of checks and balances.

### 2.5 The Trump Era (2016–Present)

**What broke:** The political culture of bipartisan consensus, trust that "institutions run themselves."

**What did not break:** The Multi-Core Structure underwent stress testing. Trump simultaneously challenged multiple Xin: disregarding legal procedure (attacking Law Above All), questioning election results (attacking Government by Consent), concentrating federal power (compressing Self-Governance) — while embracing certain facets of Inviolable Individual (individualism) and Commerce as Virtue. This was not a challenge to a single Core Xin, but an all-fronts assault on the balance of the Multi-Core Structure.

Immune responses: two impeachments, courts striking down executive orders, state election officials refusing to cooperate, the Vice President refusing to cooperate (January 6, 2021). The Multi-Core Structure bore pressure but did not collapse.

**Xin-Model prediction:** The Xin-Model predicts that a calibrating backlash will occur at some point — but the Xin-Model does not predict when or in what form. If any Xin within the Multi-Core Structure is permanently and structurally destroyed (rather than temporarily circumvented), the Xin-Model will require revision.

**What the Xin-Model does not explain:** The deeper dynamics of the Trump phenomenon — why America generated a demand for "a strongman." As an external observer, the author lacks the internal embodied sense required for this judgment; what follows is speculation, not conclusion: it may point to rising operational costs of Multi-Core Structure under polarizing conditions — Multi-Core Structure requires continuous negotiation, and negotiation perceived as paralysis under polarization generates demand to "bypass the rules and solve things directly." But this speculation has not been verified and needs revision by an operator with internal American embodied sense. Equally worth considering as alternative explanations: the cumulative effects of economic inequality, fragmentation of the media ecosystem, and identity anxiety from demographic change — these may be unrelated to the Xin-Model or only partially related.

### 2.6 Regression Test Summary

**The Xin-Model holds at all five rupture points; no structural revisions required.**

Cumulative findings:

(1) The calibration mechanism of the Multi-Core Structure exhibits delay — the time span from the emergence of a threat to an institutional response ranges from months (Watergate) to nearly a century (slavery → Civil Rights Movement).
