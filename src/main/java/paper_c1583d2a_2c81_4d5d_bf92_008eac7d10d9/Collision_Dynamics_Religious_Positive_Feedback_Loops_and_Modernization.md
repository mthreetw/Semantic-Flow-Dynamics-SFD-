# Collision Dynamics between Religious Positive Feedback Loops and Modernization

*宗教正反饋循環與現代化碰撞動力學*

Cheng Yu Huang / 黃正宇

---

## Abstract

The three major religions each followed a distinct dynamic path (Cheng Yu Huang, 2026, *Religion as a System of Information Flow*): Christianity's Gu-King Path (evolution), Islam's Lock-in Path (stable state), Buddhism's Internalization Path (self-organization). Once these paths were identified, a question remained open: when the three paths encounter modernization — an external Positive Feedback Loop — how does each respond?

This paper proposes a collision model. Religion and modernization are two Positive Feedback Loops competing for the same pool of resources. The core effect of modernization is the consumption of Family Redundancy. Family Redundancy is a temporal resource embedded in relational structures — not personal leisure, but the structural resource that sustains the conditions for the intergenerational transmission of Xin. When Family Redundancy is sufficient, transmission conditions are intact and the Religious Positive Feedback Loop operates normally; when Family Redundancy is exhausted, transmission conditions disappear and the loop loses its supply.

The three paths respond to the consumption of Family Redundancy in entirely different ways. The Gu-King Path co-evolves with modernization; each round of collision produces a new variant; variants degenerate from exclusive elimination to market competition, with the terminal state being indistinguishable from the market. The Lock-in Path refuses to let Family Redundancy be consumed and uses institutional power to maintain family structure, but faces repeated tug-of-war: the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's external force draws away Family Redundancy; the Lock-in Path pushes back; institutional power corruption contaminates the Signal end; the next generation actively rejects the entire package; suppression pushes back again — but with each round Family Redundancy's baseline is lower and the cost of holding the line higher, until it can no longer hold. The Internalization Path is not immune — consumption of Family Redundancy blocks the entry to practice; temples transform into service providers; though internal differentiation is significant: traditions incorporating more locking elements (Tibetan Buddhism) have greater resistance; the pure Internalization Path (Japanese Buddhism) degenerates most thoroughly.

This paper tests the collision model with three cases: American white Protestants (the Gu-King Path in its purest form, with the cleanest data; the Black Church as a counter-example illustrating the church-as-Family-Redundancy-supplier); Turkey (a century-long tug-of-war between the Lock-in Path and the Modernization Loop, with Iran as supplementary double-verification); and Japanese Buddhism (the complete degeneration trajectory of the Internalization Path from the Danka system to Funeral Buddhism). The core causal chain in all three cases — Modernization Positive Feedback Loop consumes Family Redundancy → intergenerational transmission of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop breaks down — is confirmed.

Family Redundancy carries not only the intergenerational transmission of religion but all non-quantifiable intergenerational transmission and risk-buffering. When Family Redundancy is exhausted, society faces two modes of death: Slow Death (intergenerational transmission breaks down → shared values disappear → fertility rate collapses) and Fast Death (risk buffers reach zero → any shock directly pierces through society). After collapse, violent reckoning ensues; survivors are forced to rebuild relational structures; Family Redundancy slowly accumulates; a new Religious Positive Feedback Loop restarts. But in the post-collapse path competition, the Lock-in Path and paths incorporating locking elements (Catholicism) have structural advantages. The endpoint of the spiral depends on the race between Family Redundancy accumulation and the restart of the Modernization Loop — if the window is long enough, a new civilization grows; if the window is too short, the spiral loses its recovery phase.

This paper is an independent verification case for *Religion as a System of Information Flow* and a mirror image of *Involution Dynamics of East Asia* — that paper treats the Transcendence Mechanism as a black box to examine modernization; this paper treats modernization as a black box to examine religion.

**Keywords:** Semantic Flow Dynamics, Positive Feedback Loop, Family Redundancy, Gu-King Path, Lock-in Path, Internalization Path, Secularization, Intergenerational Transmission

## I. The Problem

*Religion as a System of Information Flow* identified three dynamic paths. Christianity follows the Gu-King Path: Open Channel, unlocked Signals, repeated splitting and collision, surviving variants evolve. Islam follows the Lock-in Path: Closed Channel, extreme Signal locking, suppressed variation, stable state forms. Buddhism follows the Internalization Path: No Central Channel, no locking, pure market competition, no heresy adjudication.

Once the paths were identified, the original paper left an unfinished direction in its fourth chapter: "Predictions of the dynamics of civilizational collision — when religions or civilizations following different paths encounter each other, what happens at the Signal end and the semantic end of the Semantic Flow?" The original paper marked this direction as "independent verification" — phenomena not considered when the framework was being constructed are the strongest test of the framework's explanatory power.

The collision this paper addresses is not between religions but the collision all religions face together: modernization.

Modernization is a Positive Feedback Loop (Cheng Yu Huang, 2026, *Involution Dynamics of East Asia*): work → income → improved quality of life → higher expectations → more work. This loop is self-reinforcing, resource-absorbing, and irreversible. Religion is also a Positive Feedback Loop: core Signal → transmission of Xin → Functional Convergence → institutionalization → Mortality Pressure drives → loop. Two Positive Feedback Loops compete for the same pool of resources.

The question of this paper: what does the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop do to each of the three religious paths?

The significance of this question goes beyond religious studies. The terminal state of exhausted Family Redundancy is not the extinction of religion — it is civilizational collapse. Religion and modernization are both plants growing from the soil of Family Redundancy; the two plants compete for the same soil until the soil is exhausted, and both die.

**Scope limitations.** The populations covered by each path differ enormously — Afghanistan and Indonesia are both Muslim; the United States and South Africa are both Christian. The variation within a single path may be greater than the variation between paths. This paper does not attempt to use one model to explain all changes in all the world's religions. This paper selects three cases, choosing for each path the population that has followed it most purely, with the most complete historical trajectory and the cleanest data: the Gu-King Path selects American white Protestants; the Lock-in Path selects Turkey; the Internalization Path selects Japanese Buddhism. The selection criterion is not "most representative" but "best suited to testing the mechanism." Other populations (South American Catholicism, Indonesian Muslims, Taiwanese Buddhism) represent deviations from pure conditions and can be used to test model boundaries, but fall outside the scope of this paper.

The effects of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop are compressed into a single variable: the consumption of Family Redundancy.

## III. The Model

### 3.1 Resource Competition between Two Positive Feedback Loops

Religion is not a belief system — it is a Positive Feedback Loop.

The Religious Positive Feedback Loop:

- Step Zero: Core Signal enters an Individual
- Step One: Xin transforms the Signal into Semantic Flow
- Step Two: Semantic Flow achieves Functional Convergence at the group level (Signal patterns converge; Semantic Flows are not necessarily identical)
- Step Three: The products of convergence are institutionalized into Signal Containers
- Step Four: Mortality Pressure drives a new generation of Individuals to Step Zero
- → Returns to Step Zero

Modernization is also a Positive Feedback Loop (see Section 3.1 of *Involution Dynamics of East Asia*). The two Positive Feedback Loops compete for the same pool of resources: time, money, labor, and attention.

**Asymmetry of Resource Competition:** The returns of modernization are quantifiable (income, lifespan, comfort); the returns of religion are not quantifiable (sense of meaning, sense of belonging, consolation in the face of death). Quantifiable returns structurally overwhelm non-quantifiable returns in resource competition — spending three hours at church versus three hours of overtime on a Sunday: the returns from overtime are visible; the returns from church you cannot articulate. It is not that they do not exist — it is that what cannot be articulated always loses to what can be articulated in competition.

This asymmetry directly derives from the Structural Limitations of the Framework in Semantic Flow Dynamics: the semantic end cannot be quantified. Non-quantifiability is not only an analytical limitation — it is also religion's structural disadvantage in resource competition.

### 3.2 Family Redundancy

Whatever the internal mechanisms of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop, its effect on religion reduces to a single thing: families moving from having redundancy to having none.

**Terminological clarification.** "Intergenerational transmission of Xin" is shorthand. Xin is the conscious state of an Individual; it cannot be directly copied or transported — the Law of Dissipation forbids Semantic Flow from being losslessly encoded as Signals, and Xin certainly cannot be transmitted without loss. What is transmitted across generations is not Xin itself, but the conditions that allow the next generation's Xin to form: immersion in a particular semantic environment, observation of demonstrations, cohabitation with those who hold Xin. Family Redundancy carries exactly these conditions. Subsequent uses of "transmission of Xin" in this paper refer to this meaning.

**Definition.** Family Redundancy is time and resources embedded in relational structures that are not directly occupied by the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop. Isolated leisure is not Family Redundancy — time must be embedded in relational structures capable of carrying intergenerational transmission (family, community, church) to constitute Family Redundancy. A homeless person has unlimited non-market time, but zero Family Redundancy, because they have no relational structure through which time becomes transmission conditions. Conversely, when a homeless person enters a church and rebuilds relationships, their time begins to become Family Redundancy — the church's function here is not transmitting Signals but providing structure.

Family Redundancy is not a number but a set:

**Mother Time** — Mother's non-market time  
&emsp;Traditional: full-time at home; the primary transmitter of Xin  
&emsp;Effect of the Modernization Loop: enters the labor market; time goes to zero

**Child Time** — Children's non-institutionalized time  
&emsp;Traditional: immersed in the family; passively receiving Xin  
&emsp;Effect of the Modernization Loop: daycare → school → cram school → internet; all time institutionalized; only Signals received

**Elder Presence** — Elders' presence within the family  
&emsp;Traditional: three-generation households; living Signal Container + transmitter of Xin; grandmother telling stories, bringing children to rituals, demonstrating how to face death  
&emsp;Effect of the Modernization Loop: nuclearization → nursing homes; intergenerational transmission compressed from three generations to one, then to zero

**Kin Network** — Extended family network  
&emsp;Traditional: extended family, clan, church community; transmission of Xin not relying only on parents — the entire network transmits  
&emsp;Effect of the Modernization Loop: urbanization disperses the family; community functions replaced by the market

**Ritual Time** — Family ritual time  
&emsp;Traditional: Sunday, Sabbath, Ramadan, ancestor worship, festivals; fixed rhythms interrupt the Modernization Loop, forcing the family to enter the frequency of the Transcendence Mechanism  
&emsp;Effect of the Modernization Loop: rituals give way to work and consumption; Christmas becomes a shopping holiday; festivals transform from Transcendence Mechanism signals into Modernization Loop signals

**Family Space** — The family's physical space  
&emsp;Traditional: home is the site of Xin transmission; there are shrines, Bibles, prayer corners  
&emsp;Effect of the Modernization Loop: housing shrinks; space becomes functional; the living room becomes a Signal-receiving station for phones

**The Father's Position.** The father is not the transmitter of Xin but the anchor of family structure. In traditional societies, the father goes out to work — he does not directly transmit Xin; the mother does. But his presence maintains the integrity of the family structure: with the father present, the components of Family Redundancy have carriers; when the father is absent, the structure itself loosens and Family Redundancy's carrying capacity declines. The rate of faith loss in single-parent families is high not because there is one fewer transmitter but because the structure has lost an anchor. The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's effect on the father is not to take him away (he was already working outside) — it is to ensure that the resources he provides no longer convert into Family Redundancy but instead flow directly back into the Modernization Loop. Earning more but spending more; income increases but redundancy does not increase — and may even decrease.

**The Mother's Position.** The mother is not merely one component of Family Redundancy — she is the organizer of Family Redundancy. The other components — Child Time, Ritual Time, Family Space — provide raw materials (time, space, rhythm). The mother structures these raw materials into transmission conditions. When children come home from school, the remaining hours are blank — it is the mother who organizes them into dinner, bedtime stories, weekend activities, holiday rituals. School consumes the daytime; the mother converts the remaining time into Family Redundancy. When other components are consumed, the mother can still sustain transmission with what remains; when the mother is removed, all remaining time loses its organizer and is directly taken over by the Modernization Loop's institutions or screens, and everything collapses. This is why Mother Time comes last in the Sequence of Consumption yet its effect is most lethal — what comes before reduces raw materials; the consumption of Mother Time dismantles the person who organizes the raw materials.

The mother is simultaneously the primary provider of daily immersion. Children do not learn Xin in church — they learn it at home by watching how the mother lives, how she prays, how she faces death. The church provides Signals; the family transmits Xin. Signals without Xin are Noise. Grandparents, elders in the church, even teachers can partially provide transmission conditions — the definitions of Elder Presence and Kin Network themselves imply this — but the mother's dual function (organizer + immersion provider) makes her irreplaceable. Schools — including parochial schools — are secondary Signal-transmission mechanisms of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop: they can transmit knowledge and standardized value Signals, but without relational structure, without immersion, without demonstration, they cannot substitute for the function of Family Redundancy. Students who can recite the Apostles' Creed from a parochial school education does not mean the Religious Positive Feedback Loop is running.

Existing research supports this judgment. Cross-national research from the University of Münster confirms that the family is the primary site of religious socialization, with the mother as the key figure driving transmission. Global research from the University of Oxford provides clear evidence that a cross-cultural relationship between women's employment and secularization exists and has been consistently underestimated in the academic literature.

**The Sequence of Consumption of Family Redundancy.** The components of Family Redundancy do not disappear simultaneously. The expansion of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop advances along two dimensions: first disrupting space (drawing people away from their native environment), then filling time (institutionalizing the remaining non-market time).

Spatial disruption comes first. Traditional societies had no "going to work" — smallholder farmers, craftsmen, and sole traders worked where they lived. The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's first step is drawing people away from their native environments and into factories, offices, and cities. Leaving the village → Kin Network dies (family networks physically dispersed). Leaving parents → Elder Presence dies (intergenerational cohabitation disappears).

Temporal disruption follows. The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop first consumes the most easily consumed time — children's time. Schools are the institutionalized conscription of Child Time by the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop: handing children over to the state and the market, which turn them into labor. Prussia mandated compulsory education in 1763; women entering the labor market en masse was a twentieth-century development. Child Time is removed before Mother Time, not after. The legitimacy of schools preceded the legitimacy of "mothers should go to work" — the Signal "education is good" met no resistance. Schools transmit knowledge and skills; they do not transmit Xin. After children's days are institutionalized, the transmission window is compressed to after school and on weekends — but as long as the mother remains at home, she organizes remaining time into a transmission structure, and the Religious Positive Feedback Loop can still run. Then the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop removes the mother → Mother Time dies (entering the labor market). The organizer disappears; remaining time collapses into blank space, taken over by after-school programs and screens. This step requires breaking the default values of gender roles, so it comes latest — but its destructive power is greatest.

Ritual Time is squeezed on both spatial and temporal dimensions — people are no longer together (spatial), and the time when they are together is filled (temporal). Family Space disappears last, because the house is still there — it is only that it no longer has any transmission function inside.

Complete Sequence of Consumption: Kin Network → Elder Presence → Child Time → Ritual Time → Mother Time → Family Space.

This sequence is not accidental — it is determined by the logic of pressure applied by the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop: it first consumes what meets the least resistance (turning farmers into workers requires only economic incentives), then what meets the most resistance (turning mothers into workers requires the overturning of an entire society's values).

### 3.3 Zero Redundancy State and Market Substitution

When Family Redundancy is consumed down to zero, families enter the Zero Redundancy State. All soft functions — values transmission, emotional connection, ritual maintenance, crisis buffering — are either eliminated or outsourced.

The recipients of outsourcing are markets. The market's Positive Feedback Loop recognizes only one Signal: profit. Daycare does not transmit values — it transmits safety and efficiency. Schools do not transmit Xin — they transmit knowledge and skills. Cram schools do not transmit meaning — they transmit competitiveness.

Every family function outsourced to the market has its semantic end stripped off, leaving only the Signal end. Because semantics cannot be quantified, the market does not pay for what cannot be quantified, nor does it charge for what cannot be quantified.

The complete mechanism of Secularization thus consists of three steps:

Step One: The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop first removes children (schools) then removes mothers (labor market) → family enters Zero Redundancy State

Step Two: Family functions outsourced to the market → the market transmits only Signals, not Xin

Step Three: An entire generation grows up in a purely Signal environment → they acquire knowledge, skills, security, but no Xin → the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's Signals have already shaped the direction of Semantic Flow, establishing a Resistance structure (Epistemic Barrier) → when religious Signals are encountered, Filtering blocks them directly; Signals become Noise → it is not merely "no Xin to undergo Transformation," but the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's Epistemic Barrier actively repelling religious Signals

**Family Redundancy carries more than religion.** The intergenerational transmission carried by Family Redundancy is not only the conditions for the formation of Xin. Values, trust, sense of obligation, attitudes toward death, capacity for long-term thinking — all non-quantifiable intergenerational transmission travels through the same channel. When Family Redundancy reaches zero, the channel breaks, and everything on the channel dies simultaneously — not religion dying first with other things as collateral, but everything dying together. The extinction of religion is visible because it has institutionalized metrics: attendance rates, self-identification, number of churches. The extinction of values is invisible; the extinction of the sense of obligation is invisible; the disappearance of long-term thinking is invisible — you cannot even discuss them, because there are no metrics. The Social Collapse from zero Family Redundancy has two modes of death:

**Slow Death:** intergenerational transmission breaks down → shared values disappear → institutions idle (Signal end normal; semantic end at zero) → fertility rate collapses (the structural conditions for raising the next generation disappear; the costs of reproduction are entirely priced by the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop; returns are entirely non-quantifiable; rational calculation produces "don't reproduce") → population can no longer sustain institutions → collapse.

**Fast Death:** Zero Family Redundancy means all risk buffers are outsourced to the institutions of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop — insurance, hospitals, nursing homes. In traditional societies, when you fall ill, family members care for you; when you grow old, children support you. The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop outsources these functions to the market; things are fine when the Modernization Loop is running normally. But the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is a Positive Feedback Loop, and the characteristic of Positive Feedback Loops is: when running, they absorb everything; when collapsing, they reach zero overnight. A society without Family Redundancy as buffer is exposed to shocks from the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop — the destructive power of the shock depends on its scale. Japan's kodokushi (solitary death) is the terminal image of zero Family Redundancy — people die in apartments and are not discovered for weeks, because no relational structure is monitoring whether they are still alive.

Both modes of death are running simultaneously.

**Delay effect.** Changes in Family Redundancy appear in the Religious Positive Feedback Loop with a generational delay. Current-generation decline in Family Redundancy → weakening of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop in the next generation → possible breakdown of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop in the generation after.

**Post-WWII acceleration.** World War II was a turbocharger for the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop. During the war, the state directly pulled mothers out of families and into factories. Postwar welfare states, consumerism, and the women's rights movement kept them in the labor market. Within a single generation, the nursery had been dismantled. The brief religious revival in late-1940s Europe (the aftershock of Mortality Pressure) was followed by comprehensive decline from the 1960s (the delayed effect of family dissolution). Mortality Pressure is immediate; family dissolution has a generational delay. The two forces point in opposite directions with different timescales; superimposed, the result is a rise followed by a fall.

### 3.4 Path Differentiation: The Three Paths' Responses to Family Redundancy Consumption

**Degeneration of Gu-King Path: co-evolution → degeneration → disappearance.**

The core characteristic of the Gu-King Path is Open Channel plus unlocked Signals; variation is continuously generated. Faced with Family Redundancy consumption, the Gu-King Path's response is to produce new variants adapted to low-redundancy environments. Each round of collision produces variants that move closer to the market than the previous generation.

The nature of this "convergence" needs reinterpreting. The Gu-King Path was not originally market-oriented — it was exclusive elimination. Heresy adjudication, excommunication, the Inquisition: all are institutionalizations of zero-sum competition. You declare that someone's doctrine is wrong, and they must be eliminated. There is no heresy adjudication in a market — only whether you buy or not.

The "adaptation" of the Gu-King Path under Family Redundancy consumption pressure is, in essence, degeneration from exclusive elimination to market competition — abandoning zero-sum competition, beginning to serve customers. Megachurches provide childcare, social activities, entertainment, and financial seminars — supplementing the functions that were outsourced from the family, but using market logic. Prosperity Gospel goes directly to "believing in God will make you rich" — Signal Direction flips from ≠ Modernization Positive Feedback Loop to = Modernization Positive Feedback Loop.

After degenerating to the market stage, the Degeneration of Gu-King Path faces the native market species of the Internalization Path. Meditation, mindfulness, yoga, "spiritual but not religious" — all are market-derived products of the Buddhist Internalization Path. The Gu-King Path comes late to the market; the Internalization Path has always been the market (pure market competition, no heresy adjudication, skillful means = product diversification). In the market, the Gu-King Path cannot outcompete the native species of the Internalization Path, because it still carries the baggage of doctrine — no matter how market-oriented Prosperity Gospel becomes, it still has to mention Jesus Christ; a meditation app does not need to mention anyone's name.

Going deeper: the Degeneration of Gu-King Path does not eliminate demand. The human need for transcendent experience does not disappear with the Degeneration of Gu-King Path — Mortality Pressure remains; the gap in meaning remains. After the Degeneration of Gu-King Path, this demand flows toward substitute products in the market. Derivatives of the Internalization Path (meditation, mindfulness, yoga) carry no doctrinal baggage; Degeneration of Gu-King Path products (megachurches, Prosperity Gospel) still have a cross hanging on them. Those without baggage beat those with baggage in the market.

The terminal state of the Gu-King Path: adaptation until indistinguishable from the market. Not killed — adapted until disappeared. What dies is the religious quality, not the label. The label is the last thing to fall — people still check "Christian," but that check does not mean the Religious Positive Feedback Loop is still running; it means the Signal end has not yet completely detached. The internal dynamics have long since become those of the market.

**The Special Position of Catholicism.** Catholicism's Signal end appears unified (the Pope, the sacramental system), but the semantic end has long since split. South American Catholicism has absorbed large amounts of indigenous elements (Our Lady of Guadalupe, Día de Muertos); European Catholicism is institutionalized and hierarchical. The unity of the Signal end conceals the split in the semantic end. Catholicism's ability to expand globally is precisely because it permits localization of the semantic end, as long as the Signal end is not touched. But this also means: South American Catholicism's semantics have already half-internalized, giving it higher resistance to Family Redundancy consumption than European Catholicism. European Catholicism remains in an externalized institutional form; when Family Redundancy is taken away, it collapses. Where Catholicism is still doing well — South America, the Philippines, Africa — the common feature is not that the church is strong, but that mothers are still at home.

**Tug-of-War of Lock-in Path: refusal → confrontation → repeated tug-of-war → unable to hold.**

The Signal end of the Lock-in Path cannot change. The Quran is the final revelation; the Closed Channel is permanently closed. Faced with Family Redundancy consumption, the Lock-in Path cannot produce new variants to adapt as the Gu-King Path does — because any new variant implies modification of the Signal end, and the Signal end cannot be modified.

The structural response of the Lock-in Path is therefore: not letting the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop enter.

Islam's strict rules about family structure — gender roles, marriage institutions, intergenerational authority — from a dynamics perspective, represent the Lock-in Path protecting its own intergenerational transmission mechanism. Not protecting the church — protecting the family. Let the Modernization Loop in, and the family loosens; the family loosens, and Family Redundancy is consumed; Family Redundancy is consumed, and the transmission of Xin breaks; Xin breaks, and the locked-in Signals become Noise.

The Lock-in Path's means of protecting Family Redundancy is institutionalization — using religious authority to maintain family structure, restricting women's entry into the labor market, maintaining intergenerational authority. But institutionalization itself introduces a risk unique to the Lock-in Path: once the institution holds the power needed to protect Family Redundancy, that power can be corrupted and politically hijacked. The Signal end shifts from "divine revelation" to "tool of the state"; the semantic end is contaminated.

Therefore, the Lock-in Path facing pressure from the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop does not move toward a terminal state — it enters a repeated tug-of-war.

**External Pressure:** The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop forcibly removes Family Redundancy → the Lock-in Path pushes back (revolution, coup, overturn) → re-locks; Family Redundancy is temporarily preserved → but the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop has not disappeared; urbanization, education, and the internet continue drawing away Family Redundancy in the background → Family Redundancy baseline is lower than the previous round

**Internal Corruption:** The institutional power protecting Family Redundancy becomes corrupt → the Signal end is politically hijacked → the next generation actively rejects the entire package → the institution suppresses and pushes back → but each time it pushes back, the Signal end is contaminated more deeply → more people reject it in the next round

Both lines can run simultaneously or alternately. Iran is the complete case: the Shah's External Pressure → the Islamic Revolution overturns and pushes back (re-locking) → the revolutionary regime undergoes Internal Corruption → the younger generation undergoes large-scale secularization → the 2022 headscarf protests → suppression pushes back. Iran is currently running through "what happens after pushing back again" — each round with less Family Redundancy, the cost of holding the line higher. Turkey too: Kemal's forced secularization → religious pushback → military coups suppress it → AKP comes to power and re-locks → Internal Corruption begins running → younger generation secularizes. Each round of tug-of-war, the Lock-in Path operates at a lower Family Redundancy baseline.

The key is the ratchet: the Lock-in Path can push back each time, but it is pushing back against the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop's offensive; it cannot stop the Modernization Loop from continuously consuming Family Redundancy in the background. Revolutionary overturns do not reverse urbanization; suppression does not reverse the internet. Each round, there is less Family Redundancy in hand, the cost of holding the line is higher, until it can no longer hold.

The Lock-in Path and the Gu-King Path die in fundamentally different ways. The Gu-King Path is slowly diluted — diluted until unrecognizable; the label remains but the religious quality is already gone. The Lock-in Path is digital — all-or-nothing, no middle ground. This comes directly from the structure of an unchangeable Signal end: you cannot "believe half the Quran," so you can only believe all of it or none of it. Once you reject it, it is a clean break.

The terminal state of the Tug-of-War of Lock-in Path facing the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop: repeated tug-of-war; each round's Family Redundancy baseline lower; until it can no longer hold. Not a one-time death, but slow hemorrhaging. But the mode of hemorrhage is digital — each round, what is lost is not half a believer, but a whole believer.

**Commercialization of Internalization Path: commercialization → class stratification → cost transfer → physical extinction.**

The Internalization Path is not immune. Earlier analysis described the Internalization Path as "not on the same track" — semantics having entered the lifestyle; Family Redundancy consumption cannot dismantle what has already been internalized. This description is incomplete.

The effect of Family Redundancy consumption on the Internalization Path is not transmission breakdown but structural deformation. The practice of the Internalization Path — meditation, observing precepts, contemplation — requires large amounts of time; time is Family Redundancy. After Family Redundancy is absorbed by the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop, only two types of people can still walk the Internalization Path: monastics (institutionally withdrawing from the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop) and the wealthy with leisure time (Family Redundancy sufficient to simultaneously support both the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop and the Transcendence Mechanism). Ordinary believers have no Family Redundancy; they can only consume services, not genuinely practice.

Buddhism therefore splits into a producer class (the monastic community) and a consumer class (lay believers), with market transactions as the connection between them. Temples become service providers — incense lighting, dharma ceremonies, pacifying the year's bad luck, prayers for the deceased. Believers pay; temples provide rituals; transaction complete. What belongs to the semantic end is compressed into market commodities at the Signal end.

Skillful means originally meant "different methods for people of different capacities"; now it means "different products for people of different purchasing power."

Cost transfer is the most concealed deformation. The Internalization Path originally required each person to walk it themselves — you practice yourself, you realize yourself. When Family Redundancy is insufficient and the path cannot be walked, the cost is transferred to believers: "You don't have time to practice — that's fine; you donate money, and I practice on your behalf." Merit dedication, prayers for the deceased, disaster relief and blessing — these are essentially purchasing others' practice results with money. This is untenable doctrinally (enlightenment is an individual event; it cannot be substituted), but works perfectly in the market.

The Commercialization of Internalization Path is more concealed in its response to Family Redundancy consumption than the Degeneration of Gu-King Path. The Degeneration of Gu-King Path is visible — Prosperity Gospel openly says believing in God brings wealth. The Commercialization of Internalization Path wears traditional robes — temples are still temples, rituals are still rituals, but the internal dynamics have already shifted from individual practice to market transactions.

But commercialization is only the first stage. When the consumption of Family Redundancy deepens further — not just the Zero Redundancy State, but even the fertility rate has collapsed — the Internalization Path moves from dynamic rewriting toward physical extinction. Temple closures are not because Buddhism "lost" — it is because there are no longer even consumers.

Terminal state of Commercialization of Internalization Path: practice becomes a class privilege; popular faith becomes service consumption; when consumers disappear, the form itself collapses.

**Internal differentiation in the strength of the Internalization Path.** The Internalization Path is not monolithic. Tibetan Buddhism incorporates designated channels and institutionalized intergenerational transmission mechanisms (reincarnation of living Buddhas, empowerment transmission lineages, lamas as necessary conditions for practice), giving it far greater resistance to Family Redundancy consumption than the pure Internalization Path. The Tibetan tradition of becoming a monastic is endogenous and embedded in the family structure (sending one son into the monastery is a family obligation), not dependent on external administrative coercion. The Japanese Buddhist Danka system was a purely administrative binding (shogunate law), without channel control — when the administrative binding is removed, it breaks; an endogenous embedding breaks only when the family structure itself is dismantled. Japanese Buddhism is the purest case of the Internalization Path, and thus degenerates most thoroughly. The resistance of the Internalization Path is positively correlated with how many locking elements it incorporates.

**Cost differentiation of market products.** The fate of Internalization Path products in the market depends on cost structure. Products that are low-cost and solve describable problems (meditation, mindfulness — "I'm anxious," "I need to focus") sell well, but are easily replaced by purely market species — meditation apps are cheaper and more convenient than temple meditation sessions. Products that are high-cost and solve non-describable problems (empowerment, transmission lineages — you need to find a lama, build trust, follow long-term) are hard to sell but irreplaceable — because the value is entirely in the semantic end, and the semantic end cannot be marketized. Products of medium cost that solve problems with secular substitutes (Funeral Buddhism) are in the weakest position — the semantic end is already empty; the Signal end has no moat; the secular funeral industry directly competes at more transparent prices. The resistance of the Internalization Path also depends on where its core products fall on this cost spectrum.

### 3.5 Collapse-Restart Cycle

All three paths exist within the cycle.

Family Redundancy sufficient → three paths each operate

Modernization Positive Feedback Loop consumes Family Redundancy → Degeneration of Gu-King Path, Tug-of-War of Lock-in Path, Commercialization of Internalization Path

↓  
Family Redundancy accumulates after the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop collapses (no longer absorbed) → new Transcendence Mechanism grows from new Family Redundancy → new civilizational cycle begins

**Structural limitations of the Great Awakening.** The Great Awakening restarts the church end's Signals but does not rebuild the family end's transmission. The Signal end can restart in a single sermon — thousands of people weeping and coming back to faith — but Family Redundancy cannot be restored in a single sermon. The family structure has not changed; children still grow up online; a generation's revival cannot sustain two generations.

**The spiral is not a cycle.** Each turn of the spiral, Family Redundancy's baseline drops slightly. The family structure rebuilt after the first collapse is slightly looser than before the collapse. The second is looser still. Each restart operates at a lower Family Redundancy baseline, producing a religion more adapted to low Family Redundancy environments — more individualized, less dependent on family, more marketized.

**Irreversibility of the spiral (ratchet effect).** The spiral can only go down, not up. What the ratchet locks in is the share of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop — only increasing, never decreasing. The time and energy consumed by the Modernization Loop never come back: once you have used a flush toilet, you cannot go back to an outhouse; once accustomed to dual-income household earnings, you cannot go back to single-income. The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop has encoded "declining living standards" as the greatest fear; the material expectation baseline is permanently raised. Family Redundancy is the portion left over after the Modernization Loop has eaten. As the Modernization Loop's share continuously expands, Family Redundancy continuously shrinks; whether Family Redundancy is embedded in families or churches does not change this direction. Restoration of Family Redundancy requires the Modernization Loop to give back time and energy; the ratchet does not permit this, so Family Redundancy is restored only after the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop collapses — not a choice you make, but a lack of choice. War, plague, imperial collapse. After Rome, European population dropped to less than half its peak; cities dissolved; manorial economy returned; mothers went back home — not because Christianity convinced them, but because the option of "going to work" no longer existed. The Modernization Loop collapses; the ratchet loosens; Family Redundancy automatically restores; the Transcendence Mechanism grows from new Family Redundancy.

There is no historical case of "actively restoring Family Redundancy while the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is still running." The explosive growth of Korean Christianity is not a counter-example — it occurred after the Korean War, before the economic takeoff, when Mortality Pressure was extremely high and Family Redundancy had not yet been drained. After the economic takeoff, Family Redundancy was drawn away, and Korean Christianity is now also in decline. It is not a spiral upward — it is a normal pulse within the spiral: Mortality Pressure triggers a restart of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop, then the Modernization Loop continues consuming Family Redundancy, and the Religious Positive Feedback Loop continues to weaken.

**The scale of collapse.** This paper's "collapse" is not an economic shock of any size. Shocks come in three scales; only the third triggers the Collapse-Restart Cycle:

Individual unemployment — the Modernization Loop temporarily releases one person's time, but the infrastructure of the Modernization Loop (schools, markets, urban structures) is still running; the ratchet has not loosened at the social level. This person has time but no relational structure to embed in; Family Redundancy does not recover.

Economic crisis — the Modernization Loop partially stalls; a cohort of people is thrown out, but the institution remains and the Modernization Loop will restart. Financial crises pierce through individuals' risk buffers, not civilization's infrastructure. Japan's lost three decades: the Modernization Loop slowed but did not collapse; Family Redundancy did not recover; temples continued dying.

Civilizational collapse — the infrastructure of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is physically destroyed: no schools, no markets, no urban order. The ratchet loosens not because people want to go back but because there is no choice — no factory to go work at, no school to send children to, no market to outsource family functions. Only at this scale does Family Redundancy truly recover. The collapse of Rome was at this scale.

The Collapse-Restart Cycle in this paper refers to the third kind. The first two scales of shock accelerate Slow Death (piercing through individuals, exposing the fragility of zero Family Redundancy) but do not trigger a restart, because the Modernization Loop is still running and the ratchet has not loosened.

**The endpoint of the spiral.** The spiral cannot go infinitely down, because the terminal state of exhausted Family Redundancy is not the individualization of religion — it is civilization itself becoming unsustainable. After the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop collapses, Family Redundancy accumulates again, because there is no Modernization Loop to absorb it. A new Transcendence Mechanism grows from new Family Redundancy, beginning a new civilizational cycle.

**Collapse is not a peaceful transition.** Between the collapse of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop and the rebuilding of Family Redundancy there is no seamless handover — there is violent reckoning. The collapse of the Modernization Loop means overnight disruption of all functions it provided — security, food distribution, healthcare, order. Family Redundancy has already reached zero; family structure is also gone; there is no buffer. The result is violent competition for resources: war, famine, plague, sharp population decline. After the collapse of the Roman Empire, European population fell by more than half from its peak. After the violent reckoning, survivors are forced back into small-scale relational structures — because without markets, if you do not cooperate with others you die. Families, villages, and tribes re-form — not voluntarily, but driven by survival pressure. Family Redundancy at this stage is coerced, built over generations to centuries. Once Family Redundancy has accumulated to a sufficient degree, with Mortality Pressure also extremely high (the aftershock of violent reckoning), a new Religious Positive Feedback Loop has the conditions to start.

The Roman Empire is the complete case of this cycle. Rome's state religion (Christianity after Constantine) was an appendage of the Modernization Loop — Signal Direction = Modernization Positive Feedback Loop; the church was the legitimacy supplier of the empire; episcopal appointments were bound to political power; church expansion was bound to military expansion. The Signal end was Christian; the dynamics were those of the Modernization Loop. This is the early version of Internal Corruption on the Lock-in Path — the Signal end politically hijacked. The Modernization Loop collapses; the state religion dies with it. But the Signal Containers remain (scriptures, creeds, surviving believers). What survived was not the state religion — it was the monasteries. Benedictine monasteries did not depend on imperial order — they were themselves order. In the ruins they rebuilt Family Redundancy: fixed daily schedules (Ritual Time — seven prayers daily), shared living structures (Kin Network), intergenerational transmission (taking in orphans, training apprentices), physical space (the monastery itself), preservation of knowledge (copying scriptures). Monasteries were the post-collapse suppliers of Family Redundancy — the same logic as the Black Church. The medieval Catholicism that grew from the monasteries had a Signal end recognizable from the state religion (the same Bible, the same creed), but completely different dynamics — Signal Direction flipped from = Modernization Positive Feedback Loop to ≠ Modernization Positive Feedback Loop. The Investiture Controversy between the Pope and the emperor is, in essence, a fight to maintain Transcendence Mechanism ≠ Modernization Positive Feedback Loop. This is why Catholicism was able to sustain for a thousand years.

**Post-collapse path competition.** But what grows after collapse is not necessarily the Christian model. The Signal Containers of the three major religions are all sufficiently durable — scriptures do not disappear because civilization collapses; the Quran is memorized in human minds; the Bible has been printed in billions of copies; Buddhist scriptures are stored in temples. What collapse destroys is the Religious Positive Feedback Loop, not the Transcendence Mechanism. The restart of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop will borrow old containers, but the new Religious Positive Feedback Loop is not equivalent to the old. The magnitude of variation is path-dependent — the Gu-King Path varies most each round (Signal end allows rewriting); the Lock-in Path varies least (Signal end cannot be touched; changes are squeezed to the interpretation end); the Internalization Path falls in between (Signal Containers are already multiple and soft).

The post-collapse environment — low Family Redundancy, high Mortality Pressure, no order — is precisely the home turf of the Lock-in Path. The Gu-King Path has already marketized; without the market it is nothing. The Internalization Path has no organizational mobilization capacity. Only the Lock-in Path can immediately provide order in chaos (unchanging Signal end; clear rules) and actively rebuild Family Redundancy (coercive family structure norms, gender roles, intergenerational authority). Islam has historically expanded most rapidly in the ruins of collapsed empires — the Persian Empire's collapse, the dissolution of Roman order in North Africa, the Central Asian nomadic belt.

Catholicism also has structural advantages post-collapse. It is the species within the Gu-King Path closest to the Lock-in Path — the Signal end has unified authority (the Pope), inviolable core doctrines (the sacramental system), and clear family norms (anti-abortion, anti-contraception, anti-divorce). At the same time, the semantic end permits localization, allowing it to grow in the ruins of different cultures. The adaptability of the Gu-King Path plus the resistance of the Lock-in Path — having both sides.

The directional prediction the framework can produce: each Collapse-Restart Cycle, the structural advantage of the Lock-in Path and paths incorporating locking elements (Catholicism) increases, because the Gu-King Path becomes more marketized with each round, the Internalization Path becomes more commercialized with each round, and only the locking structure remains unchanged. But who ultimately prevails depends on the specific conditions of the arena — geography, population, military — which fall outside the framework's jurisdiction.

The complete cycle:

Transcendence Mechanism builds civilization → civilization generates Modernization Positive Feedback Loop → Modernization Positive Feedback Loop absorbs Family Redundancy → Religious Positive Feedback Loop deforms → Family Redundancy exhausted → Transcendence Mechanism fails → civilization loses its interruption mechanism for the Modernization Loop → Modernization Loop runs without resistance → Involution → Modernization Loop itself collapses → Family Redundancy re-accumulates → new Religious Positive Feedback Loop grows → new Transcendence Mechanism → new civilization → ...

**Limitation of causal language.** The arrows describe the sequence of transfer of dominance in the dynamic trajectory, not a linear causal chain. In a Positive Feedback Loop there is no independent variable — the Transcendence Mechanism and civilization constitute each other; the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop and Family Redundancy define each other under zero-sum constraints. "The Modernization Positive Feedback Loop consumes Family Redundancy" is shorthand; the precise expression is: the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop and Family Redundancy compete for the same pool of resources; when the Modernization Loop's share expands, Family Redundancy's share is constrained to shrink. "Family Redundancy consumption dismantles the Religious Positive Feedback Loop" is also shorthand; the precise expression is: the operating conditions of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop (Family Redundancy) are no longer satisfied. This paper uses causal syntax (subject + verb + object) in the main text for readability; the underlying logic is always simultaneous constraint relations, not one end pushing the other in a causal chain.

Family Redundancy is the soil; the Transcendence Mechanism and the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop are both plants growing from that soil. Two plants compete for the same soil. The Modernization Loop's returns are quantifiable; the Transcendence Mechanism's returns are not. The Modernization Loop structurally overwhelms the Transcendence Mechanism in competition. When the soil is exhausted, both die.

## IV. Empirical Verification

### 4.1 Gu-King Path: American White Protestants

**Why this population.** American white Protestants are the purest case of the Gu-King Path. All four Great Awakenings ran through this population. The timeline of Family Redundancy consumption is precise. GSS data begins in 1972, Pew in 2007, and PRRI in 2006, providing data spans sufficient to observe the delay effect. South African Christianity is mixed with colonial and racial variables; South American Catholicism is mixed with localization and internalization elements — neither is clean enough.

**Timeline of Family Redundancy consumption.** The American female labor force participation rate was 33.9% in 1950, rose to 43.3% in 1970, and peaked at 60% in 1999. The participation rate of prime-age women (ages 25–54) was even more dramatic: from 36.8% in 1950 to 76.8% in 1999. The most intense consumption of Mother Time occurred in the 1960s–1980s.

**Verification of the delay effect.** The model predicts that changes in Family Redundancy appear in the Religious Positive Feedback Loop with a generational delay (15–25 years). The large-scale consumption of Mother Time occurred in the 1960s–1980s; the accelerated decline of white Protestants began in the 1990s: white evangelicals declined from 23% in 2006 to 14% in 2020; white mainline Protestants from 18% to 13%. The percentage of American adults identifying as Christian fell from 78% in 2007, to 71% in 2014, to 62% in 2023–24. The time lag is approximately one generation, consistent with the model's predictions.

**Trajectory of the Degeneration of Gu-King Path.** The four Great Awakenings confirm the spiral of restarting at an ever-lower Family Redundancy baseline each round: First Great Awakening (1730s–40s) → emotional revival within the church framework. Second (1790s–1840s) → new denominations (Methodism, Mormonism). Third (1850s–1900s) → Social Gospel movement. Fourth (1960s onward) → megachurches, Prosperity Gospel. Each round produces a version more marketized than the last. Prosperity Gospel is an instance of the Gu-King Path degenerating into the market: Signal Direction = Modernization Positive Feedback Loop — "believe in God and you will prosper." Megachurches provide financial seminars, social activities, and entertainment programs. The Signal end still has a cross on it; the semantic end is already market logic.

**Explaining the "bottoming out and stabilizing."** Pew's 2023–24 data shows that the Christian percentage has stabilized since 2019, fluctuating between 60–64%. This is not a counter-example to the downward spiral — it is precisely the signal that degeneration has completed. What is stabilizing is the Signal-end self-identification label, not the intergenerational transmission of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop. What died is the religious quality, not the label. The label is the last thing to fall. 62% of people still check "Christian," but that check does not mean the Religious Positive Feedback Loop is still running — the internal dynamics have long since become those of the market. The number stabilizes there because degeneration has completed: it is no longer religion, so it also will no longer "continue declining." What declined was religion; what stabilized is a cultural relic.

**Harvesting the spiritual market.** America's "spiritual but not religious" population continues to grow. Their spiritual consumption points almost entirely toward derivatives of the Internalization Path — meditation, mindfulness, yoga, cosmic energy. The human need for transcendent experience does not disappear with the Degeneration of Gu-King Path. After the Degeneration of Gu-King Path, this demand flows toward market substitutes without doctrinal baggage — the native species of the Internalization Path.

**The Black Church as counter-example.** The resistance of American Black Christianity to secularization is far higher than that of white Protestants. The difference is not in doctrine but in the source of Family Redundancy. White Protestants' Family Redundancy was provided by the family; the church only provided Signals — when the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop took away the family's Family Redundancy, the church died with it. The Black Church itself is the supplier of Family Redundancy. Slavery destroyed all of African Americans' Family Redundancy (family networks, intergenerational structures, rituals, language); the Black Church rebuilt Family Redundancy in a Zero Redundancy State environment: community center (Kin Network), intergenerational meeting place (Elder Presence), weekly fixed rhythm (Ritual Time), physical space (Family Space), environment in which children are immersed (Child Time). Black people go to church not because they have leisure time — it is because the church itself is the structure of Family Redundancy. This refines the model: not "American Christianity is in decline," but "Christianity where Family Redundancy is supplied by the family is in decline; Christianity where Family Redundancy is supplied by the church itself is still alive." The source of Family Redundancy need not be the family — any institution that provides relational structure can become a supplier of Family Redundancy.

**People still going to church in the deepest stages of modernization.** This is not a counter-example to the model. Even in the deepest stages of modernization, Mortality Pressure remains — the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop extends lifespan but does not cancel death. Those who still go to church correspond to three different mechanisms. First, Mortality Pressure triggers: a cancer diagnosis, bereavement, a midlife crisis directly opens Step Zero; the Individual actively seeks Signals. But without Family Redundancy as support, a generation's revival cannot sustain two generations. Second, the church provides Family Redundancy: people go to church because they are lonely; the church provides community and belonging; the driver is not Step Zero but the need for Family Redundancy — this is the consumer behavior of "the label remains but the religious quality is gone." Third, surviving Family Redundancy: some families' Family Redundancy has not yet reached zero (grandmother still brings grandchildren to church; Sunday still goes to church); the Religious Positive Feedback Loop is still running, but the model predicts they are on a countdown. The trend is the second replacing the third — churches shift from carriers of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop to suppliers of Family Redundancy; religious quality disappears; the community function remains.

### 4.2 Lock-in Path: Turkey

**Why this population.** Turkey is the case with the longest tug-of-war between the Lock-in Path and the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop, with the cleanest data. Since Kemal's forced secularization in 1923, the Lock-in Path and the Modernization Loop have been in tug-of-war on the same population for a hundred years — and the tug-of-war has not yet ended. Each round has a clear historical record: Kemal's forced secularization (External Pressure) → religious pushback → military coups suppress it (External Pressure again) → AKP comes to power and re-locks (pushing back) → Internal Corruption begins running (younger generation secularizing). Indonesian Muslims have never followed a pure locking path; the variables are too dirty. Turkey provides a complete tug-of-war process — the strongest test of the model.

**Iran's supplementary value.** Iran ran the same tug-of-war but with clearer rounds: the Shah's forced modernization (External Pressure) → the Islamic Revolution overturns and pushes back (re-locking) → the revolutionary regime undergoes institutional corruption (Internal Corruption) → the younger generation undergoes large-scale secularization → the 2022 headscarf protests → suppression pushes back. Iran is currently running through "what happens after pushing back again" — each round with less Family Redundancy, the cost of holding the line higher.

**Consumption of Family Redundancy and the locking tug-of-war.** Turkish women's labor force participation shows an unusual trajectory: from approximately 30% in the 1990s, first declining to a low of 23% in 2005, then rising back to 36% in 2024. This rise-then-fall trajectory directly reflects the operation of the Lock-in Path: urbanization drew rural women away from the fields (Kin Network dies first), but the Lock-in Path kept them at home in cities (Mother Time temporarily preserved), so the female participation rate actually declined first — not the Modernization Loop retreating, but the Lock-in Path activating protection of Mother Time in the urban environment. But the Modernization Loop continued applying pressure; after 2005, the Mother Time defense line began to be breached, and the female participation rate rose again.

**Verification of External Pressure and pushback.** Both Kemal's forced secularization and the Iranian Shah's case confirm the first stage of tug-of-war: the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop forcibly removes Family Redundancy through state power → the Lock-in Path pushes back (revolution, coup, overturn). Turkey's multiple military coups to maintain the secular system are each instances of the Modernization Loop attempting to force its way in — each time the Lock-in Path pushed back, but each time Family Redundancy's baseline was lower.

**Verification of Internal Corruption.** This is the most important contribution of the Turkey case to the model. The KONDA 2024 survey shows that Turks who consider themselves "devout" fell from 55% in 2008 to 46% in 2025; those with no religion/atheists rose from 2% to 8%. More critically, this decline occurred against the backdrop of AKP's twenty years in power vigorously promoting religiosity.

Turkey's own Ministry of Education reports acknowledge that "young people are sliding toward deism." Even within imam-hatip religious schools — schools specifically for training religious personnel — students are turning toward deism and atheism. One Islamic studies professor reported that within a single year, 17 female students who still wore headscarves privately told him they had left Islam and considered themselves atheists. A Gezici Research Center survey in 2020 found that 28.5% of Turkish Generation Z identify as having no religion.

Academic research confirms that "despite AKP prioritizing religious education, expanding the Directorate of Religious Affairs' budget and influence, and raising the visibility of Islamic non-state actors, Turkish youth are actually becoming more secular, less devout, and more liberal on marriage and women's issues." Observers note that the politicization and state institutionalization of religion itself drives people toward secular identity — "government control of religion and state supply typically alienates people from religious belief."

This is not the slow dilution of the Gu-King Path. This is the digital break of the Lock-in Path: the Signal end cannot change, so you cannot "half-believe"; you can only all-believe or all-refuse. After the institution turns the Signal end into a political tool, what the young generation rejects is not "this version of Islam" (that would be the Gu-King Path response — switch versions), but the entire package. A clean break.

**The relationship between digital break and population-level data.** Population-level data shows gradual change — "devout" declined from 55% to 46% over 17 years. This is not a counter-example to the Lock-in Path. The Lock-in Path's "digital" character describes the decision structure of Individuals: Islam's behavioral requirements are interlocked — five daily prayers, dietary restrictions, Ramadan, dress codes — you cannot remove just one piece while keeping the rest. Once you stop complying, the entire behavioral structure cannot sustain itself. At the Individual level it is a switch: from complying to not complying, a clean break. But different Individuals break at different times; aggregated to the population level, this appears as a gradual curve. A cluster of switches flipping at different moments shows as gradual change statistically, but is digital at the mechanistic level. The Gu-King Path's gradual change is different — the Individual itself can half-believe, go to church on Sunday but not pray during the week, call themselves Christian but not read the Bible. The Degeneration of Gu-King Path's dilution occurs within Individuals; the Lock-in Path's break occurs between Individuals.

**Current state of the tug-of-war.** Turkey is simultaneously experiencing External Pressure and Internal Corruption: urbanization continues drawing Family Redundancy in the background (the ratchet keeps turning); AKP's religious politicization contaminates the Signal end (the Signal is hijacked). The Lock-in Path has pushed back each time — but each time there is less Family Redundancy in hand, and the rejection by younger generations is more large-scale. The speed of secularization is even faster than what the model predicts based on a single mechanism, because both lines are running simultaneously.

### 4.3 Internalization Path: Japanese Buddhism

**Why this population.** Japanese Buddhism has the most complete degeneration trajectory of the Internalization Path. The Meiji Restoration (1868) is the precise entry point of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop. From the Danka system (Family Redundancy sufficient; temples embedded in family structures) to Funeral Buddhism (Zero Redundancy State; temples left with only funeral services), to the current physical extinction of temples — each stage of degeneration has a clear historical record. Taiwanese Buddhism is mixed with folk religion; Thai Buddhism is mixed with the royal institution — neither is clean enough.

**The institutionalized form of Family Redundancy: the Danka system.** Japanese Buddhism's Family Redundancy had a unique institutionalized form. During the Edo period (1600–1868), the Tokugawa shogunate required every family to be registered with a Buddhist temple (the Danka system). Families supported temples across generations; temples provided funeral and ancestor veneration services to families. This system embedded all components of Family Redundancy into the temple-family binding: Kin Network (family networks bound through shared temples), Elder Presence (ancestor veneration maintaining intergenerational connection), Ritual Time (annual memorial services, Obon festival, monthly death anniversary services), Family Space (the home altar, butsudan). The Danka system was the embodiment of Family Redundancy — the intergenerational transmission of Xin was not spontaneous, but institutionalized into the fixed relationship between family and temple.

**Trajectory of Family Redundancy consumption.** After the Meiji Restoration, the Danka system lost its legal enforcement but continued by inertia into the postwar period. Postwar urbanization and nuclearization dismantled this structure: young people leaving rural areas (Kin Network death) → nuclear family replacing extended family (Elder Presence breakdown) → monthly memorial service customs disappear, especially in cities (Ritual Time consumption) → home altars no longer maintained (Family Space formal disappearance).

**From commercialization to physical extinction.** After the Danka system collapsed, temples lost the foundation of being embedded in family structure; only funeral services remained as a revenue source. This is "Funeral Buddhism" — Buddhism appearing only when someone dies. Funeral practices became increasingly commercialized; the role of Buddhist doctrine in funerals became increasingly vague. Monks themselves feel they are "like actors on stage" or "pawns of the funeral industry." A new secular funeral industry began competing, further squeezing the temples' last market.

Data confirms the trend toward physical extinction: between 1989 and 2019, at least 23 million people severed their connections with Buddhist temples. Within the next 25 years, an estimated 27,000 of Japan's 77,000 temples are predicted to close. In the Sōtō Zen sect (one of the major Zen sects, with 14,000 temples), the number of temples without resident priests increased by approximately 30% between 1985 and 2005; approximately 35% of temples have no successor. Some surveys predict that by 2040, 35% of Japan's religious corporations will disappear.

**Verification of and extension to the model.** Japanese Buddhism perfectly confirms the Internalization Path's degeneration sequence: when Family Redundancy is sufficient, temples are embedded in the family structure (Danka system = institutionalized form of Family Redundancy) → Modernization Positive Feedback Loop dismantles the family structure → Zero Redundancy State → temples become service providers (Funeral Buddhism) → form preserved, dynamics rewritten.

But the Japanese case also reveals a stage the initial version of the model did not fully predict: when Family Redundancy consumption deepens to the level of population collapse, the Internalization Path no longer merely undergoes "commercialization in Buddhist robes" — it moves toward physical extinction. Temple closures are not because Buddhism "lost" — it is because there are no longer even consumers. Japan's declining birth rate has pushed Family Redundancy's floor to an extreme — not just Zero Redundancy State, but the family itself is disappearing. This goes beyond the model's standard assumptions (the model assumes population exists), but it can serve as a real-time case of the spiral endpoint: the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop does not only consume Family Redundancy — it ultimately consumes the population itself.

The model's prediction: Catholicism lives where Family Redundancy is sufficient, and dies where Family Redundancy is exhausted.

Observed: at the beginning of the twentieth century, Europe had 65% of Catholics; this has fallen to approximately 20% now. African Catholics continue explosive growth. Where Catholicism is still doing well — South America, the Philippines, Africa — the common feature is that Family Redundancy is still sufficient, and mothers are still in the family's transmission structure. European Catholicism died not because Europeans stopped believing in God, but because Family Redundancy reached zero.

## V. Boundaries

**Problems this paper does not address:**

First, Exclusion of Internal Mechanisms. That is the jurisdiction of *Involution Dynamics of East Asia*. This paper only receives the effects of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop and does not explain why the Modernization Loop operates as it does.

Second, Exclusion of Truth and Value Judgments. This inherits the stance of the original paper — the framework does not judge whether God exists or whether enlightenment is real.

Third, value judgments. Mother Time → 0 is the value of a variable, not a judgment of good or bad. Whether the institutional means by which the Lock-in Path protects Family Redundancy are legitimate falls outside the jurisdiction of dynamics.

Fourth, policy recommendations. "Rebuilding Family Redundancy" is not an operation that can be executed by policy. In history, the recovery of Family Redundancy has occurred only spontaneously after the collapse of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop; there are no cases of "actively restoring Family Redundancy while the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop is still running."

Fifth, time prediction. How long each round of the spiral takes — the framework cannot derive this.

Sixth, populations outside the selected cases. This paper selects three of the purest cases to test the mechanism and does not claim the model can be directly applied to all populations. South American Catholicism (semantic end half-internalized), Indonesian Muslims (impure locking), and Taiwanese Buddhism (mixed with folk religion) are test objects for model boundaries, not within the scope of this paper.

**Structural Limitations of the Framework:**

The core mechanism of this paper — consumption of Family Redundancy, breakdown of Xin's intergenerational transmission — occurs at the semantic end. The semantic end cannot be quantified; this is not a defect of this framework but the structural ceiling of human cognition. The components of Family Redundancy can be given proxy indicators (female labor force participation, proportion of nuclear families, religious ritual attendance), but proxy indicators quantify performance at the Signal end, not transmission at the semantic end. "Whether the mother is at home" can be quantified; "whether the mother has transmitted Xin" cannot.

**Falsifiable Conditions:**

One. If someone can explain the different responses of the three paths to modernization pressure using a theory that does not involve Family Redundancy — such as purely cognitive change, institutional inertia, or economic rationality — with explanatory power equal to or better than this paper's, then this paper's core model is weakened.

Two. If the Gu-King Path produces a new variant that does not degenerate toward the market — maintaining exclusive elimination and zero-sum competition in a low Family Redundancy environment — then the prediction of Degeneration of Gu-King Path is falsified.

Three. If the Lock-in Path successfully coexists with modernization — the Signal end unchanged but Family Redundancy is allowed to be consumed, yet intergenerational transmission does not break down — then the zero-sum structure prediction of the Lock-in Path is falsified. More specifically, if after the Lock-in Path's institutional power is politically hijacked, the next generation does not actively reject but instead produces new adaptive variants, then the prediction of Internal Corruption is falsified.

Four. If the Internalization Path maintains the universality of practice — rather than stratifying it by class — in a Zero Redundancy State environment, such that ordinary believers can still engage in substantive practice rather than only consuming services in a Zero Redundancy State, then the prediction of Commercialization of Internalization Path is falsified.

Five. Challenge to the underlying framework. This paper inherits all the Falsifiable Conditions of Semantic Flow Dynamics and *Religion as a System of Information Flow*. If the underlying framework is overturned, the superstructure of analysis simultaneously fails.

Six. If a society in a Zero Redundancy State, through a completely new mechanism — for example, a digitalized immersion environment, AI-driven semantic transmission, virtual communities — provides the conditions for the intergenerational transmission of Xin with effects equivalent to what family and church provided as Family Redundancy, then the status of Family Redundancy as the core variable is undermined. No such case currently exists, but technological development may produce one in the future.

## VI. Relationship to Existing Literature

### 6.1 Inglehart & Norris: Existential Security Theory

Inglehart attributes secularization to the enhancement of existential security — modern technology extends lifespans and reduces infant mortality; Mortality Pressure weakens; demand for religion decreases.

This paper's model complements Inglehart but goes deeper. What Inglehart describes is the weakening of Mortality Pressure; what this paper describes is the collapse of the transmission mechanism of Xin. The two are mechanisms at different levels and can operate simultaneously: weakening Mortality Pressure reduces the driving force of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop; Family Redundancy consumption cuts off the transmission channel of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop. But Family Redundancy consumption is more fundamental than Mortality Pressure reduction — even if Mortality Pressure rises (war, epidemic), if Family Redundancy has already reached zero, the Religious Positive Feedback Loop still cannot restart, because the transmission channel is gone. The Great Awakening occurs every time Mortality Pressure rises, but cannot sustain two generations — precisely because it only restarts the driving force, without rebuilding the transmission channel.

### 6.2 Roof / Hervieu-Léger / Heelas & Woodhead

Roof (1999) observed the spiritual marketplace. Hervieu-Léger observed the cultural amnesia of intergenerational transmission. Heelas & Woodhead (2005) observed the shift of spirituality toward new forms.

These three observations each touched on one fragment of this paper's model. This paper's contribution is not in discovering the fragments, but in using path differentiation to connect them into a causal chain: Family Redundancy consumption → breakdown of the intergenerational transmission of Xin (what Hervieu-Léger saw) → Gu-King Path degenerates into the market (what Roof saw) → demand flows to the native species of the Internalization Path (what Heelas & Woodhead saw) → the demand was cultivated by the Gu-King Path itself (what nobody saw).

### 6.3 Interface with *Involution Dynamics of East Asia*

*Involution Dynamics of East Asia* treats the Transcendence Mechanism as a black box and demonstrates that whether the Transcendence Mechanism is present determines whether a society is crushed by the Modernization Loop. This paper treats the Modernization Loop as a black box and analyzes how the Modernization Loop dismantles the Religious Positive Feedback Loop.

The two papers can be joined: Modernization Positive Feedback Loop consumes Family Redundancy → Family Redundancy consumption dismantles the Religious Positive Feedback Loop → after the Religious Positive Feedback Loop breaks down, the Transcendence Mechanism no longer produces social effects → society loses its interruption mechanism for the Modernization Loop → the Modernization Loop runs without resistance → Involution. The Secularization Mechanism (failure of the Transcendence Mechanism) is not an independent event — it is the downstream product of the Modernization Loop consuming Family Redundancy and dismantling the Religious Positive Feedback Loop.

## VII. Conclusion: The Dynamics of Civilizational Death

On the surface, this paper is tracing the degeneration of religion. Three paths, three cases, three response patterns. But tracing to the end, what is being traced is not religion — it is Family Redundancy.

Family Redundancy is not the exclusive fuel of religion. Family Redundancy is the soil of civilization. Mother Time, family redundancy, intergenerational cohabitation — this channel carries not only the transmission conditions of Xin, but all non-quantifiable intergenerational transmission: values, aesthetics, attitudes toward death, sense of obligation to others, the capacity for long-term thinking. When Family Redundancy reaches zero, the channel breaks, and everything on the channel dies simultaneously. The extinction of religion is visible — attendance rates, self-identification percentages, church closure numbers — so this paper tracks it. But other things on the same channel are dying equally; you cannot measure them, and you can barely even discuss them, because there are no metrics. Religion's demise is visible because it has institutionalized indicators; the demise of values, the sense of obligation, and long-term thinking are invisible — you cannot even find the right metrics to discuss them.

Three papers thus represent three perspectives on the same story. *Religion as a System of Information Flow* identified the Religious Positive Feedback Loop — the three paths of the religious positive feedback loop, i.e., the mechanism by which the Transcendence Mechanism is amplified into civilizational structure. *Involution Dynamics of East Asia* defined the Transcendence Mechanism and demonstrated that whether the Transcendence Mechanism is present determines whether a society is crushed by the Modernization Loop. This paper traced how the Modernization Loop dismantles the Religious Positive Feedback Loop through the consumption of Family Redundancy, thereby causing the Transcendence Mechanism to fail.

Put together, this is one sentence:

**Civilizational Life Cycle:**  
Transcendence Mechanism builds civilization → civilization generates Modernization Positive Feedback Loop → Modernization Positive Feedback Loop consumes Family Redundancy → Family Redundancy consumption dismantles the Religious Positive Feedback Loop → Transcendence Mechanism fails  
→ civilization loses its interruption mechanism for the Modernization Loop  
→ Modernization Loop runs without resistance → Involution → Modernization Loop itself collapses  
→ Family Redundancy re-accumulates → new Religious Positive Feedback Loop grows → new Transcendence Mechanism → new civilization

(Arrows indicate the sequence of transfer of dominance, not linear causation. See Section 3.5, "Limitation of Causal Language.")

This is not the story of religion. This is the Civilizational Life Cycle.

Family Redundancy is the soil; the Transcendence Mechanism and the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop are both plants growing from that soil. The Transcendence Mechanism grows first, establishing civilization's framework — community, meaning, family structure, a solution for facing death. The Modernization Loop grows from civilization's framework, using the social infrastructure established by the Transcendence Mechanism to begin self-reinforcement. Two plants compete for the same soil. The Modernization Loop's returns are quantifiable; the Transcendence Mechanism's returns are not. The Modernization Loop structurally overwhelms the Transcendence Mechanism in competition. When the soil is exhausted, both die.

The spiral is Irreversible because the Modernization Loop permanently raises the material expectation baseline. Nobody voluntarily returns. The recovery of Family Redundancy only occurs after the Modernization Loop collapses — not a choice, but no choice.

We are now inside the spiral. The three cases are not historical narratives — they are what is currently happening. The degeneration of American white Protestants is complete; the label is still there, the religious quality is gone. Turkey's Lock-in Path is cracking from the inside; Generation Z is making clean breaks. Japanese Buddhism's temples are physically dying; there are no longer even consumers.

There is no exit without collapse. Not out of pessimism — but because the ratchet effect does not permit Family Redundancy to recover while the Modernization Loop is running, and the Modernization Loop will not voluntarily stop — Positive Feedback Loops have no built-in brakes.

The only thing that can be certain: after collapse, Family Redundancy will accumulate again; a new Religious Positive Feedback Loop will grow. It will borrow old Signal Containers — scriptures remain, buildings remain, surviving believers remain — but the new Religious Positive Feedback Loop is not equivalent to the old one. The Signal end is recognizable; the dynamics are completely different. Just as medieval Catholicism and Roman state religion read the same Bible, but one was an appendage of the Modernization Loop and the other was an independent force where Transcendence Mechanism ≠ Modernization Positive Feedback Loop. What grows next time still answers the same question: people will die — and then what?

**Prediction.** The above reasoning is built on a premise: after the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop collapses, there exists a window period that allows Family Redundancy to re-accumulate and a new Religious Positive Feedback Loop to grow. Historically this window period was measured in centuries, because knowledge and technology were largely lost in collapse — Rome's concrete formula was lost for a thousand years. But modern knowledge storage no longer depends on the continuous operation of human social structures. Papyrus burns; libraries need librarians; but a hard drive buried in the ground for a hundred years can still be read when dug up. Knowledge storage surviving collapse compresses the restart of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop from centuries to perhaps a decade or so — but rebuilding infrastructure and talent still takes time (factories destroyed in war, people need time to train). The rebuilding of Family Redundancy after collapse is coerced by survival pressure; the restart of the Religious Positive Feedback Loop can borrow old Signal Containers (scriptures, surviving institutions), without needing to start from zero. The ultimate result is a race between the accumulation of Family Redundancy and the restart of the Modernization Positive Feedback Loop — if the window is long enough, a new Religious Positive Feedback Loop grows; if the window is too short, the Modernization Loop restarts and absorbs everything before Family Redundancy has accumulated, and the spiral loses its recovery phase. The outcome of this race — the framework cannot derive it.
